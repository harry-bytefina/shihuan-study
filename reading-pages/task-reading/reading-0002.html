Passage 2(2025天津第一次) 主题：个人成长

学习、阅读与教育方式

In the small town where I grew up, graduating from high school was already a big thing. But I was driven to pursue my education and become a lawyer like my best friend's dad, one of the few role models available. After completing my first degree at a university near my parents' house, I started my graduate study of law at the University of North Carolina, as I had planned for the past 15 years.

It was my 23rd birthday, and I was celebrating it as I had for years: by watching the annual Perseid meteor shower(英仙座流星雨). But this year was different. I was away from the small town for the first time and was meeting people in fields that were new to me. My horizons had been opened up, and I began to question my life path. Should I stick to my long-standing plans? Could my lifelong love for astronomy be more than a hobby?

Coincidentally, a solar eclipse(日食) helped me make the decision. I made a trip along the winding mountain roads and found a peaceful spot in a valley. When the Moon gradually slid in front of the Sun, the world around me became dark. The water was still, and the sunset surrounded me. That moment, I knew what I had to do. I dropped out of law school to pursue my passion.

Now, about to get my doctoral degree in astronomy, I realize that my exposure to the night sky gave me a great gift. It bestowed a broad view: In the vast universe, our individual desires and insecurities are insignificant. It liberated me from allowing worries or self-doubt to stand in the way of chasing new dreams.

During this year's Perseid meteor shower, I'll be presenting my research at an international conference in Cape Town. It will be my first trip out of the country. And the little girl inside me who watched the shooting stars from her parents' backyard can't wait to watch the same stars flash across the South African sky.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Why did the author want to become a lawyer? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. (Because) her friend's father, her role model, was a lawyer. Or: (Because) her best friend's dad was a lawyer. Or: (Because) her role model was a lawyer. 细节表达题。根据文章第一段中“But I was driven to pursue my education and become a lawyer like my best friend's dad, one of the few role models available.”可知,作者想要成为律师的原因是她将朋友的父亲视为自己的榜样,而朋友的父亲是一名律师。

2How were the author's horizons expanded? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. She was away from the small town and met people in new fields. 细节表达题。根据文章第二段中“I was away from the small town for the first time and was meeting people in fields that were new to me.”可知,作者第一次离开家乡,遇到了不同领域的人,这些领域于她而言是全新的,以此拓宽了自己的视野。

3What decision did the author make after watching the solar eclipse? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. She dropped out of law school to pursue her passion. 细节表达题。根据文章第三段“Coincidentally,a solar eclipse helped me make the decision...That moment, I knew what I had to do. I dropped out of law school to pursue my passion.”可知,观测日食的经历让作者下定决心放弃法律专业追寻自己热爱的天文学。

4What does the underlined word mean in Paragraph 4? (1 word)

查看答案与讲解

4. Gave./Presented./Offered. 猜词表达题。根据上文的“my exposure to the night sky gave me a great gift”和下文的“In the vast universe, our individual desires and insecurities are insignificant.”可知,作者意识到观看夜空的经历于她而言是种礼物,给了她开阔的人生观,即在浩瀚的宇宙之中,我们个人的欲望与不安都显得微不足道。由此可推断,bestowed意为“给予”“赠送”。

5What do you think is the key factor in choosing your college major? Please explain why. (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. I think passion is the key factor because it motivates me to work hard and chase my dream. Or: My future career. Because I need to acquire relevant knowledge and professional skills at university in preparation for career success.

外刊延伸

阅读难度参考：CSE 6

2026普利策奖得主李翊云：“我不想从丧子之痛中解脱”

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    'I Don't Ever Want to Be Free From the Pain of Missing My Children'

    我永远不愿从思念子女的痛楚中解脱

  2. 02

    When four police officers arrived at Yiyun Li's home in Princeton, N.J., late on a Friday afternoon last February, she didn't wait for instructions to sit down. Her son James, a freshman at Princeton University, had died, struck by a train near the campus. A little more than six years earlier, James's brother Vincent died by suicide at age 16, also killed by an oncoming train nearby.

    今年二月一个周五的傍晚，四名警察来到李翊云（Yiyun Li）位于新泽西州普林斯顿的家中，不等警察示意，她便自己坐了下来。她的小儿子詹姆斯（James），普林斯顿大学大一新生，在学校附近遭火车撞击不幸离世。而就在六年多前，詹姆斯的哥哥文森特（Vincent）在16岁时自杀离世，同样在附近遭迎面驶来的火车撞击致死。

  3. 03

    After the officers left, Li and her husband, Dapeng Li, sat in their living room, stunned. She felt like time was collapsing around her, as though she was stuck in an eternal present. No words could capture the devastation she felt, losing both of her sons. But Li knew that words were the only way to anchor her thoughts to reality. Three months after James's death, Li started writing "Things in Nature Merely Grow," a memoir about James, Vincent and how their lives and deaths intertwined. She refuses to torture herself with endless regretful "what if" questions, choosing instead to accept her sons' decisions as an unalterable fact.

    警员离开后，李翊云和丈夫李大鹏（Dapeng Li）坐在客厅里，呆滞茫然。她感觉周遭的时间正在崩塌，感觉自己仿佛被困在永恒的当下。痛失两个儿子的悲恸，无以言表。但她明白，文字是唯一能把自己的思绪锚定在现实里的东西。詹姆斯离世三个月后，李翊云开始动笔写作《万物自然生长》（Things in Nature Merely Grow）这本书，这是一本回忆录，关于詹姆斯和文森特两个儿子，关于他们的生与死如何交织在一起。她拒绝用“假如当初”的无尽追问自我折磨，而是选择接受，儿子们的决定是无法更改的事实。

    devastation
    n. 极度悲痛，崩溃
    anchor
    v. 稳住人的思想、情绪
    unalterable
    adj. 无法更改的
    collapse
    v. 崩塌，倒塌
    intertwine
    v. 事物互相纠缠，交织在一起
    capture
    v. 精准捕捉，贴切表达；捕捉
  4. 04

    In some ways, Li's memoir is a radical rebuke of the conventions surrounding grieving. Early on, she warns those who expect a narrative of healing or solace to stop reading: This is not a story about overcoming loss or moving on. She chooses to stick firmly to her daily routine, returning to teaching, writing and gardening to steady her mind amid inconsolable sorrow.

    在某种意义上，这本回忆录彻底颠覆了围绕着哀伤的固有范式。早些时候，她警告那些期待看到疗愈和慰藉型叙事的人停止阅读：这不是一部克服失去、继续前行的故事。她坚守日常作息，重返教学与写作，重新打理花草，以此在无法抚平的悲痛中稳住心神。

    inconsolable
    adj. 不可抚慰，无法抚平的
    convention
    n. 约定俗成的一些传统、做法
  5. 05

    "I don't ever want to be free from the pain of missing my children," Li told me. "This pain is in my life for ever and ever, and I don't want to do anything to mitigate the pain, because to mitigate it means that's something bad, it's an illness or affliction."

    “我永远不想从思念孩子的痛苦中解脱出来。”李翊云说道，“这份痛苦会一直存在于我生命中，我也不愿为了减轻这份痛楚而做任何事，因为刻意减轻它，就意味着这份伤痛是个不好的东西，它是一种病痛，或者一个磨难.”

  6. 06

    Li told me she often senses that her circumstances make people uncomfortable, and she never thinks she can live according to other people's expected narrative of grief. What's perhaps most surprising is her ability to exist in two incompatible realities: one where she lives in a desolate abyss, and another where she finds fulfillment, amusement and even joy in her work, her friendships and her marriage.

    李翊云告诉我，她经常能感到，自己的状况会让其他人感到不舒服，她也不认为她可以按照别人所期待的那种对于悲伤的叙事来生活。最令人惊讶的恐怕是她同时存在于两种互不兼容的现实之间的能力：在其中一处，她身处荒芜孤寂的深渊；而在另一处，她能在工作、友情与婚姻里，找到满足、趣味乃至快乐。

    句子结构

    What's perhaps most surprising / is her ability to exist in two incompatible realities: / one where she lives in a desolate abyss, / and another where she finds fulfillment, amusement and even joy in her work, her friendships and her marriage.

    What’s perhaps most surprising 整体作主语，what 本身又是从句的主语。冒号后 one 和 another 分别代指两种处境；两个 where 说的都是她在各自处境中的感受，where 相当于 in which，并不指实际地点。

  7. 07

    "We're sad, we're very sad, but we're not unhappy," she said. "So long as we live, we carry our love for the children, even though they're not here."

    “我们很悲伤，我们非常悲伤，但我们没有不快乐。” 她说，“只要我们还活着，我们就会一直怀有对孩子的爱，哪怕他们已经不在这里。”

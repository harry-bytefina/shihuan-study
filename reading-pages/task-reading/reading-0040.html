阅读表达

02-2019天津春

学习、阅读与教育方式

My teacher Peter Bullock proved that science wasn't boring and made physics great fun. In every lesson he would produce one of those worksheets in purple ink that smelled fresh from the machine. Every sheet contained a poem or a joke and we looked forward to them being handed out. I've kept them all to show to my own children. There was one poem in particular that's stayed in my memory, which went:

In early days Man measured Time

By sunrise and sunset sublime;

...

As well as being able to turn physics into poetry, Peter had energy and passion and conveyed the sense that science was part of the world around you and not a dull academic discipline. I'm sure he was sticking pretty much to the standard topics—waves and electricity and so on—but he managed to convert everything into a joke or a story and did so in a clever and imaginative way.

Peter Bullock was not only an exciting teacher,but also the man who founded the debate club in my school. He encouraged me to join it and express myself in a forceful way that I've been doing ever since—and getting paid for it. That made me what I am today—a commercial lawyer.

Peter Bullock taught me throughout my secondary schooling and I took physics, chemistry and maths at A-level. Everyone in his class enjoyed learning. He was inspiring. He turned me on to the idea that the things you are interested in might seem dull on the surface, but you can still excite others. For example, when I tell people I'm interested in law, they may think:“How boring.” I guess being a physics teacher is much the same. Peter showed me that what matters is how you convey your own passion so that you excite other people.

不植发，不吃药，亮出光头的男人终于与脱发和解

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    No Hair Transplants, Pills or Toupees. Meet The Men Embracing Baldness.

    不做植发，不吃药，不戴假发。来认识一下这些拥抱秃顶的男士们。

    transplant
    移植。hair transplants 指把毛囊移到脱发部位的植发手术。
    toupee
    男士遮盖秃发处用的发片，通常只覆盖头顶的一部分。
  2. 02

    If you’re a man on the internet in 2026, there’s a good chance you’ve been flooded with ads for ways to help you cling to your hair. Supplements, sprays and red-light masks promise “real results!” PRP therapy, a buzzy treatment involving plasma injections into the scalp, can be administered on a lunch break.

    如果你是生活于2026年会上网的一名男性，那么你很可能已经被各种承诺帮你保住头发的广告淹没了。各种营养补充剂、喷雾和红光面罩都承诺“绝对有效！”PRP疗法（一种将血浆注射到头皮中的热门疗法）甚至可以在午休时间进行。

    administer
    给人用药或施行治疗。can be administered 说这种治疗可以安排做。
    buzzy
    眼下热议、很受关注的。这里形容广告里正流行的一种治疗。
  3. 03

    Balding is framed as “a disease to get rid of,” said Ben Sherry, 27, a lifestyle influencer. To keep his hair, Sherry tried several methods—finasteride pills, a dermarolling device that pricks the scalp to increase blood flow, and a toupee, among others—before shaving it all off in April. The video of him doing so has been viewed a million times on Instagram.

    27岁的生活方式博主本·谢里（Ben Sherry）表示，脱发被视为“一种需要根治的疾病”。为了保住头发，谢里尝试了多种方法，包括非那雄胺片、一种通过刺扎头皮来增加血流量的微针滚轮装置、以及假发等，最终他于今年4月彻底剃光了所有头发。他在Instagram上发布的剃头视频已被观看了一百万次。

  4. 04

    He is among a cohort of men who have had enough of hiding under hats and obsessively monitoring hairlines. Instead, they’re reaching for the clippers and showing off their bald heads IRL at festivals and meetups, and on social-media accounts like Bald Café and Bld Bro, where they exchange vulnerable transformation photos and virtual fistbumps.

    他属于这样一群男性群体：他们已经受够了用帽子遮住头发和强迫症一般地关注发际线。反其道而行之，他们拿起理发推子，在现实生活中的音乐节和聚会上，以及“Bald Café”和“Bld Bro”等社交媒体账号上，大方展示自己的光头。在这些账号上，他们互相分享展现脆弱一面的“蜕变”照片，并虚拟碰拳互相鼓励。

    vulnerable
    愿意让别人看到自己的脆弱。这里形容坦诚展示形象变化、容易招来评价的照片。
    cohort
    有某种共同特征的一群人。这里指一起选择不再遮掩脱发的男性。
  5. 05

    Hair-loss treatments were the fastest-growing category in beauty and personal care in 2025, according to research firm Euromonitor, with $192 million in U.S. sales. Today’s treatments, give men a “significant chance” of maintaining their hair, said Dr. Jerry Shapiro, a professor of dermatology at New York University. But they must be used indefinitely, he said, stressing that “not everything works for everybody.”

    据市场研究公司欧睿国际（Euromonitor）数据显示，2025年，生发产品是美容及个人护理领域增长最快的品类，在美国市场的销售额达1.92亿美元。纽约大学皮肤科教授杰里·夏皮罗博士（Dr. Jerry Shapiro）表示，如今的治疗方法为男性保住头发提供了“很高的成功率”。但他指出，这些方法必须长期使用，并强调“并不是任何方法都都适合所有人”。

    indefinitely
    没有明确期限地。这里说治疗要持续用，未给出一个能停下来的日期。
  6. 06

    The refreshingly wholesome resistance movement includes men who found treatments ineffective, or who blanched at side effects and costs. Others simply wanted to let nature run its course.

    参与这场令人耳目一新、健康积极的抵抗运动的，既有发现治疗无效的男性，也有因副作用和费用而望而却步的男性。其他人则只是希望让一切顺其自然。

    blanched
    因震惊或担忧而变了脸色。blanched at costs 指一想到费用就被吓住了。

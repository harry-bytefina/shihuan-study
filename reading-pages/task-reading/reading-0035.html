阅读表达

01-2021天津夏

学习、阅读与教育方式

Most of us associate learning with younger people—kids in school and college or recent graduates early in their careers. But at Udemy, an open university offering online courses, Artist Anneke Camstra is engaged in the pursuit of lifelong learning and displays her potential for teaching. She loves the experience of gaining new knowledge and also loves sharing it with others. “I'm retired, but an artist never retires,” Anneke said. “The last few years on the internet have been a great joy for me. I've taken so many Udemy courses, and it's such a great feeling to get my brain working again. I find just as much satisfaction in teaching animation(动画制作) too.”

She reaches countless people with her online courses and exposes them to what they can accomplish on their own using animation software, such as GoAnimate and PowToon. Her motivation for teaching on Udemy is “to get the tools to the people.” “You can go such a long way to make things that look professional but still have your own voice,” she explained.

Most of Anneke's students start out thinking they're not creative and can't draw. Anneke understands how fear can get in the way of so many things people dream of doing. With her five-day challenge courses, she helps them get over the fear. Anneke loves the impact she has on her students. What makes her more proud of them is that they have gone from their first animation to creating their own works.

She encourages people of all ages, especially older people, to cultivate their curiosity for life. Grandparents, for example, can learn to use GoAnimate and make animations together with their grandkids.

“I'm an older woman, teaching and having fun learning again,” Anneke said, and she wants others later in their lives to catch up with her on this journey.

外刊延伸

阅读难度参考：CSE 7

一个大学教授的心声：有效的教育，必是动情的教育

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    I Let My Students Watch Me Struggle With a Masterpiece. It Changed Everything.

    我让学生看着我和一部名著较劲，而这改变了一切。

    masterpiece
    n. 大师之作
  2. 02

    I read "The Brothers Karamazov" for the first time last semester. For someone in my line of work — I'm a professor in a great books program — having failed to read one of the greatest novels ever written is embarrassing, close to professional malpractice.

    上学期，我第一次读《卡拉马佐夫兄弟》。对于和我同行的人来说——我是一个经典名著项目的教授，竟然一直没读过这部史上最伟大小说之一，说起来实在丢人，几乎算得上失职了。

    malpractice
    n. 失职，渎职
  3. 03

    "The Brothers Karamazov" consumed me in a way I hadn't experienced in years, maybe decades. I was once so absorbed, I forgot to pick up my daughter at ballet.

    《卡拉马佐夫兄弟》把我整个人吞噬了。这种感觉我已经很多年，也许有几十年没有过了。有一回因为读得太过入迷，我甚至忘了去芭蕾舞班接女儿。

  4. 04

    And I wasn't alone. I'd hear my students chattering excitedly about Dmitry Karamazov's wild love life and self-destructive partying as if he lived in the dorm with them.

    迷上这本书的，可不止我一人。我常听见学生们凑在一起，兴奋地聊着小说里德米特里·卡拉马佐夫（Dmitry Karamazov）混乱的感情生活、自毁式的纵情狂欢，就好像这家伙就住在他们宿舍里似的。

  5. 05

    To be a realist about higher education is to acknowledge that the challenge of our moment is not primarily intellectual but sentimental. In the algorithmic age, effective teaching is affective teaching.

    要想务实地看待高等教育，就得承认：今天我们面对的难题，根本上不是脑子的问题，而是心的问题。在算法时代，有效的教学，必是动情的教学。

    sentimental
    adj. 情感的，与感受、情绪有关的
    intellectual
    adj. 智识上的，与思维、理性、知识相关的
  6. 06

    Today's educational problems are often attributed to a deficit of focus or attention, but attention is an anemic way to understand what allows education to transform people. Passion creates a unity of purpose and urgency that is the antidote to the feckless, superficial distraction of the algorithmic age.

    如今的教育问题，总被归结为学生不够专注、注意力不集中。可是"注意力"这个词太苍白了，解释不了教育何以改变一个人。而热情能创造出目的感和紧迫性的统一，它才是算法时代那种不负责任、肤浅的分心的解药。

    superficial
    adj. 肤浅的，停留在表面的
    distraction
    n. 让人分心的事物
    passion
    激情。而且这个词以复数形式出现，强调不止一种激情，而是包含了如爱、恨、信仰、怀疑、欲望等在内的多种激情。
    feckless
    adj. 没有价值的，不负责任的
  7. 07

    When I asked my students why they thought Dostoyevsky was such a hit, they routinely said two things. First, they were moved by the passions of the characters in the novel.

    我问学生为什么如此追捧陀思妥耶夫斯基，他们的回答可以归结为两条。第一，他们被书中人物的激情打动。

  8. 08

    Second, the students explained that my own enthusiasm helped stoke theirs. One of them candidly said, "Well, it really helps that you're so into it." I had the pleasure of becoming a student myself.

    第二，学生们说我的那股投入劲儿，把他们的热情也点燃了。有一个学生说得很直白："是这样，您对这书的极致着迷，让我们受益匪浅。"而我也有幸重新当了一回学生。

  9. 09

    If you expect your students to choose books, ideas and genuine learning over shortcuts and distraction, you must be a bit courageous. Cut through the haze of abstraction, the webs of scholarly terminology, the pretense of professionalization and attempt to recover the basic human experiences that can move us. Remain a student in earnest.

    如果你希望你的学生选择的是书、是思想、是真学问，而不是抄近路和找消遣，那你自己也得有点勇气：穿过抽象概念的迷雾，冲破学术名词的织网，识破职业化的伪装，尝试把那些能打动人的、最基本的人类经验找回来。认认真真，做个学生。

  10. 10

    Remaining a passionate student yourself may well feel ridiculous nowadays, but it is ultimately nothing more than realism about what education requires.

    在今天，坚持做一个满腔热情的学生，或许多少让人觉得可笑。但说到底，这无非是对"教育到底需要什么"这件事，保持一份清醒的现实主义罢了。

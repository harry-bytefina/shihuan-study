Passage 6(2026天津南开质量监测一) 主题：社会交往

动物行为与动物认知

“Why are people so unfriendly?”

I often asked myself this after my daily walks, which began 5 years ago and continued into retirement.

Maybe it was the early hour, or maybe people were simply having a bad day. For a long time, I blamed cellphones and social media. Didn't people realize that talking to strangers can lead to a happier life?

One day, my partner, tired of hearing me complain, said, “Maybe it's you.” Impossible, I thought. Just that morning, I had looked directly at someone and got no “hello” back. So rude!

But then I realized that maybe I needed to push myself out of my introverted(内向的) shell. So I tried an experiment. On each walk, I picked someone with a dog and said a simple “hello”. To my surprise, it worked. Most people smiled or even chatted briefly. Once, I was even given a piece of kibble(制成粗粒的宠物食品) to feed a dog!

Encouraged, I started greeting people who made eye contact. Later I went further, saying “good day” even without eye contact. About three out of four times, I got a kind reply.

Then I moved on to people talking on their phones. At first, I was ignored. My second try got a small grunt(嘟哝). But on the third attempt, the person actually paused to say “hi”. Over two weeks, between these phone tries and my usual greetings, I noticed something important: People were getting friendlier. Or maybe it wasn't them—it was me. My own change in behavior was making the difference.

Of course, I use common sense. I don't greet everyone, and some days I feel less outgoing. But usually, I now say hello whether people look at me or not. Most respond positively. And even if they don't, what the hey. I always end my walk feeling better.

The habit has spread beyond my walks. At the store, in restaurants, and even at the gym, I now talk to strangers. I don't expect lifelong friends, just small moments of connection.

It's simple: Treat others the way you want to be treated. And remember, someone has to make the first move.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Why did the author begin to blame cellphones and social media? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. Because he thought they made people unfriendly. 细节表达题。根据第一段“Why are people so unfriendly?”以及第四段中的“Just that morning, I had looked directly at someone and got no ‘hello’ back. So rude!”可知,作者因为人们不回应他的致意,觉得他们不友好,并将此归咎于手机和社交媒体。

2How did the author feel when his first greeting experiment worked? (no more than 5 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. He felt surprised. 细节表达题。根据第五段中的“To my surprise, it worked.”可知,当作者的第一次问候尝试成功时,他感到很惊讶。

3What is the main idea of Paragraph 8? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. The author gets into the habit of greeting people and feels happier. Or: The author usually greets others and feels better. 主旨表达题。根据第八段内容可知,本段主要讲作者在日常生活中养成了和人打招呼的习惯,并且打招呼的方式灵活有度,无论对方是否回应,自己都能因主动问好而心情更好。

4What does the underlined phrase “small moments of connection” in Paragraph 9 mean? (no more than 5 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. Friendly small talk with strangers. Or: Brief friendly interactions with others. 猜词表达题。根据第九段中的“At the store, in restaurants, and even at the gym, I now talk to strangers. I don't expect lifelong friends, just small moments of connection.”可知,作者和陌生人交流,并不期待成为终身朋友,而是希望和陌生人之间进行短暂友好的互动或闲聊。

5What have you learned from the author's story? Please explain with an example. (no more than 25 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. I have learned the importance of small talk and I now smile at neighbors and get smiles back. Or: I've learned to take the first step; I once smiled at a new classmate on Day 1, which helped me make friends.

4亿人在扛的花粉症，终于有靠谱疗法了

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    An end to hay fever? The new wave of effective cures for seasonal allergies

    花粉过敏症或将终结？新一代高效季节性过敏疗法来袭

  2. 02

    More than 400 million people worldwide experience allergic rhinitis – inflammation of the nasal passages from a reaction to airborne allergens. It happens when your immune system mistakenly identifies something like animal dander or dust mites as harmful, triggering symptoms that can include a runny nose, itchy eyes, sneezing and – at its worst – breathing difficulties.

    全球超 4 亿人患有过敏性鼻炎，这是鼻腔因对空气传播的过敏原产生反应而引发的炎症。当人体免疫系统误将动物皮屑、尘螨等物质判定为有害物质时，就会诱发相关症状，包括流鼻涕、眼痒、打喷嚏，严重时还会出现呼吸困难。

    airborne
    在空气中传播的。airborne allergens 指随空气飘散的过敏原。
    inflammation
    炎症。这里指鼻腔组织接触过敏原后出现的炎症。
    allergen
    过敏原；能引起过敏反应的物质。
  3. 03

    When it's seasonal, such as in response to pollen, allergic rhinitis is called hay fever.

    若过敏症状呈季节性发作，比如由花粉引发，这种过敏性鼻炎就被称为花粉症。

    pollen
    花粉。这里举它作引发季节性过敏性鼻炎的例子。
  4. 04

    As well as affecting quality of life, hay fever can have severe long-term consequences, including the risk of developing both respiratory infections and asthma.

    花粉症不仅会影响生活质量，还可能造成严重的长期健康隐患，比如增加患上呼吸道感染和哮喘的风险。

    respiratory
    呼吸系统的。respiratory infections 指呼吸道感染。
  5. 05

    Yet there is good news, say allergists: high-quality, effective, and safe therapies are now available for hay fever. Most treat symptoms. But one, allergen immunotherapy (AIT), is more of a cure, "teaching" the body to react less to certain allergens. Whether through oral droplets or injection, allergen immunotherapy has proven effective for thousands of patients. Allergen immunotherapy may even prevent the onset of asthma.

    但过敏专科医生表示，好消息是如今已有优质、高效且安全的花粉症治疗方案。多数疗法以缓解症状为主，而其中一种过敏原免疫疗法（AIT）则更接近根治手段 —— 它能 “训练” 人体降低对特定过敏原的反应。无论是通过口服滴剂还是注射，该疗法已在数千名患者身上验证了疗效，甚至还能预防哮喘的发生。

    onset
    开始出现。onset of asthma 指哮喘开始发作。
    immunotherapy
    免疫疗法。文中特指过敏原免疫疗法，用意是降低身体对特定过敏原的反应。
  6. 06

    Part of the problem is that even health professionals don't always take allergic rhinitis seriously, says clinical allergist Stephen Durham.

    临床过敏专科医生斯蒂芬・达勒姆（Stephen Durham）称，部分问题在于，即便医疗专业人士，也时常对过敏性鼻炎不够重视。

  7. 07

    General practitioners often tell patients just to go to the pharmacy for an antihistamine, for example, rather than referring them to an allergist or considering something preventative and long-term, like allergen immunotherapy, he says.

    他表示，全科医生通常只会让患者去药店购买抗组胺药，而非将其转诊给过敏专科医生，或是考虑过敏原免疫疗法这类预防性的长期治疗方案。

  8. 08

    "Hay fever is a major problem, and it's often trivialised by those who don't have it," Durham says. "If you think about what we do in life, we work, we sleep, and we have fun. And all of those things are seriously impacted by hay fever."

    “花粉症是个大问题，可没患上的人往往对此不以为然。” 达勒姆说，“想想我们的日常生活：工作、睡觉、休闲娱乐，而这些事都会被花粉症严重干扰。”

    trivialise
    把本来严肃的问题当成小事。这里说一些没有花粉症的人低估了它的影响。

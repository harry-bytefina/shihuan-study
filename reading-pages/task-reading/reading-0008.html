Passage 8(2022天津第一次) 主题：优秀品行

学习、阅读与教育方式

Mike Sullivan, a former construction worker, took up woodworking after he and his wife, Judy, retired. He was enjoying carving things in his workshop—but he wanted to create something with more purpose.

Sullivan had been thinking about the single present he'd received in 1954 when he was growing up in a small mining town. His father earned $1 a day and couldn't afford a New Year gift. He then made a small wooden horse, which Sullivan considered the best New Year present ever.

Sullivan talked about it with Judy and they decided to start making wooden toys to give away to kids on New Year's Day. That first year, they made 360 toys.

The Sullivans now make about 1,500 toys each year to donate to local children's charities, school districts and homeless shelters. Local businesses give them much of the wood, but the couple still dip into their own money every year to keep their workshop running. “My sales policy is not to collect a single penny. Everything we make is given away,” said Sullivan.

Last year, the couple's daughter started a GoFundMe to help cover the costs of some materials and tools. People have donated more than $1,500 so far this holiday season.

This December, all the toys they made were distributed to local charities to be handed out to children from poor families. They are now getting a head start on next year's toy workload.

The couple are now at work on the toys seven days a week, sometimes for 10 hours a day.“Making toys keeps my mind active. I can't just sit there and stare at a TV all day. The work really gives me a boost. Some days are easier than others, but they're all rewarding,” said Sullivan. “I might take a day or two off after the New Year, but that's it. As long as there's a need out there, I don't get to take many vacations.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What childhood experience gave Sullivan the idea to make free toys for kids? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. His father couldn't afford a New Year gift and made him a wooden horse. Or:His family was poor and his father made him a New Year gift. 细节表达题。根据文章第二段“...couldn't afford a New Year gift...made a small wooden horse...considered the best New Year present ever.”可知答案。

2What is the meaning of the underlined phrase in Paragraph 4? (1 word)

查看答案与讲解

2. Spend./Use./Invest. 猜词表达题。根据文章第四段内容,结合语境“Local businesses give them much of the wood...to keep their workshop running”可推断“dip into”的含义为“动用(存款)”,再根据其后的名词“money”可确定作答时要用动词。

3What help did Sullivan's daughter provide? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. She started a GoFundMe to help raise money. Or: She helped (her parents) to raise money. Or: She started a GoFundMe to help cover the costs. 细节表达题。根据文章第五段中“the couple's daughter started a GoFundMe to help cover the costs of some materials and tools”可知,这对夫妇的女儿创建了一个GoFundMe页面,以帮助支付一些材料和工具的费用。

4How does making toys for kids benefit Sullivan? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. It keeps his mind active and gives him a boost. 细节表达题。根据文章第七段中“Making toys keeps my mind active.”及“The work really gives me a boost.”可知,制作玩具让他保持头脑活跃并给了他很大的鼓舞。

5How does Sullivan's story inspire you? Please explain in your own words. (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. We should care about people around us and try our best to help those in need. Or: We should take a positive attitude and lead an active life.

90秒魔性神曲估值4亿美元：Baby Shark凭什么唱到上市？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Baby Shark: How a 90-second clip created a $400m business

    《鲨鱼宝宝》：一个90秒短片如何做成一门4亿美元的大生意

  2. 02

    When Kim Min-seok gave the go-ahead in June 2016 to publish a 90-second clip of a children's song, he had no idea what he was unleashing. It became a global phenomenon, clocking up more than 16 billion views — YouTube's most watched video ever.

    2016年6月，当金敏锡（Kim Min-seok）批准上线一段90秒的儿童歌曲视频时，他完全想不到自己即将释放出怎样一场全球热潮。这段视频后来成为全球现象级爆款，累计播放量超过160亿次—— 创下YouTube史上最高观看量纪录。

    unleash
    释放；使势头一下子起来。这里是说发布者没料到这段视频会掀起多大浪潮。
    phenomenon
    引起广泛关注的现象。a global phenomenon 指在许多国家走红的文化现象。
  3. 03

    That song was the incredibly catchy Baby Shark. Not only has it captivated toddlers and terrorised adults around the world, it laid the foundations for its creator Pinkfong to become a media business worth hundreds of millions of dollars.

    这首歌就是魔性到不行的《鲨鱼宝宝》（Baby Shark）。它不仅俘获无数小朋友、也“折磨”了世界各地的大人，还为其创作者碰碰狐公司（Pinkfong）打下了百亿美元级媒体生意的根基。

    catchy
    听几遍就能记住的；朗朗上口的。常用来形容歌曲、旋律和口号。
    captivate
    牢牢吸引住。原文把幼儿听得入迷和大人听得受不了放在一起写。
    toddler
    刚学走路的幼儿。这里描述《Baby Shark》最小的一批听众。
  4. 04

    "We didn't expect it to stand out from our other content," Mr Kim, Pinkfong's chief executive, told the BBC from the firm's headquarters in Seoul. "But looking back, it became a major turning point that set the stage for our global journey."

    “我们当时完全没想到它会从我们的内容里脱颖而出。”碰碰狐首席执行官金敏锡在首尔总部接受BBC采访时表示，“但回头看，它的确成为了公司全球化征程的关键节点，为我们铺平了道路。”

  5. 05

    On Tuesday, that journey took Pinkfong to the South Korean stock market, where its shares rose by more than 9% on their debut, giving it a valuation of more than $400m (£304m).

    这条发展之路指向了韩国股市，周二，碰碰狐挂牌上市。首日股价上涨超过9%，公司估值超过4亿美元（约合3.04亿英镑）。

    debut
    首次亮相。shares rose on their debut 指股票上市首日上涨。
    valuation
    估值。这里是依据上市时的股价得到的公司价值估计。
  6. 06

    Baby Shark is believed to have originated in the US in the 1970s and was often sung at children's summer camps. The song, which repeats the phrase "Baby shark, doo, doo, doo, doo, doo, doo" is "attractive for children, though possibly annoying for adults," said media analyst Kevin Chew from the Nanyang Technological University.

    一般认为，《鲨鱼宝宝》这首歌起源于1970年代的美国，常在儿童夏令营里传唱。这首反复出现“Baby shark, doo, doo, doo, doo, doo, doo”的歌曲，“对孩子极具吸引力，但这对大人来说可能相当抓狂”，南洋理工大学（Nanyang Technological University）媒体分析师凯文·周（Kevin Chew）说道。

  7. 07

    Mr Kim is also very aware of just how catchy it is. "It's like a K-pop song. It's very fast-paced, rhythmical and it's addictive," he said, adding that the tune has a "chanting" effect, which makes it easy for children to remember.

    金敏锡本人也非常清楚它有多“洗脑”。“它像一首韩国流行歌曲，节奏很快、韵律感强，很容易让人上头。”他还补充说，这段旋律带有一种“吟唱”式效果，所以孩子很轻松就记住了。

    addictive
    让人一再想听或想做的。这里形容旋律好记，听完还想再听。

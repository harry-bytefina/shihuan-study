Passage 10(2021天津第一次) 主题：认识自我,丰富自我,完善自我

学习、阅读与教育方式

I've always been creative since I was a little girl. My childhood was filled with arts, crafts (手工) and music, and every day was an exciting adventure of creation.

Somewhere along the way, however, I lost touch with my creative self. At 21, I landed a job at a big insurance company out of my business degree. I would go to the office, sit at my desk and repeat the same work five days a week 9 to 5. The days were long and tedious. Working this way for one year was painful enough, let alone a lifetime. Before long I decided to make a change.

One thing I had developed an interest in was sewing, so I took a sewing course at a community college. It was only a five-week course but it awakened my interest and got my confidence up.

As I dreamt bigger, I began looking into college courses for Fashion Design and three months later I signed up for a part-time design course. I attended classes two nights a week. Although exhausting, it gave me energy, enthusiasm and happiness.

As it became clearer that fashion was what I wanted to do full time, the reality of my day-to-day work at the insurance company became harder. I felt bored. No energy, no motivation and zero fulfilment (满足感).

I knew I needed to make a bigger change. After struggling for quite a while, I made a difficult decision—leaving my job and studying my design course full time. Two years later I got my diploma.

The last few years have been exciting because I've been following my heart to do what I love. Today I am living a creative life as the designer of my own fashion brand.

Sometimes it's hard to make a change, but I'm so glad I set the wheels of change in motion with that very small first step because it has led me to where I am today.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What was the author interested in when she was a little girl? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. She was interested in arts, crafts and music. Or: She was interested in creative things. 细节表达题。根据第一段中的“My childhood was filled with arts, crafts and music”可知答案。

2What does the underlined word in Paragraph 2 mean? (1 word)

查看答案与讲解

2. Boring./Dull./Uninteresting. 猜词表达题。根据第二段中的“...sit at my desk and repeat the same work five days a week 9 to 5.”可知那些日子是无聊的。

3How did the author feel when she was taking the part-time design course? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. She felt/was energetic, enthusiastic and happy. Or: She was filled with energy, enthusiasm and happiness. 细节表达题。依据文章第四段最后一句“Although exhausting, it gave me energy, enthusiasm and happiness.”可知作者对设计课程的感觉。

4What did the author do to get back to her creative life? Give two of the facts. (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. She quit/quitted her job and took the fashion-design course. Or: She attended a sewing class and took a part-time design course. 细节表达题。根据文章可知,作者为了回到以前,做了几件事:上缝纫课,上时装设计课,辞职等。

5Do you consider the author as your role model? Please explain. (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. Yes(, I do). She has the courage to follow her heart and do what she really likes. Or: Yes(, I do). I'd like to live a creative life and make changes when necessary as she did. Or: No(, I don't). I don't like changes and I want to live a secure life.

AI效率越来越高，为什么打工人反而更累了？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    AI Isn't Lightening Workloads. It's Making Them More Intense.

    AI并没有减轻工作量，反而让工作强度更高

    workload
    n. 工作量
  2. 02

    One of the great hopes for artificial intelligence—at least, among workers—is that it will ease workloads, freeing people up for more high-level, creative pursuits. So far, the opposite is happening, new data show.

    人们对人工智能抱有很大期待——尤其是打工人——希望它能减轻工作负担，让人们腾出精力去做更高阶、更有创造性的事。但最新数据显示，目前的情况恰恰相反。

    ease
    v. 减轻、缓解（reduce）

    句子结构

    One of the great hopes for artificial intelligence—at least, among workers—is that it will ease workloads, / freeing people up for more high-level, creative pursuits.

    One 是主语中心词，所以用 is；破折号之间是对「谁抱有这个期待」的补充。that it will ease workloads 是表语从句，说明期待的内容。freeing … 写的是工作负担减轻后可能产生的结果，从属于这个期待，并非作者宣告已经发生的事实。

  3. 03

    In fact, AI is increasing the speed, density and complexity of work rather than reducing it, according to an analysis of 164,000 workers' digital work activity.

    一项针对 16.4 万名员工数字办公行为的分析显示，事实上，人工智能正在提升工作的节奏、密度与复杂度，而非减轻负担。

  4. 04

    "It's not that AI doesn't create efficiency," said Gabriela Mauch, ActivTrak's head of its productivity lab. "It's that the capacity it frees up immediately gets repurposed into doing other work, and that's where the creep is likely to happen."

    ActivTrak 生产力实验室负责人加芙列拉・毛赫（Gabriela Mauch）说：“并不是 AI 没有提升效率，而是它省下来的产能立刻被拿去做了别的工作，工作负荷就是这样悄悄加重的。”

    creep
    n. 慢慢累积的（消极）变化
    repurpose
    v. 另作他用，重新分配、组合，改造，改建
  5. 05

    Such habits aren't exactly what AI evangelists have predicted. A number of tech and business leaders have suggested that AI could ultimately lead people to work less, not more, and result in a shorter workweek.

    这种情况，并不完全符合人工智能鼓吹者此前的预期。不少科技与商界领袖曾表示，AI 最终会让人们工作得更少、而非更多，甚至实现更短的工作周。

    ultimately
    adv. 最终，最后
    evangelist
    n. 带着使命感传播事业的人
  6. 06

    Yet, evidence so far suggests that many AI adopters aren't using the technology's efficiencies to give themselves a break. People often end up doing more work, not less, "because AI makes additional tasks feel easy and accessible, creating a sense of momentum," said Aruna Ranganathan, associate professor at the University of California.

    然而，目前种种迹象表明，很多AI 使用者并没有利用技术带来的效率给自己“减负”。人们最终往往干得更多，而不是更少，“因为 AI 让额外的任务看起来简单易行，从而形成一种持续推进的工作惯性”。加州大学副教授阿鲁娜・兰加纳坦（Aruna Ranganathan）说道。

    momentum
    n. 势头
  7. 07

    About 80% of employees now use AI tools at work—up from 53% two years ago—while the average time spent working with AI tools has risen eightfold. People who spent 7% to 10% of their total work hours with AI tools showed the highest productivity, yet only 3% of AI users use such tools that much. The majority spent 1% of their total work hours using AI.

    如今约有 80% 的员工在工作中使用 AI 工具，较两年前的 53% 大幅上升；而员工平均使用 AI 工具的时长更是增至原来的八倍。拿出总工作时长的 7% 至 10% 使用AI 工具的人，工作效率最高，但这类使用者仅占 AI 用户的 3%。大多数人仅用 1% 的工作时间使用 AI。

    eightfold
    n. 八倍

Passage 5(2023天津第二次) 主题：优秀品行

学习、阅读与教育方式

Herman Cruse, a school bus driver from New Jersey, has been with Middle Township Public Schools for the past nine years. He believes bus drivers are the eyes and ears of students when they're away from home and they have a gift to discern what kids are feeling.

During one morning ride, Cruse noticed a kindergartner seemed a little sad. When Cruse asked him what was wrong, the boy explained that he wasn't able to complete his reading assignment because his parents were busy with his four siblings (兄弟姐妹). An idea suddenly crossed Cruse's mind. “Listen, if you don't mind, I'd like to come to the school and read with you.” he said.

After receiving permission from the boy's teacher, Alex Bakley, Cruse showed up at her classroom the following week. When he walked in, the boy shouted proudly, “Hey, that's my bus driver!” They went into a quiet corner and began reading together. Later, a second student wanted to read with him, then a third. All the kids went to the teacher asking, “Can I read with Mr. Herman?”

Cruse now volunteers to help kindergarten students with reading two days a week, and on a third day, he instructs the school's first- and second-graders. After dropping the kids off at school, of course. Every child looks up to Cruse, both on and off the school bus. “Herman is super positive and he's a bright light at our school who makes every child feel loved and heard,” said Bakley.

For Cruse, what started out as a way to kill time has now developed into a way to make a difference in the heart of a child. He used to go to the gym or library after delivering students to schools. It wasn't until he offered to help the boy on the bus that he realized there was something more rewarding he could be doing. It's a joy for him to see the kids get excited when they learn to sound out words. He loves how reading opens up a new world for them.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What does the underlined word mean in Paragraph 1? (1 word)

查看答案与讲解

1. Notice/Detect/Understand/Know/Sense/Perceive. 猜词表达题。画线词discern是由they(指校车司机们)发出的,宾语是“孩子们的感受”。根据“...bus drivers are the eyes and ears of students...”可知,校车司机是孩子们的眼睛和耳朵,即他们用自己的眼睛和耳朵去“感知”“察觉”或“注意”孩子们的感受。

2Why did the boy on the bus look a little upset? (no more than 11 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. (Because) his parents were too busy to read with him. Or: He couldn't complete his reading assignment because his parents were busy. Or: (Because) his parents were busy (with his four siblings). Or: (Because) he couldn't complete his reading assignment. 细节表达题。根据第二段中的“...he wasn't able to complete his reading assignment because his parents were busy with his four siblings.”可知,小男孩由于家中兄弟姐妹多,父母无暇和他一起完成阅读任务。

3What help does Cruse offer after delivering students to schools? (no more than 12 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. Helping kindergarteners with reading and instructing the school's first- and second-graders. Or: He helps kindergarten students read and instructs the school's first- and second-graders. Or: He helps kindergarteners (to) read and instructs the school's first- and second-graders. Or: He reads with kindergarteners and instructs the school's first- and second-graders. 细节表达题。根据第四段中的“Cruse now volunteers to help kindergarten students with reading two days a week, and on a third day, he instructs the school's first- and second-graders.”可知,Cruse 每周两天陪伴幼儿园的孩子们读书,第三天指导一、二年级的孩子。

4Why does Cruse think his voluntary work is rewarding according to the last paragraph? (no more than 16 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. (Because) the kids get excited when reading, which opens up a new world for them. Or: (Because) the kids get excited about books and reading opens up a new world for them. 细节表达题。根据最后一段中的“It's a joy for him to see the kids get excited when they learn to sound out words. He loves how reading opens up a new world for them.”可知,Cruse很高兴看到孩子们因为学会朗读而兴奋不已的样子,同时他也为能够通过阅读为孩子们打开新世界的大门而倍感欣慰。回答中应体现“the kids get excited”和“opens up a new world”这两个主要信息点。

5Who's “a bright light” in your life? Please explain in your own words. (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. My father/mother. (Because)he/she encourages me and helps me (to) overcome the difficulties in my life. Or: My teacher. (Because) he/she cares about me and offers me advice when I encounter problems. Or: My friend. (Because) he/she is super positive and gives me a lot of encouragement and inspiration. Or: Zhong Nanshan. Because his devotion to medical science inspires me to work hard to become a doctor.

阅读难度参考：CSE 9

90年来首次，美国人正创纪录地逃离美国

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Americans Are Leaving the U.S. in Record Numbers

    美国人正以创纪录的数量离开美国

  2. 02

    In its 250th year, is America, land of immigration, becoming a country of emigration?

    在建国250周年之际，以移民之国自居的美国，是否正在变成一个向外移民的国家？

    emigration
    n. 人口外流、移民离开本国
  3. 03

    Last year the U.S. experienced something that hasn't definitively occurred since the Great Depression: More people moved out than moved in. The Trump administration has hailed the exodus—negative net migration—as the fulfillment of its promise to ramp up deportations and restrict new visas.

    去年，美国出现了自大萧条以来绝对从未有过的局面：迁出人口超过了迁入人口。特朗普政府将这一人口外流现象 —— 即净移民为负—— 吹捧为其兑现了加大驱逐（移民）力度、收紧新签证发放的承诺。

    hail
    v. 称赞、欢迎、视......为好事
  4. 04

    Beneath the stormy optics of that immigration crackdown, however, lies a less-noticed reversal: America's own citizens are leaving in record numbers, replanting themselves and their families in lands they find more affordable and safe.

    然而，在这场声势浩大的移民收紧政策背后，隐藏着一个鲜为人注意的反转：美国本国公民正以创纪录的数量离开，他们拖家带口迁往自己认为生活成本更低、更安全的国家重新定居。

    crackdown
    n. 严厉打击、强力整治
    replant
    v. 人在新的地方重新扎根

    句子结构

    Beneath the stormy optics of that immigration crackdown, however, / lies a less-noticed reversal: / America's own citizens are leaving in record numbers, / replanting themselves and their families in lands they find more affordable and safe.

    lies 是谓语，后面的 a less-noticed reversal 才是主语。Beneath 短语提前，句子用了完全倒装。冒号解释逆转是什么；lands 后省略了作 find 宾语的关系词，more affordable and safe 描述的是这些地方。

  5. 05

    The new American dream, for some of its citizens, is to no longer live there.

    对一部分美国公民而言，新的美国梦，是不再生活在美国。

  6. 06

    More than 100,000 young students are enrolled abroad for a more affordable university degree. In nursing homes mushrooming across the Mexican border, elderly Americans are turning up for low-cost care.

    超过 10 万名年轻学生赴海外就读，只为获得学费更亲民的大学学位。在墨西哥边境遍地开花的养老院里，越来越多美国老年人前往寻求平价养老服务。

  7. 07

    "Previously, the Americans leaving were super-adventurous and well-credentialed," said Expatsi (a relocation company) founder Jen Barnett, a 54-year-old Alabama native who moved to Yucatán, Mexico, in 2024. "Now they're ordinary people, like me.”

    “以前离开美国的都是极具冒险精神、资历出众的人，” 移居服务公司 Expatsi 创始人珍・巴内特（Jen Barnett）说。这位 54 岁的阿拉巴马人于 2024 年搬到了墨西哥尤卡坦州。“现在离开的都是普通人，跟我一样。”

    well-credentialed
    adj. 学历不错、履历很强、专业背景过硬的
    super-adventurous
    adj. 非常有冒险精神的，敢闯敢试的
  8. 08

    Some commentators have labeled this wave of American emigrants the "Donald Dash" since numbers have spiked under President Trump's second term. But the phenomenon has been building for years—fed by the rise of remote work, mounting living costs and an appetite for foreign lifestyles that feel within reach, especially in Europe.

    有评论人士将这波美国移民潮称为 “特朗普出逃潮”，因为人数在特朗普第二任期内急剧飙升。但这一趋势其实已酝酿多年 —— 远程办公兴起、生活成本不断攀升，加之人们对海外生活方式的向往变得触手可及，尤其是在欧洲。

  9. 09

    The exodus poses elemental questions for a country that has always prided itself as a destination. Are the new American emigrants a credit to the strength of their homeland's economy?

    对于一个向来以 “理想目的地” 自居的国家而言，这场人口外流抛出了根本性问题：这些新出走的美国人，究竟是体现了美国经济实力强劲？

    elemental
    adj. 根本性的，最基础的，触及本质的
  10. 10

    Or do these émigrés personify a loss of faith in America's future and way of life?

    还是说，这些移居海外的美国人，恰恰象征着对美国未来与生活方式失去信心？

    personify
    v. 体现、化身为、人格化地代表某种东西

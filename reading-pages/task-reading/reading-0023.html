Passage 13(2025天津部分区质量调查二) 主题：积极的生活态度

文化遗产、艺术与跨文化

When I was a child, my mother didn't force me to eat up everything in my dish, but she said I had to try every food three times. Her reason was the first time to try it, the second time to get over the act that I had just tried it, and the third to make sure that I didn't like it. After that, if I still hated the taste, she would never make me eat it again. I think her approach is reasonable and worked almost every time—except on bananas, which I still don't eat now.

As I grew older, I brought that simple rule into my life. As a girl, I had been afraid of the diving board at the pool. The diving board looked like a giant monster's mouth below me. My legs shook so badly. But I continued to make the first jump off the board. When I breathed in water, my mother jumped in to help me. Before she could even reach me, I was up and out of the pool, heading back to that diving board. I jumped off once again. More coughing followed, but I was determined to overcome this apprehension. When I got out of the pool the second time, my mom tried to cover me with a towel. Instead, I threw off the towel and kept trying. The third time there was no fear, and no water in my nose. In fact, there were cheers from the others at the pool. At that point, I fell in love with the pool.

In my mid-thirties, I left a successful job that didn't satisfy me in order to achieve my dream of being a writer. Having been refused twice, I tried for a third time. I have been happily living the life of a writer ever since.

We are all afraid of something unknown. But how can we be sure that something won't be enjoyable unless we give it a try?

全部题目与逐题讲解

1How did the author think of her mother's method for trying new food? (no more than 5 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. Reasonable and effective. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词her mother's method for trying new food可定位在第一段的最后一句。由此句可知,作者认为她的方法很合理,而且几乎每次都奏效。

2What does the underlined word in Paragraph 2 probably mean? (1 word)

查看答案与讲解

2. Fear. 猜词表达题。画线处句子意为“接着又是一阵咳嗽,但我下定决心要克服这种　　　”。再结合后文“第三次我不再害怕,鼻子里也没有进水。事实上,泳池里其他人都欢呼起来。就在那一刻,我爱上了泳池。”可推断,作者下定决心要克服这种恐惧。

3What was the reason for cheers after the author had tried for the third time? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. She overcame her fear. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词the reason for cheers after the author had tried for the third time可定位在第二段。由第二段可知,面对如怪物般可怖的跳水板,虽恐惧呛水,但是作者却坚持尝试三次,最终战胜恐惧,赢得欢呼,见证坚持的力量。

4Why did the author give up her job? (no more than 5 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. To achieve her dream. Or: To realize her dream. Or: To pursue her dream. Or: To be a writer. Or: (Because) the job was unsatisfying. Or: Because it didn't satisfy her. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词give up her job可定位在倒数第二段。由本段第一句可知,在作者三十五岁左右时,辞去了一份成功但无法让她满足的工作,为的是实现成为一名作家的梦想。

5How does the author's story inspire you? Put it in your own words. (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. The author's story inspires me to try new things / overcome my fear, because it can lead to personal growth and discovering new passions.

AI公司疯抢芯片，买单的为什么是我们普通消费者？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Memory-Chip Crisis Is Here—and You’re Footing the Bill

    内存芯片危机已然到来，而买单的，是你

  2. 02

    Want a sleek new Microsoft Surface Pro laptop? The latest models start at $1,599, or $600 more than the previous generation, the PC maker said this past week. And don’t be surprised if your iPhone 18 Pro costs an eye-popping $1,299 when it comes out in September.

    想要一台轻薄新款微软 Surface Pro 平板笔记本？这家 PC 厂商上周公布，最新机型起售价 1599 美元，较上一代贵了 600 美元。等到 9 月 iPhone 18 Pro 发布时，倘若它定价高达 1299 美元，你也不必太过惊讶。

    sleek
    adj. 光滑的，线条流畅的，时髦精致的（暗示产品有质感）
  3. 03

    The primary driver is the skyrocketing cost of memory and storage chips, especially those known as DRAM and NAND flash memory, which are essential for transferring data and storing information on devices. These chips are the same ones craved by artificial-intelligence companies, which use them to help train and run large language models, coding agents and other tools.

    价格暴涨的核心诱因是内存与存储芯片成本飙升，尤其是两种叫做DRAM和NAND闪存的芯片，它们是各类设备传输数据、存储信息的关键。人工智能企业同样对这类芯片需求旺盛，它们依靠这类芯片训练、运行大语言模型、编程智能体以及各类 AI 工具。

    crave
    v. 渴求，强烈地想要
    skyrocket
    v. 飞涨，暴涨
  4. 04

    As AI adoption has exploded, the memory-chip industry—dominated by just three companies: South Korea’s SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics and Boise, Idaho-based Micron Technology—is suffering from a major capacity crunch. This has been great for memory-chip makers, with the market values of SK Hynix and Micron both roughly quintupling to more than $1 trillion each in the past six months. Consumers are bearing the brunt.

    随着 AI 应用规模爆发式扩张，仅由三家企业主导的内存芯片行业正遭遇严重产能紧缺：韩国 SK 海力士、三星电子，以及总部位于美国爱达荷州博伊西的美光科技。这场芯片短缺对芯片厂商而言是天大的好事。过去半年里，SK 海力士与美光的市值均暴涨约五倍，双双突破一万亿美元。但消费者却首当其冲地承受着冲击。

    quintuple
    v. 翻五倍
  5. 05

    On Wednesday, The Wall Street Journal reported that Apple was planning to raise prices on its products because of rising memory prices. Apple chief Tim Cook said the company had been trying to shield customers from the increases but it was no longer possible.

    周三，《华尔街日报》报道，受内存芯片涨价影响，苹果计划上调旗下产品售价。苹果首席执行官蒂姆・库克（Tim Cook）表示，公司此前一直努力替顾客挡住涨价势头，但如今已经难以为继。

    shield
    v. 保护，遮挡某种负面事物
  6. 06

    Memory-chip makers are rushing to add new manufacturing lines, but semiconductor factories, also known as foundries or fabs, cost billions of dollars and generally take two to three years to build. Ramping up to full capacity takes additional time. Analysts expect the shortages and price increases to persist for a few more years.

    各大内存芯片厂商正加急新建生产线，但半导体工厂（又称晶圆厂）造价动辄数十亿美元，建设周期普遍长达两至三年，全线满产还需额外耗时。分析师预测，芯片短缺、价格上涨的局面还将持续数年。

    persist
    v. 持续地存在
    foundry
    n. 代工厂
  7. 07

    “These companies are trading at high valuations, and because they have to keep their margins high, the consumer is the one who loses,” said Brad Gastwirth, Circular’s head of global research. “The laptops coming at the end of the year are not going to be cheap.”

    咨询机构 Circular 全球研究主管布拉德・加斯特沃思（Brad Gastwirth）表示：“这些芯片企业当前估值居高不下，同时还要维持高额利润率，最终吃亏的只能是消费者。年底上市的笔记本电脑售价绝不会便宜。”

    margin
    n. 利润率

Passage 4(2026天津红桥一模) 主题：积极的人生态度

睡眠、运动与身心健康

My brother and I were driving home together and started talking. Because of this distraction, my brother took a wrong turn.

Unfortunately, the wrong turn took us toward a bridge, and we had no way of turning back. My brother had to pay to cross. He was clearly thwarted by the mistake and the needless waste of $4.

As we drove forward, my brother noticed a beat-up(破旧的) black Mustang(福特野马汽车) pulled over to the side of the road. A young guy was standing nearby trying to phone someone. I was busy trying to figure out which way we should go next but my brother pulled over and asked the guy if he needed any help. And he did. He had a flat tire.

My brother helped him change the flat. We started getting to know the young man who was from a rough neighborhood nearby. He said that this has been a bad week for him; earlier he had gotten into a minor car accident, and now this flat on his way home from work. But he called us “a breath of fresh air” and kept thanking us because he really would have been stuck if we hadn't come along. After we had finished the job, he thanked us again and pulled out $20 to try to give it to us. “No,” I said. “We were never supposed to even get on that bridge. We took a wrong turn. But now we know why we did. It was to help you. Thank you for turning our mistake into an opportunity to serve.”

What I loved most was watching my brother throughout this process. He was able to turn his annoyance over the mistake into positive energy. He was able to see a chance to help (which I totally missed!) even in an otherwise negative situation, which can only come from a calm mind and an open heart. That was the bridge we were meant to cross.

查看答案与讲解

1. Being focused on the conversation. 细节表达题。根据文章第一段“My brother and I were driving home together and started talking. Because of this distraction, my brother took a wrong turn.”可知,作者的哥哥因为专注于和作者聊天而拐错了弯。

2What's the meaning of the underlined word “thwarted” in Paragraph 2 (no more than 2 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. Frustrated./Defeated. 猜词表达题。根据后文the mistake和the needless waste of $4可知,作者的哥哥因为拐错弯而不得不付过桥费。由此可推知,他会感到沮丧、受挫。

3Why did the young guy call the author and his brother “a breath of fresh air”? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. Because they helped him with his flat tire. 细节表达题。根据文章第四段中的“this has been a bad week for him”以及“he really would have been stuck if we hadn't come along”可知,年轻人称作者和哥哥为“一股新鲜空气”是因为他这一周都不顺利,作者和哥哥帮助他换了轮胎,否则他会被困在路上。

4Why did the author admire his brother after the incident? (no more than 25 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. Because his brother can turn annoyance over the mistake into positive energy and find a chance to help even in a negative situation. 细节表达题。根据文章最后一段中的“He was able to turn his annoyance over the mistake into positive energy. He was able to see a chance to help...even in an otherwise negative situation”可知,作者敬佩哥哥是因为他能够把对错误的烦恼转化为正能量,并在负面情况下找到帮助他人的机会。

5Have you ever crossed such a bridge? Please explain. (no more than 25 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. Yes, I have. Because I thought they met more serious trouble than I had.

2026诺贝尔文学奖花落“文学叛逆者”，安妮·卡森是谁？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Canadian Poet Anne Carson Is Awarded Nobel Prize in Literature

    加拿大诗人安妮·卡森获诺贝尔文学奖

  2. 02

    The Canadian poet and essayist Anne Carson was awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature on Thursday by the Swedish Academy, which cited “her bold and inventive oeuvre that, in playful dialogue with the classical tradition, has created new forms for contemporary literature.”

    周四，瑞典学院将诺贝尔文学奖授予加拿大诗人、散文家安妮·卡森（Anne Carson），称“其大胆且富有创造性的作品，在与古典传统诙谐的对话中，为当代文学开创了全新的形式”。

    cite
    v. 引证
  3. 03

    Upon learning of the prize, Carson said “Yikes,” according to her agent, Nicole Aragi.

    据卡森的经纪人妮可·阿拉吉（Nicole Aragi）透露，得知获奖消息后，卡森说了一句：“哎呀！”

  4. 04

    Carson, 76, is best known for her translations of ancient Greek texts and for remixing them in a modern, playful voice.

    现年76岁的卡森最为人熟知的作品，是她对古希腊典籍的翻译，以及她用现代、灵动的语调对这些经典进行的重新演绎。

  5. 05

    “Many toil in the interstices of genre,” the poet Daisy Fried wrote. “Carson’s palatable, popular, sophisticated and who-cares approach may have done the most and best work in the last two decades to stop people worrying so much about what’s poetry and what’s not.”

    “许多人在不同文体交界的缝隙中辛勤耕耘，”诗人黛西·弗里德（Daisy Fried）如是写道，“卡森让人愉悦、广受欢迎、精巧而又满不在乎的创作方式，或许在过去二十年里做出了最大也是最佳贡献，让人们不再纠结于什么是诗、什么不是诗。”

    toil
    v. 辛勤劳作，苦干
    palatable
    adj. 令人愉悦的、容易接受的
    interstice
    n. 缝隙，微小的空隙
    genre
    n. 文学体裁，类别
    sophisticated
    adj. 精致的，复杂高雅的
  6. 06

    Sam Anderson, in a 2013 profile of Carson for The New York Times Magazine, said that the author’s work “can seduce even people who don’t like poetry.”

    2013年，萨姆·安德森（Sam Anderson）在《纽约时报杂志》撰写的一篇关于卡森的人物侧写中表示，卡森的作品“甚至能诱来那些不喜欢诗歌的人”。

    profile
    n. 人物专访，人物小传
    seduce
    v. 诱惑，勾引，使人着迷
  7. 07

    “As a classicist, poet and performer, Anne Carson has shown how the mysteries and absurdities of language and the eternal qualities of certain stories, possess us in the present,” Aragi, her agent, said in an emailed statement.

    “作为一名古典学者、诗人和表演艺术家，安妮·卡森展现了语言中的种种奥秘与荒诞，以及某些故事中永恒不变的特质，如何在当下依然深深支配着我们。”卡森的经纪人阿拉吉在一份电子邮件声明中写道。

  8. 08

    Carson is a private figure. In August 2024, she said that she had Parkinson’s disease and that her handwriting had “disintegrated.”

    卡森平素非常低调。2024年8月，她透露自己身患帕金森病，且手写字迹已经“解体崩溃”。

  9. 09

    Declan Spring, Carson’s American publisher, said that he had tried calling Carson to congratulate her on the Nobel, “but I got a message that her phone is off.”

    卡森的美国出版商德克兰·斯普林（Declan Spring）表示，他曾试着给卡森打电话祝贺她获得诺贝尔奖，“但我收到一条提示说，她的手机关机了”。

  10. 10

    During a news conference to announce the award on Thursday, Ingrid Carlberg, the Swedish Academy’s permanent secretary, said that Carson had sounded “very taken by surprise.” All the poet would say was that she was “in Iceland,” where she was “listening to the wind.”

    周四，在公布该奖项的新闻发布会上，瑞典学院常任秘书英格丽德·卡尔贝里（Ingrid Carlberg）表示，卡森听到消息时“大感意外”。除此之外，这位诗人只说了自己“在冰岛”，正在“聆听风声”。

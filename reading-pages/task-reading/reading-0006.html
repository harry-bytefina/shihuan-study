Passage 6(2023天津第一次) 主题：社会责任

食物、农业与地方身份

Growing up in San Francisco, Grace Young used to watch her father shop daily in Chinatown for whatever he needed to make traditional Chinese meals at home. As an award-winning cookbook author, Ms. Young, now 66, has spent decades shopping the same way in New York's Chinatown.

Ms. Young developed a passion for cooking at an early age. At 13, she started to sit in on cooking classes. After college, Ms. Young moved to New York and worked in a book-packaging company. In her 30s, she realized that while she had helped create more than 40 cookbooks, she didn't know how to make the dishes that tasted of home. “I knew if I recorded all of my parents' recipes, it would be a great gift that I could give my family and the next generation.” she says.

Yet what began as a recipe book became a kind of memoir. Talking about food encouraged her parents to finally open up about their past, like the fact that her father had owned a Chinatown restaurant in the 1940s. “It was really an amazing way to learn not only my family's recipes, but also my family's story.” she says. The Wisdom of the Chinese Kitchen (1999) launched Ms. Young's work in preserving and sustaining Chinese culinary (烹饪的) traditions.

Ms. Young has also devoted herself to supporting the restaurants in Chinatown. Since early 2020, Ms. Young has raised money to buy meals from Chinatown restaurants and deliver them to those in need. This year, instead of cooking at home for her husband and friends, she celebrated the Chinese New Year with various dishes from local restaurants in Chinatown. “If these restaurants don't survive, Chinese culinary traditions in our city won't survive.” she says.

1What is Ms. Young known as?(no more than 5 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. An award-winning cookbook author./A cookbook author. 细节表达题。根据第一段中的“As an award-winning cookbook author”可知,Young女士是一名获奖的烹饪书作者。

2Why did Ms. Young want to record her parents' recipes?(no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. (Because)she wanted to know how to make dishes that tasted of home. Or: (Because) the recorded recipes would be a gift for her family and the next generation. 细节表达题。根据第二段中“she didn't know how to make the dishes that tasted of home”及“I knew if I recorded all of my parents' recipes, it would be a great gift that I could give my family and the next generation.” 可知,Young女士不知道如何做具有自家味道的菜,希望记录下这些菜谱,使其成为给予家人及后代的礼物。

3What does the underlined word mean in Paragraph 3?(no more than 2 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. Family's story/Family's history/Record/Written story. 猜词表达题。根据“open up about their past(畅谈他们的过去)”和“to learn not only my family's recipes, but also my family's story(不仅可以了解家庭食谱,还可以了解家人的故事)”可以看出,这本书里记载了Young女士的家人过去的经历和故事。因此,这本食谱后来变成了一本“家庭回忆录”“家庭故事”或“家史”。

4What has Ms. Young done to help Chinatown restaurants? Please give an example. (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. She ordered dishes from Chinatown restaurants for the Chinese New Year. Or: She has raised money to buy meals from Chinatown restaurants for people in need. 细节表达题。根据第四段中的“Ms. Young has raised money to buy meals from Chinatown restaurants and deliver them to those in need”或“she celebrated the Chinese New Year with various dishes from local restaurants in Chinatown”可知,Young女士筹钱从唐人街的餐馆购买食物并送给有需要的人,她还从唐人街的餐馆订餐庆祝中国新年。

5How does Ms. Young inspire you in her efforts to preserve Chinese culinary traditions? Please explain in your own words. (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

Or: As a Chinese, I believe it's my duty to promote Chinese culture and make it known to the world. Or: We should deepen our understanding of Chinese culture before we can tell China's stories to the world.

AI时代，你“没用”的爱好正在变成一种竞争力

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Key to a Killer Résumé Is Your Perfect Scrambled Eggs

    一份亮眼简历的关键，是你能做出完美炒蛋的本事

  2. 02

    Last year, Kate Reder Sheikh placed dozens of associates and counsels at law firms across the Bay Area and Colorado. Some of their résumés stood out—but not for the honors they graduated with.

    去年，凯特·雷德·谢赫（Kate Reder Sheikh）为旧金山湾区和科罗拉多州的多家律所招募了数十名助理律师和法律顾问。其中一些人的简历格外引人注目，但并非因为他们毕业时获得了多少荣誉。

    counsel
    律师。这里指律所职位；这个词也可表示法律顾问。
  3. 03

    Beneath the prestigious firms, bar admissions and T14 law schools, the applicants listed additional skills. Training miniature dachshunds. Building birdhouses. And, in the case of one counsel, “perfecting restaurant-quality scrambled eggs.”

    除了名企、律师资格以及美国排名前14的法学院这些经历之外，这些求职者还列出了额外技能，包括训练迷你腊肠犬、制作鸟舍。还有一位法律顾问写道，自己正琢磨着“把炒蛋做到餐厅水准的美味”。

    prestigious
    声望很高的。这里是指在业内享有名望的律所。
    bar
    法律职业资格体系。bar admissions 指取得律师执业资格，并非酒吧。
  4. 04

    In a cutthroat white-collar talent market, where AI looms as a job-security threat, some recruiters are encouraging job seekers to get ahead by emphasizing decidedly human qualities like a canasta addiction or a weakness for pickling vegetables.

    在竞争激烈的白领人才市场上，随着AI对工作稳定性的威胁日益显现，一些招聘方开始鼓励求职者通过彰显鲜活的人类特质来脱颖而出——比如对凯纳斯特纸牌的痴迷，或对腌菜的热爱。

    loom
    逼近；令人担心地浮现。loom as a threat 说的是 AI 被视为越来越近的威胁。
    cutthroat
    竞争极其激烈的。这里形容白领求职市场。
    recruiter
    招聘人员；也可指为企业找人的猎头。

    句子结构

    In a cutthroat white-collar talent market, / where AI looms as a job-security threat, / some recruiters are encouraging job seekers to get ahead / by emphasizing decidedly human qualities like a canasta addiction or a weakness for pickling vegetables.

    where 从句补充说明的是 talent market，主句从 some recruiters 开始。encourage 的宾语是 job seekers，to get ahead 是希望他们做到的事。by emphasizing 说明求职者怎样争取优势，例子中的 addiction 和 weakness 写的是个人癖好。

  5. 05

    Advocates say an offbeat pastime can tempt a glassy-eyed recruiter to reread your application and serve as an icebreaker in interviews. Deciding what to include is another matter. Most importantly: Don’t you dare be vague.

    支持者认为，一项与众不同的业余爱好，可能会让已经疲惫不堪的招聘官拿起你的申请再看一遍，也能成为面试时打破僵局的谈资。但究竟该写哪些爱好，又是另一回事。最重要的一点是：千万别含糊其辞。

    offbeat
    不同寻常的，有点不按常理。这里指能让招聘者多看一眼的小众爱好。
    icebreaker
    用来打破陌生场合冷场的话题。这里是面试时可以聊起来的爱好。
  6. 06

    People will write, “‘sports, music or food’—yeah, every single person on planet Earth is interested in those things,” said Jack Kolb, a 24-year-old financial analyst in Chicago. A few years ago, as the president of the Investment Banking Academy at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Kolb helped fellow students apply for banking jobs. He urged everyone to list specific interests that showed personality.

    芝加哥一名24岁的金融分析师杰克·科尔布（Jack Kolb）说：“人们会写‘运动、音乐或美食’——拜托，地球上每个人都对这些感兴趣。”几年前，科尔布还是伊利诺伊大学厄巴纳-香槟分校投资银行学院学生主席时，曾帮助同学申请银行业的工作，他当时就敦促每个人列出能展现个性的具体兴趣爱好。

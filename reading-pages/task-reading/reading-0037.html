阅读表达

01-2020天津夏

学习、阅读与教育方式

As any younger brother will tell you, having a big brother involves a lot of walking in someone else's shadow, especially when you have a brother who is a typical example of “cool”. For years I wanted to do everything my elder brother Tyson did, but no matter how hard I tried, I was always the neglected(被忽略的) one. My legs just weren't made to run like his. I could never play football or basketball well.

I probably would have continued trying to keep up with him for the rest of my high school career, but when my family moved, everything changed. Tyson left for college, so I had to start at a new school all by myself. This new start gave me an opportunity to redefine myself and discover an entirely new version of “cool”.

I don't know what made me decide to try some new activities at the new school, but one day I chanced to show up for an after-school meeting of the Science Olympiad team. I had always been fascinated with chemistry, biology, and math, but since those interests hadn't fit Tyson's definition of “cool”, I had never pursued them. On this day, for some reason, I did.

As we organized teams, prepared resources and practiced answering questions, I felt more connected than I ever had to any sports team. I didn't feel as though I needed to keep up with anybody else; I was finally with peers who understood me. It was so good to feel accepted for what I'm good at. For the first time in my life, I actually felt cool. Now I know who I am!I'm Tyson's little brother, but that's only part of my identity. I'm a cool guy in my own way, too. I'm relieved to know that “cool” has a much broader definition than what I used to think.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1How do you understand the underlined part in Paragraph 1?(no more than 8 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. Receiving less attention than someone else. Or: Being/Feeling less successful than someone else. Or: Being/Feeling neglected. 猜词表达题。根据第一段中画线部分后面的句子“For years I wanted to do everything my elder brother Tyson did...the neglected one.”可知，多年来，作者想做哥哥泰森所做的一切，但无论如何努力，他总是被忽视。

2What changes offered a new start to the author after his family moved?(no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. That his brother left for college and he changed schools. Or: Tyson's leaving for college and the author's going to/attending a new school. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词new start可定位到文章第二段。根据第二段第二、三句可知，泰森去上大学了，所以作者不得不独自在一所新学校上学。这个新的开始让作者有机会重新定义自己，发现另一种“酷”。哥哥的离开和进入新学校都是作者改变的契机，回答问题时要确保答案的完整性。

3According to Paragraph 3, what marked the turning point in the author's growth?(no more than 7 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. Joining the Science Olympiad team. Or: That he joined the Science Olympiad team. Or: Discovering his true interests (in science). 细节表达题。根据题干定位到第三段。由该段第一句话可知，作者做出改变参与到新鲜事物中是从参加一个奥林匹克科学竞赛队的课后会议开始的。注意此处要用动名词短语或名词性从句来回答what作主语的问题。

4What does Paragraph 4 mainly talk about?(no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. (How) the author discovered a broader definition of “cool”. Or: He has discovered his own way to be cool. Or: He found his own identity. 主旨表达题。根据题干定位到第四段。该段主要讲述了作者通过参与课外小组活动重新定义了什么是“酷”，找到了自我认同。

5What is your example of “cool”? Please explain why.(no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. Playing football well is cool because I can show how strong I am. Or: Playing the piano is cool because it can give me joy. Or: Helping the poor is cool because it can give them hope and bring me pleasure.

一花一叶都是春：你观察到春天的踪迹了吗？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    I’m micro-dosing my way through this wintry moment in American history, looking closely for each tiny hint of spring-to-be. The prescription was laid out for me in the new book “Phenology” by Theresa Crimmins, a primer on the why and how of taking sharper notice of what happens when.

    我正在用一种“微剂量”的方式，一点点捱过美国历史上的这个寒冬，仔细寻找每一个春天要来的微小迹象。这个“药方”是特雷莎·克里明斯（Theresa Crimmins）的新书《物候学》（Phenology）为我开出的，这是一本入门书籍，介绍为什么以及如何更敏锐地注意到何时发生了什么事情。

    wintry
    adj. 冬天特有的
    primer
    n. 入门书，新手指南
  2. 02

    Her ask: Become an “everyday phenologist.” Phenology — from the same root as phenomenon — is about the timing of recurring seasonal events in plants and animals.

    特雷莎要求我们成为一名 “日常物候学家”。物候学（phenology）与现象（phenomenon）一词同源，它研究的是在植物和动物身上重复发生的季节性事件的时间规律。

  3. 03

    Our biggest question about timing right now is probably pretty straightforward, though: Is it spring yet? The calendar insists that the new season arrives precisely on March 20. Instead we can piece together a more textured sense of its start from real-time clues: Is the shadbush awake yet, or have the magnolias’ furry bud scales parted to make way for the blooms?

    不过，我们现在对于时机最大的疑问可能相当直白：春天到底来了没有？日历上明确写着，新的季节会在3月20日到来。但比起这固定的日期，我们其实可以从一些实时线索中，拼凑出更细腻有层次的春日初始图景：唐棣是否已经苏醒？木兰毛茸茸的花苞鳞片是否已经绽开，好让花朵盛放？

    shadbush
    n. 唐棣（一种蔷薇科落叶小乔木或灌木，多分布于北美，它的名字来源于“shad”鲱鱼和“bush”灌木）
    textured
    adj. 有质感的，有层次的
    straightforward
    adj. 简单的，容易理解的
  4. 04

    It feels as if each organism has its own answer — or maybe more accurately, holds a tiny but essential clue to the vast, intricately connected puzzle.

    这感觉就像是每个生物都有自己的答案——或者更准确地说，每个生物都掌握着一条微小但重要的线索，共同拼成这幅庞大又精妙相连的春天拼图。

    intricately
    adv. 错综复杂地（常用来表示事物内部的精密联系或细节的复杂性）
  5. 05

    “I have a strong biology background and I think I know what’s going on,” Dr. Crimmins said. “But I have witnessed so much more incredible detail, and learned a much deeper appreciation for these different organisms and what they do.”

    “我有很扎实的生物学背景，自认为对这一切了如指掌，”克里明斯博士说道。“但目睹了如此之多令人惊叹的细节，我对这些不同的生物和它们的作用有了更深刻的领悟。”

    detail
    n. 细节（不可数名词）
  6. 06

    “It’s kind of reassuring to see everything goes on, even though we’re not paying attention. And so when we do pay attention, there’s a lot of gifts for us to receive.”

    “令人感到安心的是，即使我们不曾留意，自然万物仍在如常运转。而当我们真正去关注时，会发现生活中充满了美好的馈赠。”

    reassuring
    adj. 令人安心的

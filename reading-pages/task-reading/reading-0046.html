太空、地球系统与科学探索

My name is Clara.I still remember that chilly December day,sitting in science class.I'd finished a worksheet early and picked up a TIME for Kids magazine.A piece of news caught my eye:NASA was holding an essay contest to name its Mars rover(火星探测器).Before I even knew anything else about it,a single word flooded my 11-year-old mind:Curiosity.

I couldn't wait for the bell to ring so I could get started on my essay.That afternoon,I raced home,sat down at the computer,and typed until my fingers ached.“Curiosity is an everlasting flame that burns in everyone's mind...”

Five months later,my mom received a phone call,and immediately,a wide smile spread across her face.

On August 5,2012,at 10:31 p.m.,the rover named Curiosity touched down safely on the surface of Mars,and I was honored to have a front-row seat in NASA.

Curiosity is such an important part of who I am.I have always been fascinated by the stars,the planets,the sky and the universe.I remember as a little girl,my grandmother and I would sit together in the backyard for hours.She'd tell me stories and point out the stars.Grandma lived in China,thousands of miles away from my home in Kansas,but the stars kept us together even when we were apart.They were always there,yet there was so much I didn't know about them.That's what I love so much about space.

People often ask me why we go to faraway places like Mars.My answer is simple:because we're curious.We human beings do not just hole up in one place.We are constantly wondering and trying to find out what's over the hill and beyond the horizon.

查看答案与讲解

1.By reading a TIME for Kids magazine. Or:From a TIME for Kids magazine. 原文信息在第一段第三句。

2Why did Clara have a front-row seat in NASA?(no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

2.She won the essay contest held by NASA. Or:She named the Mars rover “Curiosity”. Or:“Curiosity”was chosen as the name of the Mars rover. 根据第一段及第四段内容可知,美国航空航天局举办了为火星探测器命名的竞赛,作者的“好奇号”被采用,因此她有幸获得了坐在前排的机会。

3What does Clara remember about the time spent with Grandma?(no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

3.She sat together with Grandma listening to her stories about the stars./Her grandma told her stories about the stars./They sat together talking about the stars. 原文信息在倒数第二段第三、四两句。

4What does the underlined phrase“hole up”mean?(1 word)

查看答案与讲解

4.Stay/Remain/Live/Hide. 根据文章最后一句可知,人类总是不断地探索未知领域。由此推断出答案。

5In your opinion,why is curiosity important?(no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5.Curiosity is important because it leads to discovery or invention./With curiosity,we keep asking questions and exploring the world unknown./Curiosity drives us through our lives so that we never stop wondering about the things beyond our knowledge.　全文围绕好奇心展开叙述,正是出于好奇,人类才对未知领域不断地提出疑问,从而进行探索和发现。

    安东尼·霍普金斯爵士将于下个月发行自己的首张专辑，收录了他耗费六十年时间打磨的一系列古典音乐作品。

  6. 06

    The single, Bracken Road from Hopkins' 1947: Suite For Solo Piano And Orchestra, was influenced by the streets, meadows, farmland and mountains that surrounded the star's family home in the 1940s.

    这支名为《蕨草之路》的单曲选自霍普金斯的《1947：为独奏钢琴与管弦乐而作的组曲》，灵感来自20世纪40年代他的家宅周边的街道、草地、农田和山脉。

    suite
    n. （古典音乐专业术语）组曲；酒店套房，套间
    bracken
    n. 蕨类植物

    Sir Anthony composed the music in 1963 when he was a young actor at the Liverpool Playhouse.

    这支乐曲由霍普金斯爵士于1963年创作，当时他还是利物浦剧院的一名年轻演员。

  8. 08

    "Some of these pieces have lived with me for decades and I still find myself returning to them," Sir Anthony said.

    “这张专辑里的部分曲目已经陪伴我走过几十年，而我至今还会不断回到这些作品。”霍普金斯爵士说道。

  9. 09

    "My whole life is a dream," he added, describing signing with the Decca Classics record label as "the honour of a lifetime".

    “我的一生就是一场梦。”他补充道，形容自己能与迪卡古典唱片公司签约是“一生的荣幸”。

  10. 10

    Sir Anthony's music is performed by Grammy Award-winning conductor Gustavo Dudamel with the Philharmonia Orchestra, and will reflect on his life growing up in Wales and his family.

    这张专辑由曾获格莱美奖的指挥家古斯塔沃·杜达梅尔（Gustavo Dudamel）与爱乐乐团共同演绎，将展现霍普金斯爵士在威尔士成长的经历以及和其家庭有关的记忆。

  11. 11

    He said: "My deepest gratitude and respect go to Maestro Gustavo Dudamel, whose artistry is an integral part of this musical journey.

    他说：“我向古斯塔沃·杜达梅尔大师致以最深的感激与敬意。他的艺术造诣是这段音乐旅程不可或缺的一部分。”

    integral
    adj. 构成整体所必需的，不可或缺的
  12. 12

    "With the graceful precision of his baton, he transformed each note with profound and indelible meaning, creating a pictorial landscape that invites the listener to feel and imagine something uniquely personal."

    “随着其指挥棒优雅而精准的表达，他赋予每一个音符深刻且难以磨灭的意义，创造出一幅音乐的画卷，让听众去感受和想象属于自己的独特体验。”

    indelible
    adj. 擦不掉的，无法消除的，不可磨灭的
    baton
    n. 指挥棒
  13. 13

    Other pieces on the album draw on Sir Anthony's memories of childhood visits with his grandfather and the cinema that first caught his imagination.

    专辑中的其他曲目还取材于霍普金斯爵士童年时与祖父出行的经历，以及那座最初激发其想象力的电影院。

  14. 14

    Laura Monks, president of Decca Classics, said the team had been "thrilled" to work with "such a legend".

    迪卡古典唱片公司总裁劳拉·蒙克斯（Laura Monks）表示，制作团队非常“激动”能够与“这位传奇人物”合作。

    Monks, who witnessed Hopkins recording the album said the team had goosebumps on their arms and his "lifelong knowledge" of classical music was clear to those in the room.

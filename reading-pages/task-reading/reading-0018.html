Passage 8(2026天津河西质量调查二) 主题：个人成长

生态、气候与城市自然

I grew up in a small coastal town where the ocean was our backyard and our livelihood. My father was a fisherman, and from a young age, I was expected to follow in his wake. By the time I was sixteen, I could navigate the tides and mend nets as well as any seasoned sailor. My future seemed charted: I would take over the family boat.

Everything changed during my final year of high school. Our biology class took a field trip to a marine research station. For the first time, I saw the ocean not as a source of fish, but as a vast, interconnected ecosystem. Scientists explained how overfishing was damaging the very waters that fed my family. I was torn between tradition and a newfound sense of responsibility.

I decided to bridge the two worlds. Instead of immediately joining my father, I enrolled in a university marine biology program. The theoretical learning was challenging and often seemed disconnected from the practical world of the docks. During summer breaks, I returned home, working on our boat by day and explaining sustainable fishing practices to my father and other fishermen by night. At first, they were dubious, seeing science as an outsider's interference.

The turning point came when a harmful algal bloom(藻华) threatened our usual fishing grounds. Relying on both my father's experience and the early warning systems I learned about at university, we adjusted our routes. While others suffered losses, we had a safe and decent catch. That season, I wasn't just the fisherman's son; I became a trusted advisor.

Now, I run a community cooperative that combines local fishing knowledge with scientific data to promote sustainable practices. My father, once doubtful, is now my proudest partner. The ocean that shaped my past now guides my future—not just to take from it, but to understand and protect it for generations to come.

12月的音乐榜单，为什么总被圣诞歌占据？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Why Christmas Music Is More Popular Than Ever

    圣诞音乐为何比以往更受欢迎

  2. 02

    Listeners are embracing Christmas music earlier than ever—and playing more of it. On Dec. 1, 2019, 14 holiday songs were in the top 50 on Spotify in the U.S. This Dec. 1, the tally was already at 30.

    听众比以往更早地开始听圣诞音乐，而且播放量也更多。2019年12月1日，美国Spotify平台的单曲榜前50名中仅有14首节日歌曲；而今年的12月1日，这一数字已攀升至30首。

    tally
    累计数。这里指进入榜单前 50 的圣诞歌曲总数。
  3. 03

    By Dec. 10, 20 of the top 25 tracks were Christmas-themed. The only non-holiday songs able to withstand the deluge are juggernauts like "The Fate of Ophelia" by Taylor Swift and "Golden" from the hit Netflix movie "KPop Demon Hunters."

    截至12月10日，榜单前25名里有20首都是圣诞主题歌曲。唯一能在这场圣诞音乐浪潮中站稳脚跟的非节日歌曲，只有泰勒·斯威夫特的《奥菲莉亚的命运》以及网飞热门剧集《韩流驱魔师》的插曲《金色年华》这类爆款作品。

    withstand
    经受住。这里指非节日歌曲顶住圣诞歌的冲击，仍留在排行榜上。
    deluge
    洪水；比喻大量涌来的事物。这里说圣诞歌一下子占据了榜单。
    juggernaut
    势头强到难以阻挡的事物。这里指不容易被挤下榜的热门歌曲。
  4. 04

    Matt Bailey, founder of the music analytics company Hit Momentum, suggested that during periods of stress, listeners are more likely to return to familiar comforts. "The traditions of the holiday season, especially the music, provide us an emotional anchor," Bailey said.

    音乐分析公司“Hit Momentum”创始人马特·贝利（Matt Bailey）表示，人们在压力大的时期，更倾向于回归那些熟悉的慰藉之物。“节日季的传统习俗，尤其是节日音乐，能为我们提供情感的锚点。”贝利说道。

    anchor
    锚。emotional anchor 指能让情绪安定下来、给人熟悉感的东西。
  5. 05

    The usual suspects led the holiday charge on this year's chart: Mariah Carey's stampeding anthem "All I Want for Christmas Is You," Brenda Lee's country-tinged confection "Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree," and Wham!'s wistful gem "Last Christmas."

    今年的榜单上，依旧是那些熟悉的热门圣诞金曲占据主导地位：玛丽亚·凯莉（Mariah Carey）那首气势如虹的代表作《我想要的圣诞礼物只有你》、布兰达·李带（Brenda Lee）乡村甜美金曲《在圣诞树下摇摆》，以及Wham!乐队的感伤之作《去年圣诞》。

    wistful
    略带惆怅和怀念的。这里形容《Last Christmas》的情绪。
  6. 06

    Christmas songs "are fun to write and low pressure," said the music manager Jonathan Daniel. "People don't judge Christmas music the way they judge other releases, especially for the big pop stars," Daniel added.

    音乐经纪人乔纳森·丹尼尔（Jonathan Daniel）表示“创作圣诞歌曲充满乐趣，而且压力很小”。他还补充道：“人们不会像评价其他发行的音乐那样去评价圣诞歌曲，尤其是大牌流行歌手的作品。”

  7. 07

    It's not easy to break into the holiday canon with a new track. But artists are incentivized to try anyway. Daniel described a holiday hit as a "little ATM machine."

    想要凭借一首新歌跻身圣诞经典曲目之列并非易事，但这依然挡不住艺人们的尝试热情。丹尼尔形容成功的圣诞金曲就是一个“小型ATM机”。

    incentivize
    用利益或奖励促使人去做某事。圣诞歌可能年年带来收入，歌手因此愿意尝试。
    canon
    公认的经典作品。holiday canon 指每逢节日就会被反复播放的那批经典歌曲。
  8. 08

    Sia scored with her 2017 album "Everyday Is Christmas," which is earning close to 8 million streams a day. She wrote every song on the release, and "Snowman" has become a particular favorite, with more than 1.3 billion plays on Spotify alone since its release.

    希雅（Sia）在2017年发行的圣诞专辑《每天都是圣诞节》大获成功，该专辑每天的播放量接近800万次。专辑中所有歌曲均由希雅亲自创作，其中《雪人》一曲更是脱颖而出，备受喜爱。自发行以来，这首歌仅在Spotify平台的播放量就已突破13亿次。

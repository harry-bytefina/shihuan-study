02-2020天津春

成长、韧性与心理调适

I remember the day shyness took over my life. I was 14 and having a French lesson. The teacher told us to record ourselves speaking about a holiday. Perfect! I recorded myself with confidence. Then, I pressed “play” to listen back. All I heard was a little kid talking. My immediate reaction was to assume I was listening to someone else's recording. But that was my voice. I realised there was something strange about me.

My little voice has made me feel really shy. And my shyness has caused me to avoid attending events, not make important connections, and keep my ideas to myself. As a shy person, working from home, hiding behind my computer was brilliant. But the more time I spent hidden away, the more my comfort zone shrank (缩小). Everyday interactions, like ordering a coffee, became increasingly awkward and uncomfortable.

And then, one day I was at a big conference and it suddenly hit me like lightning. All speakers were confident, comfortable with being seen. There were no quieter voices. But the trouble is, if the loud voices are the only ones you hear, we quiet people feel even more alienated (疏远的). The world needs a mixture of personalities. Success is not just about who can shout the loudest. The world needs quieter people, too. That day I chose to stop hiding and embrace my shyness. No more feeling like I needed to change who I am in order to succeed. No more living a small life.

Then I created the Shy and Mighty Society, a space for shy people like me to shine, and in the process of helping others, I could also push myself forward, bit by bit. Now, I'm happy to tell people I feel shy. I'm not ashamed any more. And I know that I'm not alone.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Why did the author feel strange about herself after listening to the recording? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. Because her voice was different from what she had expected. Or: Because all she heard was a little kid talking. Or: Because of her little voice. 细节表达题。根据文章第一段中的“All I heard was a little kid talking.”及后文可知，她听到自己的录音感到陌生的原因是她的声音太小，像小孩子的声音，和自己想象中的不一样。

2What is the main idea of Para. 2? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. Her shyness had a/an (bad) influence/effect/impact on her (life). Or: Her shyness kept her hidden away. Or: Her shyness kept her away from other people. 主旨表达题。第二段讲的是作者的羞怯导致她远离他人。将第二段第二句“And my shyness has caused me to avoid attending events, not make important connections, and keep my ideas to myself.”改写成符合词数要求的答案即可。

3What does the underlined word in Para. 3 mean? (1 word)

查看答案与讲解

3. Accept./Welcome. 猜词表达题。根据上下文可推断词义。根据文中“That day I chose to stop hiding”以及“No more feeling like I needed to change who I am in order to succeed.”可知，作者接受了自己的羞怯。

4Why did the author create the Shy and Mighty Society? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. To help shy people (to shine) and push herself forward. Or: To help other shy people and herself. 细节表达题。根据最后一段第一句“Then I created the Shy and Mighty Society, a space for shy people like me to shine, and in the process of helping others, I could also push myself forward, bit by bit.”可知，作者建立这个组织的目的有两个：一是帮助其他与作者一样羞怯的人，二是推动自己逐渐进步。两点缺一不可。

5Suppose you are in charge of the Shy and Mighty Society, what will you do to help other shy people? And why? (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. I will organize a speech contest so that they can walk out of their comfort zone and become more confident. Or: I will share my experience with them, telling them how I handle my shyness. Or: I will encourage them to take part in various activities to improve their social skills.

83岁罹患帕金森，当身体开始"背叛"，他依然站在指挥台

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Parkinson's Made Barenboim Step Back. But He's on the Podium Again.

    帕金森病曾迫使巴伦博伊姆退居幕后。但他如今又重返指挥台。

  2. 02

    In a 2006 interview, the conductor Daniel Barenboim declared a special affinity for the precocious Mozart. "Look, I know very well what it means always to be too young for everything," Barenboim, a former child prodigy, said. That rings true. He was an international concert pianist at 10, debuting at Carnegie Hall at 14.

    在2006年的一次采访中，指挥家丹尼尔·巴伦博伊姆（Daniel Barenboim）表示，自己对早慧的莫扎特有一种特殊的亲近感。"你知道，我非常清楚，无论做什么事情，总是显得'太年轻'，是一种什么样的感觉，"这位昔日的神童说道。这话确实不假——他10岁就已经是国际知名的音乐会钢琴家，14岁便在卡内基音乐厅完成了自己的首秀。

  3. 03

    Now 83 and navigating a debilitating illness, he seems suddenly, sadly too old for many things. It was only last year, though, when his frailty had become visible and undeniable, that he confirmed that his condition was Parkinson's disease.

    如今，83岁的他正与一种使人衰弱的疾病作斗争，突然之间，他似乎因年事已高而对太多事力有不逮，这令人心痛。不过，直到去年，当他身体的虚弱变得肉眼可见、再也无法否认时，他才最终确认，自己所患的正是帕金森病。

    navigate
    v. 在……中艰难前行、努力应对
    frailty
    n. 虚弱，衰弱
  4. 04

    The illness has done little, however, to deter Barenboim's commitment to the West-Eastern Divan Orchestra, the ensemble he founded in 1999 with the Palestinian intellectual Edward Said to bring young Arab and Israeli musicians together. On Thursday, Barenboim appeared with the orchestra at the Waldbühne amphitheater in Berlin before a large and grateful audience. Ambitiously, he is scheduled to tour with the orchestra for nine more engagements in total.

    然而，疾病几乎没有动摇巴伦博伊姆对"西东合集管弦乐团"的投入。这支乐团由他本人于1999年，与巴勒斯坦学者爱德华·萨义德（Edward Said）共同创立，宗旨是将年轻的阿拉伯音乐家与以色列音乐家聚集在一起。周四，巴伦博伊姆在柏林的森林露天剧场，与乐团一同出现在了大批满怀感激的观众面前。他雄心不减，计划随乐团进行总计九场以上的巡演。

    deter
    v. 阻止、使某人打消念头
    ensemble
    n. 乐团、合奏组合；管弦乐团，小型室内乐组合
    amphitheater
    n. 露天圆形剧场
  5. 05

    Thursday's concert was one of four this month in which the West-Eastern Divan Orchestra will be joined by Yo-Yo Ma—another child prodigy—for Dvorak's Cello Concerto in B Minor. Ma's recent experiment aligns with the West-Eastern Divan's musical and humanitarian ethos, which Barenboim articulated succinctly in the wake of the Hamas-led attack on Israel on Oct. 7, 2023: "We have to listen to each other."

    周四的音乐会是本月的四场演出之一。在这些演出中，西东合集管弦乐团将与另一位神童出身的音乐家马友友合作，演绎德沃夏克的《B小调大提琴协奏曲》。马友友最近在音乐上的尝试，与西东合集乐团的音乐和人道主义理念不谋而合。2023年10月7日哈马斯主导的对以色列袭击事件发生后，巴伦博伊姆曾简洁地表述过这一理念：“我们必须倾听彼此。”

    articulate
    v. 清晰地表达、阐明
    succinctly
    adv. 简明扼要地
  6. 06

    He has spent more than 25 years giving this orchestra what they could not get without him: a sound. Weighty but transparent. Old fashioned, in a way, but not fussy. It is undoubtedly Barenboim's sound, but it's also the orchestra's to run with.

    25年以来，甚至更久，他倾力赋予这支乐团一样只有他才能赋予的东西——一种独属于这支乐团的声音。厚重，却又通透；老派，却不浮夸。这无疑是巴伦博伊姆的声音，但也是乐团可以传承的声音。

    weighty
    adj. 厚重的，有分量的

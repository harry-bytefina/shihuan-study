Passage 9(2026天津和平质量调查二) 主题：积极的人生态度

志愿服务、社区与包容

For a year after my multiple sclerosis(多发性硬化症) diagnosis, I navigated life from a wheelchair, adapting to profound changes. I trained myself to accept new limits, giving up my demanding hospital job and learning to rely on help from my husband, children, and friends. While the wheelchair brought a degree of independence, a deeper restlessness grew within me—a longing for the spontaneous, adventurous spirit that had defined my life before illness. I longed to seize a moment of pure joy again.

One night, I quietly wished for a sign, a direction toward something that could bring back that spark. Soon after, returning from my daughter's concert, a large roadside advertisement for tandem skydiving(双人跳伞) caught my eye. An immediate sense of certainty washed over me. Despite my family's worried reactions, I contacted the company and scheduled the jump.

On a perfect day, my family waited below. After the safety briefing in the hangar(飞机库), I boarded the small aircraft calmly alongside others. As we climbed with the open door, the thrilling rush of wind filled me with anticipation.

When it was my turn, my partner and I moved to the open doorway, our feet dangling over the edge. With a final countdown, we pushed off into the sky. The initial rapid fall took my breath away, followed by an immense, peaceful silence as the parachute opened. Gliding through the air, I felt a profound sense of liberation and lightness I had not experienced in years.

Touching down gently, I was met with cheers and embraces. The celebration at the hangar was filled with shared excitement and relief. Weeks later, when the professional photos arrived, we gathered to look through them. As I described each thrilling moment, I noticed a familiar face in nearly every picture—the kind skydiver who had driven me back up the hill after landing. Unaware at the time, I realized he had been quietly present throughout the entire experience, a watchful and reassuring presence from takeoff to touchdown.

This adventure restored my perspective. Choosing courage over caution, and discovering seen and unseen support, I reclaimed a part of my lost life. I learned that great limitations can lead to unexpected, soaring freedom.

1What limits did the author face after being diagnosed with illness? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. It refers to the spontaneous, adventurous spirit. 猜词表达题。根据第一段中a longing for the spontaneous, adventurous spirit以及第二段中“I quietly wished for a sign, a direction toward something that could bring back that spark”可知,作者渴望找回曾经自发、爱冒险的生活精神。

3What does the word “dangling” mean in the passage? (1 word)

查看答案与讲解

3. Hanging./Swinging. 猜词表达题。根据第四段中“When it was my turn, my partner and I moved to the open doorway”可知,作者和同伴身处飞机舱门口,双脚应悬空下垂,由此推断,画线单词意为“悬挂,垂吊”。

4Why did the familiar face in photos leave a deep impression on the author? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. Because he had offered her quiet and reassuring support from takeoff to touchdown. 细节理解题。根据第五段中“I realized he had been quietly present throughout the entire experience, a watchful and reassuring presence from takeoff to touchdown”可知,这位跳伞者全程安静的陪伴与守护让作者印象深刻。

查看答案与讲解

BBC报道：这个河北村庄几乎制造了中国所有灯笼

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The little town making nearly all of China's lanterns

    几乎包揽中国灯笼生产的小镇

  2. 02

    In China, red lanterns could guide a traveller to safety in cold winter alleyways, be symbols of power outside an imperial hall, or act as a call to religious devotion when hung in a temple.

    在中国，大红灯笼既可以在寒冷的冬日巷道中为行人指引方向，也曾悬于皇宫殿堂之外成为权力的象征，抑或是高挂在寺庙之中，召唤着虔诚信仰。

    imperial
    帝王的；皇室的。an imperial hall 指宫殿里的大殿。
    alleyway
    小巷。这里写灯笼在冬夜为行路人照路。
    devotion
    虔诚或全心投入。religious devotion 指宗教上的虔敬。
  3. 03

    They are also absolutely synonymous with Chinese new year celebrations right across the vast country and link today's Chinese people with the culture of their ancestors. To imagine how many lanterns would be hung here, at any given time, is like trying to count how many trees would be in the Amazon.

    它们更是中国农历新年庆祝活动的标志性元素，遍布这片广袤国土，将今天的中国人与祖辈的文化传统紧紧相连。要想象在任何一个时刻这里究竟悬挂着多少盏灯笼，就如同试图数清亚马逊雨林里有多少棵树一样困难。

    synonymous
    常和某事紧密相联。synonymous with Chinese new year celebrations 指人们一看到红灯笼就想到过年。
    ancestor
    祖先。这里通过灯笼把今天的生活与前人的传统连起来。
  4. 04

    And yet it is claimed that a remarkable 80% of the country's lanterns are still made – by hand – in one small town in Hebei Province.

    然而，据称全中国多达80%的灯笼，至今仍由河北省一个小村镇以纯手工方式制作完成。

  5. 05

    Nobody seems to know just how many hundreds of years ago the craft started in this part of northern China but, if you're born in Tuntou, you are immersed in lantern making from a very young age.

    没有人确切知道这门手艺究竟是在数百年前的何时在中国北方这片土地上兴起的。但如果你出生在屯头，从很小的时候起，你就会沉浸在制作灯笼的氛围之中。

    immerse
    让人浸在某种环境或活动中。be immersed in lantern making 说的是从小就日常接触制灯。
    craft
    需要手上功夫的技艺。这里专指手工制灯笼的本领。
  6. 06

    "When you see others in your family doing it every day, you get the hang of it quickly," one woman told the BBC, adding that "Kids here can pick it up at from around 10-years-old."

    “当你每天看着家里人都在做这件事，很快就能学会。”一位女士在接受BBC采访时说，她还补充道：“这里的孩子大概从十岁左右就能上手了。”

  7. 07

    It seems remarkable that, in an era of high-tech production lines, this town has been able to maintain a stranglehold on the lantern market, using production techniques from another era.

    令人惊叹的是，在这个高科技流水线生产的时代，这座小镇依然能够凭借另一个时代的制作技艺，牢牢占据灯笼市场。

    stranglehold
    牢牢控制。maintain a stranglehold on the market 指长期占住市场主导地位。
  8. 08

    In the future, modern production may find a way to match what this village can do by hand. However, for now, this is China's lantern town, and its residents are proud that it is known this way.

    未来，现代化生产或许会找到办法追赶上屯头村的手工艺水准。然而至少在当下，这里就是中国的“灯笼之乡”，而居民们也为这一称号深感自豪。

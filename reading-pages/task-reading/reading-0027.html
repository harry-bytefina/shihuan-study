01-2025天津夏

学习、阅读与教育方式

In the morning, I write this to-do list:

  1. Go to the bank.
  2. Get a nice haircut.
  3. Pick up my academic statement from college.
  4. Submit my poems.

I think,“That's enough for one day.”

Up until a few years ago, I didn't aim to be anywhere. And in order to be nowhere, one does not need a to-do list as a guide. Focusing only on the most pressing matter in front of me was enough. Let the next urgent moment think for me.

My mother had tried to teach me about planning my day ever since I was 10. But I never practiced it until I was 25 and done with college. That's when I awoke to the fact that I didn't want to waste any more time. I live in Nigeria, where life and success can be difficult. There seem to be many roads to failure here.

I grew up seeing my mother firmly in charge of her day. She knew—and still knows—where she needed to be at every moment, in every detail. She may not be good at singing, acting, or sports. But she is excellent at planning. Every day she writes a list. I never understood why she had to write down a list that consists of items you can easily hold in your mind. “Your to-do list is like your second mind,”she told my younger self. “If you ever feel lost or overwhelmed, you consult your second mind.”

I didn't understand what she meant by “a second mind” until I seemed to lose my first mind in my everyday life. Sometimes, it seems as though it's the rainy season in my mind, flooded with confused and distracting thoughts that lead nowhere. I need a second mind—now!

As I sit in the barbershop awaiting my nice haircut, a friend asks me, “What's next?”

“Pick up my academic statement from college,” I say. “Then submit my poems.”

“You've got it figured out like a granny,” he jokes.

“No,” I say, “like my mother.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Why did the author believe he didn't need a to-do list before he turned 25? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. (Because) he didn't aim to be anywhere. Or: (Because) he aimed to be nowhere. 细节表达题。根据文章第三段中“Up until a few years ago, I didn't aim to be anywhere. And in order to be nowhere, one does not need a to-do list as a guide.”可知,作者在25岁之前认为自己不需要日常规划,是因为他没有人生目标。

2What does the underlined phrase mean? (no more than 3 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. Became aware of./Was aware of./Realized./Came to understand. 猜词表达题。从上文的“But I never practiced it until...”和下文的“...I didn't want to waste any more time”可知,作者的态度发生转变,意识到人生需要规划,不能再浪费时间了。由此可推断awoke to意为“意识到”“开始懂得”。

3What kind of person is the author's mother? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. She is excellent at planning. Or: She is well-organized. Or: She likes making a to-do list. 细节表达题。根据文章第五段中“But she is excellent at planning.”可知,作者的母亲是一位善于规划的女性。

4When did the author turn to “a second mind” for help? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. When he seemed to lose his first mind. Or: When he felt confused and lost in his life. 细节表达题。根据第五段中“If you ever feel lost or overwhelmed, you consult your second mind.”以及第六段中“until I seemed to lose my first mind in my everyday life”可知,当作者在人生中感到迷茫和困惑时,就会求助于“第二大脑”。

5How have you benefited from some advice given by a family member? Please explain with an example. (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. I follow my mom's advice to read books and have learned a lot. Or: I have adopted my father's suggestion of “first things first”, making me well-organized and efficient. Or: My brother's advice of planning ahead has helped me make good use of my time.

外刊延伸

“温柔育儿”退场，这届父母用“FAFO”教孩子长记性

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Goodbye Gentle Parenting, Hello 'F—Around and Find Out'

    再见吧，“温和式育儿”；你好，“作死就自找苦吃吧”

  2. 02

    Carla Dillon tried lots of ways to discipline her rambunctious 13-year-old. But when he sprayed her with a water gun at a campground after she asked him not to, she saw only one option: She threw him in the pond, clothes and all. "Some of the best lessons in life are the hard ones," she said.

    卡拉·迪伦（Carla Dillon）曾尝试过各种方法来管教自己那位精力过剩的13岁儿子。但有一次在露营地，当卡拉让儿子别再玩水枪时，他仍然朝自己喷了一身水。卡拉觉得自己别无选择——便直接把儿子一整个扔水里，连衣服也没脱。“人生中最重要的一些教训，往往来自艰难的经历。”她说道。

    discipline
    v. 教导，训练
    rambunctious
    adj. 精力旺盛的
    campground
    n. 露营地
  3. 03

    The internet calls it "FAFO," short for "F—Around and Find Out." It's a child-rearing style that elevates consequences over the "gentle parenting" methods that have helped shape Gen Z.

    网络上把这种做法称为 “FAFO”，即“你要是作死，就自找苦吃吧（F— Around and Find Out）”的缩写。这种育儿方式比“温和式育儿法”更强调行为的后果，而后者正是塑造Z世代成长的重要理念。

    elevate
    v. 提升
    child-rearing
    n. 子女养育
  4. 04

    Parenting that's light on discipline has dominated the culture in recent decades. But critics blame the approach for some of Gen Z's problems in adulthood. They cite surveys that show young adults struggling with workplace relationships and suffering from depression and anxiety.

    近几十年来，轻纪律、重理解的育儿方式几乎主导着社会氛围。但批评者认为，这种方式导致Z世代在成年后遭遇了诸多困境。他们引用的调查指出，许多年轻人难以适应职场人际关系，并普遍受到抑郁和焦虑的困扰。

  5. 05

    FAFO parenting goes by lots of names: Tough love, authoritative parenting. Hard-line parenting is necessary for a child's survival in a harsh world, said FAFO father of five Jon Wellington. The era of the participation trophy is over, he said. "It caused us to get a little soft."

    FAFO式育儿有很多别称，比如“严厉的爱（tough love）”或“权威型教养（authoritative parenting）”。乔恩·威灵顿（Jon Wellington）有五个孩子，他表示为了在残酷的现实世界里存活，严苛管教对于孩子们来说是必须的。那个只要参与就人人有奖的时代已经结束了，他说道，“这种做法只能让人们变得脆弱。”

    authoritative
    adj. 权威的
  6. 06

    Becky Kennedy, the psychologist known as "Dr. Becky", sees aspects of FAFO working. All that mindful adulting has robbed parents of their freedom and left them tiptoeing around their children's feelings. She argues a gentler style doesn't advocate for those things, but it has been distorted in a culture that thrives on extremes.

    人称“贝琪博士”的心理学家贝琪·肯尼迪（Becky Kennedy）认为，FAFO育儿法理念中确实有可取之处。那种“正念式育儿”剥夺了父母的自由，让家长不得不小心翼翼地迎合孩子的情绪。她强调“温和式育儿”并非倡导这种走极端，只不过极端主义盛行的文化环境已扭曲了其本意。

    distort
    v. 扭曲，歪曲
  7. 07

    Still, the author of the parenting book "Good Inside" opposes FAFO's authoritative methods. "There's just a very old idea that somehow feelings get kind of equated with raising snowflakes, like feelings are weak," she said.

    不过，这位育儿书籍《温柔内在》（Good Inside）的作者仍然反对FAFO式的权威型教养。“人们总有一种陈旧的观念，觉得只要你重视情感，就会把孩子养成‘玻璃心’，仿佛有情感就是软弱的表现，”她说道。

    snowflake
    n. 玻璃心的、过分敏感的人

02-2024天津春

食物、农业与地方身份

Awake from a short sleep in her chair, my grandmother ran her fingers through her wavy white hair, looked out her window at the blue sky, and asked me what I would wish for if I had just one wish.

She often asks this, and I always answer the same way—“To have Granddad back”—which usually gets her reminiscing about him. Memories of their 67-year life together would always make her smile.

My grandmother was born in Ireland. In her teens, she moved to a poor village in England. At 28, my grandmother walked herself through the snow to birth her first child. When she was 50, she survived an emergency surgery. In her 60s, she suffered from arthritis (关节炎) but still managed to climb Snowdon, Wales's tallest peak.

About a decade ago, I noticed that she began to lose her hearing. If I asked what she had for lunch, she might say, “Oh, the weather has been lovely today.” She seemed to recycle the same handful of answers to my questions.

In recent years, I've been trying to show up for her more, in person. One day after I made us coffee, I asked her: “What's the secret to being successful in your 90s?”

“Oh Richard, so many people are old at 60. They just want to sit all day. You won't make it to 90 like that. You have to try.”

“Try what?”

“Try walking,” she said. “Try gardening. Try cooking. Trying doesn't require a lot of trying. Just try a little. Like, with this coffee you've made us. I know you tried.”

Even at 93, my grandmother still knits blankets for the local hospital's babies and buys books to continue with her French. “Age is just another bother attempting to convince you of the impossible in the world, but actually the world is filled with possibilities,” she once said.

1What does the underlined phrase mean in Paragraph 2? (no more than 2 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. Remembering./Thinking of./Thinking about./Missing. 猜词表达题。根据第二段中的“Memories of their 67-year life together would always make her smile.”可知,作者的回答让奶奶想起了和爷爷在一起的时光。

2What is the main idea of Paragraph 3? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. My grandmother has/had a rich life experience./My grandmother has gone through a lot./My grandmother is/was an extraordinary woman. 主旨表达题。根据第三段中的“was born”“In her teens”“At 28”“When she was 50”“In her 60s”可以看出,作者描写了奶奶人生中几个重要的事情,包括跨国迁居、踏雪分娩、术后康复和患病登山。这些事件展现了奶奶的坚韧不拔,反映出她是一个非凡的女性。因此如果从事件本身出发,可以概括该段主旨为:奶奶的生活经历丰富(My grandmother has/had a rich life experience)或是奶奶经历了很多(My grandmother has gone through a lot);如果从事件反映出的形象出发,可以将主旨概括为:奶奶是一个非凡的女性(My grandmother is/was an extraordinary woman)。

3Why did the author's grandmother recycle the same answers to his questions? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. (Because) she began to lose her hearing./(Because) she could not hear clearly. 细节表达题。根据第四段中的“I noticed that she began to lose her hearing”可知,作者的奶奶因为听力开始衰退,所以用相同的答复回应作者不同的询问。

4How can people be successful in old age according to the author's grandmother? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. They should remain active in old age./By trying different things./They should try doing things. 细节表达题。从第六至八段可知, 作者的奶奶认为老年人尝试做各种各样的事情是健康长寿的秘诀。

5In your opinion, what can young people do to improve the quality of old people's life? (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. We can help old people to learn new skills so that they can keep pace with society./They can visit their grandparents more often and spend time with them.

外刊延伸

下班小酌已成往事，“happy hour”为什么消失了？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Once a staple of professional life, happy hour is now a casualty of a corporate cultural reset. Many people still aren't coming to the office. Those who do just want to get home by 6. Business budgets for mandatory fun have shrunk. Everyone's drinking less.

    曾是职场生活标配的“欢乐时光”（happy hour），如今成了企业文化重塑浪潮下的牺牲品。许多人依旧不回办公室上班，即便到岗的人，也只想在傍晚6点前回到家。公司用于“强制性社交娱乐活动”的预算大幅缩减，人们的饮酒量也普遍下降。

    staple
    常有的一部分。a staple of professional life 指下班小酌曾经是职场生活的惯例。
    casualty
    因变化而受损或消失的人或事。这里指小酌聚会成了职场文化转变的牺牲品。
    mandatory
    必须参加或遵守的。mandatory fun 带点讽刺：单位组织的玩乐也像是任务。
  2. 02

    These trend lines may be good for work-life balance, but the downside for some is profound: Young workers say they're missing out on opportunities to make friends and find mentors. Bar owners are hurting, too.

    这些变化趋势或许有益于工作与生活的平衡，但对一部分人而言，负面影响却十分深远：年轻职场人表示，自己错失了结交朋友、寻找导师的机会，酒吧经营者的生意也因此惨淡不已。

    mentor
    愿意指导后辈的前辈。这里指年轻员工原本可在聚会中结识的职业引路人。
    profound
    深远的。这里指少了下班聚会，对部分年轻人的社交和职业帮助影响很大。
  3. 03

    "Companies would come out with the boss, who would throw their credit card down and everybody would be drinking throughout the evening," said Andrew Rigie, the executive director of the New York City Hospitality Alliance. "There's a lot less of that now." He said that for some young people, run clubs and other fitness activities have become a replacement for letting loose after work.

    纽约市酒店业联盟执行董事安德鲁·里吉（Andrew Rigie）表示：“过去，公司会由老板带队集体外出，老板潇洒地甩出一张信用卡，大家便能畅饮一整晚。如今这样的光景已经少了太多。”他提到，对一些年轻人来说，跑步俱乐部这类健身活动，已经取代了下班后的放松小酌。

    hospitality
    餐饮、酒店等接待服务行业。这里是纽约餐饮服务行业协会的名称用词。
  4. 04

    Omar Khatib, 30, lives in New York and works fully remote. During the week, "a lot of my guy friends are not really getting together for drinks ever," he said. When he travels to the cities where his company has offices—London, Amsterdam, Prague and Paris—the cultural difference is stark. "Almost all the time at 5, 5:30, people are like, 'We're going to go grab drinks. You want to go?'" said Khatib.

    30岁的奥马尔·哈提卜（Omar Khatib）住在纽约，从事全职远程工作。他说：“平日里，我的那帮男性好友几乎再也不会聚在一起喝一杯了。”当他前往公司设有办事处的城市——伦敦、阿姆斯特丹、布拉格和巴黎出差时，能明显感受到文化差异。哈提卜说：“在那些地方，几乎每天下午5点到5点半，总会有人招呼说‘我们要去喝酒，要不要一起？’”

    stark
    非常明显的。the cultural difference is stark 指下班聚会习惯的差别一眼就能看出。
  5. 05

    There are signs the tide could be turning. Some companies have found that the way to gather colleagues over beers is to make the office the venue. Kobby Adu-Diawuo, an investor, said that every Wednesday or Thursday, his firm in Newark, N.J., enjoys a full spread of drinks and snacks. Almost everyone hangs out for at least about an hour.

    也有迹象表明，这股潮流或许正在悄然转向。一些公司发现，要想把同事们聚在一起喝啤酒，一个好方法就是把办公室直接设为聚会场地。投资人科比·阿杜-迪亚沃（Kobby Adu-Diawuo）就职于新泽西州纽瓦克市（Newark, N.J.）一家公司，他表示，公司每周三或周四都会备好丰富的酒水和零食，几乎所有员工都会留下来欢聚至少一小时。

    venue
    举办活动的场地。这里把办公室本身当作聚会地点。

Passage 1 主题：乐于学习,善于学习

Passage 1 主题：乐于学习,善于学习 · 学习、阅读与教育方式

I arrived home from work, my mind racing. I was scheduled to perform an experiment using a pricey piece of equipment, but I had spent the whole day worrying about the experiment which was at the top of my agenda (日程), and I amazingly longed to shut off my anxious thoughts. So after dinner, I made a cup of hot chocolate, stretched myself on my sofa, and opened a page. Almost instantly, my mind left behind the details of experimental design and stepped into a land of monsters, magic dust, and man-like bears comprising a wonderful world distinct from my daily life. It was exactly what I needed.

Growing up, I was virtually never a big fan of novels. My classmates would be crazy about the latest Harry Potter book, while my copy sat collecting dust on a shelf. Instead, I would choose to read through the reference books related to my academic fields for better grades. After I started graduate school, extracurricular reading didn't appeal to me at all but I spent most of my time on campus digesting research papers and textbooks and my mind couldn't handle processing fact-filled nonfiction books at home, too. So I knew that I needed to find a way to enjoy reading again.

To help me stick with it, I made a New Year's resolution: I would read two fiction books per month for the entire year. The result was an almost instant appreciation of fiction, along with many unexpected results including ones that have benefited my schoolwork.

What surprised me the most was how much I learned. Some of the best books I read were historical fiction stories happening in a realistic historical setting. I learned about life in different countries, as well as struggles people there faced during difficult periods in their histories. The books have helped me build sympathy and understanding, with an unexpected benefit: I've started to think more deeply about the diversity issues in the scientific community and could serve it, heart and soul.

“总统红利”没了？拜登卸任后的“困顿”人生

By Emily Glazer, Annie Linskey and Erich Schwartzel

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Joe Biden Is Struggling to Cash In on His Presidency

    卸任后，拜登难以“兑现”他的总统身份红利

  2. 02

    Biden, 82 years old, is charting a postpresidency that is less lucrative than what he'd expected when he left office. Options for big jobs are limited by his advanced age, his unpopularity in Democratic circles and companies—concerned about retribution from President Trump—that aren't offering speaking gigs.

    现年82岁的拜登已卸下总统职务，正为以后的日子做打算，新生活并未如他当初卸任时所预期的那般赚钱。可供他选择的高薪工作相当有限，这是多重因素导致的：一是他年事已高，二是他在民主党内人气不旺，三是企业界因为担心遭到总统特朗普的报复，不愿邀他做演讲。

    lucrative
    adj. 利润丰厚的
    retribution
    n. （带有惩戒意味的）报复
    chart
    v. 画图表，做规划；n. 图表
    advance
    n. （商业领域；出版领域）预付款
  3. 03

    The result for Biden is a leaner next chapter that lacks the well-funded foundations, plans for exquisite libraries and full calendar of paid speeches his peers enjoyed.

    对拜登来说，这意味着一个更为“简朴”的新篇章：没有资金雄厚的基金会，没有计划建设华丽的图书馆，也没有前任总统们那样排得满满当当的付费演讲日程。

    exquisite
    adj. 精美的，品味高的
  4. 04

    In the weeks after Biden left office, he confided in some that he intended to pay off around $800,000 in personal debt he had accumulated, including through loans on the $2.7 million Rehoboth Beach house he and Jill Biden had purchased in June 2017.

    拜登在卸任后的几周里曾向一些人透露，他打算还清累积约80万美元的个人债务，其中包括通过贷款购置房产所产生的债务。这套房产是他与妻子吉尔·拜登（Jill Biden）于2017年6月以270万美元买下的里霍博斯比奇市（Rehoboth Beach）住宅。

  5. 05

    On top of bills for Hunter Biden's legal woes, Biden also wanted to help his daughter Ashley, who filed for divorce last month, and ensure money is left for his grandchildren, people close to the Bidens said.

    据熟悉拜登一家的人士透露，除了要为儿子亨特·拜登（Hunter Biden）的法律纠纷支付费用外，拜登还希望能帮助上个月提出离婚的女儿阿什莉（Ashley），并确保能给孙辈们留下一些钱。

  6. 06

    The former first son, and father of five, has money troubles. Hunter Biden’s former wife, and the mother of three of his children, has had to go to court repeatedly over the last six years to request alimony, court papers show. He owed her more than $3 million in April 2023, according to a filing by her attorney that month, in a case that remains unresolved.

    这位前“第一儿子”（亨特·拜登）是五个孩子的父亲，目前深陷财务困境。法庭文件显示，过去六年里，他的前妻，也是他三个孩子的母亲，不得不多次诉诸法庭，要求他支付赡养费。根据其前妻律师2023年4月提交的文件，截至当时，亨特·拜登尚欠她超过300万美元，而该案件至今仍未解决。

    attorney
    n. 律师（全称是attorney at law，有执业资质，可代理案件可出庭，在美国语境中，这个词比lawyer更正式，出现频率也更高。）
    alimony
    n.（法律术语）离婚赡养费
    repeatedly
    adv. 反复地
  7. 07

    Biden this summer sold his presidential memoir to the Hachette Book Group for an advance in the range of $10 million. The amount is far less than the $60 million deal secured by the Obamas when they left office. Biden said at an event in July that he was "working my tail off" to write a memoir.

    今年夏天，拜登将自己的总统回忆录出版权出售给哈谢特出版集团（Hachette Book Group），拿到的预付款金额约为1000万美元，这个数额远低于奥巴马夫妇在离开白宫后获得的6000万美元出版合约。拜登在7月的一场活动中表示，他正在“拼命”撰写这本回忆录。

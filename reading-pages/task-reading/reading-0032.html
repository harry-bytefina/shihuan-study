02-2023天津春

食物、农业与地方身份

Growing up in San Francisco, Grace Young used to watch her father shop daily in Chinatown for whatever he needed to make traditional Chinese meals at home. As an award-winning cookbook author, Ms. Young, now 66, has spent decades shopping the same way in New York's Chinatown.

Ms. Young developed a passion for cooking at an early age. At 13, she started to sit in on cooking classes. After college, Ms. Young moved to New York and worked in a book-packaging company. In her 30s, she realized that while she had helped create more than 40 cookbooks, she didn't know how to make the dishes that tasted of home. “I knew if I recorded all of my parents' recipes, it would be a great gift that I could give my family and the next generation.” she says.

Yet what began as a recipe book became a kind of memoir. Talking about food encouraged her parents to finally open up about their past, like the fact that her father had owned a Chinatown restaurant in the 1940s. “It was really an amazing way to learn not only my family's recipes, but also my family's story.” she says. The Wisdom of the Chinese Kitchen (1999) launched Ms. Young's work in preserving and sustaining Chinese culinary (烹饪的) traditions.

Ms. Young has also devoted herself to supporting the restaurants in Chinatown. Since early 2020, Ms. Young has raised money to buy meals from Chinatown restaurants and deliver them to those in need. This year, instead of cooking at home for her husband and friends, she celebrated the Chinese New Year with various dishes from local restaurants in Chinatown. “If these restaurants don't survive, Chinese culinary traditions in our city won't survive.” she says.

为了证明自己没用AI，作家们开始故意留错别字了

词汇梳理背景补充
  1. 01

    Writers Are Going to Extremes to Prove They Didn't Use AI

    为了证明自己没用 AI，作家们正走向极端

  2. 02

    As a professional writer, Sarah Suzuki Harvard says she isn’t inclined toward overtly exuberant prose. But these days, she finds herself going rogue.“I’ll use aggressively casual language, like,‘hey yo, for real,’or drop a bunch of exclamation points.”

    作为一名职业作家，萨拉・铃木・哈佛（Sarah Suzuki Harvard）表示，她向来不喜欢过于华丽浮夸的文风。但如今，她却开始刻意不走寻常路。“我会用极度口语化的表达，比如‘嘿哟，说真的’，或者堆砌一堆感叹号。”

    exuberant
    adj. 文字非常绚丽、文风张扬的
    overtly
    adv. 公开地；明显地
  3. 03

    Like many writers, Harvard fears being accused of wielding machine-made material. She’s seen it happen to others and is proactively trying to prove her human bona fides.

    和许多作家一样，哈佛害怕被指控使用 AI 生成的内容。她亲眼见过别人遭遇这种事，因此正主动采取行动，证明自己作为人类作者的真实性。

    wield
    v. 使用
    proactively
    adj. 积极主动地（强调提前预判风险，提前准备）
  4. 04

    Controversy over authors using AI has made regular headlines, including in March when publisher Hachette pulled the book“Shy Girl”over allegations that it was partly AI-composed. Schools and teachers have used AI-detecting software for years to catch students cutting corners, often with mixed success.

    关于作家使用 AI 的争议频频登上头条，今年 3 月就发生过一起：出版商阿歇特因被指控部分内容由 AI 撰写，下架了《羞涩女孩》/《害羞女孩》（Shy Girl ）一书。多年来，学校和老师一直在用 AI 检测软件抓投机取巧的学生，但效果始终参差不齐。

    controversy
    n. 争论事件，争议
    allegation
    n. 尚未被证实的指控
  5. 05

    Andy O’Bryan, who co-founded an online group for entrepreneurs interested in AI, said he’s seen more people trying to scuff up AI-generated prose with typos or run-on sentences.

    安迪・奥布赖恩（Andy O’Bryan）联合创立了一个爱好AI 创业者的线上社群。他说，自己看到越来越多人试图用错别字、连写长句来把 AI 生成的文本 “弄糙”。

  6. 06

    As AI models advance, figuring out what’s written by bots has gotten harder, said Ivan Jackson, 27, founder of the startup Writehuman. His company’s software edits AI-generated text to make it sound more human with monthly updates to capture trends.

    27 岁的伊万・杰克逊（Ivan Jackson）是初创公司 Writehuman 的创始人。他表示，随着 AI 模型不断升级，分辨哪些内容是机器人写的变得越来越难。他的公司开发的软件专门对 AI 生成文本进行润色，使其看起来更像人类手笔，并且会每月更新以捕捉最新的语言趋势。

    句子结构

    As AI models advance, / figuring out what’s written by bots / has gotten harder, / said Ivan Jackson, 27, founder of the startup Writehuman.

    先读主干 figuring out … has gotten harder：「弄清……变得更难了」。主语是整个 -ing 短语，what’s written by bots 作 figuring out 的宾语，指「哪些文字由软件写成」。As 表示两件事随时间变化。句尾才给出说话人，founder of … 是补充身份的同位语。

  7. 07

    Its analysis suggests current hallmarks include overreliance on phrases such as“rather than”and“essential for.”At the same time, Jackson said text rewritten by humans is increasingly flagged by AI detectors, a trend he chalks up to people unconsciously starting to copy AI’s style.

    该公司的分析显示，当前 AI 文本的典型特征包括过度使用 “而非”“至关重要” 这类短语。与此同时，杰克逊指出，人类改写的文本也越来越多地被 AI 检测工具误判为 AI 生成，他将这一趋势归因于人们已经在无意识地模仿 AI 的写作风格。

    hallmark
    n. 标志性特征
  8. 08

    Ryan Johnson, 33, quit using ChatGPT last fall, worried its smooth output was eliminating what once made his blog sound distinct.“It’s like the restaurant that starts to water down the soup,”he said.“People don’t leave immediately, but eventually they’re like, eh, it doesn’t have the same kick.”

    33 岁的瑞安・约翰逊（Ryan Johnson）去年秋天停止使用 ChatGPT。他担心 AI 流畅却千篇一律的输出，正在磨灭他博客原本独特的风格。“这就像餐馆开始给汤兑水，” 他说，“人们不会立刻走掉，但最终总会觉得，嗯，这汤没原来那个味儿了。”

    distinct
    adj. 独特的，截然不同的

背景补充

被怀疑使用 AI 和证实使用 AI 是两回事

报道并列写了出版方撤书、学校使用检测软件和作者担心被误判三种处境。Over allegations 说明决定涉及一项指称，with mixed success 则限定软件的实际效果。读这类报道时，记清谁提出指称、谁作出决定，以及是否另有证据支持。

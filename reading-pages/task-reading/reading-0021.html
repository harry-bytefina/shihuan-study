Passage 11(2025天津河东二模) 主题：跨文化沟通

文化遗产、艺术与跨文化

Like many of my friends in China and across the world, I was thrilled to view the blockbuster Ne Zha 2 recently and I must say I thoroughly enjoyed it! What amazed me most was the high-quality animation (动画制作) and vivid imagery used to retell this classic Chinese epic in a truly compelling way for the new age. Indeed, the film serves as an important medium to transport audiences from reality to the mystical (as in Ne Zha), to places far removed from our own neighborhoods, and to hear stories from our shared human experience.

Some of the first images that I had of China as a child were from Hong Kong kung fu films that were extremely popular in Trinidad and Tobago in the 1980s and 1990s. Besides, names of martial arts stars such as Bruce Lee, Jackie Chan and Jet Li were known in every household, giving many their first look into a world across the seas and a culture, language and way of life that, while present in my own home as a member of the small but long-standing local Chinese community, remained a mystery to the majority.

Later on, popular films such as Walt Disney's Mulan and the Oscar-winning Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon further enamored my generation with the stories of China, allowing us to eagerly catch up with what domestic audiences here had long enjoyed—a dynamic Chinese film industry telling authentic stories from their own view.

In today's world where there seems to be more division than consensus (共识), and where we seem to judge first and ask questions later, film remains a powerful medium and channel for fostering more empathy and mutual understanding and bridging divides across regions despite differences in language and cultural affinities—leading to a greater appreciation of the complexities, richness, and diversity of the human experience. Coming from a place of common understanding, only then can we have a more impactful dialogue on the global ambitions that we collectively envision.

1According to the passage, what aspects of Ne Zha 2 impressed the author most? (no more than 8 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. The high-quality animation and vivid imagery. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词impressed the author most可定位在第一段。根据“What amazed me most was the high-quality animation and vivid imagery...for the new age.”可知,最让作者惊叹的是,它采用了高品质的动画制作以及生动的画面,以一种真正引人入胜的方式,为新时代重新讲述了这部中国经典史诗。

2Why does the author think Ne Zha 2 is an important medium? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. (Because) it bridges reality to the mystical, sharing human stories. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词Ne Zha 2 is an important medium可定位在第一段。根据“Indeed, the film serves as an important medium to transport audiences from reality to the mystical...to hear stories from our shared human experience.”可知,这部电影是一个重要媒介,它将观众从现实带入神秘世界(如《哪吒》中的那样),带到与我们的街区完全不同的地方,让观众聆听来自人类共同经历的故事。

3What made the author have the first impressions of China as a child? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. Hong Kong kung fu films and martial arts stars. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词have the first impressions of China as a child可定位在第二段。根据第二段内容可知,作者小时候对中国的一些最初印象来自20世纪80年代和90年代在特立尼达和多巴哥极受欢迎的香港功夫电影。此外,像李小龙、成龙和李连杰等武术明星的名字家喻户晓,让许多人首次见识到了大洋彼岸的一个世界,以及一种文化、语言和生活方式。

4What does the underlined word “enamored” in Paragraph 3 most likely mean? (1 word)

5Can films promote cross-cultural understanding? Please explain. (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. Yes. Film is a powerful medium and channel for fostering more empathy and mutual understanding.

    Months after her painting“The Dream (The Bed)”sold for $54.7 million—crowning her the world’s most expensive female artist at auction—the Mexican painter is poised to be the subject of several museum blockbusters this year.

    就在其画作《梦（床）》以 5470 万美元高价拍出、一举成为全球拍卖史上成交价最高的女性艺术家作品数月后，这位墨西哥女画家今年还将成为多家博物馆重磅大展的核心主题。

    poised
    adj. 蓄势待发、准备好做某事的

    v. 为……加冕
  2. 02

    “Every decade has its ups and downs with Frida, but she’s the girl who refuses to go away,”said Mari Carmen Ramírez, a curator at the Museum of Fine Arts in Houston.

    休斯顿美术馆策展人玛丽・卡门・拉米雷斯（Mari Carmen Ramírez）表示：“弗里达的热度每十年都会起起落落，但她始终经久不衰，从未淡出大众视野。”

  3. 03

    Today, she is a pop-culture icon whose indigenous fashion, famous unibrow and braided updos are as commercialized as her artistic output. Curators say they’re looking at her oeuvre more closely now because her influence on younger artists keeps expanding, lately to those with disabilities who admire her raw, vulnerable portrayal of her own pain.

    如今，她已是流行文化标杆。独具本土风情的穿搭、标志性连心眉与编发盘发造型，和她的艺术作品一样被高度商业化。策展人表示，学界如今愈发深入研究她的全部创作，因为她对新锐艺术家的影响力持续扩大，尤其深刻影响着残障艺术创作者 —— 他们钦佩于她直白又脆弱的自我痛苦刻画。

    vulnerable
    adj. 脆弱的；容易受伤的
    oeuvre
    n.（艺术家、作家或作曲家的）全部作品
  4. 04

    She created roughly 200 paintings, but many of them are legally barred from leaving Mexico. So it’s no small feat that Ramírez in Houston amassed 35 of Kahlo’s works for“Frida Kahlo: The Making of an Icon.”

    她一生创作约 200 幅画作，其中大量作品受法律限制，禁止流出墨西哥。因此，休斯顿的拉米雷斯为《弗里达・卡罗：偶像的诞生》展览集结 35 幅卡罗原作，这绝非易事。

  5. 05

    The show includes some early gems, including her 1926 self-portrait and“Henry Ford Hospital”from 1932, which offers an unsparing look at the artist following a miscarriage, in which blood vessels fan out like ribbons and objects like snails hint at the slowness of her recovery.

    本次展览收录多幅早期佳作，包括 1926 年自画像与 1932 年的《亨利・福特医院》。这幅作品毫无保留地展现了艺术家流产后的状态：血管如丝带般舒展蔓延，蜗牛等意象暗喻她漫长缓慢的康复过程。

    ribbon
    n. 丝带，绸带
    unsparing
    adj. 不留情面的；毫不掩饰的
  6. 06

    The motif of blood-vessels-as-ropes also appears in the Museum of Modern Art’s exhibit,“Frida and Diego: The Last Dream,”which opens March 21 and runs through Sept. 12. Curator Beverly Adams said ropes, scaffolding and draping blue fabric will be used to create theatrical set pieces around the show’s seven Kahlo works and dozen-odd Riveras.

    “血管化作绳索” 这一艺术母题，同样出现在纽约现代艺术博物馆的展览《弗里达与迭戈：最后的梦》中。该展 3 月 21 日开展，展期至 9 月 12 日。策展人贝弗利・亚当斯（Beverly Adams）称，展厅将利用绳索、脚手架与垂坠蓝色织物，围绕 7 幅弗里达画作及十余幅里维拉作品，打造极具戏剧感的展陈布景。

    motif
    n. 文学或艺术作品中反复出现的主题或中心思想
  7. 07

    “All of Kahlo’s works speak to her different trajectories and her self-constructions,”Adams said.“One doesn’t encapsulate her.”

    encapsulate
    v. 高度概括、浓缩

01-2022天津夏

食物、农业与地方身份

It was a dark and stormy night. The ferocious wind shook the windows wildly, as though someone outside were beating on the glass. It was also New Year's Eve. We were having our annual party and had a house full of people just starting to celebrate.

Suddenly, we heard loud explosions. Looking outside and up into the hills, we saw sparks(火花)flying from electrical transformers(变压器). One area after another went dark up in those hills. Then there was the loudest explosion of them all and our house went dark too. I tried to find every candle we had and lit them. The candles made everything look lovely. But we had problems. We had fifteen people standing around and we still had to cook dinner. How would we do that without electricity?

The barbecue! Why not cook on the barbecue? We men went outside, some holding flashlights and others cooking. We did a wonderful job. The women stayed inside and got the salads ready. Everything was delicious. There were still a few hours to go before the beginning of the new year, so we all sat around the dining room table and sang up until a few minutes before midnight. We couldn't watch the ball drop in Times Square on television but that wouldn't stop us from celebrating. I stood on a chair and, with the help of someone's watch to tell us the time, we all counted down and I dropped a tennis ball! We all screamed Happy New Year. We didn't need electricity for that!

Nowadays, we still get together with the same group to celebrate the New Year and we still talk about that special night. I don't think we have ever laughed so much as we did on that New Year's Eve.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What does the underlined word mean in Paragraph 1?(1 word)

查看答案与讲解

1. Fierce/Strong/Violent. 猜词表达题。根据文章第一段中“The ferocious wind shook the windows wildly, as though someone outside were beating on the glass.”可知狂风摇晃着窗子,仿佛人在击打着窗玻璃。故此处表“大风,强风”。

2What made dinner preparation difficult according to Paragraph 2?(no more than 6 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. No electricity. Or: The power failure. Or: There was no electricity. Or: Electricity was gone. 细节表达题。根据文章第二段中“We had fifteen people standing around and we still had to cook dinner. How would we do that without electricity?”可知，当时有15个人准备一起迎接新年，如何在断电的情况下准备这么多人的晚餐是一件极其困难的事。

3How did the people celebrate on New Year's Eve according to the passage?(no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. They cooked on the barbecue, sang, and counted down. Or: By cooking on the barbecue, singing, and counting down. 细节表达题。根据文章第三段中的“Why not cook on the barbecue?”“...we all sat around the dining room table and sang up until a few minutes before midnight.”和“...we all counted down and I dropped a tennis ball!”可知大家在断电的情况下,想方设法度过了一个难忘的除夕夜。

4How does the author feel about that particular New Year's Eve?(no more than 8 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. It was the most exciting one. Or:It was a night filled with laughter. Or:It was special. Or:Exciting./Pleasant./Unforgettable./Joyful./ Different./Memorable./Unique. 细节表达题。根据第四段中“we still talk about that special night”可知，在断电情况下度过的除夕夜始终令作者念念不忘。

5What do you think is the most necessary quality when dealing with an unexpected difficult situation? Please explain why.(no more than 25 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. It is courage,for it gives us strength, which enables us to deal with difficulties.

人均1500美元的“世界第一餐厅”，主厨竟被曝殴打员工？

词汇梳理背景补充
  1. 01

    Punching, Slamming, Screaming: A Chef's Past Abuse Haunts Noma, the World's Top-Rated Restaurant

    拳打、猛推、吼叫：主厨昔日的虐待行为，如阴影般笼罩着全球顶级餐厅诺玛

  2. 02

    Since 2004, Mr. Redzepi has been rewriting the rules of fine dining, preaching sustainable eating and creating jewel-like plates that earned Noma three Michelin stars and landed it five times at No. 1 on the World's 50 Best Restaurants List.

    自2004年以来，雷哲皮（Mr. Redzepi）一直在改写高端餐饮的规则，他倡导“可持续饮食”，打造出如珠宝般精致的菜品，让诺玛餐厅（Noma）拿下米其林三星的评级，还5次登上“世界50佳餐厅”排行榜首位。

  3. 03

    But in recent weeks, Noma's upcoming pop-up in Los Angeles, a $1,500-per-person dinner series that begins on March 11, has sparked a public conversation about Mr. Redzepi's past behavior.

    但最近几周，诺玛餐厅计划在洛杉矶推出的快闪店活动，引发了公众对雷哲皮过往行为的广泛讨论。该活动定于3月11日开始，以一系列晚餐形式呈现，单人收费1500美元。

    pop-up
    短期营业的快闪店。这里是餐厅在另一座城市短期开设的一系列餐饮活动。
  4. 04

    Since Mr. Redzepi was first captured on camera screaming at cooks in the 2008 documentary "Noma at Boiling Point," he has made several public apologies.

    2008年的纪录片《沸腾的诺玛餐厅》中，雷哲皮对着厨师大吼大叫的画面首次被镜头拍下，此后他已多次公开道歉。

  5. 05

    One former cook, who requested anonymity because he feared retaliation, said Mr. Redzepi had physically attacked him more times than he could remember during his time at Noma. Some 30 former employees said that being hit by Mr. Redzepi, and by the senior cooks who ran the kitchen, was routine.

    一位因担心遭到报复而要求匿名的前厨师表示，在诺玛餐厅工作期间，雷哲皮对他进行身体攻击的次数多到他都记不清了。大约30名前员工表示，被雷哲皮以及掌管厨房的资深厨师殴打，在那里是家常便饭。

    anonymity
    匿名。request anonymity 指要求报道不要公开身份。
    retaliation
    报复。这里说一位前员工担心受访后遭到报复。
    routine
    经常发生的；成了常态。前员工用它形容所称的挨打经历。
  6. 06

    Many dishes at Noma and its pop-ups include 20 or more components, and its signature style includes complex, fragile items like insects made of fruit leather and tiny plums wrapped in kelp. That workload, plus the perfectionism that Mr. Redzepi enforced from the top, they said, generated constant, frantic urgency. “It felt like we were working in an E.R., or a submarine that was going down,” said Ben, a chef in Australia, who worked at Noma in 2012. “It was hell, but I learned so much that I can’t say I regret it.”

    诺玛餐厅及其快闪店的许多菜肴，都用到至少20种食材，它的标志性风格就是打造复杂又精致易碎的菜品——比如用果泥干制作的昆虫造型料理，还有用海带包裹的迷你李子。前员工们说，如此繁重的工作量，再加上雷哲皮从上至下强压的完美主义，制造出了一种持续不断、近乎疯狂的紧迫感。“感觉就像在急诊室，或是在一艘正在下沉的潜艇里工作，”2012年曾在诺玛餐厅任职、如今在澳大利亚担任厨师的本（Ben）说，“那简直是地狱，但我学到了太多，以至于没法说自己后悔。”

    frantic
    忙乱而急迫的。这里写厨房里赶工的紧张程度。
    signature
    有代表性的、招牌式的。signature style 指客人一看就能认出的餐厅风格。
  7. 07

    Marco Cerruti, a chef in Los Angeles who worked at the Copenhagen restaurant in 2015, said that while Mr. Redzepi’s creativity remains unmatched, his status as a global leader is no longer deserved, and his legacy is diminished.

    马尔科·切鲁蒂（Marco Cerruti）是洛杉矶的一位厨师，2015年曾在位于哥本哈根的诺玛餐厅工作，切鲁蒂说，尽管雷哲皮的创造力仍然无人能及，但他作为全球餐饮界领袖的地位已名不副实，他留下的行业遗产也因此蒙尘褪色。

    diminish
    减弱；使分量变小。这里转述一位前同事的看法：主厨留在行业里的声誉已不如从前。
    legacy
    长期留下的影响或名声。一位前同事认为，主厨过往的行为影响了他的行业声誉。

背景补充

米其林星级评价什么？

米其林星评给餐厅，依据包括食材、味道、技法、菜品如何体现主厨风格，以及水准是否稳定。星级说明的是餐饮表现，无法替读者判断厨房里的劳动条件。报道把奖项和前员工的说法并列，是因为两者回答的问题不同。

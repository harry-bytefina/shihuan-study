Passage 9(2021天津第二次) 主题：终身学习

学习、阅读与教育方式

Most of us associate learning with younger people—kids in school and college or recent graduates early in their careers. But at Udemy, an open university offering online courses, Artist Anneke Camstra is engaged in the pursuit of lifelong learning and displays her potential for teaching. She loves the experience of gaining new knowledge and also loves sharing it with others. “I'm retired, but an artist never retires,” Anneke said. “The last few years on the internet have been a great joy for me. I've taken so many Udemy courses, and it's such a great feeling to get my brain working again. I find just as much satisfaction in teaching animation(动画制作) too.”

She reaches countless people with her online courses and exposes them to what they can accomplish on their own using animation software, such as GoAnimate and PowToon. Her motivation for teaching on Udemy is “to get the tools to the people.” “You can go such a long way to make things that look professional but still have your own voice,” she explained.

Most of Anneke's students start out thinking they're not creative and can't draw. Anneke understands how fear can get in the way of so many things people dream of doing. With her five-day challenge courses, she helps them get over the fear. Anneke loves the impact she has on her students. What makes her more proud of them is that they have gone from their first animation to creating their own works.

She encourages people of all ages, especially older people, to cultivate their curiosity for life. Grandparents, for example, can learn to use GoAnimate and make animations together with their grandkids.

“I'm an older woman, teaching and having fun learning again,” Anneke said, and she wants others later in their lives to catch up with her on this journey.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What does Anneke enjoy doing after her retirement? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. Gaining new knowledge and sharing it with others. Or: Taking (Udemy) courses and teaching animation. Or: Learning and teaching (online courses). 细节表达题。根据题干关键词“after her retirement”可定位到第一段中的“She loves the experience of gaining new knowledge...others. ‘I'm retired, but...I've taken so many Udemy courses, and...I find just as much satisfaction in teaching animation too.’”。由此可知她在退休后不但自己在上大学课程,同时也在教别人动画制作。

2According to Para. 2, what does Anneke expect her students to do with animation software? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. To make animations and have their own voice. Or: To make professional products and express themselves/convey their messages. Or: To accomplish/complete professional and unique works. Or: To achieve their goals/what they dream of. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词“Para. 2”以及“animation software”可定位到第二段中的“She reaches countless people...what they can accomplish on their own using animation software”及“look professional but still have your own voice”,由此可知她希望自己的学生能利用动画软件完成专业的作品并且还能有他们自己的想法。

3What is the main idea of Para. 3? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. Anneke helps her students get over/overcome the fear and create their own works/animations. Or: Anneke has a great influence on her students and finds their efforts paid off. 主旨表达题。根据第三段中的“With her five-day challenge courses, she helps them get over the fear. Anneke loves the impact she has on her students. (她通过为期五天的挑战课程帮助他们克服恐惧。Anneke喜欢自己对学生的影响)”,由此可知本段主旨。

4Please explain the meaning of the underlined word in Para. 4.(1 word)

查看答案与讲解

4. Develop./Nurture./Foster./Encourage./Train.(2 分) Arouse./Stimulate./Inspire.(1 分) 猜词表达题。首先找到画线词所在的句子“She encourages people of all ages, especially older people, to cultivate their curiosity for life.(她鼓励所有年龄段的人们,尤其是老年人,培养他们对生活的好奇心)”,根据语境可知cultivate意为“培养”。

5What do you suggest old people learn to do? Please give your reason(s). (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. To learn to use social media, because it allows them to get to know the outside world. Or: To learn to use social media, which allows them to get to know the outside world. Or: To use social media to get to know the outside world. Or: To learn painting/cooking/gardening. In that way, their brains will become active again.

98亿防洪款“付诸东流”：菲律宾为何越治越淹？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Philippines Spent Big on Flood Control, but the Water Keeps Rising

    菲律宾斥巨资防洪，但水位仍在一直上涨

  2. 02

    Cynthia Colindres remembers her low-lying village flooding every two or three years when she was growing up, a fact of life in the typhoon-plagued Philippines. Nowadays, she said, it happens two or three times a year.

    辛西娅·科林德雷斯（Cynthia Colindres）记得在自己成长过程中，她那地势低洼的村庄每两三年就会被洪水淹一次——在深受台风侵扰的菲律宾，这便是要面对的现实。但如今，她说道，洪水每年会来两三次。

  3. 03

    The perennial flooding in Bulacan and elsewhere has now become the symbol of a scandal engulfing the Philippines. Many of the billions of dollars' worth of flood-control projects like river walls and dikes approved by the authorities in recent years, it turns out, were never built, while others were not finished or proved to be severely defective.

    布拉干省及其他地区持续不断的洪水，如今已成为一桩吞噬菲律宾的丑闻的象征。近几年来，当局批准的价值数十亿美元的防洪工程，如河岸护堤、防洪堤坝等，许多根本从未动工，而其他的要么没有完工，要么被证实存在严重缺陷。

    perennial
    adj. 四季不断的
    engulf
    v. 使吞没、淹没
    dike
    n. 堤坝（dyke）

    句子结构

    Many of the billions of dollars' worth of flood-control projects like river walls and dikes / approved by the authorities in recent years, / it turns out, / were never built, / while others were not finished or proved to be severely defective.

    approved by … 表示这些项目获批，过去分词短语修饰 projects；主句谓语是 were never built。it turns out 是插入语。while 作对照，others 指其他项目：有的未完工，有的被发现存在严重缺陷。

  4. 04

    President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. acknowledged in August that a multiyear, 545 billion peso (roughly $9.8 billion) program for alleviating flooding was riddled with corruption. But the scandal has become a ballooning crisis for Mr. Marcos.

    总统费迪南德·R·小马科斯（Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr.）在8月承认，一项历时多年的、规模达5450亿比索（约合98亿美元）的治洪项目充斥着各种腐败问题。而这桩丑闻对马科斯来说，现已演变为一场日益严重的危机。

    alleviate
    v. 减轻，缓解
    peso
    n. 比索（菲律宾的货币名称）
  5. 05

    Weena Gera, an associate professor of political science at the University of the Philippines, has written that the corruption scheme had two main mechanisms: inflating budgets through congressional amendments and padded appropriations, and then rigging the bidding process. Networks tied to high-ranking officials pressured contractors for illicit payments, Ms. Gera said. Some lawmakers received up to 30 percent of a project's cost as kickbacks, she said.

    菲律宾大学政治学助理教授薇娜·婕拉（Weena Gera）写道，整个腐败计划主要有两大运作方式，一是通过国会修正案和虚增拨款来抬高预算；二是操纵招标过程。她表示，和高级官员绑定的利益关系网会向承包商施压，索取非法报酬，一些立法者从中拿到的回扣高达项目成本的30%。

    mechanism
    n. 达成目标的方式、手段；机制，运作方式
    scheme
    n. 计划，方案
    inflate
    v. 抬高，夸大
  6. 06

    In Calumpit, Ms. Colindres is still waiting for the government to do its job. "We hope there's a real plan of action, some concrete way forward," she said, "hopefully as soon as possible—that something is really done, because so many people are suffering, especially now with climate change."

    在卡隆皮特，科林德雷斯女士至今仍在等待政府履行职责。她说：“我们希望政府能有真正的行动方案，能有些具体的、往前推进的办法，越快越好——要真的落实下去，因为受苦的人太多了，尤其是在当前气候变化的背景下。”

睡眠、运动与身心健康

Six days a week,up and down the red hills of northeast Georgia,my grandfather brought the mail to the folks there.At age 68,he retired from the post office,but he never stopped serving the community.

On his 80th birthday,I sent him a letter,noting the things we all should be thankful for—good health,good friends and good outcomes.By most measurements he was a happy man.Then I suggested it was time for him to slow down.At long last,in a comfortable home,with a generous pension,he should learn to take things easy.

“Thank you for your nice words,”he wrote in his letter back,“and I know what you meant,but slowing down scares me.Life isn't having it made;it's getting it made.”

“The finest and happiest years of our lives were not when all the debts were paid,and all difficult experiences had passed,and we had settled into a comfortable home.No.I go back years ago,when we lived in a three-room house,when we got up before daylight and worked till after dark to make ends meet.I rarely had more than four hours of sleep.But what I still can't figure out is why I never got tired,never felt better in my life.I guess the answer is,we were fighting for survival,protecting and providing for those we loved.What matters are not the great moments,but the partial victories,the waiting,and even the defeats.It's the journey,not the arrival,that counts.”

The letter ended with a personal request:“Boy,on my next birthday,just tell me to wake up and get going,because I will have one less year to do things—and there are ten million things waiting to be done.”

Christina Rossetti,an English poet,once said:“Does the road wind uphill all the way?Yes,to the very end.”Today,at 96,my grandfather is still on that long road,climbing.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What was the author's grandfather before he retired?(no more than 5 words)

查看答案与讲解

1.He was a postman.　根据第一段中的he retired from the post office可知作者的爷爷在退休前在邮局工作。

2What did the author advise his grandfather to do in his letter?(no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

2.He advised him to slow down(and take things easy)./He advised he/his grandfather(should)slow down.　根据第二段中的Then I suggested it was time for him to slow down.可知作者建议爷爷该把生活节奏放慢。

3What is the grandfather's view on life according to his letter back?(no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3.The process is more important than the result. Or:A man should not slow down however old he is. Or:It's the journey,not the arrival,that counts. 第四段中,爷爷在回信中介绍了他对生活的理解和观点,最后一句为结论:It's the journey,not the arrival,that counts.这道题需要对爷爷在信中说的话进行分析和充分理解,从中提炼出他生活观的精髓,结合文章中的句子运用合理句式进行回答。

4How do you understand the underlined sentence in the last paragraph?(no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

4.My/His/The grandfather is still busy doing meaningful things. Or:The grandfather is still living an active life. Or:The grandfather is still involved in whatever he can do. 画线句子的意思是:我爷爷仍然在那条长长的路上攀登着。结合上文“爷爷退休后还继续为社区服务”可以推断出:我爷爷仍然在忙碌地做着那些有意义的事情。

5Do you agree with the grandfather's view on life?Give reasons in your own words.(no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5.Yes.One should always be full of passion in his life no matter how old he is. Or:Yes.People should make full use of their time to do something meaningful. No.It is more sensible for people to slow down and enjoy an easy life in their old age. Or:No.Slowing down contributes to better health and longer life for people in old age. 同意这种生活观点的,需从爷爷的身上和话语中提炼出答案,运用正确的句式表达出来。不同意这种观点的,需根据自己对生活的理解,运用正确的句式把自己的观点表达出来。

AI翻译得越来越好，人类译者还剩下什么优势？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    How long will humans be needed for translation?

    人类翻译还有多久会被淘汰？

  2. 02

    "Il pleut des cordes," say the French when it's raining hard. "It's raining ropes," is the direct translation. It's the kind of idiom which can trip up an AI translator. "You can imagine the dilemma this throws AI into," says François Yvon, a research director specialised in automatic translation. He says a well-trained system will know that ropes are not literally falling from the sky and use the English phrase "it's raining cats and dogs".

    大雨滂沱时，法国人会说：“Il pleut des cordes.” 直译过来就是：“正在下绳子。”这类习语很容易让 AI 翻译栽跟头。专攻机器翻译的研究主任弗朗索瓦・伊冯说：“你可以想象，这会让 AI 陷入何等困境。” 他表示，训练有素的系统会明白，天上不可能真的下绳子，所以会转而使用英语里对应的习语—— it's raining cats and dogs。

    idiom
    习语。整体意思须按惯用法理解，不能逐字照译。
  3. 03

    A comment on the weather is pretty low-risk, but more and more translation is being done by machines, and on topics with higher stakes than the weather.

    谈论天气时，翻译出错的风险很低。但如今越来越多翻译工作由机器完成，涉及的主题也远比天气重要，风险也更高。

    stakes
    事情可能造成的后果。higher stakes 指一旦译错，代价会更大。
  4. 04

    The consequences of mistranslation in the legal sphere can be serious. In the US, for example, errors in machine-translated asylum applications have led to delays in the release of detainees.

    法律领域里，误译的后果可能十分严重。比如在美国，机器翻译的庇护申请材料出现错误，导致被拘留者迟迟无法获释。

    asylum
    庇护。asylum applications 指因担心遭受迫害等原因提出的庇护申请。
    mistranslation
    误译。这里着重说错误翻译给法律程序带来的影响。
    detainee
    被拘留的人。这里说申请材料误译可能耽误他们获释。
  5. 05

    Accuracy is a matter of life and death in the medical field. "The units for dosages and blood tests often include Greek letters," says Carol Bereuter, who is a specialist in medical translations. "If you give someone a milligram when it's meant to be a microgram, you are giving them a thousand times the dose," points out Bereuter.

    而在医疗行业，翻译准确与否关乎生死。医疗翻译专家卡罗尔・贝鲁特介绍：“药物剂量、验血指标的计量单位里常会出现希腊字母。” 贝鲁特指出：“本该给病人微克剂量的药物，结果给了毫克剂量的，那么药量就是原本的一千倍。”

  6. 06

    She also makes the point that, as AI translators have got better, catching errors has got more difficult. "Whereas in the past, it would sound clunky, today it sounds perfectly legitimate. It sounds fluid. So the errors are a lot harder to spot."

    她还提到，随着 AI 翻译水平提升，排查错误变得更难了。“过去，机器译文读起来生硬别扭，现在译文通顺自然、看着合情合理，因此错误也隐蔽得多。”

    legitimate
    看起来合理可信的。这里说译文读着像样，未必准确。
    fluid
    流畅的。这里指译文读起来连贯自然，错误反而不容易被看出。
    clunky
    笨拙、不顺畅。这里说早期机器译文一读就觉得生硬。
  7. 07

    Perhaps the big moment will be when AI can show doubt. "The real breakthrough won't come until these translation systems are not merely capable of translating but also of telling us when they're not sure," Yvon says.

    或许， AI翻译真正迎来突破的时刻是它们学会表达质疑的那一天。伊冯说：“真正的突破，要等到这些翻译系统不只会翻译，还能告诉我们它们也不确定的时候，才会到来。”

Passage 2(2026天津河东质量检测一) 主题：完善自我

志愿服务、社区与包容

I could feel the excitement rising in me as I held the beautifully wrapped present in my hands. Unable to contain my curiosity any longer, I opened it quickly. It was a brand-new laptop. I had scored straight As for my O-level examinations and this was my reward. I had always envied my friends who were exchanging news about “chat rooms”, “instant messaging” and “emailing”, all of which I knew nothing about. Now I was going to find out.

It started out simply enough. I was searching for some information online when I came across a social networking site. Soon I became glued to my new-found electronic friend. I began to spend more and more time on video browsing and became enslaved by social media. I had never imagined that the day would come when I would be so focused on the glowing screen before me.

As time passed, I realized that I was hooked on the Internet. During weekends, when I had more time on my hands, I could easily spend a few hours on my laptop. It came to a point where I chose to lock myself in my room, refusing to come out and take part in family discussions. I preferred to stay connected to the people I befriended in online space although I had never met them. Soon, my sleep was reduced to only scattered(分散的) naps, and my grades at school also began getting worse.

It was about this time that my parents started voicing their disapproval. It became obvious to them that I was spending too much time on the Internet. I was asked to reduce my usage of the Internet. Yet, however hard I tried, I was unable to pull myself away, unable to terminate(终止) my chat sessions, and unable to concentrate on my lessons. I was desperate for just another click and another look at my message board. Then one day, my father took away my laptop. I complained but in vain.

Over time, I realized that Dad and Mom had always wanted the best for me. That was when I decided to take the big step—to conquer my internet addiction. Quitting was harder than I expected. But with dogged determination, I knew that I would turn over a new leaf.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What reward did the author get for scoring straight As in the O-level examinations? (no more than 8 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. A brand-new laptop. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词straight As in the O-level examinations可定位至第一段。由本段第三句话可知,作者在O-level考试中全得A的奖励是一台全新的笔记本电脑。

2Why did the author want a laptop? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. Because the author wanted to explore the online world. 细节表达题。根据第一段中的“I had always envied my friends who were exchanging news about ‘chat rooms’, ‘instant messaging’ and ‘emailing’, all of which I knew nothing about. Now I was going to find out.”可知,作者想要一台笔记本电脑是因为想探索网络世界。

3What does the underlined sentence mean in Paragraph 2? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. Soon I became completely addicted to my new laptop and could not stop using it. Or: Soon I became absorbed in/fixed on/interested in my new laptop and could not stop using it. 猜词表达题。根据后文“I began to spend more and more time on video browsing and became enslaved by social media.”可知,作者完全沉迷于新发现的电子朋友(指新笔记本电脑)之中,无法停止使用它。

4How did the author's parents show their disapproval to the author's addiction to the Internet? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. They asked for less usage and took away the laptop. Or: They asked him to reduce the usage of the Internet and took away the laptop. 细节表达题。根据第四段中的“I was asked to reduce my usage of the Internet.”以及“Then one day, my father took away my laptop.”可知,作者父母通过要求作者减少上网时间并拿走笔记本电脑来表示对其沉迷于网络的不赞同。

5Have you ever had a similar experience of being addicted to something? Please explain. (no more than 25 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. Yes, I have been addicted to games. I limited the time, got rid of the habit and improved grades finally.

AI 诈骗横行时代，每个家庭都该有一个“安全暗号”

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Criminals increasingly use generative AI to mimic real people's voices and con their loved ones out of money. There's a simple solution to this high-tech problem: a code word.

    犯罪分子正越来越多地利用生成式人工智能模仿真人声音，骗取亲友钱财。这个高科技难题有个简单的解决办法：设置一个暗号。

    mimic
    模仿。这里是模仿真人说话的声音，让接电话的人误以为是亲友。
    generative
    能生成内容的。generative AI 这里指能合成逼真人声的人工智能。
    con
    诈骗。con sb out of money 指用骗局让某人交出钱。
  2. 02

    If you receive a call from someone who sounds just like your grandson and says he needs money or a gift card, the best thing to do is hang up and call your grandson. But if the voice is so convincing that you can't bear to do that, ask for your family code word. If the caller can't produce it, hang up. Likewise, if you are legitimately in trouble and need to call a loved one for help, say the code word so your relative knows it's really you.

    如果你接到一个听起来酷似自己孙子的人打来的电话，说他需要钱或礼品卡，最好的做法是挂断电话，然后亲自给孙子回拨。不过如果对方的声音实在太逼真，你不忍心挂断，就要求对方说出家庭暗号。如果来电者说不出，立刻挂断。同样，如果你确实遇到麻烦，需要给亲友打电话求助，说出暗号，让对方知道打电话的确实是你。

    legitimately
    确实；有正当理由地。这里指求助的人确实遇到了麻烦。
    convincing
    让人信以为真的。这里说合成的声音太像本人。

    句子结构

    If you receive a call from someone / who sounds just like your grandson and says he needs money or a gift card, / the best thing to do is hang up and call your grandson.

    who 回指 someone，sounds 和 says 共用这个主语。整段「接到一个自称需要钱的电话」都在 If 从句里。主句 is 后并列 hang up 和 call；主语含有 do 时，表语可以直接用动词原形，因此 is hang up 并非缺词。

  3. 03

    People who haven't implemented a family code word have been burned by bad actors.

    那些没有设置家庭暗号的家庭，已经遭到不法分子的欺骗。

    implement
    把方案付诸使用。这里指家人约定暗号并实际用它核验身份。
  4. 04

    Here are some tips on how to create and manage a family code word:

    以下是关于如何创建和管理家庭暗号的一些建议：

  5. 05

    Keep it simple but strong. The code word (or phrase) should be easy to remember and something only family members would know. Criminals can find out a lot about you online, including on social media and publicly available databases.

    简洁但要独特。暗号（或短语）应该易于记忆，且只有家庭成员知晓。犯罪分子可以在网上，包括社交媒体和公开数据库中查到大量关于你的信息。

  6. 06

    Have a Plan B. Some families prefer to have a code question to which only a family member would know the answer. Vince Martino, a certified fraud examiner, suggests pairing questions and answers that aren't available publicly, such as, "What was the name of the motel we went to at the beach when I was growing up?"

    准备备用方案。有些家庭更倾向于设置一个只有家庭成员才知道答案的暗号问题。注册欺诈审查员文斯・马蒂诺（Vince Martino）建议选择那些无法在公开渠道查到的问题和配对答案，比如：“我小时候咱们去海边住的那家汽车旅馆叫什么名字？”

    fraud
    欺诈。fraud examiner 查的是通过欺骗获取钱财或利益的行为。
    certified
    持有资格认证的。certified fraud examiner 指有资格认证的反舞弊调查人员。

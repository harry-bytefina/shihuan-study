Passage 14(2025天津十二区重点学校联考一) 主题：人生态度

动物行为与动物认知

Not long ago, my 12-year-old son, Lucian, and I were standing on the knife's edge. I mean this quite literally. We had just completed a four-day hike in Maine that concluded by climbing Mount Katahdin. At its highest peak, Mount Katahdin has an extremely dramatic, mile-long ridge(山脊)known as the Knife Edge. It's scary and quite dangerous: The mountain has claimed at least 17 lives. We knew we had to be careful, but surprisingly, the danger was part of the attraction. Lucian and I were both longing for a bit of risk.

We proceeded along the ridge and Lucian moved with such agility(灵敏) and balance. Then suddenly, right in the center of the Knife Edge, we encountered a group of bushes. And that's when a killer bee stung(蜇伤) Lucian. Lucian was pale-faced, and he told me very calmly, “Dad, this is serious. I could die.” If this were a novel, the perfectly timed disaster, and my son's calm yet serious remark would be cut by some clever editor. But this was not a novel. It was real life.

“What's your favorite video game?” I asked my son. He shot me a confused look. I pressed him, asking him again. Unwillingly, he began answering my question—or more accurately, he began asking me why I talked about video games at a moment like this. By the time he finished, a minute had passed. He showed no signs of having an allergic reaction. I pointed this out to him. Another minute passed. And then, unexpectedly, we realized, he was totally fine.

When we returned home, Lucian and I both recounted our adventure—to my wife, his brother, and all the grandparents—until it quickly became a family tale. And it occurred to me that on some level, Lucian and I are both fond of exploring courageously. And this is how we found ourselves at the top of Mount Katahdin, crossing over the Knife Edge.

1What did the author and his son do in Maine? (no more than 5 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. They took/completed a four-day hike. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词the author and his son do in Maine可定位在第一段。由第一段可知,他们刚刚在缅因州完成了一次为期四天的徒步旅行,最后以攀登卡塔丁山结束。

2What does the underlined part in Paragraph 1 probably mean? (no more than 12 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. At least 17 people lost their lives in the mountain. 猜词表达题。画线处所在句及前句意为“在其最高峰,卡塔丁山有一处极其壮观的、一英里长的山脊,被称为‘刀锋’。它很可怕,也相当危险: 　　　”。由此推断,画线处意为这座山至少夺去了17条生命。

3What happened to Lucian in the center of the Knife Edge? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. He was attacked by a killer bee. Or: A killer bee stung Lucian/him. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词in the center of the Knife Edge可定位在第二段。由第二段可知,突然就在“刀峰”的正中央,他们遇到了一丛灌木。就在那时,一只非洲杀人蜂蜇伤了Lucian。

4Why did the author ask Lucian about his favorite video game? (no more than 10 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. To distract Lucian. Or: To shift Lucian's attention from the incident. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词ask Lucian about his favorite video game可定位在第三段。由第三段可知,父亲通过谈论游戏话题转移被非洲杀人蜂蜇伤的儿子的注意力。

5Do you think it's good to seek adventures? Why or why not? (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. Yes. Because it gives us the chance to explore a new world and leaves us exciting and unusual experiences.

54年来首次，日本进入无熊猫时代

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Japanese Panda Fans Bid Farewell to Furry Ambassadors

    日本熊猫迷依依惜别毛茸茸的“外交使者”

  2. 02

    Lucky lottery winners got one minute to bid goodbye to Xiao Xiao and Lei Lei, twin pandas set to leave Japan on Tuesday amid mounting tensions with China. Getting in to the Ueno Zoo in Tokyo for a last look at the beloved Chinese diplomats was not easy, but visitors came in droves.

    幸运抽中签的游客有一分钟的时间向“晓晓”和“蕾蕾”告别，在中日关系日益紧张的背景下，这对双胞胎大熊猫将按计划于周二离开日本。想要进入东京上野动物园（Ueno Zoo），最后再看一眼这对备受喜爱的“中国外交官”并不容易，但游客们仍然蜂拥而至。

    lottery
    n. 抽奖，抽签
    diplomat
    n. （统称）外交官
  3. 03

    Xiao Xiao and Lei Lei were born at the Ueno Zoo in 2021, and remained on loan there as part of China’s longstanding global panda diplomacy program.

    “晓晓”和“蕾蕾”于2021年出生在上野动物园，它们作为中国由来已久的熊猫外交项目的一部分，一直以租借形式留在日本。

  4. 04

    Though their departure had been planned previously, negotiations over potential replacements stalled after comments in November by Japan’s prime minister, Sanae Takaichi.

    尽管它们的回国之旅早有安排，但去年11月，日本首相高市早苗（Sanae Takaichi）发表了一些言论，之后关于潜在接替方案的谈判陷入停滞。

    stall
    v. 停滞，搁置
    replacement
    n. 接替，接班
  5. 05

    Panda diplomacy has a long history in China, dating back more than 1,300 years. Historical records show that the Empress Wu Zetian of the Tang Dynasty sent a pair of pandas in cages gilded with red flowers to the Emperor Tenmu in Japan around 660 A.D. Centuries later, in 1972, pandas, an endangered species, again played a role after China and Japan normalized ties following a 35-year rupture.

    熊猫外交在中国有着久远的历史，可以追溯到1300多年前。史料记载，约在公元660年，唐代女皇武则天曾将一对熊猫装在镶有红花的镀金笼中赠予日本天武天皇（Emperor Tenmu）。几个世纪后，1972年，在中日结束长达35年的断交期并恢复正常邦交后，作为濒危物种的熊猫再度了发挥外交作用。

    rupture
    n. 断裂，破裂
    gild
    v. 给……镀金，染上金色
    empress
    n. 女皇，皇后
  6. 06

    The departure of Xiao Xiao and Lei Lei on Tuesday will mark the first time that Japan has been without pandas in more than half a century, though there is hope for those devastated by the looming absence. Pauses in panda diplomacy have happened before. In 2024, Washington welcomed two pandas to the National Zoo after nearly a year without one.

    随着“晓晓”和“蕾蕾”于周二离开，日本将迎来半个多世纪以来首次境内没有大熊猫的局面。不过，对于因这一空缺而感到伤心的人们来说，仍然存在希望——熊猫外交的暂停期并非没有先例。2024年，在经历近一年没有大熊猫的空窗期后，美国华盛顿国家动物园迎来了两只大熊猫。

    looming
    adj.（不愉快事物）逼近的，即将来临的

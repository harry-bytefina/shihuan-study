Passage 1(2026天津河西质量调查一) 主题：积极的生活态度

学习、阅读与教育方式

We found out that Jenny was hearing-impaired(有听觉缺陷的) when she was four and a half years old. Several operations and speech classes later, when she was seven, we were told that Jenny had Juvenile Rheumatoid Arthritis(幼年型类风湿性关节炎). She could not put pressure on the heels of her feet, so she walked on tiptoe.

All through grade school, and on into high school, Jenny suffered, yet never complained. She took her medicine, and I would often wrap her feet in towels and hold her until her pain disappeared. But, as soon as she could bear the pain, Jenny immediately carried on, as though she were free of pain.

She was very popular and funny, attending every football game and cheering the team on, carrying her pillow everywhere she went, so that she could reduce the pain. Then came her senior year. She would be considered for a scholarship; however, school activities, especially sports, could often mean the difference between receiving an award and losing out.

So Jenny came to a decision, and in her strange manner, she began to bombard the high school football coach with requests. She promised. She got her best friend to sign up with her. Finally, the coach gave in, with the warning, “If you miss one game, you are out!”

She carried big buckets of water to her teammates. She bandaged knees and ankles before every game. Often, Jenny could be seen carrying a bucket of water in each hand, nearly dragging them, along with her pillow pressed under her arms.

When asked why he thought that the team was winning all their games, even in the face of injury, one member of Jenny's team explained, “Well, when you've been knocked down and you can't seem to move, you look up and see Jenny Lewis, limping(跛行) across the field, dragging her buckets and carrying her pillow. It makes anything that the rest of us may suffer seem pretty insignificant.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1When Jenny was seven, why did she walk on tiptoe? (no more than 11 words)

查看答案与讲解

1. Because she could not put pressure on her heels. Or: Because her heels couldn't take pressure. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词walk on tiptoe可定位至第一段。由本段最后一句可知,Jenny七岁时踮着脚尖走路是因为她不能给脚后跟施加压力。

2Why did Jenny decide to take part in school activities? (no more than 20 words)

查看答案与讲解

2. Because activities could affect scholarship chances. Or: Because school activities could often mean the difference between receiving an award and losing out. 细节表达题。根据题干关键词school activities可定位至第三段。由本段最后一句可知,Jenny决定参加学校活动是因为活动会影响她获得奖学金的机会。

3What does the underlined sentence in the fourth paragraph mean? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

3. She kept asking the coach repeatedly to let her join the team. 猜词表达题。根据画线句中的with requests和其后的“Finally, the coach gave in”可推知,Jenny不断地请求教练让她加入球队,最终才获得了机会。

4What made Jenny's team encouraged in the face of difficulties? (no more than 15 words)

查看答案与讲解

4. Jenny's effort and determination despite her pain. 细节表达题。根据第六段中的“when you've been knocked down...you look up and see Jenny Lewis, limping...carrying her pillow. It makes anything that the rest of us may suffer seem pretty insignificant.”可知,尽管Jenny身体疼痛,但她仍然努力为球队做出贡献,这种努力和决心让她的队友在面对困难时受到鼓舞。

5What have you learned from Jenny? (no more than 25 words)

查看答案与讲解

5. We should never give up despite suffering. Or: Courage and perseverance can inspire others and overcome difficulties.

外刊延伸

阅读难度参考：CSE 6

“奥德赛时期”全网刷屏，人生是一场漫长的远航

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    There used to be four common life phases: childhood, adolescence, adulthood and old age. Now, there are at least six: childhood, adolescence, odyssey, adulthood, active retirement and old age. Of the new ones, the least understood is odyssey, the decade of wandering that frequently occurs between adolescence and adulthood.

    过去，人们常把人生划分为四个阶段：童年、青春期、成年与老年。如今，人生阶段至少有六个：童年、青春期、奥德赛期、成年期、活力退休期与老年期。在这些新增阶段中，最不为人所理解的便是“奥德赛”——在青春期与成年期之间，往往出现一段长达十年的漂泊岁月。

    odyssey
    n. 充满考验、却始终有坚守的成长之旅
  2. 02

    During this decade, 20-somethings go to school and take breaks from school. They live with friends and they live at home. They fall in and out of love. They try one career and then try another.

    在这十年里，二十多岁的年轻人求学深造，也休学休整。他们与朋友合租，也会搬回父母家居住。他们坠入爱河，又黯然分手。他们尝试一份职业，而后又转向另一份。

  3. 03

    Their parents grow increasingly anxious. These parents understand that there's bound to be a transition phase between student life and adult life. But when they look at their own grown children, they see the transition stretching five years, seven and beyond. The parents don't even detect a clear sense of direction in their children's lives. They look at them and see the things that are being delayed. They see that people in this age bracket are delaying marriage. They're delaying having children. They're delaying permanent employment.

    他们的父母愈发焦虑。这些父母明白，从学生阶段步入成人生活，本就必然存在一段过渡期。可看着已然成年的子女，他们发现过渡期竟拉长至五年、七年，甚至更久。父母们甚至看不出孩子的人生有清晰的方向。在他们眼中，这个年龄段的人处处推迟人生大事：推迟结婚，推迟生育，迟迟不谋求稳定的正式工作。

    detect
    v. 察觉；发现；探测
  4. 04

    Yet with a little imagination it's possible even for baby boomers to understand what it's like to be in the middle of the odyssey years. It's possible to see that this period of improvisation is a sensible response to modern conditions.

    不过，即便对婴儿潮一代的父母而言，只要稍加想象，也能体会身处奥德赛岁月是种什么感受。人们也能意识到，这段随性探索的时期，其实是对当下社会现状的理性回应。

    improvisation
    n. 随性探索
  5. 05

    Young people grow up in tightly structured childhoods, but then graduate into a world characterized by uncertainty, diversity, searching and tinkering. Old success recipes don't apply, new norms have not been established and everything seems to give way to a less permanent version of itself. Dating gives way to Facebook and hooking up. Marriage gives way to cohabitation. The job market is fluid.

    年轻人在高度规序井然的童年中长大，毕业后却踏入一个充满不确定性、多元性，需要不断探寻与尝试的世界。旧有的成功法则不再适用，新的社会规范又尚未确立，一切似乎都朝着更不稳定的形态转变。传统约会被脸书社交与随性的亲密关系取代，婚姻让位于同居，就业市场也始终变幻不定。

    fluid
    adj. 流动的、不稳定的；n. 液体
    tinker
    v. 小幅尝试、轻微调整
  6. 06

    The odyssey years are not about slacking off. There are intense competitive pressures as a result of the vast numbers of people chasing relatively few opportunities. Rather, what we're seeing is the creation of a new life phase, just as adolescence came into being a century ago.

    奥德赛岁月并不是懈怠躺平。由于大量人群争抢有限的机会，激烈的竞争压力始终存在。确切来说，我们见证的是一个全新人生阶段的诞生——正如一个世纪前，“青春期” 这一概念应运而生。

  7. 07

    And as the new generational structure solidifies, social and economic entrepreneurs will create new rites and institutions. Someday people will look back and wonder at the vast social changes wrought by the emerging social group that saw their situations first captured by“Friends”and later by “Knocked Up.”

    随着这一新的代际结构逐步稳固，社会与经济领域的开拓者会缔造出新的惯例与制度。终有一天，人们回望过往，会惊叹于这个新兴社会群体带来的巨大社会变革 —— 他们的生活状态，最早被《老友记》所捕捉，后来又在《一夜大肚》里得以呈现。

    entrepreneur
    n. 企业家；创造新事物、推动变化的人
    capture
    v. 表达、描述
    rite
    n. 仪式；惯例，行为规范

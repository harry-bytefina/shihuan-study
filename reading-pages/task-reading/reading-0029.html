01-2024天津夏

学习、阅读与教育方式

Studies show teaching children how to cook at an early age helps with reading comprehension and fine motor ability, in addition to learning about nutrition and food safety. In 2015 Stephanie Drewry was looking for a cooking summer camp for her three children. To her dismay, she quickly realized such a camp didn't exist.

“I have my degree in education, but I had been staying home with my kids,” Drewry says. “I love working with kids, so I just decided to take a spare bedroom and turn it into a one-room cooking school called Sprouts Cooking School.”

The school grew in popularity so quickly that Drewry realized she would need more space outside her home for the classes. In 2017 she moved Sprouts into a 1,400-square-foot storefront (临街店面) in Carmel, Indiana. As demand grew, Drewry opened another Sprouts in the same area in 2022.

“Our classrooms are built with kids in mind. The worktops (操作台) in the classrooms are slightly lower than normal to fit in with their height,” Drewry says. “All of the cooking facilities are domestic ones. I wanted to have them feel like cooking in their own home.”

The school offers activities targeted at kids aged 3 to 13. Younger kids might make cookies while older ones are cooking soup and meatballs. There are birthday parties and summer camps as well. The fun part of the activities is that the kids can eat or take home whatever they make. They can also enjoy themselves in the themed classes like Harry Potter or Winter Wonderland Baking. With all these activities, the school is extremely popular.

Teaching kids, especially those picky eaters, to cook helps a lot because they're more willing to eat something made by themselves. More importantly, it is also about releasing children into the world with a life skill they'll need as an adult when they're no longer in their parents' care.

“茶杯猪”真的长不大吗？200斤只是起步体重

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Teacup. Pixie. Itty-bitty. Micro. Pocket-size.

    茶杯猪。精灵猪。迷你猪。微型猪。口袋猪。

  2. 02

    When Jessica Moul went scrolling through Craigslist for a new family pet, she knew she wanted an itty-bitty pig.

    当杰西卡·莫尔（Jessica Moul）在Craigslist上浏览寻找新宠物时，她知道自己想要一只迷你猪。

  3. 03

    Beguiled by photos of teensy piglets, she chose the runt of the litter—a tiny, shivering nugget for $350. The seller promised the "teacup" pig would top out at 40 pounds and sent her on her way.

    杰西卡被那些小猪的照片所吸引，她选中了这一窝里最弱小的一只——一只瑟瑟发抖的小不点，花了350美元买下。卖家承诺这只“茶杯猪”长大后体重不会超过40磅，之后便让她带走了。

    beguile
    被吸引得一时失去判断。beguiled by photos 指被小猪的照片打动，轻信了卖家的说法。
    runt
    同窝里最小或最瘦弱的一只。文中的买家特意挑了这样一只。
    litter
    同一胎出生的一窝幼崽。the runt of the litter 是一窝里最小的一只。
  4. 04

    But when Moul introduced her new companion to an online pig-owner group, she got a quick education.

    但当莫尔在一个线上养猪社群里介绍她的新伙伴时，她立刻被上了一课。

  5. 05

    "Everybody's laughing at me," she recalled. "They're like, 'There's no such thing, lady.'"

    “大家都在笑话我。他们的意思是说：‘女士，没有这种东西的。’”她回忆道。

  6. 06

    Today, that piglet is Tucker—a 200-pound porker who has outgrown the family bathroom, shredded a couch and required the installation of heavy-duty horse panels to keep him from escaping the yard of his Arizona home.

    如今，那只小猪已经变身成了“塔克”（Tucker）——一只200磅的猪。它已经挤不进家里的浴室了，还把沙发撕得粉碎，为了防止它从亚利桑那州家中的院子里逃出去，家里不得不安装加强型马用围栏片。

    shred
    扯碎。shredded a couch 说的是猪把沙发扯得破破烂烂。
    outgrown
    长大到原来容纳不下。这里说猪长大后，家里的卫生间都容不下它了。
  7. 07

    Plenty of people have fallen for the promise of a forever-small pig. Around the pandemic, when Americans were homebound and scrolling for companionship, interest in unconventional pets picked up. Pigs can keep growing for up to five years, and some of those purchases are only now settling into their full size.

    许多人都曾被“小猪永远长不大”的承诺所迷惑。在疫情期间，当美国人居家隔离、上网浏览寻找陪伴时，人们对非传统宠物的兴趣日益浓厚。猪的生长周期可长达五年，因此人们在疫情时购买的小猪，一些直到现在才长到成年的体型。

    homebound
    长时间待在家里的。这里说疫情期间许多人居家，想找宠物陪伴。
  8. 08

    There is no regulatory definition of a "teacup" or "micro" pig. In breeder parlance, miniature can apply to any pig under 300 pounds. It's a technical distinction that separates them from 1,000 pound commercial hogs but offers little comfort to a suburban homeowner expecting a permanent lap pet.

    “茶杯猪”或“微型猪”目前尚无官方定义。在养殖者的行话中，“微型”一词通常指体重不足300磅的猪。这种技术性的区分，旨在将“微型猪”与体重达1000磅的商业肉猪区分开来。但对于那些本以为能养一只永远可以抱在膝上的宠物猪的郊区房主来说，这几乎没有任何安慰作用。

    parlance
    某一行业的说法。in breeder parlance 指按养殖者的行话。
    regulatory
    与监管规定有关的。no regulatory definition 指没有法规规定“茶杯猪”该有多小。
  9. 09

    "When people say, 'Oh, it's a teacup pig,' I'm like, 'That is not a thing. That does not exist,'" said Lynsey Rosen, a veterinarian in Southern California who treats dozens of mini pigs each month. Many of her clients are surprised by how much their pets fill out.

    “当人们说‘哦，这是一只茶杯猪’时，我会说：‘根本没有这种东西，那是不存在的。’”南加州的兽医林赛·罗森（Lynsey Rosen）说道。她每个月要为数十只迷你猪看病。许多客户都为自己的宠物能长到如此之大感到惊讶。

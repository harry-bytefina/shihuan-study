02-2023天津夏，B

学习、阅读与教育方式

I looked through the window of the charming little violin shop, and my heart began to race.

I'd been out to dinner that evening. Since it wasn't dark yet after the meal, I decided to walk home from the restaurant. I had traveled that way before, yet I had never noticed that old little shop. But that night I felt drawn to the violin shop the moment I came across it.

I wiped the dirt from the window to get a better look inside. Several violins hung from the dark walls, quietly waiting to be chosen. As my eyes rested on them, I felt as though I were looking through a window into my own past.

My childhood was all about the pursuits I had attempted, most of which had been chosen by Mom. She was like, “Join the swim team, Tara. Your sister is a good swimmer;surely you will be, too.” What she refused to acknowledge, however, was that I was visibly afraid of water.

Every Saturday I begged Mom not to make me go to the swim meet, but had little chance of success. That said, with a bang of the starting gun, I would dive into the cold water with all my strength and swim to the other side of the pool as fast as I could, only to find that the other swimmers slid past me. I would have given it up if I had not heard my father's encouraging shouts to cheer me on. When at last my hand would grab the edge of the pool, he would always be there with a warm, dry towel, telling me how proud he was of my desperate efforts.

Then came a turning point in my life the day our school orchestra(管弦乐队) visited my class, and gave a demonstration. The drums annoyed me. The flutes(笛子) bored me. But the violin…ah, the violin. It made the sweetest sound I'd ever heard!My heart was dancing along with its flowing tune. For the first time in my life, I went so wild with joy.

Tightly holding the permission slip from the orchestra director, I ran all the way home after school, and shakily handed it to my parents with a fear that they might dismiss my desire. They didn't. Mom was thrilled to see me finally excited about something, and Dad winked(眨眼示意) at me while eagerly signing the slip.

I began practicing the violin with great passion, and rose quickly in ability. Before long I had won the first seat in the community orchestra...

亚马逊万人大裁员：AI失业潮的冰山已现？

By Lindsay Ellis, Owen Tucker-Smith and Allison Pohle

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Tens of Thousands of White-Collar Jobs Are Disappearing as AI Starts to Bite

    数以万计的白领岗位正在消失，AI的冲击开始显现

    bite
    v. 产生不良影响
  2. 02

    The nation's largest employers have a new message for office workers: help not wanted.

    美国规模最大的一批雇主向办公室职员们传递了一个新信号：我们不再需要人手了。

  3. 03

    Amazon.com said this week that it would cut 14,000 corporate jobs, with plans to eliminate as much as 10% of its white-collar workforce eventually. United Parcel Service said Tuesday that it had reduced its management workforce by about 14,000 positions over the past 22 months, days after the retailer Target said it would cut 1,800 corporate roles.

    亚马逊（Amazon.com）本周表示将裁减14000个公司职位，并计划最终裁掉多达10%的白领员工。美国联合包裹运送服务公司（United Parcel Service）周二表示，在过去22个月里，公司已削减了14000个管理岗位，而就在几天前，零售企业塔吉特（Target）也表示将减少1800个公司职位。

    eliminate
    v. 把一些岗位永久性剔除
    cut
    v. 企业瘦身、裁员
  4. 04

    Behind the wave of white-collar layoffs, in part, is the embrace by companies of artificial intelligence, which executives hope can handle more of the work that well-compensated white-collar workers have been doing.

    这一波白领裁员潮的背后，部分原因是企业对人工智能（AI）的采用。高管们希望AI能处理更多过去由高薪白领员工从事的工作。

    more of
    (在某一整体范畴中）更多（xx类型/属性的……）

    句子结构

    Behind the wave of white-collar layoffs, in part, / is the embrace by companies of artificial intelligence, / which executives hope can handle more of the work / that well-compensated white-collar workers have been doing.

    开头的地点性介词短语提前，is 后的 the embrace 才是主语。还原为 The embrace … is behind …。which 回指 artificial intelligence，在 hope 后的从句里作 can handle 的主语；末尾 that 则回指 work，在 have been doing 后补宾语。

  5. 05

    Investors have pushed the C-suite to work more efficiently with fewer employees. Factors driving slower hiring include political uncertainty and higher costs.

    投资者们也在向企业管理层施压，要求他们用更少的员工实现更高的工作效率。此外，政治不确定性和成本上升等因素，也导致企业招聘步伐放缓。

  6. 06

    Meanwhile, opportunities for front-line, blue-collar or specialized workers are growing. Companies describe shortages of trade, healthcare, hospitality and construction employees, while pausing hiring for consultants and managers, laying off staff in retail and finance, and deploying AI to do work in accounting and fraud monitoring.

    与此同时，一线员工、蓝领工人及专业技术工人的岗位机会正在增加。企业表示，技术工种、医疗健康、酒店业和建筑业存在人手短缺，同时暂停招聘顾问和经理，裁减零售和金融业员工，并部署AI从事会计和欺诈监控等工作。

    deploy
    v. 部署，投产使用
    blue-collar
    adj. 蓝领的（强调用体力和操作技能换取劳动报酬）
    trade
    n. 技术工种，手艺
  7. 07

    Now what was once a stable position feels like a ticking time bomb, with employees who worked their way up the corporate ladder awaiting their turn for a video call announcing their last day.

    如今，曾经稳定的岗位仿佛变成了一颗定时炸弹，那些在公司里步步晋升的员工，都在提心吊胆地等着也许哪天就轮到自己，接到通知裁员的视频电话。

  8. 08

    Even as the economy grows, hiring has weakened, with economists expecting slower job creation this fall. In such an environment, companies are becoming choosier—and both experienced white-collar workers and new college graduates seeking their first jobs are getting squeezed.

    即使经济仍在增长，招聘力度也在减弱，经济学家预计，今年秋季的就业增长将进一步放缓。在这样的环境下，企业招聘正变得更加挑剔——无论是经验丰富的白领，还是寻求第一份工作的应届大学毕业生，都感受到了巨大压力。

    squeeze
    v. 因机会被压缩，身处压力之下喘不过气

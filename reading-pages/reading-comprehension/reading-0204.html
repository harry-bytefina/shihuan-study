全部题目与逐题讲解

1Which of the tours is available in March?

  • A.San Francisco Winery Tour.
  • B.Back to the Fifties Tour.
  • C.Spooky Halloween Tour.
  • D.Holiday Lights Tour.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1.A　细节理解题。根据短文中对这四项活动的运行时间的介绍可知,第一项活动的时间是 February 1st through April 30th,即从2月1日到4月30日,所以答案为A项。

2What can tourists do on Back to the Fifties Tour?

  • A.Go to Treasure Island.
  • B.Enjoy the holiday scenes.
  • C.Have free ice cream.
  • D.Visit the Presidio district.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　细节理解题。根据第二项活动中的 “as we take you through San Francisco for a free taste of ice cream”可知,参加这项游玩活动你可以品尝到免费的冰激凌,所以答案为C项。

3What are tourists required to do to go on Holiday Lights Tour?

  • A.Take some drinks.
  • B.Set off early in the morning.
  • C.Wear warm clothes.
  • D.Make reservations in advance.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　细节理解题。根据最后一项活动中的最后一句话 “Advance reservations required.”可知,参加这项游玩活动需要提前预订,所以答案为D项。

纽约为什么选出了一个社会主义穆斯林做市长？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    How Zohran Mamdani Beat Back New York’s Elite and Was Elected Mayor

    佐赫兰·马姆达尼如何击退纽约精英，成功当选纽约市长

  2. 02

    Zohran Mamdani was still asleep early in the morning after June’s Democratic primary when the phone calls started flooding in.

    6月民主党初选结束后的次日清晨，佐赫兰·马姆达尼仍在睡梦中，电话便已接踵而至。

  3. 03

    A young democratic socialist, Mr. Mamdani had just toppled former Gov. Andrew M. Cuomo, upending New York’s power structure in an upset so stunning and so swift that even he had not fully seen it coming.

    这位年轻的民主社会主义者马姆达尼，刚刚击败前州长安德鲁·M·库默，以一场爆冷胜利颠覆了纽约的权力格局，结果之震惊、速度之迅猛，连他自己都没有完全预料到。

    topple
    v. 颠覆
    upend
    v. 使颠倒
  4. 04

    Mr. Mamdani’s political rise may be remembered for what came first: the buoyant, flamboyant, rule-breaking primary run that united a new coalition of Brooklyn gentrifiers and Queens cabbies around the city’s growing affordability crisis and the birth of a megawatt talent.

    人们或许还记得马姆达尼政治崛起的开端：那场充满活力、高调吸睛且突破常规的初选竞选。这场初选围绕城市日益严峻的生活成本危机，联合起了布鲁克林的新中产与皇后区的出租车司机这一新联盟，这也让一颗耀眼的政治新星就此诞生。

    coalition
    n. 原本不同党派或群体的组成的联盟，联盟
    buoyant
    adj. 欢欣鼓舞，情绪高涨的
    gentrifier
    n. 新搬来的中产
  5. 05

    But his election on Tuesday as the 111th mayor of New York owes as much to the equally improbable backroom campaign that followed. In Midtown C-suites and intimate phone calls, a left-wing populist who had built his brand on taxing the rich wooed, charmed and delicately disarmed some of the most powerful people in America.

    但他在周二（当地时间2025年11月4日）当选纽约第 111 任市长，同样离不开初选后那场胜算渺茫的幕后游说。在曼哈顿中城的高管办公室里，在私密的电话沟通中，这位以 “向富人征税” 为核心理念的左翼民粹主义者，成功争取、打动并巧妙化解了美国部分最有权势人物的疑虑。

    disarm
    v. 让某人卸下心防，征服某人
    charm
    v. 施展魅力
  6. 06

    The arc of his success is nothing short of staggering. At the start of the year, Mr. Mamdani was polling at 1 percent, tied, as he likes to say, with the candidate known as “someone else.” Few New Yorkers recognized his name, and his own political team put the odds of winning as low as 3 percent.

    马姆达尼的成功轨迹堪称惊人。年初时，马姆达尼的民调支持率仅为1%—— 正如他常自嘲的那样，与被称作 “其他人” 的候选人并列。当时鲜有纽约人知晓他的名字，就连他自己的竞选团队预估胜率都低至3%。

    arc
    n. 发展轨迹
  7. 07

    Mr. Mamdani foregrounded the city’s affordability crisis when rivals focused elsewhere, lapped them with viral social media videos and benefited from Democrats’ hunger for generational change.

    当竞选对手聚焦于其他议题时，马姆达尼始终将城市的生活成本危机置于核心，他凭借病毒式传播的社交媒体视频遥遥领先于对手，同时这也得益于民主党内部对代际更替的渴望。

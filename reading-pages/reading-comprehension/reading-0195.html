阅读理解

12-2018课标全国Ⅲ，D

学习、阅读与教育方式

Adults understand what it feels like to be flooded with objects. Why do we often assume that more is more when it comes to kids and their belongings?The good news is that I can help my own kids learn earlier than I did how to live more with less.

I found the pre⁃holidays a good time to encourage young children to donate less⁃used things, and it worked. Because of our efforts, our daughter Georgia did decide to donate a large bag of toys to a little girl whose mother was unable to pay for her holiday due to illness. She chose to sell a few larger objects that were less often used when we promised to put the money into her school fund(基金)(our kindergarten daughter is serious about becoming a doctor).

For weeks, I've been thinking of bigger, deeper questions: How do we make it a habit for them?And how do we train ourselves to help them live with, need, and use less?Yesterday, I sat with my son, Shepherd, determined to test my own theory on this. I decided to play with him with only one toy for as long as it would keep his interest. I expected that one toy would keep his attention for about five minutes, ten minutes, max. I chose a red rubber ball—simple, universally available. We passed it, he tried to put it in his mouth, he tried bouncing it, rolling it, sitting on it, throwing it. It was totally, completely enough for him. Before I knew it an hour had passed and it was time to move on to lunch.

We both became absorbed in the simplicity of playing together. He had my full attention and I had his. My little experiment to find joy in a single object worked for both of us.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What do the words “more is more” in paragraph 1 probably mean?

  • A.The more, the better.
  • B.Enough is enough.
  • C.More money, more worries.
  • D.Earn more and spend more.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1.A　猜测词义题。根据第一段最后一句话可知,作者帮自己的孩子学会了用更少的东西生活得更好,由此推断出画线词语的意思是“越多越好”,所以答案为A项。

2What made Georgia agree to sell some of her objects?

  • A.Saving up for her holiday.
  • B.Raising money for a poor girl.
  • C.Adding the money to her fund.
  • D.Giving the money to a sick mother.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　细节理解题。根据第二段最后一句话可知,在被承诺把这笔钱放进她的学校基金后,作者的女儿选择卖掉几件较大而不太常用的东西,因为她想把这笔钱放到自己的基金中去,所以答案为C项。

3Why did the author play the ball with Shepherd?

  • A.To try out an idea.
  • B.To show a parent's love.
  • C.To train his attention.
  • D.To help him start a hobby.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

3.A　推理判断题。根据第三段的Yesterday, I sat with my son, Shepherd, determined to test my own theory on this.可以推断出作者和儿子一起玩球是为了检验一下自己的理论。

4What can be a suitable title for the text?

  • A.Take It or Leave It
  • B.A Lesson from Kids
  • C.Live More with Less
  • D.The Pleasure of Giving
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

4.C　主旨要义题。作者在第一段的最后就提出了本文的主题:how to live more with less,后面几段描写了作者在自己的孩子身上证明了自己的这个观点是正确的,所以答案为C项。

外刊延伸

阅读难度参考：CSE 9

特朗普根本不知道如何结束伊朗战争

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Now, we're more than a week into the war with Iran launched by President Trump and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of Israel, and the biggest question I have is this: What if the necessary is impossible? What if the transformation of Iran is so much more important than the war’s critics admit, but so much more difficult than the war’s designers understand?

    如今，美国总统特朗普与以色列总理内塔尼亚胡发动的这场对伊战争已经进行了一个多星期，而我心中最大的疑问是：如果所谓的“必要之事”根本无法实现呢？如果说，改造伊朗这件事比战争批评者所承认的重要得多，但又远比战争策划者所理解的困难得多，那又该怎么办？

  2. 02

    What if the Islamic regime in Tehran is also so embedded that, despite its unpopularity with a majority of Iranians, it can’t be removed without plunging the entire Iranian landmass, about a sixth the size of the United States and home to 90 million people, into chaos? What if the only quick alternative to Iran’s Islamic autocracy is not democracy but disorder on an epic scale?

    尽管大多数伊朗人并不喜欢德黑兰的伊斯兰政权，但它是如此根深蒂固，要是不让整个伊朗国土陷入巨大混乱，就无法将其推翻该怎么办呢？它的国土面积约为美国的六分之一，人口可是达到了九千万。要是取代伊朗伊斯兰独裁政权的唯一快速方案不是民主，而是史诗级规模的动荡，又该如何？

    regime
    政权；指作者讨论的伊朗现有统治体系，不是泛指国家或国民。
    plunging
    使骤然陷入；plunge a country into chaos 指使整个国家突然陷入混乱。
    landmass
    大片陆地；Iranian landmass 在此指伊朗国土，后文用面积作比较。
    autocracy
    专制统治；与 democracy 对举，指权力高度集中的政治体制。
    embedded
    根深蒂固的；说的是这个统治体系在当地难以轻易被移除。
  3. 03

    Trump has been all over the map when talking about the morning after in Iran — and saying truly ridiculous and often contradictory things that reveal a commander in chief who is just making it up as he goes along.

    在谈到伊朗的“战后第二天”会发生什么时，特朗普的说法一直前后不一、摇摆不定——他说过许多属实荒谬且经常自相矛盾的话，这些话让人看到一个“边走边编”的三军统帅。

    contradictory
    彼此矛盾的；指作者认为关于战后局势的不同说法无法同时成立。
  4. 04

    That said, Trump and Netanyahu appear to have significantly knocked back Iran’s nuclear capabilities and its ability to project power through its navy, air force and missiles. The wise thing to do now is pause and see how this plays out on what I call "the morning after the morning after." My guess is that this regime will break only from the top, which will be a process that will start only after there is a cease-fire.

    不过话说回来，特朗普与内塔尼亚胡似乎确实大幅削弱了伊朗的核能力，以及它通过海军、空军和导弹系统进行军力投射的能力。现在明智的做法是先停一停，看看局势在我所谓的“战争次日的次日”会如何发展。我的猜测是，这个政权最终只会从内部顶层开始破裂，而这样的过程只有在停火之后才会开始。

  5. 05

    The best that the Trump-Netanyahu bombs-away strategy can do is start that process. The worst the strategy can do is so devastate Iran with endless aerial bombardments that it becomes ungovernable for anyone. That would be a disaster of incalculable proportions.

    特朗普和内塔尼亚胡这种“炸了再说”的策略，最好的结果不过是启动这一内部瓦解进程。而最坏的情况，则是用无休止的空中轰炸把伊朗彻底摧毁，以至于它将沦为一个任何人都无法治理的烂摊子。那将是一场无法估量的灾难。

    devastate
    严重摧毁；作者用它描述自己担忧的最坏后果。
    incalculable
    无法估量的；a disaster of incalculable proportions 强调潜在灾难的规模难以估算。

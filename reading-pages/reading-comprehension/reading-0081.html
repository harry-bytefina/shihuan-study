02-2013天津，B

城市、交通与公共空间

Last night's meteor(流星)shower left many people in the community dissatisfied and demanding answers.According to Gabe Rothschild,Emerald Valley's mayor,people gathered in the suburbs of the city,carrying heavy telescopes,expecting to watch the brightly burning meteors passing through the sky.What they found instead was a sky so brightened by the city's lights that it darkened the light of the meteors passing overhead.

“My family was so frustrated,”admitted town resident Duane Cosby.“We wanted to make this an unforgettable family outing,but it turned out to be a huge disappointment.”

Astronomers—scientists who study stars and planets—have been complaining about this problem for decades.They say that light pollution prevents them from seeing objects in the sky that they could see quite easily in the past.They call on people and the government to take measures to fight against it.

There is yet a population besides professional and amateur star observers that suffers even more from light pollution.This population consists of birds,bats,frogs,snakes,etc.For example,outdoor lighting severely affects migrating(迁徙的)birds.According to the International Dark-Sky Association,“100 million birds a year throughout North America die in crashes with lighted buildings and towers.”

Countless more animal casualties(伤亡)result from the use of artificial lighting.Clearly,people enjoy the benefits of lighting their evenings,but some scientists think it can be harmful for humans,too.They worry that exposure to light while sleeping can increase a person's chances of getting cancer.

Emerald Valley is only one community that is becoming aware of the negative effects of light pollution.For years,Flagstaff,Arizona,has enforced lighting regulations in its city in order to assist astronomers at the Lowell Observatory.Similar efforts have been made worldwide,and a movement is underway to remind us to turn off lights when we are not using them,so that other creatures can share the night.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1It happened last night that _____ .

  • A.the city's lights affected the meteor watching
  • B.the meteors flew past before being noticed
  • C.the city light show attracted many people
  • D.the meteor watching ended up a social outing
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1.A　细节理解题。根据第一段最后一句可知,城市的灯光影响了人们对流星雨的观看。故选A项。

2What do the astronomers complain about?

  • A.Meteor showers occur less often than before.
  • B.Their observation equipment is in poor repair.
  • C.Light pollution has remained unsolved for years.
  • D.Their eyesight is failing due to artificial lighting.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　细节理解题。根据第三段第一句可知,研究星系的科学家几十年来一直抱怨光污染问题。故选C项。

3What is the author concerned about according to Paragraph 4?

  • A.Birds may take other migration paths.
  • B.Animals' living habits may change suddenly.
  • C.Varieties of animals will become sharply reduced.
  • D.Animals' survival is threatened by outdoor lighting.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　细节理解题。根据第四段最后一句可知,在北美,每年近亿只鸟因为撞击灯火通明的建筑物而死亡。故选D项。

4Lighting regulations in Flagstaff, Arizona are put into effect to _____ .

  • A.lessen the chance of getting cancer
  • B.create an ideal observation condition
  • C.ensure citizens a good sleep at night
  • D.enable all creatures to live in harmony
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4.B　细节理解题。根据最后一段第二句可知,Flagstaff,Arizona强制执行灯火管制,目的是帮助Lowell Observatory的天文学家,即为他们创造理想的观察条件。故选B项。

5What message does the author most want to give us?

  • A.Saving wildlife is saving ourselves.
  • B.Great efforts should be made to save energy.
  • C.Human activities should be environmentally friendly.
  • D.New equipment should be introduced for space study.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

5.C　推理判断题。文章介绍了人类活动造成的光污染带来的方方面面的负面影响,故C项(人类活动应该是环保的)是作者最想要传递给我们的信息。

中美科技巨头，为何扎堆去这个国家建数据中心？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    How One Country Became the Unlikely Crossroads of the Global A.I. Boom

    一个国家何以意外成为全球AI热潮的交汇点

    crossroads
    n. 各种事物的交汇之处、汇集点
  2. 02

    Across a stretch of southern Malaysia once carpeted with palm-oil and pineapple plantations, signs of China’s global technology ambitions are taking shape.

    在马来西亚南部一片曾经遍布油棕与菠萝种植园的土地上，中国布局全球的科技雄心正一步步落地成形。

    plantation
    n. 种植园
    pineapple
    n. 菠萝
  3. 03

    More than 15 cranes tower over 100 acres of excavated earth, where DayOne, a company with corporate roots in one of China’s biggest data center operators, is building a sprawling complex.

    超过15台起重机高耸在一片占地100英亩、已经开挖的土地上。可溯源至中国一家头部数据中心运营商的企业 DayOne，正在此地兴建一座规模庞大的园区。

    excavate
    v. 挖掘、开凿
    sprawling
    adj. （杂乱地）铺展开的
  4. 04

    Inside these data centers will be some of the world’s most advanced artificial intelligence chips. The semiconductors, made by Nvidia, are largely banned from shipment to China under American export controls. Yet those restrictions generally do not prevent Chinese companies like ByteDance — DayOne’s biggest customer — from gaining access to the same chips in data centers outside China.

    这些数据中心内部将部署一批全球最先进的AI芯片。受美国出口管制规定，这批由英伟达生产的半导体芯片大多被禁止运往中国本土。但这套管制措施，通常没法阻碍诸如字节跳动（DayOne的最大客户）等中国企业从设在境外的数据中心获得同款芯片。

    semiconductor
    n. 半导体
  5. 05

    That distinction has helped turn Malaysia, a country of 35 million people, into an unlikely frontier of the A.I. boom. Drawn by cheap land and electricity, and proximity to undersea cables that carry internet traffic to China and the rest of the world, technology companies are pouring billions of dollars into the country.

    正是这一差异，让马来西亚——这个拥有3500万人口的国家——意外成为AI浪潮中的前沿热土。低廉的土地与电力成本，及其毗邻连通中国及全球其他地区的海底光缆的区位优势，吸引科技企业前来，给这个国家投入数十亿美元资金。

  6. 06

    Malaysia has carved out an uncommon position in an A.I. industry increasingly divided along geopolitical lines between China and the United States. Rather than choosing sides, it has welcomed both. In some of Johor’s industrial parks, infrastructure serving American and Chinese companies is being built almost side by side.

    在AI产业日益沿着中美地缘政治分界线走向分化的当下，马来西亚开辟了一条不同寻常的道路：它没有选边站队，而是同时对中美两国的企业敞开大门。在柔佛州的一些工业园区里，服务于美方与中方企业的基础设施几乎比邻而建。

    geopolitical
    adj. 地缘政治的
  7. 07

    “This is the only place in the world at the moment where we are seeing this,” said Lee Ting Han, a Johor government official involved in data center approvals. “What makes Malaysia so special for this A.I. wave at the moment is our nonaligned neutral status.”

    “目前，全世界只有这里能见到这样的景象。”柔佛州一位参与数据中心审批工作的官员李廷汉（Lee Ting Han）表示，“在这一轮 AI 浪潮中，马来西亚之所以特别，就在于我们奉行不结盟的中立立场。”

    nonaligned
    adj. 不结盟的

学习、阅读与教育方式

Salvador Dali(1904—1989)was one of the most popular of modern artists.The Pompidou Centre in Paris is showing its respect and admiration for the artist and his powerful personality with an exhibition bringing together over 200 paintings,sculptures,drawings and more.Among the works and masterworks on exhibition the visitor will find the best pieces,most importantly The Persistence of Memory.There is also L'Enigme sans Fin from 1938,works on paper,objects,and projects for stage and screen and selected parts from television programmes reflecting the artist's showman qualities.

The visitor will enter the World of Dali through an egg and is met with the beginning,the world of birth.The exhibition follows a path of time and subject with the visitor exiting through the brain.

The exhibition shows how Dali draws the viewer between two infinities(无限).“From the infinity small to the infinity large,contraction and expansion coming in and out of focus:amazing Flemish accuracy and the showy Baroque of old painting that he used in his museum-theatre in Figueras,”explains the Pompidou Centre.

The fine selection of the major works was done in close collaboration(合作)with the Museo Nacional Reina Sofia in Madrid,Spain,and with contributions from other institutions like the Salvador Dali Museum in St.Petersburg,Florida.

闹钟没发明前，人们靠什么准时起床？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Knocker uppers and candle clocks: How people woke up before alarm clocks

    敲窗人与蜡烛钟：在闹钟出现之前，人们是如何起床的

  2. 02

    During Britain's industrial revolution, new factories faced a need for strict timekeeping – including far more specific start times for workers.

    在英国工业革命时期，新兴工厂对精准计时有着迫切需求 —— 其中就包括对工人上班时间更为严格的规定。

    timekeeping
    计时、按时间行事；strict timekeeping 在本文指工厂严格管理上下班时间。
  3. 03

    A worker arriving even five minutes late could hold up an entire assembly line, losing their employers' profit. They needed a means to wake up on time, and while early alarm clocks existed at this time, they were far too expensive for a typical worker.

    哪怕迟到五分钟，整条生产线都可能因此停滞，给雇主造成经济损失。工人们需要一种能准时起床的方法。尽管当时已有早期闹钟，但对普通工人而言，价格实在太过昂贵。

  4. 04

    Instead, an entire profession dedicated to awakening people sprouted up: knocker uppers. These human alarm clocks would work their way down streets and sometimes whole neighbourhoods knocking or tapping on windows, or shooting peas at them.

    于是，一个专门负责叫醒人们的职业应运而生：敲窗人（knocker-upper）。这些 “人形闹钟” 会穿梭在大街小巷，有时甚至走遍整个社区，用手敲、用小棍轻敲窗户，或是用豌豆弹射窗户叫醒住户。

    profession
    职业；an entire profession 指敲窗叫人起床在当时形成了专门职业。
  5. 05

    Throughout history, people have had plenty of other inventive ways of waking up, from simply keeping roosters to clever candle clocks that dropped needles into metal trays every hour.

    纵观历史，人类还发明过许多充满创意的起床方式：从饲养公鸡报晓，到设计巧妙的蜡烛钟 —— 它们每隔一小时就会有针坠落到金属托盘里。

    inventive
    富有巧思的；修饰人们利用公鸡和蜡烛等想出的起床办法。
  6. 06

    The noises of waking animals could perhaps be thought of as humans' first auditory alarm clocks. The rooster crowing with the dawn is a common signal that the day has begun, says Sasha Handley, a professor of history who led a project on sleep in the early modern age.

    研究近代早期睡眠项目的历史学教授萨沙・汉德利表示，动物苏醒时发出的声响，或许可以被视为人类最早的 “听觉闹钟”。黎明时分公鸡啼鸣，便是一天开始的常见信号。

    auditory
    听觉的；auditory alarm clocks 特别强调通过声音把人叫醒。
    crowing
    啼叫；rooster crowing 指公鸡报晓时发出的典型声音。
  7. 07

    Candle clocks, with markings for incremental measurements of the passing of time, go all the way back to Ancient China. These were sometimes cleverly devised so that a nail would fall out into a little metal tray approximately every hour, says Handley. Incense was also used to keep time in China, sometimes with metal balls hanging by threads which would fall into a tray below, acting as gongs.

    带有时间刻度、用以计量流逝时间的蜡烛钟，其历史最早可追溯到古代中国。汉德利介绍，这类蜡烛钟设计精巧，大约每隔一小时，就会有一枚钉子脱落，掉进下方的小金属托盘中。中国古代还曾使用线香计时，有时会用细线悬挂金属球，线烧断后小球便会落入盘中，发出类似铜锣的声响。

    incremental
    按一定增量的；incremental measurements 指借助刻度按小段时间累计计时。
    devised
    设计出来的；cleverly devised 修饰能让小钉子按时落下的蜡烛钟。
    incense
    熏香；原文介绍古人也靠香缓慢燃烧的过程计量时间。
  8. 08

    By the 1920s, though, the knocker upper profession had largely died out, as alarm clocks became more commonplace and affordable. Daily routines that had once been more flexible gradually became organised around the clock.

    然而到了 20 世纪 20 年代，随着闹钟变得普及且价格亲民，敲窗人这一职业基本消失。曾经相对灵活的日常作息，也逐渐围绕时钟变得规整有序。

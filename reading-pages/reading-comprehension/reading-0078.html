阅读理解

03-2014天津，C

学习、阅读与教育方式

“Dad,”I say one day,“let's take a trip.Why don't you fly out and meet me?”

My father had just retired after 27 years as a manager for IBM.His job filled his day,his thoughts,his life.While he woke up and took a warm shower,I screamed under a freezing waterfall in Peru.While he tied a tie and put on the same Swiss watch,I rowed a boat across Lake of the Ozarks.

My father sees me drifting aimlessly,nothing to show for my 33 years but a passport full of funny stamps.He wants me to settle down,but now I want him to find an adventure.

He agrees to travel with me through the national parks.We meet four weeks later in Rapid City.

“What's our first stop?”asks my father.

“What time is it?”

“Still don't have a watch?”

Less than an hour away is Mount Rushmore.As he stares up at the four Presidents carved in granite(花岗岩),his mouth and eyes open slowly,like those of a little boy.

“Unbelievable,”he says.“How was this done?”

A film in the information center shows sculptor Gutzon Borglum devoted 14 years to the sculpture and then left the final touches to his son.

We stare up and I ask myself,Would I ever devote my life to anything?

No directions,no goals.I always used to hear those words in my father's voice.Now I hear them in my own.

The next day we're at Yellowstone National Park,where we have a picnic.

“Did you ever travel with your dad?”I ask.

“Only once,”he says.“I never spoke much with my father.We loved each other—but never said it.Whatever he could give me,he gave.”

That last sentence—it's probably the same thing I'd say about my father.And what I'd want my child to say about me.

In Glacier National Park,my father says,“I've never seen water so blue.”I have,in several places of the world.I can keep traveling,I realize—and maybe a regular job won't be as dull as I feared.

Weeks after our trip,I call my father.

“The photos from the trip are wonderful,”he says.“We've got to take another trip like that sometime.”

I tell him I've decided to settle down,and I'm wearing a watch.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1We can learn from Paragraphs 2 and 3 that the father _____ .

  • A.followed the fashion
  • B.got bored with his job
  • C.was unhappy with the author's lifestyle
  • D.liked the author's collection of stamps
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1.C　推理判断题。根据第二段可知,父亲打他的领带,戴他的名表,而我我行我素,无所事事,百无聊赖地在Ozarks湖上划着小船;第三段最后一句表明:父亲想让我定居下来,而我想让他去冒险。由此可以判断,父亲对我的生活方式不满。故选择C项。

2What does the author realize at Mount Rushmore?

  • A.His father is interested in sculpture.
  • B.His father is as innocent as a little boy.
  • C.He should learn sculpture in the future.
  • D.He should pursue a specific aim in life.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　推理判断题。从No directions,no goals.I always used to hear those words in my father's voice.Now I hear them in my own.可以推测出,作者感叹自己应该有个明确的人生目标并去努力追求。故选择D项。

3From the underlined paragraph, we can see that the author _____ .

  • A.wants his children to learn from their grandfather
  • B.comes to understand what parental love means
  • C.learns how to communicate with his father
  • D.hopes to give whatever he can to his father
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3.B　推理判断题。画线句子的意思是“那最后一句很可能是我对我的父亲的评价,也是我希望我的孩子评价我的话”。而That last sentence指的是上文中的Whatever he could give me,he gave.由此可推断出画线的句子让作者意识到了父爱的含义。故选择B项。

4What could be inferred about the author and his father from the end of the story?

  • A.The call solves their disagreements.
  • B.The Swiss watch has drawn them closer.
  • C.They decide to learn photography together.
  • D.They begin to change their attitudes to life.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　推理判断题。从最后几段中的We've got to take another trip like that sometime.I tell him I've decided to settle down,and I'm wearing a watch.可以推测出,父子二人对生活的态度都不约而同地发生着改变。故选择D项。

5What could be the best title for the passage?

  • A.Love Nature,Love Life
  • B.A Son Lost in Adventure
  • C.A Journey with Dad
  • D.The Art of Travel
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

5.C　主旨要义题。文章开门见山,直接点题。讲述了作者和父亲的一次旅行改变了二人对生活的态度。故选择C项。

阅读难度参考：CSE 6

朋友之间，少联络反而更亲密？最新研究帮我们重新定义友情

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Scientific Case for Being Less in Touch With Your Friends

    减少和朋友联系，是有科学根据的

  2. 02

    Amid a national loneliness crisis, many people believe that more contact is the key to connection. Teens commit to daily Snap streaks with their friends. Colleagues try to get closer through regular happy hours. Former roommates meet up for brunch every Sunday.

    在一场全国性的孤独危机之中，许多人相信增加联系是建立连接的关键。于是，青少年会和朋友坚持每天互发Snap，维持连续互动纪录。同事们试图通过定期参加“欢乐时光”增进关系。曾经的室友每周日相约一起吃早午餐。

    streak
    n. 一件事连续进行了多少天
    amid
    prep. 在……之中，在……背景下（常表示一个人或一件事正处在某种复杂、持续的环境之中）
  3. 03

    Being in constant communication can be nice, but it can also create a lot of pressure — on ourselves and others — and a lot of opportunities to be offended when lapses occur.

    频繁保持联系固然很好，但它也会给自己和别人带来很大压力——一旦联系出现中断，就很容易引发不快。

    lapse
    n. （因为遗忘或疏忽，原本持续的状态忽然）掉了一拍
    offend
    v. 冒犯，得罪某人
  4. 04

    Research reveals that the frequency of interaction does not determine the quality of a relationship. In fact, time apart can actually bring us closer together.

    研究表明，互动的频率并不能决定一段关系的质量。事实上，分开的时光反而能让我们更加亲近。

  5. 05

    After leaving for college, kids often get along better with their parents and their siblings. It’s true at work too: From software to sales, the most productive, creative and resourceful teams don’t interact in steady streams. They alternate between periods of low communication and surges of high communication.

    离家上大学后，年轻人往往会和父母、兄弟姐妹相处得更好。职场中也是如此：无论是软件开发还是销售，最高效、最有创造力、最善于解决问题的团队，并不会持续不断地互动。他们会在沟通较少和沟通密集的状态之间切换。

    surge
    n. 突然的上升，涌动
  6. 06

    Continuing a Snap streak doesn’t bolster bonds, but there’s another kind of digital communication that can. It’s called pebbling, named after a ritual that some penguins use to court potential mates. The digital equivalent of pebbling is exchanging memes, links or reels. It’s about sending little things that remind you of each other. It signals “I’m thinking of you.”

    保持Snap的连续使用记录并不能巩固关系；但还有另一种数字沟通方式可以做到这一点。这种方式叫作“送石子”，得名于某些企鹅向潜在伴侣求偶时的一种仪式。它在数字世界里的对应形式是跟好朋友分享表情包、链接或者短视频。也就是发送一些让你们想到彼此的小东西，传达一个信息：“我正在想你。”

    reel
    n. 社交媒体上的一条条短视频
    pebbling
    n. 一种互发表情包、链接或者短视频的数字交流方式
    bolster
    v. 支持，增强，巩固；n. 承托身体的长枕、靠垫

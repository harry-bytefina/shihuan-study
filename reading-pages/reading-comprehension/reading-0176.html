My Favourite Books

Jo Usmar is a writer for Cosmopolitan and co⁃author of the This Book Will series(系列) of lifestyle books. Here she picks her top reads.

Matilda

Roald Dahl

I once wrote a paper on the influence of fairy tales on Roald Dahl's writing and it gave me a new appreciation for his strange and delightful worlds. Matilda's battles with her cruel parents and the bossy headmistress, Miss Trunchbull, are equally funny and frightening, but they're also aspirational.

After Dark

Haruki Murakami

It's about two sisters—Eri, a model who either won't or can't stop sleeping, and Mari, a young student. In trying to connect to her sister, Mari starts changing her life and discovers a world of diverse“night people”who are hiding secrets.

Gone Girl

Gillian Flynn

There was a bit of me that didn't want to love this when everyone else on the planet did, but the horror story is brilliant. There's tension and anxiety from the beginning as Nick and Amy battle for your trust. It's a real whodunit and the frustration when you realise what's going on is horribly enjoyable.

The Stand

Stephen King

This is an excellent fantasy novel from one of the best storytellers around. After a serious flu outbreak wipes out 99.4% of the world's population, a battle unfolds between good and evil among those left. Randall Flagg is one of the scariest characters ever.

1Who does “I” refer to in the text?

  • A.Stephen King.
  • B.Gillian Flynn.
  • C.Jo Usmar.
  • D.Roald Dahl.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1.C　猜测词义题。根据第一段可知本文是作家Jo Usmar的书评。下文以第一人称推荐书籍,因此I指的是Jo Usmar,故选C项。

2Which of the following tells about Mari and Eri?

  • A.Cosmopolitan.
  • B.Matilda.
  • C.After Dark.
  • D.The Stand.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　细节理解题。在文章有关After Dark的书评中,作者介绍了Eri和Mari,因此C项正确。

3What kind of book is Gone Girl?

  • A.A folk tale.
  • B.A biography.
  • C.A love story.
  • D.A horror story.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　推理判断题。根据文章Gone Girl部分的介绍,尤其是“but the horror story is brilliant”可知,这本书是关于恐怖故事的,故D项正确。

总理年薪近300万美元，新加坡为何给公职人员开高薪？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    For a city-state with neither abundant natural resources nor a large population, the key to prosperity was ensuring things just worked, said Lee Kuan Yew, the founding father of modern Singapore. And keeping the country running smoothly, from its seaports and airports to financial services, were strict and unbendable rules, enforced by a phalanx of well-paid public servants.

    对于一个既没有丰富的自然资源，也没有庞大人口的城市国家来说，繁荣的关键就在于确保一切正常运转——现代新加坡的“国父”李光耀如是说。而要让这个国家平稳运行，从海港、机场到金融服务，都离不开严格、不可动摇的规则，以及一支执行这些规则、待遇优厚的公务员队伍。

    unbendable
    adj. 无法变通的，不能改变、动摇的
  2. 02

    The idea was reaffirmed on Tuesday, as Prime Minister Lawrence Wong announced ministerial wage increases that will bump his annual salary by 64 percent to $3.6 million Singapore dollars ($2.85 million).

    这一理念在周二（注：9月8日）再次得到确认，新加坡总理黄循财宣布上调部长级官员的薪酬。这项调整将使其年薪上涨64%，达到360万新币（约合285万美元）。

    bump
    v. 提高，增加
    reaffirm
    v. 再次声明，重申，强调
  3. 03

    In a statement delivered to the country’s Parliament, Mr. Wong said that political salaries had been stagnant for 15 years, and that it was becoming harder to recruit top talent from the private sector at a time when the country needed to furnish the next generation of leaders.

    黄循财在新加坡国会发表的一份声明中表示，公职人员薪酬已经15年没有调整了，而如今，新加坡正面临越来越难从私企招募到顶尖人才的问题，而此时又正值国家要为下一代领导班子做好人才储备。

    stagnant
    adj. 停滞的
    furnish
    v. 提供，供应（supply）
  4. 04

    Even before the latest raise, the prime minister of Singapore was already the highest-paid public servant in the world, far out-earning the president of the United States.

    即便在此次加薪之前，新加坡总理也已经是全球薪酬最高的公职人员，收入远超美国总统。

  5. 05

    In 2012, cabinet salaries were slashed by around 36 percent amid accusations that high ministerial wages, which are pegged to the median annual income of Singapore’s top 1,000 earners, made the country’s leaders out of touch with the realities of ordinary citizens.

    2012年，因外界指责部长级官员薪酬过高至脱离普通民众生活，新加坡政府曾将内阁成员薪酬大幅削减约36%。新加坡内阁成员的薪酬是按照该国收入最高的1000人的年收入中位数来确定的。

    slash
    v. 大幅减少
    cabinet
    n. 内阁
  6. 06

    On Tuesday, Mr. Wong said that, in light of his role in the salary adjustments, he would donate the full increase in his salary to charitable causes.

    周二，黄循财表示，鉴于自己也参与了此次薪酬调整，他将把自己本次加薪所得全部捐给慈善事业。

  7. 07

    “This is my own decision,” he said. “I do not expect other officeholders to do the same.”

    “这是我个人的决定，”他说，“我并不期待其他公职人员也这么做。”

    officeholder
    n. 公职人员

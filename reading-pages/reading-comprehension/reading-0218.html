11-2016课标全国Ⅲ，C

食物、农业与地方身份

If you are a fruit grower—or would like to become one—take advantage of Apple Day to see what's around. It's called Apple Day but in practice it's more like Apple Month. The day itself is on October 21, but since it has caught on, events now spread out over most of October around Britain.

Visiting an apple event is a good chance to see, and often taste, a wide variety of apples. To people who are used to the limited choice of apples such as Golden Delicious and Royal Gala in supermarkets, it can be quite an eye opener to see the range of classical apples still in existence, such as Decio which was grown by the Romans. Although it doesn't taste of anything special, it's still worth a try, as is the knobbly(多疙瘩的)Cat's Head which is more of a curiosity than anything else.

There are also varieties developed to suit specific local conditions. One of the very best varieties for eating quality is Orleans Reinette, but you'll need a warm, sheltered place with perfect soil to grow it, so it's a pipe dream for most apple lovers who fall for it.

At the events, you can meet expert growers and discuss which ones will best suit your conditions, and because these are family affairs, children are well catered for with apple-themed fun and games.

Apple Days are being held at all sorts of places with an interest in fruit, including stately gardens and commercial orchards(果园). If you want to have a real orchard experience, try visiting the National Fruit Collection at Brogdale, near Faversham in Kent.

邪修戒手机大法，屏幕时长暴跌！《纽约时报》记者亲测有效

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    I Killed Color on My Phone. The Result Shocked Me.

    我把手机里的颜色“杀”掉了，结果把我自己吓了一跳

  2. 02

    I have long been skeptical of the current hysteria about how our phones are addictive machines destroying our previously pristine analog world. But a few months ago, even I began to be vaguely worried that I was devoting too many hours to an embarrassing medley of political commentary and makeup application TikTok videos. So when I spied an article about the benefits of switching one’s phone to a black-and-white color scheme, I figured I’d give it a whirl.

    一直以来，我都对这种“谈虎色变”的说法持怀疑态度——手机正在变成一种成瘾机器，摧毁我们原本纯净的、未被数字设备侵蚀的世界。但几个月前，就连我也开始隐约担心，自己是不是把太多时间耗在刷TikTok上，一会儿是政治评论，一会儿又是美妆教程，说出来还有点不好意思。所以，当我偶然看到一篇文章，讲的是把手机切换成黑白显示模式的好处时，我心想不妨试试看。

    hysteria
    过度的集体恐慌；作者用它表明自己一开始不认同围绕手机成瘾的部分舆论。
    pristine
    洁净得仿佛未被碰过的；说以往的非数字世界纯净无瑕，带有一点反讽。
    analog
    模拟的、非数字的；analog world 在本文泛指数字设备普及前的生活。
    medley
    各种事物混在一起的一组；a medley of videos 指政治评论、化妆等混杂的视频。
    spied
    无意中瞥见、注意到；作者是偶然看到了一篇关于手机黑白设置的文章。
  3. 03

    The result was shocking. The moment I switched, I no longer felt an urgent need to look at my iPhone. I experienced a full-bodied sense of relief when the colors faded to gray.

    结果真的把我震住了。从切换的那一刻起，我就不再感到那种迫切的冲动，非得盯着我的iPhone不放。当屏幕上的颜色褪成灰度时，我整个人都松了下来。

  4. 04

    I would leave my phone in my room when I walked from my home office to the kitchen. I would forget to check it for hours. When I did check it, I put it down quickly as soon as the task was done. The number of hours I spent per day on the phone plummeted 40 percent.

    我会把手机留在房间里，从家庭办公室走到厨房也不带着它，有时候一连好几个小时都忘记看。即便拿起来，也是在事情一办完就立刻放下。我每天花在手机上的时间，直接暴跌了40%。

    plummeted
    骤降；指作者每天的手机使用时长明显下降。
  5. 05

    There are some small downsides to my black-and-white online life. Because the answer and decline buttons on my phone are both gray, I’ll sometimes hang up on a call I want to answer. The hardest adjustment was photos. My husband sent me gray pictures of a beautiful sunset.

    当然，黑白色彩下的网络生活也有一些小小的不便。比如手机上的“接听”和“拒绝”按钮现在都是灰色的，我偶尔会在本想接听的时候，直接把电话给挂了。最难适应的还是照片——我丈夫给我发来了一张绝美日落的照片，不过是灰色的。

    downsides
    不利之处；例如按钮失去颜色区别后，作者有时会误挂电话。
  6. 06

    It may sound cheesy, but to be honest, I feel like turning off color on my phone made me more aware and more appreciative of the color and beauty in real life. And those are things I do want to be addicted to.

    听起来可能有点老套，但说实话，我真的觉得把手机里的颜色关掉，让我对现实生活中的色彩与美丽变得更加敏锐，也更加珍惜。而这些，才是我真正愿意“上瘾”的东西。

    cheesy
    俗套得有点难为情的；作者觉得『更欣赏现实中的色彩』听上去可能有点老套。

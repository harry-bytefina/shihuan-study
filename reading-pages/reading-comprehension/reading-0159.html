阅读理解

08-2021全国甲，D

学习、阅读与教育方式

Who is a genius?This question has greatly interested humankind for centuries.

Let's state clearly:Einstein was a genius. His face is almost the international symbol for genius. But we want to go beyond one man and explore the nature of genius itself. Why is it that some people are so much more intelligent or creative than the rest of us?And who are they?

In the sciences and arts, those praised as geniuses were most often white men, of European origin. Perhaps this is not a surprise. It's said that history is written by the victors, and those victors set the standards for admission to the genius club. When contributions were made by geniuses outside the club—women, or people of a different color or belief—they were unacknowledged and rejected by others.

A study recently published by Science found that as young as age six, girls are less likely than boys to say that members of their gender(性别)are “really, really smart.” Even worse, the study found that girls act on that belief:Around age six they start to avoid activities said to be for children who are “really, really smart.”Can our planet afford to have any great thinkers become discouraged and give up?It doesn't take a genius to know the answer:absolutely not.

Here's the good news. In a wired world with constant global communication, we're all positioned to see flashes of genius wherever they appear. And the more we look, the more we will see that social factors(因素)like gender, race, and class do not determine the appearance of genius. As a writer says, future geniuses come from those with “intelligence, creativity, perseverance(毅力), and simple good fortune, who are able to change the world.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What does the author think of victors' standards for joining the genius club?

  • A.They're unfair.
  • B.They're conservative.
  • C.They're objective.
  • D.They're strict.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1.A　推理判断题。根据第三段第三、四句可知,胜利者为天才俱乐部设定了入会标准。当俱乐部之外的天才做出贡献时,他们是不会被认可的,而且会被其他人拒绝。由此可推断出这样的标准是不公平的。

2What can we infer about girls from the study in Science?

  • A.They think themselves smart.
  • B.They look up to great thinkers.
  • C.They see gender differences earlier than boys.
  • D.They are likely to be influenced by social beliefs.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　推理判断题。根据第四段第一、二句可知,女孩在六岁时可能就形成了一个不说自己的同性非常非常聪明的习惯,而且她们在六岁时就开始避免参加为“非常非常聪明”的孩子们准备的活动。由此可以推出她们可能受社会观念的影响。

3Why are more geniuses known to the public?

  • A.Improved global communication.
  • B.Less discrimination against women.
  • C.Acceptance of victors' concepts.
  • D.Changes in people's social positions.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

3.A　细节理解题。由最后一段第二句可知,随着持续的全球交流,会有更多天才被公众所知,故选A。

4What is the best title for the text?

  • A.Geniuses Think Alike
  • B.Genius Takes Many Forms
  • C.Genius and Intelligence
  • D.Genius and Luck
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4.B　主旨要义题。由最后一段最后两句可知,社会因素,比如性别、种族、阶级,并不能决定天才的出现。未来的天才来自拥有智慧、创造力、毅力以及单纯的好运气的人,即天才有很多种形式。

重塑千亿市场！ 跨国巨头为何抢购中国“减肥神药”？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Race for the Next Big Weight-Loss Drug Is On—and China Wants In

    下一代重磅减肥药的研发竞赛已经打响——中国也想参与其中

  2. 02

    Chinese biotech companies, rapidly evolving from manufacturers of low-end generic drugs to serious competitors to American pharma giants, are training their sights on one of the most lucrative medicines: GLP-1s.

    中国生物科技公司正迅速从低端仿制药生产商转型为美国制药巨头的强劲竞争对手，如今它们将目光投向了利润最丰厚的药物之一：GLP-1类药物。

    lucrative
    利润丰厚的；文章用它比较同类药在不同市场的获利机会。
    generic
    仿制的；generic drugs 指与原研品牌药对应的仿制药。
  3. 03

    Today Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk, with drugs like Mounjaro and Ozempic, are the undisputed leaders of the $80-billion global GLP-1 market, which is dominated by U.S. sales.

    如今，礼来（Eli Lilly）和诺和诺德（Novo Nordisk）凭借替尔泊肽（Mounjaro）和司美格鲁肽（Ozempic）等药物，已成为规模800亿美元的全球GLP-1市场中无可争议的领导者，而该市场的销售额主要来自美国。

    undisputed
    无可争议的；undisputed leaders 指在报道所谈的市场中领先地位公认的企业。
  4. 04

    But Chinese biotechs are pouring in. In the last two decades, China has transformed from a basic manufacturer of generics and precursor chemicals into a hotbed of scientific discovery, boosted by government support and low operating costs. Global pharmaceutical companies are now trawling for promising drugs from labs in biotech centers such as Shanghai and Suzhou. Last year, China licensed more than $130 billion in new drugs overseas, across cancer, weight-loss and immunology therapies, according to PharmCube.

    但中国的生物科技公司正蜂拥而至。在过去的二十年里，在中国政府的支持和低运营成本的推动下，中国已从一家仿制药和前体化学品的基础生产国，转型为一片科学发现的温床。全球制药公司如今正从上海、苏州这样的生物科技中心的实验室中广撒网，物色有前景的药物。据医药魔方（PharmCube）统计，去年中国对海外授权的新药总价值超过1300亿美元，涵盖癌症、减肥和免疫疗法等领域。

    hotbed
    某类活动蓬勃发生的地方；a hotbed of scientific discovery 指医药研发活跃的环境。
    precursor
    前体、上游原料；precursor chemicals 是用来制造其他化合物的起始物质。
    trawling
    广泛搜寻；trawling for promising drugs 指大型药企在许多实验室里寻找有前景的候选药。
    licensed
    授权使用或经营；licensed drugs overseas 指通过协议把相关药品权益授权给海外合作方。
  5. 05

    Chinese biotechs are pushing overseas in part because the domestic market is far less lucrative. China has a large—and growing—obesity problem and counts nearly 150 million diabetic adults. But the slice of the population taking GLP-1s is small compared with the U.S., while drug pricing is far lower in China. The national drug reimbursement system also covers the medications only for diabetes, not obesity, forcing most patients to pay out of pocket.

    中国生物科技公司之所以积极拓展海外市场，部分原因在于国内市场的利润远不如海外丰厚。中国的肥胖问题规模大且日益加剧，成年糖尿病患者人数接近1.5亿。但与美国相比，中国使用GLP-1药物的患者人群占比很小，且药品定价远低于美国。此外，国家医保制度仅将此类药物纳入糖尿病治疗范围，而不涵盖肥胖症治疗，这迫使大多数患者只能自掏腰包。

    reimbursement
    费用报销或补偿；drug reimbursement system 指按规定承担部分或全部药费的制度。
  6. 06

    "You must develop step by step—you cannot jump to first-in-class drug when you just start," Sciwind's Pan said. "You should learn how to make generics first, then how to make "me too" drugs, then 'me better' drugs, then innovative drugs. It's a process."

    先为达生物（Sciwind）的潘海说：“必须循序渐进——刚起步时不可能一步登天开发创新药。应该先学会生产仿制药，然后学会生产‘跟风药’，接着是‘改良药’，最后才是创新药。这是一个必经的过程。”

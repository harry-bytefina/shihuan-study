全部题目与逐题讲解

1When can you watch the Chinese drum dance?

  • A.On July 2.
  • B.On July 3.
  • C.On July 6.
  • D.On July 8.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1.A　细节理解题。根据题干中的“Chinese drum dance”可知本题的答案区间为第一项活动。根据Sunday 29 June and Wednesday 2 July in the Turbine Hall,at 11.30 am & 1.30 pm.可知答案是A项。

2To learn the magic tricks, you can go to _____ .

  • A.Kids Activity
  • B.Chinese Youth League
  • C.Club Med Circus School
  • D.Children's Arts Theatre
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　细节理解题。由“Club Med Circus School”中Learn circus skills,including the trapeze,trampolining and magic.可知C是正确的。

3What is required if you want to enjoy free visits to the Museum?

  • A.Calling (02)9217 0600.
  • B.Gaining family membership.
  • C.Coming for the holiday fun.
  • D.Paying Powerline $50.00 a year.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3.B　细节理解题。由倒数第二段中Enjoy unlimited free visits and many other benefits by becoming a Family member of the Powerhouse.可知,享受免费游览的条件是成为他们的会员。故B项为正确答案。

4What is the main purpose of the text?

  • A.To attract visitors.
  • B.To present schedules.
  • C.To report the performances.
  • D.To teach kids Chinese arts.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4.A　推理判断题。纵观全文,作者介绍Powerhouse各个项目为的是吸引更多的游客,尤其最后两段介绍推出的优惠政策,这一定会让更多的游客动心。由此,to attract visitors 是作者写这篇文章的真实意图。故正确答案为A项。

Things to Do in Yorkshire This Summer

Harrogate Music Festival

Since its birth, Harrogate Music Festival has gone from strength to strength. This year, we are celebrating our 50th anniversary. We begin on 1st June with Manchester Camerata and Nicola Benedetti, presenting an amazing programme of Mozart pieces.

Dates: 1 June-31 July

Tickets: ￡12-￡96

Jodie's Fitness Summer Classes

As the summer months roll in, our Georgian country estate makes the perfect setting for an outdoor fitness session. Come and work out with our qualified personal trainer, Jodie McGregor, on the grounds of the Middleton Lodge estate.

We will be holding a free taster session on 23rd May, at 10 am, to demonstrate the variety of effective and active exercises. There are eight spaces available for the taster session. Advance bookings are required (info@middletonlodge.co.uk).

Dates: 23 May-11 July

Tickets: ￡7.50 per session

Felt Picture Making

Working from an inspirational picture, this workshop at Helmsley Arts Centre will teach you the techniques you will need to recreate your picture in wool.

We will also discuss the origins of felt(毛毡), what enables wool fibres to become felt and how the processes we use work.

Dates: 12 June-12 July

Tickets: ￡40 including materials

Figure It Out!—Playing with Math

A new exhibition in Halifax uses everyday activities to explain the hidden math principles we all use on a regular basis. Pack a bag, cut a cake, guess which juice container holds the most liquid, and much more. Discover how architects, product designers and scientists use similar skills in their work.

Dates: 7 May-10 June

Tickets: Free

意志力会消失，但恨一个人能让你开挂

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    You've tried shadow journaling, manifesting and microdosing. But to really succeed in life, have you considered getting a nemesis?

    阴影日志、愿景显化、微量用药——这些你都已经尝试过了。可为了取得真正的人生成就，你有没有考虑过给自己找一个宿敌？

    microdosing
    n. 微量用药
    manifesting
    n. 愿景显化
  2. 02

    I totally recommend it. After all, willpower fades. Apps rarely change your life. Even frenemies aren't reliably hateable. You need the kind of fuel that's born from unadulterated jealousy, from focused indignation. It's a feeling that boils down to: That guy?

    我强烈推荐这个方法。毕竟，意志力早晚会消退，app很少能改变人生，就连“塑料死对头”都无法完全让你狠下心来。你需要一种内心燃料——它源自纯粹的嫉妒和始终如一的愤懑。那种感觉，最后往往浓缩成一句话：“就这家伙？”

    indignation
    n. （因为受到不公正对待而产生的）愤懑
    frenemy
    n. 塑料友谊；假装是朋友，其实是敌人的人
    unadulterated
    adj. 完全的；纯粹的
  3. 03

    Many successful people understand the power of a grudge — athletes, pop stars, your mother-in-law, our president. Emotion can pick up the slack even after training and talent have reached their limits.

    许多成功人士都深谙怨妒的力量——运动员、流行歌手、你婆婆或岳母、还有我们的总统。即便训练和天赋让他们到达了极限，这种情绪依然能够往前推自己一把，再创新高。

    grudge
    n. 怀恨，不满，积怨
  4. 04

    Yes, celebrities often seem to be handed ready-made nemeses: opposing teams, Oscar competitors. But grievances abound. You just have to find the right nemesis for you. To whittle down the possibilities, concentrate on people whose curated accomplishments are exactly what you secretly wish you could brag about, too.

    没错，名人们往往自带现成的宿敌，例如对手球队、奥斯卡竞争者。不过现实中，怨气也从不稀缺。你只需要找到最适合你的那个宿敌。不妨缩小范围，把注意力放在这样的人身上——那些被精心包装的成就，恰好也是你内心深处渴望能拿出来炫耀的东西。

    abound
    v. 大量存在，有很多
  5. 05

    I know, deep down, that my desire for a nemesis often comes back to my own insecurity. I am afraid I can't do something — win the job, excel at the new hobby — so I refuse to try … until I get mad enough. It took a work reorganization and parenting two toddlers during a pandemic to finally get me to lace up my running shoes, a longtime goal.

    其实打从心底深处我很清楚，我对“宿敌”的渴望源于自己的不安全感。我太害怕自己做不到了——无论是获得某份工作，还是精通某种爱好——所以干脆连试也不试……直到我怒火中烧，气到不行。比如真正把我逼到去系上跑鞋鞋带的，是一次工作上的变动重组，还有在疫情期间照料两个蹒跚学步的孩子，而跑步就是我心里攒了很久的目标。

  6. 06

    My routine is thus: I put on '90s rap music. I wave goodbye to my kids. I set off down the street. A montage starts playing in my mind, like that crescendoing scene in a movie where they're preparing to fight the aliens, except this time it's just me on an imaginary track, sprinting past everyone who's ever wronged me.

    于是，我的日常现在是这样：戴上耳机，放九十年代的说唱，再跟孩子们挥挥手，然后沿着街道一路跑出去。我的脑海里自动开始播放蒙太奇——就像电影里那样，随着音乐渐强，全员集结准备迎战外星人。只不过这一次，只有我一个人，我在想象中一路冲刺，把所有曾经伤害过我、冤枉过我的人，统统甩在身后。

    sprint
    v. 全速奔跑
  7. 07

    Cheerfully to combat and to feast, not to sulk, not to dream, to be ready for what is hardest as for a festival, to be sound and innocent.

    高高兴兴去战斗、去赴宴，不做忧郁的人，不做空想的人，准备应付至难之事，就像去赴宴一样，要健康而完好。

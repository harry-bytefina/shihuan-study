03-2018天津夏，C

食物、农业与地方身份

There's a new frontier in 3D printing that's beginning to come into focus:food. Recent development has made possible machines that print, cook, and serve foods on a mass scale. And the industry isn't stopping there.

Food production

With a 3D printer, a cook can print complicated chocolate sculptures and beautiful pieces for decoration on a wedding cake. Not everybody can do that—it takes years of experience, but a printer makes it easy. A restaurant in Spain uses a Foodini to“re-create forms and pieces”of food that are“exactly the same,”freeing cooks to complete other tasks. In another restaurant, all of the dishes and desserts it serves are 3D-printed, rather than farm to table.

Sustainability(可持续性)

The global population is expected to grow to 9.6 billion by 2050, and some analysts estimate that food production will need to be raised by 50 percent to maintain current levels. Sustainability is becoming a necessity. 3D food printing could probably contribute to the solution. Some experts believe printers could use hydrocolloids(水解胶体)from plentiful renewables like algae(藻类)and grass to replace the familiar ingredients(烹饪原料). 3D printing can reduce fuel use and emissions. Grocery stores of the future might stock“food”that lasts years on end, freeing up shelf space and reducing transportation and storage requirements.

Nutrition

Future 3D food printers could make processed food healthier. Hod Lipson, a professor at Columbia University, said,“Food printing could allow consumers to print food with customized nutritional content, like vitamins. So instead of eating a piece of yesterday's bread from the supermarket, you'd eat something baked just for you on demand.”

Challenges

Despite recent advancements in 3D food printing, the industry has many challenges to overcome. Currently, most ingredients must be changed to a paste(糊状物)before a printer can use them, and the printing process is quite time-consuming, because ingredients interact with each other in very complex ways. On top of that, most of the 3D food printers now are restricted to dry ingredients because meat and milk products may easily go bad. Some experts are skeptical about 3D food printers, believing they are better suited for fast food restaurants than homes and high-end restaurants.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What benefit does 3D printing bring to food production?

  • A.It helps cooks to create new dishes.
  • B.It saves time and effort in cooking.
  • C.It improves the cooking conditions.
  • D.It contributes to restaurant decorations.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　推理判断题。根据第一段中关键信息on a mass scale(大量地)和第二段第二句话(不是每一个人都能做到那一点,它需要花费数年的经验,但是一台打印机使它变得简单)可知,在制作食物时3D打印技术可以节省时间,减少制作食物付出的努力,故选B项。

2What can we learn about 3D food printing from Paragraph 3?

  • A.It solves food shortages easily.
  • B.It quickens the transportation of food.
  • C.It needs no space for the storage of food.
  • D.It uses renewable materials as sources of food.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　细节理解题。根据第三段第四句(一些专家认为打印机能够使用从像藻类和草等丰富的可再生资源中获得的水解胶体来代替熟悉的烹饪原料)可确定答案为D项。

3According to Paragraph 4, 3D-printed food _____ .

  • A.is more available to consumers
  • B.can meet individual nutritional needs
  • C.is more tasty than food in supermarkets
  • D.can keep all the nutrition in raw materials
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3.B　推理判断题。根据第四段直接引语中的第一句话(食物打印能使客户打印含有定制的有营养的比如维他命的内容的食物)可确定答案为B项。

4What is the main factor that prevents 3D food printing from spreading widely?

  • A.The printing process is complicated.
  • B.3D food printers are too expensive.
  • C.Food materials have to be dry.
  • D.Some experts doubt 3D food printing.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

4.C　细节理解题。根据最后一段第三句话(除了那一点以外,绝大部分3D食物打印机现在局限于干燥的原料,因为肉和牛奶产品可能容易变质)可确定答案为C项。

5What could be the best title of the passage?

  • A.3D Food Printing:Delicious New Technology
  • B.A New Way to Improve 3D Food Printing
  • C.The Challenges for 3D Food Production
  • D.3D Food Printing:From Farm to Table
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

5.A　主旨要义题。文章第一句为主题句,它告诉我们文章的主题为3D打印技术的新领域——食品,接下来主要介绍了这一应用的优点,也提及了它面临的挑战。故选A项。

    Unitree's IPO May Be Just the Beginning of an Investor Frenzy Over Humanoid-Robot Stocks

    宇树科技的首次公开募股（IPO）可能只是投资者对人形机器人股票狂热追捧的开始

    frenzy
    n. 狂热
  2. 02

    Unitree Robotics is set to become the first public humanoid-robotics company in mainland China, and it's riding the robotics hype straight to the bank. Last week, the Hangzhou-based company priced its shares at about $22, seeking to raise about $900 million in an initial public offering on the Shanghai Stock Exchange. On Monday, it said the IPO was more than 8,000 times oversubscribed by retail investors, a sign that interest in the burgeoning industry is strong.

    宇树机器人即将成为中国大陆首家上市的人形机器人公司，并正借助机器人领域的市场热潮大举融资。上周，这家总部位于杭州的公司将股票定价定在22美元左右，计划在于上海证券交易所首次进行的公开募股（IPO）中筹集约9亿美元。周一，该公司表示，此次IPO获得了散户投资者超过8000倍的超额认购，这表明市场对这一蓬勃发展的新兴行业兴趣浓厚。

    oversubscribe
    v. 超额认购
    burgeoning
    adj. 新兴的；快速冒头的
  3. 03

    The market for humanoid robots is still pretty small — just 15,000 or so units were deployed last year — but advocates like Tesla CEO Elon Musk say the potential is massive. For now, Unitree sits near the top of the food chain.

    人形机器人的市场规模目前仍然很小——去年仅部署了约1.5万台——但特斯拉首席执行官埃隆·马斯克等支持者认为，其市场潜力巨大。就目前而言，宇树科技处于该行业食物链的顶端。

    deploy
    v. 部署设备、人手、资金
    advocate
    n. 主张者，鼓吹者
  4. 04

    Unitree was the single biggest supplier of humanoid robots last year, although it was outsold in the first half of 2026 by Chinese rival Agibot, according to a report from Smart Analytics Global. The top five suppliers were all Chinese firms. That's largely because of how cheap Chinese robots are compared with rival products.

    根据Smart Analytics Global的一份报告，宇树科技是去年人形机器人的最大单一供应商，尽管在2026年上半年，其销量被中国竞争对手智元机器人（Agibot）超越。前五大供应商均为中国企业。这很大程度上是因为中国机器人相较于竞争对手价格更为低廉。

  5. 05

    Unitree is profitable, a relatively uncommon feat in the world of humanoid-robotics companies. Overseas sales accounted for over 40% of Unitree's revenue in 2025, although the company has warned that geopolitical tensions could hamper its expansion. Last month, the U.S. Federal Communications Commission banned new foreign-made humanoid and quadruped robots.

    宇树科技已实现盈利，这在人形机器人行业中是难得的成就。2025年，海外销售额占宇树科技营收的40%以上，不过该公司已发出预警，地缘政治的紧张局势可能会阻碍其扩大规模。上个月，美国联邦通信委员会（FCC, Federal Communications Commission）开始禁止新的国外制造的人形机器人和四足机器人。

    hamper
    v. 阻碍；拖累
    feat
    n. 需要本事才能做成的事
  6. 06

    Unitree is not the only such firm seeking to go public. "Listing is a must," LimX Dynamics founder Will Zhang told reporters in July.

    宇树科技并非唯一一家寻求上市的公司。今年7月，逐际动力（LimX Dynamics）创始人张巍（Will Zhang）对记者表示：“上市是必须的。”

  7. 07

    Agility Robotics plans to become the first pure-play public humanoid-robotics company in the U.S. The firm’s Digit robots are operating in commercial environments with GXO Logistics. "We have not achieved [return on investment] on humanoids yet," Patrick Kelleher, the CEO of GXO, told investors last week.

    Agility Robotics计划成为美国第一家纯做人形机器人业务的上市公司。该公司的Digit机器人已经在GXO Logistics的商业环境里投入运行。GXO首席执行官帕特里克·凯莱赫（Patrick Kelleher）上周对投资者表示：“我们尚未在人形机器人上实现投资回报。”

    pure-play
    n. 单一业务的

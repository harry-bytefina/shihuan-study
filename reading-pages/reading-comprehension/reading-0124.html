POETRY CHALLENGE

Write a poem about how courage, determination, and strength have helped you face challenges in your life.

Prizes

3 Grand Prizes:Trip to Washington, D.C. for each of three winners, a parent and one other person of the winner's choice. Trip includes roundtrip air tickets, hotel stay for two nights, and tours of the National Air and Space Museum and the office of National Geographic World.

6 First Prizes:The book Sky Pioneer:A Photobiography of Amelia Earhart signed by author Corinne Szabo and pilot Linda Finch.

50 Honorable Mentions:Judges will choose up to 50 honorable mention winners, who will each receive a T-shirt in memory of Earhart’s final flight.

Rules

Follow all rules carefully to prevent disqualification.

■Write a poem using 100 words or fewer. Your poem can be any format, any number of lines.

■Write by hand or type on a single sheet of paper. You may use both the front and back of the paper.

■On the same sheet of paper, write or type your name, address, telephone number, and birth date.

■Mail your entry to us by October 31 this year.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1How many people can each grand prize winner take on the free trip?

  • A.Two.
  • B.Three.
  • C.Four.
  • D.Six.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1. A细节理解题。根据Prizes部分的“3 Grand Prizes:Trip to Washington,D.C. for each of three winners,a parent and one other person of the winner’s choice.”可知答案为A项（父母之一和自己选择的另一个人）。

2What will each of the honorable mention winners get?

  • A.A plane ticket.
  • B.A book by Corinne Szabo.
  • C.A special T-shirt.
  • D.A photo of Amelia Earhart.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2. C细节理解题。根据Prizes部分的“50 Honorable Mentions:Judges will choose up to 50 honorable mention winners, who will each receive a T-shirt in memory of Earhart’s final flight.（评委将选出最多50名荣誉奖获得者，他们将各自获得一件T恤衫，以纪念Earhart的最后一次飞行。）”可知答案为C项（一件特别的T恤衫）。

3Which of the following will result in disqualification?

  • A.Typing your poem out.
  • B.Writing a poem of 120 words.
  • C.Using both sides of the paper.
  • D.Mailing your entry on October 30.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3. B细节理解题。根据Rules部分的“Write a poem using 100 words or fewer.（用100个或更少的词写一首诗。）”可知答案为B项（写一首120词的诗）。

继香烟之后，餐厅开始对手机下“逐客令”

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    At Restaurants, Phones Are Becoming the New Cigarettes

    在餐厅里，手机正在成为新的香烟

  2. 02

    It wasn’t all that long ago that restaurants and bars around the country were dappled in the glow of cigarettes. Those days are over, but diners haven’t stopped lighting up—now it’s just with the blinding OLED gleam from their phones. Today, phones are as common in a table-setting as napkins and silverware.

    曾几何时，美国各地的餐厅和酒吧还笼罩在香烟的点点微光中。那个时代虽已过去，但食客们并未停止“点亮”什么——只不过现在换成了手机OLED屏幕刺眼的光。如今，手机已和餐巾、餐具一样，成为餐桌上的标配。

    gleam
    一束亮光或反光；the blinding OLED gleam 指手机屏幕刺眼的光。
    dappled
    布满明暗交错的光斑；这里形容餐厅里散布着点点烟火。
  3. 03

    Many restaurants and patrons alike are pushing back against the shift that has turned collegial, communal spaces into places to watch TikTok while you eat. Here’s what some are doing—and what we can learn from them.

    许多餐厅和食客都在抵制这种转变：原本旨在交谈、共处的社交空间，如今变成了边吃边刷TikTok的场所。以下是一些餐厅采取的应对措施，以及我们可以从中汲取的经验。

    collegial
    气氛友好、可以平等交流的；形容餐厅原先适合轻松相处。
    communal
    由多人共享的；communal spaces 指供大家一起用餐或交流的空间。
    patrons
    顾客、常客；在餐饮语境里指来消费的人。
  4. 04

    Antagonist, a cocktail bar in Charlotte, N.C., is pretty antagonistic about phones. Upon entry, each customer receives a lockable pouch—the same bag some schools use to store students’ phones—that can only be opened by the staff.

    位于北卡罗来纳州夏洛特市的鸡尾酒吧Antagonist对手机持排斥态度。顾客一进门就会收到一个可上锁、只有由工作人员打开的收纳袋——这种袋子常被学校用来存放学生的手机。

    antagonistic
    抱有敌意、明确反对的；作者借酒吧的名字说它对手机的态度强硬。
  5. 05

    “The main goal of our whole concept here really isn’t to take away their property,” Adam Horner, Antagonist’s general manager, said. “It’s essentially just to bring back a little slice of human connection that we all feel has been lost with technology.”

    “这一理念的核心不是要没收顾客的财物，”Antagonist酒吧总经理亚当·霍纳（Adam Horner）说，“我们真正想做的，是找回一点人与人之间的连接。在我们看来，这种连接已随着技术的发展逐渐消失了。”

  6. 06

    Even if your dinner destination doesn’t have the same rules, you can still borrow the basic idea. Keep your phone somewhere inconvenient or even mildly inaccessible: in your coat pocket, for instance, or your purse.

    即便你去的餐厅没有给出这类规定，你依然可以借鉴这一基本思路。不妨把手机放在一个不太方便拿取、甚至稍微有些够不着的地方，比如外套口袋或手提包里。

    inaccessible
    不易拿到的；把手机放得稍远些，让人在饭桌上不便随手取用。
  7. 07

    The Edge, a local spot in Manhattan, is a bit less rigid about phones. There’s no ban on them, but the restaurant doesn’t offer internet access. A sign at its bar reads: “No Wi-Fi. No Screens. Connect With Each Other.”

    曼哈顿的一家本地餐厅The Edge对待手机的态度没有那么苛刻。虽然这里并不禁止使用手机，但餐厅不提供网络服务。吧台上立着一块告示，写着：“店内无Wi-Fi，别打开屏幕，专注于彼此的交流。”

  8. 08

    The directive is meant to encourage “stepping away from the digital and being present in the moment,” said Justine Masters, who owns The Edge with her sister Juliet.

    贾斯汀·马斯特（Justine Masters）表示，这一指引的初衷是鼓励人们“暂时远离数字世界，活在当下这一刻”。The Edge由她和姐妹朱丽叶（Juliet）共同经营。

    directive
    明确的指示；在这里指酒吧告示上请顾客放下屏幕、彼此交流的要求。

文化遗产、艺术与跨文化

Each ARTS FIRST festival is a unique annual celebration of the Harvard community's artistic creativity. We invite you to join us for the coming ARTS FIRST. We look forward to welcoming you as we showcase the creativity of the Harvard arts community through performances, art exhibitions and art-making activities. The festival is a public event for Harvard and community members of all ages.

Light Awash in Watercolor

Learn about the materials and qualities of watercolor paint with experts from the Harvard Art Museums Materials Lab. Try your hand at some of the painting tricks used by artists whose works will be in the upcoming exhibition of American Watercolors,1880-1990:Into the Light.

Spineless Artists:Invertebrate Creativity

From webs to cocoons, invertebrates(无脊椎动物) create some of nature's most delicate and beautiful designs. Join Javier Marin from the Harvard Museum of Natural History to learn how insects and other invertebrates dance, inspire fashion and create art, while making your own spineless artists out of craft (手工艺) materials.

Wheel Throwing

Join instructors from the Ceramics Program and great potters from Quincy, Cabot and Mather Houses for demonstrations using the potter's wheel. Then create your own masterpiece!

Knitting and Pom-Pom Making

Join the Harvard Undergraduate Knitting Circle to make pom-poms and tassels out of thread, or pick up a pair of needles and learn to knit(编织).

1What do we know about ARTS FIRST?

  • A.It is an exhibition of oil paintings.
  • B.It offers art courses for all ages.
  • C.It presents recreational activities.
  • D.It is a major tourist attraction.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C　细节理解题。根据题干关键词ARTS FIRST可定位到第一段。根据第三句“...as we showcase the creativity of the Harvard arts community through performances, art exhibitions and art-making activities.”可知,ARTS FIRST将会展现一系列娱乐活动(recreational activities)。故选C。

2Which program will you join if you're interested in drawing pictures?

  • A.Light Awash in Watercolor.
  • B.Spineless Artists:Invertebrate Creativity.
  • C.Wheel Throwing.
  • D.Knitting and Pom-Pom Making.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2. A　细节理解题。根据Light Awash in Watercolor部分中的“Learn about the materials and qualities of watercolor paint”以及“Try your hand at some of the painting tricks”可知,此项目与绘画有关。故选A。

3What can you do together with Javier Marin?

  • A.Practice a traditional dance.
  • B.Make handcrafts.
  • C.Visit a local museum.
  • D.Feed invertebrates.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3. B　细节理解题。 根据题干关键词Javier Marin可以定位到Spineless Artists: Invertebrate Creativity部分第二句。根据 “Join Javier Marin...inspire fashion and create art, while making your own spineless artists out of craft materials.”可知,可以和Javier Marin一起做手工艺品(handcrafts)。故选B。

外刊延伸

精选｜电影榜单 = 人生整理清单？试试看！

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    What did you rank as your top movies of the 21st century? I struggled to determine how I would rank a movie as one of "the best." Was it one that left me astonished when I saw it? One that stayed with me long after watching? Or should I choose films that somehow felt important in the history of cinema?

    评选21世纪最佳电影，你选了哪几部呢？在决定要把哪些电影排进我的“最佳”榜单时，我非常纠结。是选那种看完后让我惊叹不已的？还是那种看完后让我久久不能忘怀的？又或者，该选那种在电影史上具有重要意义的作品？

    rank
    v. 排名，排序
    struggle to determine
    挣扎着做决定
  2. 02

    What is the purpose of a list ranking "the best" of something, anyway? Is it to establish a canon, a definitive record, etched in stone? Is it to inspire questions and conversations and arguments about what makes something good?

    给某样事物排出一张“最佳”列表，意义到底是什么？是为了确立一套权威准则，打造一份盖棺定论、不容修改的记录吗？还是说，是为了引发人们对“何为优秀”的追问、探讨与争论？

    canon
    n. 经典作品集，标杆式的作品；某一文学艺术门类中的正典；标准、权威准则
  3. 03

    I love the way a big list forces me to question and define my tastes, to consider what I like and don't and why, to sharpen my critical takes against those of others. But the best part of engaging with the films of the 21st century is how the list prompted a cascade of memories of the past 25 years. I remember the exact theater in which I saw "Y Tu Mamá También" in 2002, the friends I was with, where we ate afterward. That restaurant is definitely not there anymore.

    我喜欢一个长长的榜单会促使我去做的事，让我去审视和定义自己的品味，让我认真思考自己喜欢什么、不喜欢什么，以及原因何在；还能让我在与他人观点的碰撞中，打磨自己的判断力。对21世纪的电影做一次认真回顾，最妙的地方在于，这份榜单勾起了我对过去25年的一连串回忆。我清楚地记得2002年看《你妈妈也一样》时所在的电影院，记得当时一起看电影的朋友，也记得我们看完电影后去吃饭的地方。那家餐厅现在肯定已经不在了。

    prompt
    v. 激起
  4. 04

    The objective quality of a film is fun to debate, but it's a lovely sort of ecstasy to think back over one's quarter-century of movie-watching experiences, to use those movies to populate a memory palace. The film is just the catalyst for a million other reminiscences.

    客观探讨一部电影的质量好坏固然有趣，但回想自己过去25年的观影经历，用那些电影搭建起一座记忆宫殿，则更令人心醉神迷。我们记忆中的电影就是一种催化剂，可以激活无数电影之外的回忆。

    ecstasy
    n. 狂喜
    memory palace
    记忆宫殿（一种帮助记忆大量信息的技巧，通过将要记住的内容“放置”在想象中的房间或路径上来加深记忆。）
  5. 05

    Making a list of the movies you loved over the past 25 years is a way of organizing those years, a kind of post-factum diary. If you were to riff on each of your top 10 movies, what long-forgotten details from your history might be dislodged? We're forever cramming our brains with more information. Take these 10 movies and use them to sift through some of the accumulated sediment, to make order out of the chaos.

    做一份片单，列出过去25年间你最爱的电影，这么做其实是在整理岁月，相当于一本事后补写的日记。如果要你展开讲讲自己心目中的十佳电影，你的人生中，有哪些早已被遗忘的往事细节，会被重新勾起呢？我们总是在不停地往脑子里塞更多信息。不妨把这10部电影当作一把筛子，把多年来堆在你脑子里的沉淀物都筛一遍，从混乱之中理出秩序。

    post-factum
    在事情发生之后（拉丁语表示“after the fact”）
    dislodge
    v. 让……松动，取出、移动……

Writing Competitions 2025

The Caledonia Novel Award

The award is for the best novel manuscript(原稿). All entries must be original manuscripts at least 50,000 words long by unpublished writers.

The first prize is ￡l,500. There is a special prize of a free place on a creative writing course at Moniack Mhor Centre, the sponsor of the competition.

The entry fee is ￡28. A number of sponsored places are available for writers on low incomes.

The closing date is 15 October.

Website: https://thecnaward.com/submit

The Deborah Rogers Foundation

Writers Award

The DRF Writers Award invites submissions of 15,000-20,000 words of a work in progress, which may be fiction or non-fiction.

Writers must not previously have published any work.

The winner will receive ￡10,000 and two shortlisted writers will receive ￡1,000.

The closing date is 31 May.

Website: https://www.drf.org/enter

Imagine 2100 Climate Fiction Competition

Writers are invited to “envision(展望) a future where humanity overcomes the climate crisis and builds a more sustainable world.” Your story can be set sometime between the near future and the year 2100.

Stories should be unpublished, between 2,500-5,000 words. Entry is free, with the first prize of ￡3,000.

The winning stories will be published on the competition's website.

The deadline is 24 June.

Website: https://climatefiction.org/submit

New Writers Flash Fiction Competition

New Writers is inviting entries of short fiction on any theme up to 300 words.

The first prize is ￡1,100. The winning entries will be published on the New Writers website.

All entries must be original and unpublished.

There is an entry fee of ￡10. ￡1 from each entry will be donated to First Story, the creative writing charity for young people. A limited number of free entries are available for low-income writers.

The closing date is 31 March.

Website: https://newwriters.net/compete

全部题目与逐题讲解

1How many words are required for the entries to the competition sponsored by Moniack Mhor Centre?

  • A.Up to 300.
  • B.2,500 to 5,000.
  • C.15,000 to 20,000.
  • D.At least 50,000.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

1. D　细节理解题。根据题干关键词 words are required for the entries和Moniack Mhor Centre定位至第一个比赛信息中的“All entries must be original manuscripts at least 50,000 words long”,故选D。

2The DRF Writers Award differs from the other competitions in that _____ .

  • A.it awards money prizes
  • B.it offers a free writing course
  • C.it accepts non-fiction entries
  • D.it publishes winning stories online
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2. C　细节理解题。对比四个比赛的信息,由“for the best novel manuscript”“which may be fiction or non-fiction”“envision a future”和“inviting entries of short fiction”可知,DRF Writers Award的不同之处在于它接受非虚构作品。故选C。

3Which competition involves donation to young writers?

  • A.The Caledonia Novel Award.
  • B.New Writers Flash Fiction Competition.
  • C.Imagine 2100 Climate Fiction Competition.
  • D.The Deborah Rogers Foundation Writers Award.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3. B　细节理解题。根据题干关键词donation to young writers可定位至New Writers Flash Fiction Competition部分第四段的will be donated to First Story, the creative writing charity for young people,故选B。

4What do the four competitions have in common?

  • A.Entries must not be previously published.
  • B.They all fund low-income writers.
  • C.All competitions are entry free.
  • D.Participants should write on particular themes.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4. A　细节理解题。根据四个比赛要求中的“original manuscripts...by unpublished writers”“must not previously have published any work”“Stories should be unpublished”和“All entries must be original and unpublished.”可知,四个比赛的共同之处在于参赛作品不得被公开发表过。故选A。

为什么有人天生合得来？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The peculiar alchemy that causes us to click with some people and not with others—romantically or platonically—has been a preoccupation of poets for centuries, but it has only recently drawn the attention of scientists.

    这种让我们与某些人一拍即合，但与另一些人无缘的奇妙化学反应——无论是爱情还是友情——几个世纪以来一直是诗人津津乐道的话题，但直到最近才引起科学家的注意。

    peculiar
    adj. 奇怪的，独特的，难以言喻的
    platonically
    adv. 纯友谊地（不涉及浪漫关系，源自柏拉图《会饮篇》）
    preoccupation
    n. 执念、心头大事、萦绕心头的事（pre-提前 + occupy占据）
    alchemy
    n. 炼金术，比喻神秘的转化力量（能把A变成B）

    句子结构

    The peculiar alchemy that causes us to click with some people and not with others—romantically or platonically—has been a preoccupation of poets for centuries, / but it has only recently drawn the attention of scientists.

    主句主语的中心词是 alchemy，that causes … 限定这种微妙作用，has been 才是主句谓语。but 后的 it 仍指它；for centuries 与 only recently 对照，区分诗人的长期关注和科学界较晚的兴趣。

  2. 02

    During meaningful conversations, there is an associated syncing, or coupling, of neural patterns or brain waves. You and the other person are, at that moment, of like minds. Neuroscientists call such coordinated movement and autonomic activity “interpersonal synchrony.” You can think of it as a kind of Spidey sense that flags whether someone is friend or foe.

    在有意义的对话中，神经模式或脑电波会出现同步或耦合。在那一刻，你和对方仿佛心有灵犀。神经科学家将这种协调的动作和自主神经活动称为“人际同步”。你可以把它想象成一种蜘蛛侠式的直觉，能够分辨敌友。

  3. 03

    By subconsciously mirroring even the subtlest twitches of expression and biological rhythms of other people, we can channel their thoughts and feelings. The downside is that we can sometimes get so swept up in other people’s drama, anger or fear that we lose sight of where others end and we begin.

    通过潜意识地模仿他人——哪怕是其最细微的表情变化和生理节律，我们能够对他们的想法和感受感同身受。但缺点是，我们有时会被他人的际遇、愤怒或恐惧所裹挟，以至于分不清哪些是别人的，哪些是自己的。

    subconsciously
    adv. 潜意识地
  4. 04

    Given the dynamic, subconscious and autonomic nature of interpersonal synchrony, we should all probably give ourselves a break if we cannot claim to like everyone we meet. While we should all try to be courteous and get along, part of maturity is realizing that you won’t click with everyone.

    鉴于人际同步这种多变、潜意识和自主发生的特性，如果我们无法声称对遇到的每个人都产生好感，或许应该放过自己。虽然我们理应努力保持礼貌、与人好好相处，但成熟的标志之一，是意识到你不可能与每个人都合得来。

    courteous
    adj. 有礼貌的、体贴的
  5. 05

    Sometimes we need to disconnect to recalibrate and reclaim ourselves. The relationships that endure, however, are the ones where you are in sync more than you are not.

    有时，我们需要断开连接，重新调整与找回自我。而那些能够长久维系的关系，往往同频的时候更多、出现分歧的时候更少。

    reclaim
    v. 收回、夺回
    recalibrate
    v. 重新校准、调整

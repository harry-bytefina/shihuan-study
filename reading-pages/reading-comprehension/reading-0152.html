The Biggest Stadiums in the World

People have been pouring into stadiums since the days of ancient Greece. In around 80 A.D., the Romans built the Colosseum, which remains the world's best known stadium and continues to inform contemporary design. Rome's Colosseum was 157 feet tall and had 80 entrances, seating 50,000 people. However, that was small fry compared with the city's Circus Maximus, which accommodated around 250,000 people.

These days, safety regulations—not to mention the modern sports fan's desire for a good view and a comfortable seat—tend to keep stadium capacities(容量) slightly lower. Even soccer fans tend to have a seat each; gone are the days of thousands standing to watch the match.

For the biggest stadiums in the world, we have used data supplied by the World Atlas list so far, which ranks them by their stated permanent capacity, as well as updated information from official stadium websites.

All these stadiums are still functional, still open and still hosting the biggest events in world sport.

·Rungrado 1st of May Stadium,Pyongyang, D.P.R. Korea. Capacity:150,000. Opened: May 1, 1989.

·Michigan Stadium,Ann Arbor,Michigan,U.S. Capacity:107,601. Opened:October 1, 1927.

·Beaver Stadium, State College, Pennsylvania, U.S. Capacity:106,572. Opened:September 17, 1960.

·Ohio Stadium, Columbus, Ohio,U.S. Capacity:104,944. Opened:October 7,1922.

·Kyle Field, College Station, Texas,U.S. Capacity:102,512. Opened:September 24,1927.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1How many people could the Circus Maximus hold?

  • A.104,944.
  • B.107,601.
  • C.About 150,000.
  • D.About 250,000.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

1.D　细节理解题。根据题干关键词Circus Maximus定位到首段最后一句话“...Circus Maximus, which accommodated around 250,000 people.”,因此选择D项。

2Of the following stadiums, which is the oldest?

  • A.Michigan Stadium.
  • B.Beaver Stadium.
  • C.Ohio Stadium.
  • D.Kyle Field.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　细节理解题。将文章最后一部分的信息进行对比,Michigan Stadium:1927年10月1日;Beaver Stadium:1960年9月17日;Ohio Stadium:1922年10月7日;Kyle Field:1927年9月24日。故选择C项。

3What do the listed stadiums have in common?

  • A.They host big games.
  • B.They have become tourist attractions.
  • C.They were built by Americans.
  • D.They are favored by architects.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

3.A　细节理解题。文章最后一部分在列出体育场信息之前提到“All these stadiums are still functional, still open and still hosting the biggest events in world sport.”,故答案为A项。

阅读难度参考：CSE 6

那些读几页就想放弃的书，到底该怎么啃下来？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Secret to Getting Through Big, Dense, Difficult Books

    读书秘诀：如何攻克晦涩难懂的大部头

    dense
    adj. 信息量过大过密的，难以理解的，读不下去的
  2. 02

    Late last spring I went through some personal troubles and felt I was in need of spiritual assistance. Unfortunately, I am perfectly secular, so I knew this would have to come in the form of literature. I wanted a book that could offer me advice about how to, well, live the rest of my life forever meaningfully.

    去年暮春，我遭遇了一些个人困境，感觉需要一些精神上的慰藉。可惜我是个完全世俗的人，所以我深知这种慰藉只能从文学中寻觅。我想要这么一本书，它能告诉我该如何有意义地度过余生。

    secular
    adj. 不信教的
  3. 03

    I had long wanted to read "The Ethics," Spinoza's magnum opus, and thought it was finally time to be consoled by him. The problem was that the book has a reputation for being abstruse.

    我一直都很想读斯宾诺莎的代表巨著《伦理学》，当时觉得终于是时候从他那儿获得慰藉了。但问题是，这本书出了名的深奥难懂。

    console
    v. 安抚，慰藉
    abstruse
    adj. 艰深难懂的
  4. 04

    So I posted on my social media asking friends if they would like to join me in reading the book together. To my surprise, nearly 40 people responded. I drafted a "syllabus" that spanned 10 weeks, and we were off. Nearly half dropped out, but the rest remained committed: We met every Sunday on Zoom, often entering our meetings puzzled by Spinoza's declarations. But after an hour of discussion, we'd leave with a little more clarity. Our collective mood by the time we arrived at the book's final chapter was nothing short of ebullient.

    于是我在社交媒体上发帖问朋友们，有没有人愿意跟我一起读这本书，结果居然有将近四十个人回复。我拟定了一份为期十周的“课程大纲”，然后我们就读起来了。有将近半数的人中途退出，但剩下的人都坚持了下来。我们每周日在Zoom上见面，每次开场时都对斯宾诺莎的论述一头雾水。但经过一小时的讨论，离场时我们总会多一分豁然开朗。读到最后一章时，我们所有人的心情都无比欢欣鼓舞。

    syllabus
    n. 课程大纲，课表
    span
    v. 持续（一段时间）
    ebullient
    adj. 兴高采烈的，情绪高涨的
  5. 05

    Why are we doing this to ourselves? None of us are academic philosophers; we have busy jobs and other pressing responsibilities. But the process has proved fruitful. Learning is both painful and pleasant — and above all, communal.

    我们为什么要给自己找罪受呢？毕竟我们没有一个人是专门研究哲学的学者，大家都有繁忙的工作和其它紧迫的责任。可事实证明，这个过程收获满满。学习既有艰辛也有乐趣——而最重要的是，它是一种集体行为。

  6. 06

    I like easy things too. But I've found the world has incrementally foisted upon me a preponderance of quick and simple easiness. And reading a difficult book is not going to change that, or anything. Still, for at least a few hours a week, I have a chance to dedicate myself, among friends, toward material that requires sincere mental devotion, and I feel the satisfying kind of exhaustion.

    我也喜欢轻松的事物。但我发现，这个世界渐渐把大量快捷简单的“轻松”强加给了我。而读一本难懂的书并不会改变这一点，或是改变任何事情。不过，每周至少有几个小时，我得以和朋友们一起，全身心投入到需要真正专注思考的内容中，而随之而来的疲惫感，是如此令人心满意足。

    incrementally
    adv. 逐步递增地
  7. 07

    If the road I have pointed out ... seems very difficult, yet it can be found. Indeed, what is so rarely discovered is bound to be hard. ... All things excellent are as difficult as they are rare.

    我指出的这条路...看似艰难，但也并非遥不可及。诚然，鲜为人知的道路必然充满艰险。... 但凡是美好卓绝的事物，它的艰难程度，也唯有它的珍稀程度能与之匹敌。

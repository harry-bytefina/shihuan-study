Welcome to Holker Hall & Gardens

Visitor Information

How to Get to Holker

By Car:Follow brown signs on A590 from J36, M6. Approximate travel times:Windermere—20 minutes, Kendal—25 minutes, Lancaster—45 minutes, Manchester—1 hour 30 minutes.

By Rail: The nearest station is Cark⁃in⁃Cartmel with trains to Carnforth, Lancaster and Preston for connections to major cities & airports.

Opening Times

Sunday-Friday(closed on Saturday)11:00 am-4:00 pm, 30th March-2nd November.

Admission Charges

Hall & Gardens　　　Gardens

Adults:　　　　　￡12.00　　　　　￡8.00

Groups:　　　　　￡9.00　　　　　￡5.50

Special Events

Producers' Market　13th April

Join us to taste a variety of fresh local food and drinks. Meet the producers and get some excellent recipe ideas.

Holker Garden Festival　30th May

The event celebrates its 22nd anniversary with a great show of the very best of gardening, making it one of the most popular events in gardening.

National Garden Day　28th August

Holker once again opens its gardens in aid of the disadvantaged. For just a small donation you can take a tour with our garden guide.

Winter Market　8th November

This is an event for all the family. Wander among a variety of shops selling gifts while enjoying a live music show and nice street entertainment.

那些又土又没用的纪念品，为什么成了我们旅行的灵魂？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    I Confessed My Tacky Souvenir Habit. Readers Told Me I'm in Good Company.

    我承认了自己购买俗气纪念品的习惯。读者们告诉我，我不是一个人。

  2. 02

    No matter your travel budget, it turns out, there's something deeply satisfying about bringing home a trinket or two in addition to a gazillion photos. All the better if you make a game out of the souvenir hunt or turn your top tchotchkes into home decor.

    无论你的旅行预算是多是少，事实证明，除了带回来海量照片之外，如果能再带回一两件小纪念品，总会让人有种深深的满足感。要是你能把搜寻纪念品当成一场游戏，或者把你最心爱的小物件变成家居装饰，那就更好不过了。

    trinket
    便宜的小摆件或饰物；在这里指旅行者带回来的小纪念品。
    gazillion
    多得数不清；a gazillion photos 是口语夸张，作者说旅行照片已经多得不得了。
    tchotchkes
    零碎小摆设；美式口语中常用来称所收集的廉价小物件。
  3. 03

    Retired attorney David Powell calls his magnets-on-sheet-metal collection the Museum of Magnet Art and says it's about to get a new wing in his Florida home.

    退休律师戴维·鲍威尔（David Powell）把他收集在金属板上的冰箱贴称为“磁铁艺术博物馆”，并表示他即将在位于佛罗里达州的家中增设一个新展区。

  4. 04

    Anne Laband brings back a little something from every trip and turns it into a Christmas ornament. There's a friendship bracelet from a volunteer trip to Haiti, a horseshoe found on a Grand Canyon trail and antique lace from a market in Bruges, Belgium. The collection has grown so large, the souvenirs earned their own tree.

    安妮·拉班德（Anne Laband）每次旅行都会带点小东西回来，然后把它变成圣诞树挂饰。去海地做志愿者时她带回过友谊手链，在大峡谷的步道上她捡到过马蹄铁，在比利时布鲁日的市场她淘到过古董蕾丝。她的收藏越积越多，最后这些纪念品干脆拥有了自己专属的圣诞树。

    ornament
    装饰品；a Christmas ornament 指挂在圣诞树上的小物件。
  5. 05

    "Every item on the tree carries a story. Most are happy, some more sobering," the Alabama woman says. "Pick one—I'll tell you about it!" The souvenir displays often double as conversation starters.

    “树上的每件饰品都承载着一个故事。大部分是开心的，也有一些更加发人深省，”这位阿拉巴马州的女士说道。“随便挑一件——我都能有故事讲给你！”这些纪念品布置往往还能开启一场交谈。

    sobering
    令人严肃起来、发人深省的；形容纪念品背后的某些故事不轻松。
  6. 06

    Souvenir hunting is straightforward—unless you have rules. Some travelers set dollar limits like $5 or $10. Others get more specific. Melissa Green and her husband, instituted the TUT policy. The trinket must be tacky, useless and tasteless, said Green.

    搜寻纪念品本来很简单——除非你自己定下规则。有些旅行者会设定价格上限，比如5美元或10美元。有些人的规则更具体。梅丽莎·格林（Melissa Green）和她的丈夫制定了“TUT”原则。格林说，纪念品必须符合三个标准：俗气、无用且没品位。

    instituted
    设立并开始实行；旅行者给自己定下挑纪念品的 TUT 规则。
  7. 07

    Souvenirs only make the cut for Californian Rob Singler and his family if they meet three criteria: "godawful" embarrassing, tiny and bearing the name of the vacation spot.

    对于加州人罗伯·辛格勒（Rob Singler）和他的家人来说，一件纪念品要想入选，必须满足三个标准：“极其”尴尬、体积小巧，并且印有度假地的名称。

    criteria
    评判标准；three criteria 指三个筛选条件。单数是 criterion。
  8. 08

    Souvenir collecting is often a solo sport, with spouses and other travel partners tolerating it or quietly culling the collection when no one's looking.

    收集纪念品往往是一项“单人运动”，对于收藏品，配偶或其他旅行伙伴要么忍耐包容，要么趁没人注意时悄悄清理掉一部分。

    culling
    通过挑选而淘汰一部分；同伴会悄悄把一些纪念品从收藏里清掉。

03-2024全国甲，C

睡眠、运动与身心健康

The Saint Lukas train doesn't accept passengers—it accepts only the sick. The Saint Lukas is one of five government-sponsored medical trains that travel to remote towns in central and eastern Russia. Each stop lasts an average of two days, and during that time the doctors and nurses on board provide rural(乡村的) populations with basic medical care, X-ray scans and prescriptions.

“People started queuing to make an appointment early in the morning,” says Emile Ducke, a German photographer who traveled with the staff of the Saint Lukas for a two-week trip in November through the vast regions(区域) of Krasnoyarsk and Khakassia.

Russia's public health care service has been in serious need of modernization. The government has struggled to come up with measures to address the problem, particularly in the poorer, rural areas east of the Volga River, including arranging doctor's appointments by video chat and expanding financial aid programs to motivate doctors to practice medicine in remote parts of the country like Krasnoyarsk.

The annual arrival of the Saint Lukas is another attempt to improve the situation. For 10 months every year, the train stops at about eight stations over two weeks, before returning to the regional capital to refuel and restock(补给). Then it starts all over again the next month. Most stations wait about a year between visits.

Doctors see up to 150 patients every day. The train's equipment allows for basic checkups. “I was very impressed by the doctors and their assistants working and living in such little space but still staying focused and very concerned,” says Ducke. “They were the best chance for many rural people to get the treatment they want.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1How is the Saint Lukas different from other trains?

  • A.It runs across countries.
  • B.It reserves seats for the seniors.
  • C.It functions as a hospital.
  • D.It travels along a river.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C　细节理解题。根据第一段中的“The Saint Lukas train doesn't accept passengers—it accepts only the sick. The Saint Lukas is one of five government-sponsored medical trains”可知,圣卢卡斯列车是五列政府资助的医疗列车之一,不接纳乘客,只接纳病人。也就是说,它起医院的作用。故选C。function as...表示“起……作用”。

2What can we infer from paragraph 3 about Krasnoyarsk?

  • A.It is heavily populated.
  • B.It offers training for doctors.
  • C.It is a modern city.
  • D.It needs medical aid.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2. D　推理判断题。由第三段中的“Russia's public health care service has been in serious need of modernization...particularly in the poorer, rural areas...like Krasnoyarsk.”可知,克拉斯诺亚尔斯克地区急需医疗援助(medical aid)。故选D。

3How long can the Saint Lukas work with one supply?

  • A.About a year.
  • B.About ten months.
  • C.About two months.
  • D.About two weeks.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D　细节理解题。根据第四段第二句中“the train stops at about eight stations over two weeks, before returning to the regional capital to refuel and restock”可知,圣卢卡斯医疗列车在两周的时间里停靠约8个车站,然后要加油和进行补给。故选D。

4What is Ducke's attitude toward the Saint Lukas' services?

  • A.Appreciative.
  • B.Doubtful.
  • C.Ambiguous.
  • D.Cautious.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4. A　推理判断题,考查Ducke对圣卢卡斯列车服务的看法。根据最后一段中的“I was very impressed by the doctors and their assistants”以及“They were the best chance for many rural people to get the treatment they want.”可知,列车上的医生和助手给Ducke留下深刻印象,Ducke认为他们是很多希望得到治疗的乡村人民最好的机会。由此可推知,Ducke对圣卢卡斯列车服务是赞赏的(appreciative)。故选A。

每天带饭，40岁退休：揭秘火遍全球的“FIRE”攒钱退休法

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    We had packed lunches every day for 10 years and retired at 40

    我们每天带饭坚持了十年，然后在 40 岁退了休

  2. 02

    Every winter, Alan and Katie Donegan would avoid turning on the heating at their home in the south of England. "Instead, we wore extra layers and used hot water bottles—we turned it into a game," says Alan. "It wasn't suffering, it was strategy." While the couple admit that others thought they were "extreme" or "mad" to put so much emphasis on not spending money, Alan explains that they were "laser-focused on buying freedom".

    每年冬天，艾伦（Alan）和凯蒂·多尼根（Katie Donegan）都会尽量不开他们英格兰南部家里的暖气。“相反，我们会多穿几层衣服，用热水袋取暖——我们把它变成了一种游戏，”艾伦说，“这并不是一种煎熬，而是一种策略。”虽然这对夫妇知道，在别人眼中他们如此重视省钱是“极端”或“疯狂”的，但艾伦解释说，他们这是在“激光般地聚焦于购买自由”。

    laser-focused
    adj. 像激光一样聚焦目标的
    suffering
    n. 受难
  3. 03

    The two rarely had takeaways and always took packed lunches to work. "We were £40,000 better off over 10 years from just that one lunch habit," says Alan. "We even charged our phones while out and hunted for discarded Nectar vouchers. You can decide if that's crazy or genius, but it worked." They quit work after their savings hit £1m.

    两人很少点外卖，总是带饭上班。“光靠这个午餐带饭的习惯，10年来我们就多省下了四万英镑，”艾伦说。“我们甚至会在家外面给手机充电，还会四处寻找别人扔掉的Nectar优惠券。你可以自己判断这是疯狂还是天才之举，但确实奏效了。”当他们的积蓄达到100万英镑时，他们便辞去了工作。

    discarded
    adj. 被丢弃的，废弃的
    better off
    日子更宽裕、好过了
  4. 04

    Alan and Katie are part of a small but growing global movement called Fire, which stands for "Financially Independent, Retire Early". The central tenet is that you live extremely frugally during your working life, so that you can retire as soon as possible.

    艾伦和凯蒂是“Fire”这一规模虽小但正在不断壮大的全球运动中的一员，该运动的英文全称是“财务独立，提前退休”（Financially Independent, Retire Early）。其核心理念是：在工作期间过极其节俭的生活，以便尽早退休。

    frugally
    adv. 节俭地
    tenet
    n. 原则，准则
  5. 05

    For most of us, being able to quit working life early is just a dream. Last year, average retirement ages in the UK hit record highs of 65.8 years for men and 64.7 for women, official data showed.

    对我们大多数人来说，提前结束职业生涯，只是一个梦。官方数据显示，去年英国男性的平均退休年龄达到65.8岁，女性为64.7岁，均创下历史新高。

    hit record highs
    创下历史新高
  6. 06

    Carol Schleif is chief market strategist at BMO Private Wealth, a Toronto-based financial advisory business. "If you retire early but don't have friendships, health or a sense of purpose, you've achieved one goal but sacrificed other things…you have to wonder if it's worth it," says Schleif.

    卡罗尔·施莱夫（Carol Schleif）是总部位于多伦多的财务咨询公司“蒙特利尔银行私人财富”（BMO Private Wealth）的首席市场策略师。“如果你提前退休，却没有朋友、没有健康、也没有生活目标，那你虽然实现了一个目标，却牺牲了其他东西……你必须要思考一下这样做是否值得，”施莱夫说道。

  7. 07

    Within the Fire community, some are now following this less intense route, leading to the creation of sub-genres such as "Barista Fire". This focuses on saving enough money so that your investment income can cover most life expenses.

    在“Fire”社群中，现在有些人走上了一条不那么高强度的路线，由此催生了“咖啡师Fire”这样的细分流派。这一流派侧重于：存钱存到投资收入能覆盖你大部分生活开销的程度即可。

    sub-genre
    n. 亚型、细分流派

学习、阅读与教育方式

Pre-school children who spend time together take on one another's personalities, a new study has found.The study shows that environment plays a key role in shaping people's personalities.While genetics still forms the central part of the human psyche(心理), the research finds that personality traits(特征)are “contagious”(传染的)among children.“Our finding flies in the face of common assumptions that personality can't be changed,” said Dr. Jennifer Neal, co-author of the study.

The researchers studied the personalities and social networks of two pre-school classes for a full school year.One of the classes was a set of three-year-olds, and the other a set of four-year-olds.Children whose friends were hard-working or outgoing gradually took on these personality traits over time.Psychology expert Dr.Emily Dublin said kids are having a far bigger effect on each other than people may realize.

The new study is not the first to explore the contagious effects of personality traits.A 2015 psychology study found that rudeness at work can be contagious as it travels from person to person “like a disease”.The study found that seeing a boss being rude to an employee was enough to cause people to be rude to those around them.

The researchers questioned 6,000 people on the social “climate” in their workplaces, which included offices, hotels and restaurants.They found 75 percent of those who took part said they had been treated rudely at least once in the past year.And the study also suggests that merely seeing other people being subjected to rudeness made it more likely that a person would treat their colleagues in the same way.Rudeness could include leaving someone off an invite to a company event, sending unkind emails, finding fault with others or failing to give praise.

Dr.Torkelson believes companies need to be more aware of the harm that rudeness in the workplace can do as it can damage the working environment.She said better training could help to fight against the problem.

拒绝AI代工！全球掀起纯人工设计浪潮

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Is this product 'human-made'? The race to establish an AI-free logo

    这款产品是 “人类制造” 吗？一场打造无 AI 标识的竞赛

  2. 02

    Organisations worldwide are racing to develop a universally recognised label for "human-made" products and services as part of the growing backlash against AI use. Declarations like "Proudly Human", "Human-made", "No A.I" and "AI-free" are appearing across films, marketing, books and websites.

    全球各类机构正争相为 “人工制造” 的产品与服务研发一款获得普遍认可的标识，这也是社会各界对人工智能应用逐渐反感的表现之一。“人工制造为荣”“纯人力打造”“无人工智能参与”“不含 AI 技术” 等声明，开始出现在影视、营销物料、书籍与各类网站上。

    backlash
    强烈反弹或抵制；指有人反对 AI 介入创作，并推出纯人工标识。
  3. 03

    It is in response to fears that jobs or entire professions are being swept away in a wave of AI-powered automation.

    这一趋势源于人们的担忧：大量工作岗位乃至整个行业，正被人工智能驱动的自动化浪潮席卷取代。

  4. 04

    "AI is creating significant disruption and competing definitions of what is 'human made' are confusing consumers. A universal definition is essential to build trust, clarification and confidence," says consumer expert Dr Amna Khan.

    消费者专家阿姆娜・汗博士（Dr Amna Khan）表示：“人工智能正造成巨大的行业冲击，而‘人工制造’的定义众说纷纭，让消费者困惑不已。要建立信任、厘清概念、增强信心，统一的定义必不可少。”

    disruption
    对原有运行方式的冲击；这里说 AI 改变行业做事方式，也让消费者更难判断作品来源。
  5. 05

    But AI experts say that getting industries to agree what truly counts as "human made" will prove complicated as AI is integrated into so many everyday tools.

    但人工智能领域专家指出，由于 AI 已融入海量日常工具，想要让各行各业就 “真正意义上的人工制造” 达成共识，将会十分复杂。

    integrated
    融入、整合进；be integrated into everyday tools 指 AI 功能已嵌入许多日常工具。
  6. 06

    "From a technical perspective, it's hard to implement. I think that AI is a spectrum, and we need more comprehensive certification systems, rather than a binary with AI/AI-free approach," AI Research Scientist Sasha Luccioni said.

    “从技术角度来看，这一标准很难落地推行。我认为人工智能的应用是一个连续范围，我们需要更全面的认证体系，而非简单粗暴地划分‘含 AI / 不含 AI’二元标准。”人工智能研究科学家萨莎・卢乔尼（Sasha Luccioni）表示。

    spectrum
    一系列连续变化的程度；AI is a spectrum 指作品使用 AI 的程度有多种，而非简单二选一。
    binary
    二分法；a binary 在这里指把作品仅分为使用 AI 和完全不用 AI。
  7. 07

    In the book industry, Books by People charges publishers and requires them to carry out questionnaires about their practises and how they vet their authors. The company also checks samples of books periodically to check for AI writing.

    在图书行业，“Books by People” 机构向出版商收取费用，并要求他们填写问卷，说明自身运营模式以及审核作者的方式。该机构还会定期抽查书籍样本，检测是否存在人工智能代笔的情况。

    vet
    审核、查验；这里是动词，指出版社如何审核作者。
  8. 08

    In Australia, a competing company Proudly Human uses a similar but even more rigorous system. Their auditors carry out checks at every stage of publication, including checking any changes made from manuscript to ebook edition.

    澳大利亚一家竞争对手 “Proudly Human” 则采用了一套类似但更为严苛的体系。其审核人员会在出版的每个环节进行核查，包括核对从手稿到电子书版本的所有修改内容。

    manuscript
    未正式出版的稿件；from manuscript to ebook edition 写的是从原稿到电子书的过程。
    auditors
    负责核查标准是否得到遵守的人；此处核对出版过程有没有使用 AI。
  9. 09

    Company boss Alan Finkel says systems like his are vital because industry efforts to analyse and label content as being made with AI have failed. "A certification of 'human origin' is needed but self certification is not good enough so we have a full verification process to make sure that it’s truly human originated material," he said.

    该公司负责人艾伦・芬克尔（Alan Finkel）表示，这类体系至关重要，因为行业内试图分析内容并标注 AI 参与的尝试均以失败告终。“我们需要‘人类原创’认证，但自我认证并不具备说服力，因此我们建立了一套完整的核验流程，确保相关内容确为人类原创。”

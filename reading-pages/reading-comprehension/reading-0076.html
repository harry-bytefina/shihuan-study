A Guide to the University

Food

The TWU Cafeteria is open 7 am to 8 pm.It serves snacks(小吃),drinks,ice cream bars and meals.You can pay with cash or your ID cards.You can add meal money to your ID cards at the Front Desk.Even if you do not buy your food in the cafeteria,you can use the tables to eat your lunch,to have meetings and to study.

If you are on campus in the evening or late at night,you can buy snacks,fast food,and drinks in the Lower Café located in the bottom level of the Douglas Centre.This area is often used for entertainment such as concerts,games or TV watching.

Relaxation

The Globe,located in the bottom level of McMillan Hall,is available for relaxing,studying,cooking,and eating.Monthly activities are held here for all international students.Hours are 10 am to 10 pm,closed on Sundays.

Health

Located on the top floor of Douglas Hall,the Wellness Centre is committed to physical,emotional and social health.A doctor and nurse is available if you have health questions or need immediate medical help or personal advice.The cost of this is included in your medical insurance.Hours are Monday to Friday,9 am to noon and 1:00 to 4:30 pm.

Academic Support

All students have access to the Writing Centre on the upper floor of Douglas Hall.Here,qualified volunteers will work with you on written work,grammar,vocabulary,and other academic skills.You can sign up for an appointment on the sign-up sheet outside the door:two 30-minute appointments per week maximum.This service is free.

Transportation

The TWU Express is a shuttle(班车)service. The shuttle transports students between campus and the shopping centre, leaving from the Mattson Centre. Operation hours are between 9 am and 3 pm, Saturdays only. Round trip fare is $1.

阅读难度参考：CSE 6

哈佛教授的“友谊经济学”：为什么我们交不到朋友了

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The ferry crossing from Chappaquiddick to Edgartown, Mass., is only 527 feet long. But no matter how rich or important you are, you have to wait to get on the boat. Someone complains about the line, the weather, politics. Someone else laughs. A conversation begins.

    从马萨诸塞州查帕奎迪克（Chappaquiddick）到埃德加敦（Edgartown）的渡轮，航程只有527英尺。但是，无论你多有钱、多重要，你都得排队等船。有人抱怨队伍、抱怨天气、抱怨政治。然后有人笑起来。一段对话，就这样开始了。

  2. 02

    But spending time around that ferry has made me wonder if modern life has undersold inconvenience. We treat waiting as wasted time. But sometimes waiting does something useful: It forces people into the same place long enough for conversation to begin.

    但在渡轮那里待了一段时间后，我开始怀疑：现代生活是不是低估了"不便"的价值。我们总把等待当作浪费的时间。但有时候，等待也有它的用处：它强制人们在同一个地方待上足够长的时间，从而让一段对话得以开始。

  3. 03

    Loneliness has become one of the stranger problems of modern life. In 2023 the U.S. surgeon general called loneliness a public-health problem.

    孤独已成为现代生活中一个颇为奇特的问题。2023年，美国卫生总监将孤独定义为一个公共卫生问题。

  4. 04

    Which brings me back to that ridiculously inconvenient ferry and what I call the friction theory of friendship. Some inconveniences aren't merely costs. They are the hidden scaffolding of social life. If I had to estimate my own production function for friendship, it would look something like this: friendship = (proximity × repetition) + (idle time × low stakes) + shared irritation.

    由此我们可以说回到那艘极其不便的渡轮，以及我所谓的"友谊摩擦理论"。有些"不便"，并不只是代价。它们是社交生活隐秘的脚手架。如果要我估算自己的"友谊生产函数"，它看起来大概会是这个样子：友谊 =(距离近 × 重复遇见)+（闲暇时间×低利害情境）+共同的不满。

    proximity
    n. 接近，邻近
    irritation
    n. 烦躁、不满
    friction
    n. 生活中的不便，一切“不顺畅的阻力”
    repetition
    n. 重复
    idle
    adj. 空闲的；闲置的
  5. 05

    Proximity matters most when it recurs. Idle time matters most when nobody is trying to impress anyone. Shared irritation helps, but with diminishing returns: A delayed ferry creates conversation; a broken ferry creates rage.

    距离近，在反复出现时最起作用。闲暇，在没人刻意表现自己的时候，最有价值。共同的不满有帮助，但效用递减：延误的渡轮，催生交谈；坏掉的渡轮，只会制造愤怒。

    recur
    v. 再次发生、反复出现
    rage
    n. 失控的暴怒
  6. 06

    This is why friendship is often a product of something else: work, school, church, children, sports, errands, waiting rooms. Modern life has spent decades eliminating that friction. We can work without offices, shop without stores, exercise without gyms and communicate without looking anyone in the eye. Together they have made interaction with other people increasingly optional.

    正因如此，友谊往往是其他事物的副产品：工作、学校、教堂、孩子、体育运动、跑腿办事、候诊室等等。然而几十年来，现代生活一直在消除这种摩擦。我们可以不用办公室工作，不用实体店购物，不用健身房锻炼，也不用与人四目相对就能交流。这些便利加在一起，让人与人之间的交往，变得越来越可有可无。

    errand
    n. 跑腿，杂事
  7. 07

    The solution isn't to make life harder for its own sake. But we should ask what social infrastructure is being lost.

    解决之道，不是刻意把生活变难。但我们应该追问：我们正在失去什么样的社会基础设施。

  8. 08

    Schools, for example, aren't only human-capital institutions. They are social-capital institutions. Much of what we call "community" used to be produced by institutions as a byproduct.

    例如，学校不仅仅是人力资本机构。它还是社会资本机构。我们所说的"社区"，在很大程度上，曾经是各类机构无意之中生产的副产品。

  9. 09

    That is why I've come to love the Chappaquiddick ferry. It gives people nothing to do for a few minutes except notice one another.

    这就是为什么，我渐渐爱上了查帕奎迪克渡轮。在那几分钟里，人们除了互相打量之外，别无他事可做。

  10. 10

    Convenience has saved us time. The hidden price is that it has also saved us from one another.

    便利为我们节省了时间。但代价是，它也让我们丢失了彼此。

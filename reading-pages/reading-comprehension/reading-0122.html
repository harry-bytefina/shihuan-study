07-2021新高考Ⅱ，C

学习、阅读与教育方式

A British woman who won a $1 million prize after she was named the World's Best Teacher will use the cash to bring inspirational figures into UK schools.

Andria Zafirakou, a north London secondary school teacher, said she wanted to bring about a classroom revolution(变革). “We are going to make a change,” she said. “I've started a project to promote the teaching of the arts in our schools.”

The project results from the difficulties many schools have in getting artists of any sort—whether an up⁃and⁃coming local musician or a major movie star—into schools to work with and inspire children.

Zafirakou began the project at Alperton Community School, her place of work for the past twelve years. “I've seen those magic moments when children are talking to someone they are inspired by—their eyes are shining and their faces light up,” she said. “We need artists, more than ever in our schools.”

Artist Michael Craig⁃Martin said, “Andria's brilliant project to bring artists from all fields into direct contact with children is particularly welcome at a time when the arts are being downgraded in schools.” It was a mistake to see the arts as unnecessary, he added.

Historian Sir Simon Schama is also a supporter of the project. He said that arts education in schools was not just an add⁃on. “It is absolutely necessary. The future depends on creativity and creativity depends on the young. What will remain of us when artificial intelligence takes over will be our creativity, and it is our creative spirit, our visionary sense of freshness, that has been our strength for centuries.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What will Zafirakou do with her prize money?

  • A.Make a movie.
  • B.Build new schools.
  • C.Run a project.
  • D.Help local musicians.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1.C　细节理解题。根据第二段中she wanted to bring about a classroom revolution和I've started a project to promote the teaching of the arts in our schools.可知,Zafirakou打算用自己的奖金运行一个项目。

2What does Craig⁃Martin think of the teaching of the arts in UK schools?

  • A.It is particularly difficult.
  • B.It increases artists' income.
  • C.It opens children's mind.
  • D.It deserves greater attention.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　推理判断题。根据第五段中的“at a time when the arts are being downgraded in schools”和“It was a mistake to see arts as unnecessary”可知,Craig⁃Martin认为英国学校不够重视艺术,且认为艺术没有必要的观点是错的。由此可知他认为英国学校的艺术教育应受到更多关注。

3What should be stressed in school education according to Schama?

  • A.Moral principles.
  • B.Interpersonal skills.
  • C.Creative abilities.
  • D.Positive worldviews.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　细节理解题。根据文章最后一段中It is absolutely necessary. The future depends on creativity and creativity depends on the young.可知,Schama认为学校教育应该强调创造力。

4Which of the following is a suitable title for the text?

  • A.Bring Artists to Schools
  • B.When Historians Meet Artists
  • C.Arts Education in Britain
  • D.The World's Best Arts Teacher
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4.A　主旨要义题。文章讲述了Andria Zafirakou用自己的奖金启动了一个项目,将艺术家带进学校,促进艺术教学。

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Statistically speaking, you've probably already pressed a like button several times today. Perhaps you recently posted on Instagram or Facebook and have been eagerly checking as the like count rises on your phone. But why do we like the like button so much?

    从统计数据来看，你今天可能已经点了好几次赞了。也许你最近在照片墙（Instagram）或脸书（ Facebook）上发了帖，然后一直在手机上急切地查看点赞数的增长情况。但我们为什么这么喜欢“点赞”按钮呢？

  2. 02

    According to Nicholas Christakis, a sociologist and physician at Yale University, it is because "the like button is built, in a very deep and distant way, on the back of evolutionary biology."

    耶鲁大学社会学家兼医师尼古拉斯·克里斯塔基斯（Nicholas Christakis）认为，这是因为“点赞按钮的设计其实是利用了进化生物学原理，二者联系极为深远。”

  3. 03

    All animals learn and evolve as they interact with a challenging world, but only certain primates have the ability to learn from the experience of other individuals. "Social learning" conveyed immense evolutionary benefits, allowing early hominids to raise their chances of survival by avoiding the mistakes they saw others making, and emulating the successful moves.

    在与这个充满挑战的世界互动时，所有动物都会学习和进化，但只有某些灵长类动物具备从同类经验中学习的能力。“社会化学习”带来了巨大的进化优势，让早期原始人通过观察别人的失败来避免犯错，并模仿那些成功的做法，从而提高生存几率。

  4. 04

    There are other corollaries to an evolved social learning capacity. They include our habits of expressing gratitude and acknowledgment—essentially saying "thank you for letting me learn from you." Because we are happy to get pointers about threats and to gain opportunities, we receive knowledge in a fashion that will encourage more of it to come our way. In turn, we as givers respond to that flattering affirmation. It's what keeps us sharing what we discover.

    进化而来的社会化学习能力还衍生了其他产物。其中包括我们表达感激与认可的习惯——本质上就是在说“感谢你让我向你学习”。由于我们乐于获取风险提示和机遇信息，就会以一种能够鼓励更多知识传递给我们的方式来接收知识。反过来，作为分享者，我们会对这种讨人喜欢的肯定做出回应。正是这种机制促使我们不断分享自己的发现。

  5. 05

    All this explains why we have evolved to get such a buzz from liking and being liked. It's just not possible for something to become so popular without connecting with something fundamental in our biology.

    这一切都解释了为何我们会通过进化，对“点赞”和“被点赞”产生如此强烈的兴奋感。如果某样事物没有触及我们生物本能中的根本之处，它就不可能如此受欢迎。

动物行为与动物认知

When the explorers first set foot upon the continent of North America, the skies and lands were alive with an astonishing variety of wildlife. Native Americans had taken care of these precious natural resources wisely. Unfortunately, it took the explorers and the settlers who followed only a few decades to decimate a large part of these resources. Millions of waterfowl（水禽） were killed at the hands of market hunters and a handful of overly ambitious sportsmen. Millions of acres of wetlands were dried to feed and house the ever-increasing populations, greatly reducing waterfowl habitat (栖息地).

In 1934，with the passage of the Migratory Bird Hunting Stamp Act (Act), an increasingly concerned nation took firm action to stop the destruction of migratory(迁徙的)waterfowl and the wetlands so vital to their survival. Under this Act, all waterfowl hunters 16 years of age and over must annually purchase and carry a Federal Duck Stamp. The very first Federal Duck Stamp was designed by J.N. “Ding” Darling, a political cartoonist from Des Moines, Iowa, who at that time was appointed by President Franklin Roosevelt as Director of the Bureau of Biological Survey. Hunters willingly pay the stamp price to ensure the survival of our natural resources.

About 98 cents of every duck stamp dollar goes directly into the Migratory Bird Conservation Fund to purchase wetlands and wildlife habitat for inclusion into the National Wildlife Refuge System—a fact that ensures this land will be protected and available for all generations to come. Since 1934, better than half a billion dollars has gone into that Fund to purchase more than 5 million acres of habitat. Little wonder the Federal Duck Stamp Program has been called one of the most successful conservation programs ever initiated.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What was a cause of the waterfowl population decline in North America?

  • A.Loss of wetlands.
  • B.Popularity of water sports.
  • C.Pollution of rivers.
  • D.Arrival of other wild animals.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1. A细节理解题。根据文章第一段最后两句“...Millions of acres of wetlands were dried to feed and house the ever-increasing populations, greatly reducing waterfowl habitat.”可知湿地损失是水禽数量下降的一个原因。

2What does the underlined word “decimate” mean in the first paragraph?

  • A.Acquire.
  • B.Export.
  • C.Destroy.
  • D.Distribute.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2. C猜测词义题。根据画线词上句（美洲土著居民聪明地保护这些宝贵的自然资源）和画线词所在句中的“Unfortunately”及画线词下句中的“were killed”可推知画线词是“破坏”的意思，故答案为C项。

3What is a direct result of the Act passed in 1934?

  • A.The stamp price has gone down.
  • B.The migratory birds have flown away.
  • C.The hunters have stopped hunting.
  • D.The government has collected money.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D推理判断题。根据第二段第二句（根据该法案，所有16岁及以上的水禽狩猎者每年必须购买并携带一张联邦鸭票）可知这个法案使联邦政府收取了一些钱款，故答案为D项。

4Which of the following is a suitable title for the text?

  • A.The Federal Duck Stamp Story
  • B.The National Wildlife Refuge System
  • C.The Benefits of Saving Waterfowl
  • D.The History of Migratory Bird Hunting
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4. A主旨要义题。主旨要义题一般要锁定文章的开头和结尾部分，本文第一段是介绍背景，故答案区域应该在最后一段。由文章最后一句“Little wonder the Federal Duck Stamp Program has been called one of the most successful conservation programs ever initiated.”可知文章标题应该与the Federal Duck Stamp有关，故答案为A项。 长难句 Millions of acres of wetlands were dried to feed and house the everincreasing populations, greatly reducing waterfowl habitat. 译文 数百万英亩的湿地被弄干，以养活和容纳不断增加的人口，大大减少了水禽的栖息地。 分析 本句是一个简单句。不定式短语to feed and house the everincreasing populations作目的状语。现在分词短语greatly reducing waterfowl habitat作结果状语。

爱与账单：治愈我们的“毛孩子”，带来了多少意外开销？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Unexpected Cost of Pet Care Has Young Adults Rethinking Their Finances

    养宠的意外开销，促使年轻人重新盘算自己的财务

  2. 02

    When we found blood in our cat Gumdrop's stool, my boyfriend and I rushed him to the vet. The doctor couldn't tell us what was wrong, but he said an $800 ultrasound might give us more information.

    当我们发现猫咪 Gumdrop 的粪便里有血时，我和男友赶紧带它去看兽医。医生说不清它究竟出了什么问题，但表示，花 800 美元做一次超声检查，或许能获得更多信息。

    ultrasound
    n. 超声波检查（B超）
    vet
    n. 兽医（veterinarian 的简称）
  3. 03

    I couldn't decide which was more ridiculous: that pet care could actually be this expensive or that we were considering paying the $800.

    我一时说不上哪件事更荒谬：养宠物竟然能贵到这个程度，还是我们居然真的准备为这800美元买单。

  4. 04

    Turns out, a growing number of young adults are just like us, bringing home furry friends to then be shocked by the cost of caring for them. Households spend an average of about $1,700 annually on their pets, according to the American Veterinary Medical Association.

    事实证明，越来越多的年轻人和我们一样：把毛孩子带回家后才发现，宠物护理的成本高的惊人。据美国兽医协会统计，美国家庭平均每年会在宠物身上花费约 1700 美元。

    household
    n. 家庭（基本的社会经济与人口统计单位）
  5. 05

    Ashley Bleckner, a wealth adviser, says incorporating pet care into a younger person's limited budget means your emergency fund should be expanded to include your pet's emergencies.

    财富顾问阿什莉·布莱克纳（Ashley Bleckner）说，把宠物护理纳入年轻人本就有限的预算，也意味着紧急备用金应该相应增加，把宠物的突发状况也考虑进去。

    incorporate
    将……作为组成要素纳入
  6. 06

    Pet insurance is one way to mitigate some costs, says Bleckner. But she adds that policies typically have limits and exemptions and usually only cover reimbursements, meaning pet owners will still need to cover an expense upfront.

    布莱克纳说，宠物保险是减轻部分开支的一种方式。但她补充道，保单通常设有保额上限和免责条款，而且大多只提供事后报销；这意味着，宠物主人仍然需要先自行垫付费用。

    mitigate
    v. 减轻，缓和（风险、损失、伤害等负面因素）
    exemptions
    n. 除外责任，免责条款
    reimbursement
    n. 报销
    upfront
    adv. 预先，事前
  7. 07

    There are other indirect costs of pet ownership that particularly impact younger adults. My friend Arran recently got a new job, but he hasn’t found a potential roommate who is OK living with two cats. He’s currently searching for a one-bedroom apartment instead, and expanding his rent budget because of it.

    养宠还有一些间接成本，对年轻人的影响尤其明显。我的朋友 Arran 最近刚找到一份新工作，但他一直找不到愿意和两只猫同住的室友。如今，他只能去找一居室公寓，也不得不因此提高自己的租房预算。

  8. 08

    So as my friends and I work through the shorter-term strain pets are having on our spending, we can take solace in some longer-term benefits. Having a dog gets you out on regular walks, for instance. And pets can help alleviate conditions like anxiety and PTSD.

    当我和朋友们努力应对宠物给日常开支带来的短期压力时，也可以从一些长期益处中得到安慰。比如，养狗会让你定期出门散步；宠物还可以帮助缓解焦虑、创伤后应激障碍等症状。

  9. 09

    Omelette and Gumdrop are now both an important part of my home—and my budget.

    Omelette 和 Gumdrop 如今都是我家中重要的一员——也是我预算中重要的一部分。

阅读理解

02-2018课标全国Ⅰ，B

食物、农业与地方身份

Good Morning Britain's Susanna Reid is used to grilling guests on the sofa every morning, but she is cooking up a storm in her latest role—showing families how to prepare delicious and nutritious meals on a tight budget.

In Save Money:Good Food, she visits a different home each week and with the help of chef Matt Tebbutt offers top tips on how to reduce food waste, while preparing recipes for under ￡5 per family a day. And the Good Morning Britain presenter says she's been able to put a lot of what she's learnt into practice in her own home, preparing meals for sons, Sam, 14, Finn, 13, and Jack,11.

“We love Mexican churros, so I buy them on my phone from my local Mexican takeaway restaurant,”she explains.“I pay ￡5 for a portion(一份), but Matt makes them for 26p a portion, because they are flour, water, sugar and oil. Everybody can buy takeaway food, but sometimes we're not aware how cheaply we can make this food ourselves.”

The eight⁃part series(系列节目), Save Money:Good Food, follows in the footsteps of ITV's Save Money: Good Health, which gave viewers advice on how to get value from the vast range of health products on the market.

With food our biggest weekly household expense, Susanna and Matt spend time with a different family each week. In tonight's Easter special they come to the aid of a family in need of some delicious inspiration on a budget. The team transforms the family's long weekend of celebration with less expensive but still tasty recipes.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What do we know about Susanna Reid?

  • A.She enjoys embarrassing her guests.
  • B.She has started a new programme.
  • C.She dislikes working early in the morning.
  • D.She has had a tight budget for her family.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　细节理解题。根据第一段的but she is cooking up a storm in her latest role和第四段中的The eight⁃part series可知,她创办了一档新节目,故选B项。

2How does Matt Tebbutt help Susanna?

  • A.He buys cooking materials for her.
  • B.He prepares food for her kids.
  • C.He assists her in cooking matters.
  • D.He invites guest families for her.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　推理判断题。根据第二段第一句话(在节目Save Money:Good Food中,她每周拜访一个不同的家庭,并且在厨师Matt Tebbutt的帮助下在准备每个家庭每天低于5英镑的食谱时提供如何减少食物浪费的很好的建议)可知,Matt Tebbutt在做饭方面帮助了Susanna,故选C项。

3What does the author intend to do in paragraph 4?

  • A.Summarize the previous paragraphs.
  • B.Provide some advice for the readers.
  • C.Add some background information.
  • D.Introduce a new topic for discussion.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　推理判断题。文章前三段主要介绍了新节目Save Money:Good Food的相关情况,而第四段介绍了它和节目Save Money:Good Health的关系。由此推断本段主要补充这一节目的背景知识,故选C项。

4What can be a suitable title for the text?

  • A.Keeping Fit by Eating Smart
  • B.Balancing Our Daily Diet
  • C.Making Yourself a Perfect Chef
  • D.Cooking Well for Less
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　主旨要义题。通读全文可知,文章介绍了英国节目主持人Susanna Reid通过创办新节目Save Money:Good Food教给观众如何花更少的钱制作更美味的食物。故选D项。

阅读难度参考：CSE 7

蜜雪冰城凭什么超过麦当劳，成为全球门店最多的餐饮巨头？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    They Built the World’s Biggest Fast-Food Chain With the Dollar General Playbook

    他们用“达乐模式”打造了全球最大的快餐连锁品牌

  2. 02

    To understand how a Chinese ice-cream shop surpassed McDonald’s in-store locations, skip Beijing or Shanghai. Instead, look at a Dollar General; in Dayton, Ohio, or Lubbock, Texas.

    想知道一家中国冰淇淋店的门店数量是如何超过麦当劳的，不妨跳过北京或上海，先来看看达乐（Dollar General）位于俄亥俄州代顿或得克萨斯州拉伯克的门店。

    surpass
    v. 超过
  3. 03

    Over seven decades, the American discount chain built a 21,000-store juggernaut on a simple formula: Go where Walmart won’t, keep stores bare-bones and sell cheap staples to people stretching their paychecks.

    七十多年来，这家美国折扣连锁靠着一个简单的模式，建立起拥有2.1万家门店的商业帝国：去沃尔玛不愿意去的地方，门店尽量简陋，向那些精打细算过日子的人出售廉价日用品。

    staple
    n. （商业语境）日常生活中经常需要的基本用品；主食；adj. 主要的，基本的
    formula
    n. 成功的方法，成熟的模式
  4. 04

    Halfway across the world, the Zhang brothers independently saw the same opportunity in China. Instead of $1 paper towels, they sold 45-cent soft-serve cones and cheap drinks.

    在世界的另一端，中国的张氏兄弟也独立地看到了同样的机会。只不过，他们卖的不是1美元的纸巾，而是45美分的冰淇淋甜筒和便宜饮料。

  5. 05

    Today, Mixue is the world’s biggest food-and-beverage chain, with more than 47,000 locations last year and the company has added thousands more this year. Here’s how Mixue conquered the discount world.

    如今，蜜雪冰城已经成为全球最大的餐饮连锁品牌：去年已坐拥超4.7万家门店，今年继续增加数千家。来看看蜜雪冰城是如何征服平价消费市场的。

    conquer
    v. 攻占（市场），征服
  6. 06

    Go where they ain’t. Three-quarters of Mixue’s shops in China are in second- and third-tier cities, where the company says it has fewer competitors.

    去别人不去的地方。蜜雪冰城在中国约四分之三的门店位于二、三线城市。公司表示，这些地方的竞争对手相对较少。

  7. 07

    No frills. Instead of buying real estate, Dollar General mostly leases bare-bones, company-operated stores. Mixue squeezed this blueprint into franchised stalls as small as 45 square feet, the size of a walk-in closet.

    不讲究排场。达乐不热衷于购买房产，而主要租用简陋的店面，自行经营。蜜雪冰城则把这套模式进一步压缩，其加盟店店面小至45平方英尺（约4.2平方米）——也就一个步入式衣帽间那么大。

    lease
    v. 租赁
  8. 08

    Beware the upmarket trap. In 2009, Co–chairman Zhang Hongfu opened an upmarket rival with fresh ingredients and premium decor. After 2½ years, he closed the store after generating just $900 in profit. “I should have just stuck to selling at the prices I’m best at,” he said. “Do not put on airs.”

    当心高端化陷阱。2009年，蜜雪冰城联合董事长张洪福曾开过一家走高端路线的竞争品牌，使用新鲜食材，采用高档装潢。两年半后，他关掉了这家店，总共只赚了900美元。“我本该坚持经营自己最擅长的那个价位，”他说。“不该装腔作势。”

    beware
    v. 警惕、当心
    generate
    v. 产生，获得
    upmarket
    adj. 高端定位的（面向更高收入的消费者，提供更高价、更高端的产品或服务）
  9. 09

    Expanding in the U.S., Mixue has strayed from its playbook by opening in expensive Manhattan and Hollywood ZIP Codes. Yet one thing remains constant: low prices. A soft-serve cone costs $1.19.

    如今，正在美国扩张的蜜雪冰城已经偏离了原本的打法，把门店开进了曼哈顿和好莱坞等寸土寸金的区域。但有一点始终没变：低价。一个冰淇淋甜筒只卖1.19美元。

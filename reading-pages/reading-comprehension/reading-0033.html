阅读理解

06-2021天津春，B

学习、阅读与教育方式

About five weeks ago, I noticed the skin of our pet lizard (蜥蜴) was growing dusty.It worried me.I reported the strange surface on the skin of the lizard to my husband and children the next morning. Seconds later, our lizard emerged from its tank with its old skin flowing behind it.

I didn't think about it much until a morning last week when I knocked my favorite teapot off the table. It burst into hundreds of pieces.As I swept up the mess, I wondered why we had been breaking so many things over the months.

The destruction started three months ago.It was my husband's birthday.He had just lost his job. The uncertainty was starting to wear us out, so I wanted to do something special.

“Let's make a cake for Dad!” I cried.

My kids screamed with joy.We baked, iced and sprinkled for most of the day.Candles on the cake! Balloons on the walls! Flowers on the table!

Two hours before my husband came back home from another job interview, my daughter climbed up to grab a glass vase from a high shelf.It fell and crashed beside the cake.Tiny pieces of glass were everywhere.She sobbed loudly as I threw the cake away.My husband had banana pudding for his birthday.

Three days ago, the light in our living room suddenly went out.After several frustrating hours of unsuccessful attempts to fix it, my husband suggested watching the Michael Jordan documentary (纪录片) series The Last Dance.

The poignancy (辛酸) of Jordan retiring from his beloved basketball to play baseball and what had pushed him to make such a tough decision took me by surprise.As I watched him take off his basketball uniform and replace it with a baseball uniform, I saw him leaving behind the layer that no longer served him, just as our lizard had.Neither of them chose the moment that had transformed them.But they had to live with who they were after everything was different.Just like us.I realized that we have to learn to leave the past behind.

Humans do not shed skin (蜕皮) as easily as other animals.The beginning of change is upsetting.The process is tiring.Damage changes us before we are ready.I see our lizard, raw and nearly new.

Jordan said that no matter how it ends, it starts with hope.With our tender, hopeful skin, that is where we begin.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What can we learn about the pet lizard from Paragraph 1?

  • A.Its tank grew dirty.
  • B.Its old skin came off.
  • C.It got a skin disease.
  • D.It went missing.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　推理判断题。根据第一段最后一句Seconds later, our lizard emerged from its tank with its old skin flowing behind it.(几秒钟后,我们的蜥蜴从箱里出来,旧皮在后面飘动。)可知蜥蜴在经历蜕皮过程。故选B。

2Why did the author's husband have banana pudding for his birthday?

  • A.The birthday cake was ruined.
  • B.The author made good puddings.
  • C.Pudding was his favorite dessert.
  • D.They couldn't afford a birthday cake.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2.A　推理判断题。根据第六段中的my daughter climbed up to grab a glass vase... She sobbed loudly as I threw the cake away.可知丈夫吃香蕉布丁的原因是生日蛋糕被毁了。

3Why does the author mention The Last Dance in the passage?

  • A.To prove a theory.
  • B.To define a concept.
  • C.To develop the theme.
  • D.To provide the background.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　推理判断题。作者在文中提到The Last Dance是为了引出下文的感悟,故选C。

4The underlined part “leaving behind the layer” in Paragraph 8 can be understood as _____ .

  • A.letting go of the past
  • B.looking for a new job
  • C.getting rid of a bad habit
  • D.giving up an opportunity
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4.A　词义猜测题。根据第八段最后一句话I realized that we have to learn to leave the past behind.(我意识到我们必须学会把过去抛在脑后。)可知选A。

5What does the author most likely want to tell us?

  • A.Love of family helps us survive great hardships.
  • B.It's not the end of the world if we break things.
  • C.We should move on no matter what happens.
  • D.Past experiences should be treasured.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

5.C　推理判断题。这篇文章从开头提到蜥蜴蜕皮到观看乔丹的纪录片都是为了引出主题:learn to leave the past behind。故选C(不管发生什么,我们都应该往前走)。

和父母断绝关系，能治愈原生家庭的创伤吗？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Are Therapists to Blame for the Rise of Adults Cutting Off Their Parents?

    成年人与父母断绝关系的现象日益增多，心理治疗师该为此负责吗？

    therapist
    n. 心理治疗师
  2. 02

    After decades of urging families to communicate better and work on their issues together, some therapists are now taking an opposite tack: They’re encouraging estrangement as a means of self-care.

    数十年来，心理治疗师一直劝导家庭成员加强沟通、共同解决问题。然而一些心理治疗师现在却采取了截然相反的做法：他们鼓励通过疏远亲人来实现自我关怀。

    tack
    n. 方向、策略
    estrangement
    n. 疏远、隔离、关系断裂
    issue
    n. 问题、情结、心结
  3. 03

    “We are in a culture where we emphasize fragility, everything becomes trauma, and the solution increasingly is to split and leave,”says William Doherty, a therapist and professor emeritus of family social science at the University of Minnesota.“But it should be the tragic exception that you cut off a parent or loved one, not business as usual in therapy.”

    “我们身处一种强调脆弱的文化中，万事万物都被视为创伤，而解决之道越来越倾向于切割关系、转身离开，”明尼苏达大学家庭社会科学专业荣休教授、心理治疗师威廉·多尔蒂（William Doherty）表示。“但与父母或亲人断绝关系本应是悲剧性的例外，而非心理治疗中的常态。”

    trauma
    n. 创伤、精神打击
    fragility
    n. 脆弱、易碎
  4. 04

    This can lead to two major problems, according to Jay Lebow, senior scholar and senior therapist at the Family Institute at Northwestern University. “You are not solving the right problem, which is the mental-health crisis,” he says. “And—this is the most tragic part—you may be encouraging them to cut off their best source of support.”

    西北大学家庭研究所的高级学者兼高级治疗师杰伊·莱鲍（Jay Lebow）指出，这可能会导致两个主要问题。“你并没有解决真正的问题——心理健康危机，”他说，“而且——这才是最令人痛心的一点——你可能正在鼓励他们切断自己最好的支持来源。”

  5. 05

    Critics also argue that there is no scientific evidence that separating from a close relative is inherently beneficial. And the long-term damage is often unanticipated. While people who start a cutoff often feel a temporary sense of relief, they also experience significantly higher rates of chronic unhappiness and depression over time. He calls this the “exiter cost.”

    批评者还指出，目前尚无科学证据表明与亲近的亲属断绝关系本身具有益处。而且，这种行为造成的长期伤害往往难以预料。虽然主动断绝关系的人起初常会感到暂时的解脱，但随着时间的推移，他们出现慢性不快乐和抑郁的概率会显著升高。他将此称为“退出者代价”。

    chronic
    adj. 长期的、慢性的、反复的
    exiter
    n. 离开者、退出者（本文指断亲者）

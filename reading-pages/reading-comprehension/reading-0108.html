学习、阅读与教育方式

Grading Policies for Introduction to Literature

Grading Scale

90-100, A;80-89, B;70-79, C;60-69, D;Below 60, E.

Essays (60%)

Your four major essays will combine to form the main part of the grade for this course:Essay 1=10%;Essay 2=15%;Essay 3=15%;Essay 4=20%.

Group Assignments (30%)

Students will work in groups to complete four assignments during the course. All the assignments will be submitted by the assigned date through Blackboard, our online learning and course management system.

Daily Work/In-Class Writings and Tests/Group Work/Homework (10%)

Class activities will vary from day to day, but students must be ready to complete short in-class writings or tests drawn directly from assigned readings or notes from the previous class’ lecture/discussion, so it is important to take careful notes during class. Additionally, from time to time I will assign group work to be completed in class or short assignments to be completed at home, both of which will be graded.

Late Work

An essay not submitted in class on the due date will lose a letter grade for each class period it is late. If it is not turned in by the 4th day after the due date, it will earn a zero. Daily assignments not completed during class will get a zero. Short writings missed as a result of an excused absence will be accepted.

1Where is this text probably taken from?

  • A.A textbook.
  • B.An exam paper.
  • C.A course plan.
  • D.An academic article.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C推理判断题。由文章题目Grading Policies for Introduction to Literature（文学导论评分原则）和文章中小标题Grading Scale（评分等级）及下面小标题说明的每项学习内容所占的成绩比例可知，本文选自一项课程计划。故选C。

2How many parts is a student’s final grade made up of?

  • A.Two.
  • B.Three.
  • C.Four.
  • D.Five.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2. B细节理解题。由文中所列的成绩构成比例Essays（60%）（论文占60%）、Group Assignments （30%）（小组作业占30%）、Daily Work/In-Class Writings and Tests/Group Work/Homework （10%）（日常学习/课上写作与测试/小组学习/课外作业占10%）可知，学生的最终成绩由三部分组成。故选B。

3What will happen if you submit an essay one week after the due date?

  • A.You will receive a zero.
  • B.You will lose a letter grade.
  • C.You will be given a test.
  • D.You will have to rewrite it.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

3. A细节理解题。由Late Work部分第二句“If it is not turned in by the 4th day after the due date, it will earn a zero. (如果截止日期后第四天还没有上交，将得零分)”可知，晚交一周会得零分。故选A。

    Is TikTok the new frontier for fashion reinvention?

    frontier
    有待开拓的新领域；问短视频能不能为服装设计打开新机会。
  2. 02

    Designer and content creator Alexei Hamblin has put it to the test. The 23-year-old began a video series on the platform in 2025, where he suggested how he would revamp "dead" sports brands.

    设计师兼内容创作者阿列克谢·汉布林（Alexei Hamblin）对此进行了验证。这位23岁的年轻人2025年在平台上发布了一组系列视频，在视频里他大谈如何重塑一些“过气”的运动品牌。

    revamp
    翻新、改造；revamp dead sports brands 指给失去活力的运动品牌换一种设计思路。
  3. 03

    It generated hundreds of thousands of views and also got the attention of the retailer's owner, Frasers Group, which invited Hamblin to come and meet with their team.

    这些视频获得了数十万的浏览量，也成功引起了品牌零售商所有者——弗雷泽集团（Frasers Group）的注意，汉布林被邀请与他们的团队见面。

  4. 04

    He expected to receive a cease and desist letter from the retail giant after describing some of the clothes as "boring" and "tacky PE kit". Instead, he was brought on as a consultant after he pitched his vision of how they could reinvent legacy sports brand Slazenger.

    他本以为会收到这家零售巨头的“禁止令”，因为他形容他们的一些服装“乏味”、“像廉价的体育课运动服”。然而当他详细阐释了如何重振老牌运动品牌 Slazenger 的构想后，反而被聘为了顾问。

    legacy
    长期传承下来的；legacy sports brand 指历史悠久的运动品牌。
    cease
    停止；cease and desist letter 是要求对方停止某种行为、不要再继续的正式函件。
    tacky
    俗气、缺乏品位的；用来尖锐评价旧设计的外观。
    consultant
    顾问；品牌方请这位创作者提供建议，而不是向他发律师函。
    pitched
    推介、向人提出一套方案；pitch one’s vision 指拿自己的改造构想去说服对方。
  5. 05

    Hamblin, who hasn't been to fashion school, says he became interested in how fashion helps people find their "identity". He taught himself to use digital tools, such as Photoshop, to design clothes he would want to wear and shared the ideas on TikTok.

    汉布林虽未就读过时装院校，却对时尚如何帮助人们找到“身份认同”产生了兴趣。他通过自学Photoshop等数字工具来设计自己心仪的服饰，并将创意在TikTok上分享。

  6. 06

    He went on to freelance for clothing companies and start-ups before launching his own brand in 2021.

    此后他以自由职业者的身份和服装公司及初创企业合作，并于2021年创立了个人品牌。

    freelance
    以自由职业者身份接活；freelance for clothing companies 指独立为服装公司做项目。
  7. 07

    Fashion journalist Renee Washington says fashion content creators are having a "big impact" on the industry and feels they are making it "more accessible". "You don't need to be sitting front row at Fashion Week anymore to shape taste, you can influence right from your bedroom and that shift has been very powerful", she says.

    时尚记者蕾妮·华盛顿（Renee Washington）指出，时尚内容创作者正对行业产生“巨大影响”，她感觉他们正让时尚变得“更触手可及”。“如今你无需坐在时装周前排就能塑造审美，你甚至在卧室里就能影响潮流——这种转变极具影响力。”蕾妮如是说。

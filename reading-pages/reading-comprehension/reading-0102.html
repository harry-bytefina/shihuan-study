阅读理解

03-2023新课标Ⅰ，C

AI、脑科学与新技术

The goal of this book is to make the case for digital minimalism, including a detailed exploration of what it asks and why it works, and then to teach you how to adopt this philosophy if you decide it's right for you.

To do so, I divided the book into two parts. In part one, I describe the philosophical foundations of digital minimalism, starting with an examination of the forces that are making so many people's digital lives increasingly intolerable, before moving on to a detailed discussion of the digital minimalism philosophy.

Part one concludes by introducing my suggested method for adopting this philosophy: the digital declutter. This process requires you to step away from optional online activities for thirty days. At the end of the thirty days, you will then add back a small number of carefully chosen online activities that you believe will provide massive benefits to the things you value.

In the final chapter of part one, I'll guide you through carrying out your own digital declutter. In doing so, I'll draw on an experiment I ran in 2018 in which over 1,600 people agreed to perform a digital declutter. You'll hear these participants' stories and learn what strategies worked well for them, and what traps they encountered that you should avoid.

The second part of this book takes a closer look at some ideas that will help you cultivate(培养) a sustainable digital minimalism lifestyle. In these chapters, I examine issues such as the importance of solitude(独处) and the necessity of cultivating high-quality leisure to replace the time most now spend on mindless device use. Each chapter concludes with a collection of practices, which are designed to help you act on the big ideas of the chapter. You can view these practices as a toolbox meant to aid your efforts to build a minimalist lifestyle that works for your particular circumstances.

  • A.Teaching critical thinking skills.
  • B.Advocating a simple digital lifestyle.

答案：B

1. B细节理解题。根据题干关键词aimed at可定位到第一段中的goal。根据第一段内容可知，这本书的目的是解释数字极简主义（make the case for digital minimalism），包括详细探索它的要求是什么和它为什么有效，并且教你如何采用这种生活方式。其中teach you how to adopt和digital minimalism分别与B项中的Advocating和simple digital lifestyle相呼应。故选B（倡导一种简单的数字生活方式）。A项意为“教授批判性思维技巧”，C项意为“解决哲学问题”，D项意为“推广数码设备的使用”。

阅读难度参考：CSE 7

困住现代人的“注意力危机”，两千年前早有对策

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    There’s an Ancient Solution to Our Modern Crisis of Attention

    现代注意力危机的古老解决之道

    solution
    n. 解决方案
  2. 02

    About 2,000 years ago, the Roman philosopher Seneca warned of a crisis of attention. The problem wasn’t caused by smartphones or TikTok; it was because papyrus had become more widely available.

    两千年前，古罗马哲学家塞涅卡（Seneca）就警告过，人类将面临一场注意力危机。 这一问题并非源于智能手机，或TikTok，而是因为纸莎草纸变得更加普及。

  3. 03

    The lesson then was no less true than it is now, in our perpetually distracted, screen-addled, multitasking age. When we allow ideas to come and go in rapid succession, we keep our minds too busy and wear them out. Nothing sticks.“One who is everywhere is nowhere,”Seneca cautioned.

    当年的教训，放到今天这个长期分心、因沉迷屏幕而变得认知迟钝、习惯多任务处理的时代，依然成立。随着各种想法快速涌入又迅速消散，我们的大脑始终处于忙碌状态，最终筋疲力尽，什么也没留下。“一个无处不在的人，实际上哪里也不在。”塞涅卡如是告诫。

    screen-addled
    adj. 因过度使用屏幕而头脑混乱、认知迟钝的
  4. 04

    But we’re overcomplicating a very old challenge that is more moral than digital. Seneca rightly saw distraction as a failure of character. We need to relearn how to sit still with our own thoughts.

    然而，我们把一个古老的问题想得过于复杂了。与其说它是一个数字时代的问题，不如说是一个道德层面的问题。塞涅卡正确地指出，分心是一种品格缺陷。我们需要重新学习如何安静地和自己的想法共处。

    distraction
    n. 分心，注意力涣散
  5. 05

    How exactly do you do this? Seneca had some practical advice, which he outlined in his“Letters From a Stoic”: Devote your attention to one idea a day.

    具体该怎么做？塞涅卡在《道德书简》中列出了简单实用的建议：每天专注于一个想法。

  6. 06

    For the past 20 years, I’ve practiced a simple discipline inspired by this advice. First thing in the morning, I forage in a book for my one idea. I’m not looking for a memorable quotation or aphorism; I’m looking for a passage that challenges or better illuminates how I see the world.

    受此启发，过去二十年里，我一直实践着一个简单的自我训练：每天晨起第一件事，就是翻书寻找一段妙思。我要找的不是名言警句，而是一段能够挑战或更好地启发我如何看待世界的文字。

    illuminate
    v. 让某事物更清楚，获得新的理解
    discipline
    n. 自律
  7. 07

    Seneca compared the benefits of deep reflection to the alchemy by which bees transform nectar into honey. Think of the nectar as information and the honey as wisdom. Whereas nectar sours within a matter of days, honey doesn’t spoil.

    塞涅卡把深度思考比作蜜蜂把花蜜酿成蜂蜜的神奇过程。你可以把花蜜视作信息，蜂蜜则是智慧。花蜜没几天就变质，而蜂蜜却不会腐坏。

    spoil
    v. （食物）腐败、变质
    whereas
    conj. 然而，反之
  8. 08

    Seneca’s honey metaphor corresponds to what psychologists call deep processing: By returning to the same idea repeatedly, we signal to our brain that the idea is worth moving into the architecture of long-term memory.

    塞涅卡这个蜂蜜的比喻与心理学家所说的“深度加工”不谋而合。当我们反复思考同一个想法时，其实就是向大脑发出这样的信号：这个想法值得被纳入长期记忆的架构之下。

  9. 09

    By anchoring each day in a single idea, I’ve spent 20 years trying to avoid the Autodidact’s hollow fate. I no longer chase the endlessly receding horizon of staying informed. I’ve traded the anxiety of the shallows for the untapped wisdom of deep waters.

    二十年来，我每天都用一个想法作为锚点，努力避免落入那个“自学者”的空洞命运。我不再追逐永远追不上的“热点信息”，而是用探寻深水的智慧取代了困在浅滩的焦虑。

    anchor
    v. 把……固定住，让……有稳定的支点；n. 船锚
  10. 10

    We are often told that more information is the answer, but Seneca knew better. Wisdom is found not in the nectar we gather, but in the honey we take the time to make with it.

    我们总被告知，更多的信息就是答案，而塞涅卡早知并非如此。智慧，不在于我们采集了多少花蜜，而在于我们愿意花多少时间，把它酿成蜂蜜。

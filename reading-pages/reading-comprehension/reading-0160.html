Train Information

All customers travelling on TransLink services must be in possession of a valid ticket before boarding. For ticket information, please ask at your local station or call 13 12 30.

While Queensland Rail makes every effort to ensure trains run as scheduled, there can be no guarantee of connections between trains or between train services and bus services.

Lost property(失物招领)

Call Lost Property on 13 16 17 during business hours for items lost on Queensland Rail services. The lost property office is open Monday to Friday 7:30 am to 5:00 pm and is located(位于)at Roma Street station.

Public holidays

On public holidays, generally a Sunday timetable operates. On certain major event days, i.e. Australia Day, Anzac Day, sporting and cultural days, special additional services may operate. Christmas Day services operate to a Christmas Day timetable. Before travel please visit translink.com.au or call TransLink on 13 12 30 anytime.

Customers using mobility devices

Many stations have wheelchair access from the car park or entrance to the station platforms. For assistance, please call Queensland Rail on 13 16 17.

外刊延伸

怀旧杀！香港最后一代“点心车阿姨”，推着流沙包和旧时光退场了

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Hong Kong's Dim Sum Cart 'Aunties' Make Their Final Round

    香港点心车阿姨的最后巡礼

  2. 02

    It was nearing peak lunch service at the Metropol, one of Hong Kong's largest dim sum restaurants, when So Yim-ha emerged from the kitchen into the cavernous dining hall pushing a stainless steel cart stacked high with bamboo steamers.

    临近午餐高峰，在香港规模最大的茶餐厅之一名都酒楼（Metropol）内，苏艳霞（So Yim-ha）推着一辆堆满竹蒸笼的不锈钢点心车，从厨房走进宽敞的用餐大厅。

    steamer
    n. 蒸笼
    cavernous
    adj. 像大洞穴一样，又大又空旷的
  3. 03

    Fighting to be heard over the din of the room filled with hundreds of customers, Ms. So cleared her throat and began advertising the small, scrumptious dishes in her cart. "Pork ribs! Beef meatballs! Quail egg siu mai!"

    喧闹的大堂里坐满了几百号食客，苏姨不得不提高嗓门才能让客人听清，她清了清嗓子，开始吆喝起推车里那些香气冲天的精致小点：“排骨！牛肉丸！鹌鹑蛋烧卖！”

    din
    n. 噪音
    scrumptious
    adj. 好吃的
  4. 04

    Diners pointed at the dishes, enticed by the perfume of soy and steamed shrimp in rice rolls, and the sight of fluffy barbecued pork buns or glistening yellow egg tarts.

    蒸笼里的酱油和鲜虾肠粉香气四溢，再加上松软的叉烧包、油亮亮的金黄蛋挞，令人食指大动，纷纷点餐下单。

    perfume
    n. 让人舒服的香气
    glistening
    adj. 亮晶晶，油光锃亮的
  5. 05

    But the Metropol will close for good on Sept. 27, after 35 years in business. And along with it, a beloved fixture of Hong Kong dining will fade away: the dim sum cart auntie.

    但这家营业了35年的名都酒楼，将于9月27日永久结业。随之远去的，还有香港饮食文化中一道深入人心的风景线——点心车阿姨。

    fixture
    n. 长期存在、不可或缺的人或事物
  6. 06

    The women can be equal parts charming and gruff, as service staff tend to be in Hong Kong. But they always add a human touch to one of the city's most cherished rituals in a way that a server with a menu could never.

    点心车阿姨和香港多数餐饮从业者一样，既有亲切迷人的一面，也带着几分凶巴巴。但她们总是能给香港这最珍贵的饮食仪式之一增添一抹人情味，而这种温度，是拿着菜单点餐的服务员永远也给不了的。

    gruff
    adj.（态度）生硬、不耐烦；（声音）低沉、沙哑
  7. 07

    The closure is also a reflection of Hong Kong's changing fortunes. Large restaurants like the Metropol, which are big enough to roll out dim sum carts, are a throwback to the high-flying decades starting in the 1980s. Today's economics do not allow for that. To survive, restaurants in Hong Kong often need to be smaller and leaner.

    酒楼结业也折射出香港时代命运的变迁。像名都这样能容纳点心车穿梭的大型酒楼，兴盛于上世纪80年代香港经济开始腾飞的时期，是那段繁荣留下的产物。可如今的经济环境已不允许这类业态存续了。香港的餐厅要生存，不得不走小而精简的经营模式。

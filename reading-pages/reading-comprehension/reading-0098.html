食物、农业与地方身份

We all know fresh is best when it comes to food. However, most produce at the store went through weeks of travel and covered hundreds of miles before reaching the table. While farmer's markets are a solid choice to reduce the journey, Babylon Micro-Farm (BMF) shortens it even more.

BMF is an indoor garden system. It can be set up for a family. Additionally, it could serve a larger audience such as a hospital, restaurant or school. The innovative design requires little effort to achieve a reliable weekly supply of fresh greens.

Specifically, it's a farm that relies on new technology. By connecting through the Cloud, BMF is remotely monitored. Also, there is a convenient app that provides growing data in real time. Because the system is automated, it significantly reduces the amount of water needed to grow plants. Rather than watering rows of soil, the system provides just the right amount to each plant. After harvest, users simply replace the plants with a new pre-seeded pod(容器) to get the next growth cycle started.

Moreover, having a system in the same building where it's eaten means zero emissions(排放) from transporting plants from soil to salad. In addition, there's no need for pesticides and other chemicals that pollute traditional farms and the surrounding environment.

BMF employees live out sustainability in their everyday lives. About half of them walk or bike to work. Inside the office, they encourage recycling and waste reduction by limiting garbage cans and avoiding single-use plastic. “We are passionate about reducing waste, carbon and chemicals in our environment,” said a BMF employee.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What can be learned about BMF from paragraph 1?

  • A.It guarantees the variety of food.
  • B.It requires day-to-day care.
  • C.It cuts the farm-to-table distance.
  • D.It relies on farmer's markets.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C　细节理解题。根据第一段中的“While farmer's markets are a solid choice to reduce the journey, Babylon Micro-Farm(BMF) shortens it even more.”可知,句中的it指the journey,而the journey指的是上句提到的农产品到达餐桌的时间和距离,由此可知BMF缩短了农产品从农场到餐桌的距离。故选C。

2What information does the convenient app offer?

  • A.Real-time weather changes.
  • B.Current condition of the plants.
  • C.Chemical pollutants in the soil.
  • D.Availability of pre-seeded pods.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2. B　细节理解题。根据题干关键词the convenient app可定位到第三段。根据第三句“Also, there is a convenient app that provides growing data in real time.”可知,这个便捷的应用程序提供实时的生长数据,即提供植物当前的状况。故选B。

3What can be concluded about BMF employees?

  • A.They have a great passion for sports.
  • B.They are devoted to community service.
  • C.They are fond of sharing daily experiences.
  • D.They have a strong environmental awareness.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D　推理判断题。根据题干关键词BMF employees可定位到最后一段。根据“About half of them walk or bike to work. Inside the office, they encourage recycling and waste reduction by limiting garbage cans and avoiding single-use plastic.”可知,大约一半的BMF员工走路或骑车上班,他们鼓励回收和减少垃圾。由此可以推断出,他们有很强的环保意识。故选D。

4What does the text mainly talk about?

  • A.BMF's major strengths.
  • B.BMF's general management.
  • C.BMF's global influence.
  • D.BMF's technical standards.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4. A　主旨要义题,考查文章主旨。文章首段提出说明对象:BMF;第二段介绍BMF作为室内菜园系统,供应对象广泛且供应量稳定;第三段介绍该系统依靠新技术,操作很便捷;第四段介绍该系统环保无害;第五段介绍其员工具有很强的环保意识。综上所述,本文介绍的是BMF的主要优势。故选A。 重点词汇　 innovative adj.革新的,创新的　specifically adv.具体来说,确切地说　automate v.使自动化　sustainability n.可持续性　passionate adj.热诚的,狂热的

想辞职开咖啡店？先看看韩国人的前车之鉴

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    "If I could start over," said Ko Jang-su, "I would do anything but open a cafe."

    “如果人生能重来，”高章洙（Ko Jang-su）说，“我干什么都行，但绝不开咖啡店了。”

  2. 02

    Mr. Ko's cafe is one of the busiest coffee shops in his densely populated neighborhood of Seoul. Still, on weekday mornings it sits empty.

    高先生的咖啡店开在首尔一个人口密集的街区里，算是那儿生意最红火的店铺之一。然而，在工作日的早晨，店里依然空无一人。

    densely populated
    人口密集的
  3. 03

    It is not hard to understand why: Mr. Ko has more than 50 competitors nearby, and in South Korea, that is hardly unusual. In Seoul, the density of cafes rivals that of Paris.

    原因不难理解：高先生周边有超过50个竞争对手，而在韩国，这种情况并不稀奇。在首尔，咖啡馆的密集程度足以与巴黎比肩。

    rival
    v. 与……相匹敌，媲美
  4. 04

    The passion for coffee—one national survey suggested that Koreans now reach for it more often than rice—has bred a fantasy among some hoping to cash in and escape the 9-to-5 grind: Why not open a cafe of their own?

    一项全国调查显示，韩国人现在喝咖啡的频率甚至超过了吃米饭。这种咖啡狂热让一些想要借此大赚一笔、逃离朝九晚五苦海的人萌生幻想：为什么不开一家自己的咖啡店呢？

  5. 05

    Trend-chasing cafe hoppers determined to get in early on the next "it" place and post about it to Instagram often mill in front of newly opened shops. That has added to the illusion of easy money.

    还有一群追逐潮流的“咖啡店打卡族”，他们热衷于抢先体验下一个“网红”打卡地，并拍照发到照片墙（Instagram）上，这类人群经常在新开的店铺前转悠。这也让人误以为开咖啡店可以轻松赚钱。

    trend-chasing
    adj. 追逐潮流的
    mill
    v. （人群）无目的地聚集，徘徊
  6. 06

    "People see long lines form in front of other cafes and think running one is simple," said Mr. Ko. "But the work is grueling, and the profits are slim." Many owners net just $2,700 to $3,400 a month—a little over the minimum wage. And that's in return for putting in more than 13 hours a day. Many quit once their first leases expire after just a year or two. As more coffee shops open, their life span is getting shorter.

    “人们看到其他咖啡馆门前排起长队，就觉得经营咖啡馆很简单，”高先生说，“但这份工作非常辛苦，而且利润微薄。”许多店主每月净收入仅为2700至3400美元——只比最低工资略高一点（译者注：此处表述略有夸张。据韩国2026年最低工资标准，每月工作209小时的最低工资约合1458.6美元）。而这需要他们每天付出超过13小时的劳动。许多人干了一两年，在第一份租约到期后便选择关门大吉。随着咖啡店越开越多，它们的经营寿命正变得越来越短。

    n. 租约
    grueling
    adj. 极其累人的，让人筋疲力尽的
    slim
    adj. （利润、机会、可能性等）微小的，渺茫的；苗条的
    net
    v. 净得，净赚
  7. 07

    "A cafe is not a place to get rich," he said. "It's just a place to go and drink coffee."

    “咖啡馆不是致富的地方，”他说，“它只是让人来喝杯咖啡的场所。”

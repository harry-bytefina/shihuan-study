08-2025天津春，D

成长、韧性与心理调适

Ask most people what “the good life” involves, and they will likely draw up a rapid list of goods—money, friendship, a rewarding career, etc. Even Aristotle, who believed that the key to happiness is the cultivation(培养) and exercise of virtue, still thought certain goods were either necessary conditions for happiness or were goods that came to virtuous people.

If we're comparing how happy various people's lives are, it seems natural to look for certain goods, and compare who has more of them. Let's imagine the lives of two actors, Toby and Ybot. Toby struggles to get work for many years, suffering all sorts of difficulties. He becomes a famous and wealthy actor, and wins an Oscar for his final movie before retiring. Ybot, however, wins an Oscar for his very first film role. But over time he becomes less famous and less well paid, and he begins to suffer difficulties. In the end, he retires as a nobody.

These two actors' lives, like their names, are mirror-images. Toby and Ybot both seem to lead lives of equal net worth. Yet most of us would think that the ordering of these goods does make a decisive difference to how we compare Toby and Ybot's careers. Someone who climbs a mountain will experience more struggle than someone who gets carried to the peak by bus, but the climber will also have a sense of accomplishment the other person may well envy. In that sense, we might think Toby's life is better because his highs seem more well-earned than Ybot's do.

But that's not the whole story. The narrative(叙述) itself seems to make a difference. A life of early success followed by decline seems like a less overall successful life than one where success comes later in the day. Where the story ends seems to matter in itself. We value happy endings, not just happy moments.

This immediate judgment finds expression in what's been called the “shape of life” hypothesis(假说). On this view, living a good life is not simply about having the right kind of experiences or life-events, but of having them in the right order. The hypothesis suggests that our lives are not simply bags into which we stuff good things and bad things. The type of life—the narrative of that life-story—matters too. Without the right shape, even a life full of wondrous things can end up as a tale we'd prefer not to live out.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What can be learned about Aristotle's view on happiness?

  • A.Goods contribute to happiness.
  • B.Virtuous people may not be happy.
  • C.Too many goods reduce happiness.
  • D.Happy people care less about goods.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1. A　细节理解题。根据第一段中“Even Aristotle, who believed...certain goods were either necessary conditions for happiness or were goods that came to virtuous people.”可知,亚里士多德认为某些事物是幸福的必要条件。故选A。

2According to the author, what do both Toby and Ybot experience?

  • A.They start from a humble beginning.
  • B.They reach the same height in career.
  • C.They recover from the same sufferings.
  • D.They retire with honour from the film industry.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2. B　细节理解题。根据第二段中“Toby...wins an Oscar for his final movie before retiring.”和“Ybot, however, wins an Oscar for his very first film role.”可知,两个人都获得了奥斯卡奖,故选B(他们在职业生涯中达到了相同的高度)。

3What does the author try to convey with the example of mountain climbing?

  • A.Easy success is often envied.
  • B.There are various paths to success.
  • C.Fierce struggle is vital to success.
  • D.Hard-earned success is treasured more.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D　写作意图题。根据第三段中“most of us would think that the ordering of these goods does make a decisive difference”以及“Toby's life is better because his highs seem more well-earned than Ybot's do”可知,作者借助登山的例子来表达人们会觉得来之不易的成功更弥足珍贵。故选D。

4In writing Paragraph 4, the author tries to _____ .

  • A.introduce another topic
  • B.draw a conclusion
  • C.further an argument
  • D.provide new evidence
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

4. C　语篇结构题。第三段最后一句话提到人们或许会觉得托比的人生更有价值,因为他的成功来之不易,而第四段第一句(But that's not the whole story.)表示事情并非仅此而已,进而指出叙述本身也会影响整体评价,本质是在原有观点的基础上补充新的内容。故选C。

5What could be the best title for the passage?

  • A.To Shape Your Life with Good Deeds.
  • B.Defining the Shape of a Good Life.
  • C.The Essential Components of Good Life.
  • D.How We Can Have a Good Shape of Life.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

5. B　主旨要义题。文章最后一段引出并阐释“shape of life” hypothesis,强调美好生活并非简单拥有恰当的经历或人生事件,而在于以对的顺序拥有它们,探讨了美好人生的定义方式。故选B。

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    This AI Tech Billionaire Just Doubled His Net Worth. His Ex Asked for Half.

    这位 AI 科技亿万富豪的身家刚刚翻倍。他的前妻要求分得一半。

  2. 02

    Chey Tae-won, the billionaire atop blockbuster memory chip maker SK Hynix, announced the breakup of his fairy tale marriage to the daughter of a past president in a three-page letter to a South Korean newspaper a decade ago.

    崔泰源（Chey Tae-won），这位坐拥顶尖内存芯片制造商 SK 海力士的亿万富豪，十年前曾给一家韩国报纸写了一封长达三页的信，宣布了他与前总统之女的童话婚姻的破裂。

    blockbuster
    adj. 炙手可热的，爆款的
    atop
    prep. 高踞其上
  3. 03

    The yearslong divorce battle nears a climax Friday, when a panel of appeals court judges is scheduled to decide how to divide Chey’s roughly $5 billion fortune with his 65-year-old ex-wife, Roh Soh-yeong. The case, known here as the divorce of the century, has mesmerized South Koreans—a real-life K-drama of money, romance, corruption and betrayal.

    这场旷日持久的离婚大战，本周五将迫近高潮，届时上诉法院的法官们将裁定，如何在崔泰源和他 65 岁的前妻卢素英（Roh Soh-yeong）之间，分割他约50亿美元的财富。这桩在韩国被称为"世纪离婚"的案子，让全体韩国人看得如痴如醉——它宛如一部现实版韩剧，主题是金钱、爱情、腐败与背叛。

    climax
    n. 冲突的顶点；高潮
    mesmerize
    v. 像催眠一样把人迷住
  4. 04

    SK Hynix’s stock jumped roughly 10-fold since the start of 2025, helping more than double Chey’s net worth. Chey has argued that Roh played no role in SK’s recent success and that judges should base the divvy on an earlier measure—before his company’s stock boom.

    自2025年初以来，SK海力士的股价飙升了约10倍，这使得崔泰源的净资产翻了不止一番。崔争辩说，卢素英对SK近来的成功没有任何贡献，法官应以公司股价暴涨之前的估值作为财产分割的依据。

    divvy
    n. 分账，份子钱；v. 分账
  5. 05

    South Korean newspapers declared their 1988 nuptials as “the wedding of the century.” The ceremony was held at the presidential Blue House just months after Roh’s father, Roh Tae-woo, became South Korea’s first president after the collapse of the country’s military dictatorship.

    韩国报纸将他们1988年的婚礼称为“世纪婚礼”。这场婚礼在总统府“青瓦台”举行。就在几个月前，卢素英的父亲卢泰愚（Roh Tae-woo），刚刚成为这个国家军事独裁体制垮台之后的第一位总统。

    nuptials
    n. 婚礼（正式书面拉丁语用词，一般用复数）

    句子结构

    The ceremony was held at the presidential Blue House / just months after Roh’s father, Roh Tae-woo, became South Korea’s first president / after the collapse of the country’s military dictatorship.

    just months after 后有完整的主谓结构，说明婚礼办在什么事之后。第二个 after 后是名词短语，交代上任的背景。按时间顺序是军事独裁结束、卢泰愚就任、婚礼举行；Roh Tae-woo 是 Roh’s father 的同位语。

  6. 06

    In 1992, SK was awarded a license to operate the country’s second mobile-phone network. Kim Dae-jung, an opposition lawmaker who later became president, called it a “shameless act of presidential nepotism” and the “absolute climax of corrupt, political-business collusion.” Within days, SK returned the license.

    1992年，SK获得了运营韩国第二个移动电话网络的牌照。时任反对党议员、后来成为总统的金大中称此举是“无耻的总统裙带主义”，也是“政商勾结的腐败极致”。几天之内，SK交还了牌照。

    nepotism
    n. 裙带主义
    political-business collusion
    政商勾结
  7. 07

    Roh Soh-yeong’s lawyers submitted a handwritten note that Roh’s mother had apparently saved. It wasn’t clear who wrote it, but it showed Sunkyong—SK’s prior name—written alongside “30 billion won,” roughly $23 million. The note and other evidence persuaded an appeals court in 2024 to order Chey to give his ex-wife 35% of his assets, amounting to nearly $1 billion at the time.

    卢素英的律师提交了一份手写便条，据称是卢素英的母亲保存下来的。虽然不清楚便条出自谁之手，但上面写着“鲜京”（Sunkyong）——SK 的旧名——旁边写着"300 亿韩元"，约合 2300 万美元。这张便条及其他证据说服了上诉法院于2024年裁定：崔泰源须分给前妻35%的资产——当时价值近10亿美元。

阅读理解

02-2019课标全国Ⅰ，B

学习、阅读与教育方式

For Canaan Elementary's second grade in Patchogue, N.Y., today is speech day, and right now it's Chris Palaez's turn. The 8⁃year⁃old is the joker of the class. With shining dark eyes, he seems like the kind of kid who would enjoy public speaking.

But he's nervous.“I'm here to tell you today why you should...should...”Chris trips on the “⁃ld,” a pronunciation difficulty for many non⁃native English speakers. His teacher, Thomas Whaley, is next to him, whispering support.“...Vote for...me...”Except for some stumbles, Chris is doing amazingly well. When he brings his speech to a nice conclusion, Whaley invites the rest of the class to praise him.

A son of immigrants, Chris started learning English a little over three years ago. Whaley recalls(回想起)how at the beginning of the year, when called upon to read, Chris would excuse himself to go to the bathroom.

Learning English as a second language can be a painful experience. What you need is a great teacher who lets you make mistakes.“It takes a lot for any student,”Whaley explains, “especially for a student who is learning English as their new language, to feel confident enough to say,‘I don't know, but I want to know.’”

Whaley got the idea of this second⁃grade presidential campaign project when he asked the children one day to raise their hands if they thought they could never be a president. The answer broke his heart. Whaley says the project is about more than just learning to read and speak in public. He wants these kids to learn to boast(夸耀)about themselves.

“Boasting about yourself, and your best qualities,”Whaley says,“is very difficult for a child who came into the classroom not feeling confident.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What made Chris nervous?

  • A.Telling a story.
  • B.Making a speech.
  • C.Taking a test.
  • D.Answering a question.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　细节理解题。根据第一段第一句(……今天是演讲日,现在轮到Chris Palaez了)和第二段第一句(但是他很紧张)可知,B项正确。

2What does the underlined word “stumbles” in paragraph 2 refer to?

  • A.Improper pauses.
  • B.Bad manners.
  • C.Spelling mistakes.
  • D.Silly jokes.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2.A　猜测词义题。根据第二段中的“you should...should...”Chris trips on the “⁃ld,”...可知,他有些发音上的问题,讲话磕磕绊绊的,应该是有些不合适的停顿。故A选项(不合适的停顿)正确。

3We can infer that the purpose of Whaley's project is to _____ .

  • A.help students see their own strengths
  • B.assess students' public speaking skills
  • C.prepare students for their future jobs
  • D.inspire students' love for politics
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

3.A　推理判断题。根据文章倒数第二段最后一句(他想要这些孩子学会夸耀自己)可知答案为A项(帮助学生看到他们自己的优点)。

4Which of the following best describes Whaley as a teacher?

  • A.Humorous.
  • B.Ambitious.
  • C.Caring.
  • D.Demanding.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

4.C　推理判断题。根据第二段他低声耳语支持学生、第五段中他想让学生夸耀自己和第六段中他认为夸耀自己和自己最好的品质对一个没有自信的孩子是很难的,可知他理解、关心学生们,故选C项(关心他人的)。

女性真正想要的：晚10点后不用回复工作消息

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    What Women Really Want: To Not Answer Work Emails at 10 p.m.

    女性真正想要的：晚上 10 点不再回复工作邮件

  2. 02

    Women, especially mothers, don't necessarily need remote work. What we need are plain old boundaries — jobs where work stops at a set time and allows other parts of life to exist without interruption.

    女性，尤其是职场妈妈，未必需要远程办公。我们真正想要的是纯粹的边界感——一份能在固定时间收尾、让生活其他部分不受打扰的工作。

  3. 03

    A 2017 paper showed that all workers disliked employer discretion jobs and were willing to take hefty pay cuts to avoid them. Yet these jobs are a significant part of our economy.

    2017 年的一项研究显示，所有劳动者都厌恶 “雇主自主决定工时” 的岗位，甚至愿意接受大幅降薪来避开这类工作。然而，这类岗位在我们的经济环境中仍占相当大的比例。

    hefty
    adj. 大幅的，高额的，特别沉重的
    discretion
    n. 自主决定的自由，自主权定权
  4. 04

    On-demand scheduling allows companies to set workers’ hours at will, sending them home from scheduled shifts, calling them in abruptly or shifting schedules at short notice. These demands are especially costly to women, who still carry the majority of home and child care responsibilities.

    “按需排班” 让企业可以随意安排员工工时：已排好的班次变动于是遣返员工回家、突然通知到岗，或是短时间内调整排班。这些要求对女性的影响尤为显著，因为她们仍然承担着大部分的家务和育儿责任。

    costly
    adj. 代价高昂的
    shift
    n. 排班，轮班
  5. 05

    Almost two thirds of corporate leaders who mandated return-to-office policies after the pandemic saw disproportionately higher numbers of women leave their companies.

    疫情后，近三分之二强制要求员工返岗的企业管理者发现，离职的女性比例明显偏高。

    mandate
    v. 强制执行
  6. 06

    But women's choices don't back up the idea that in-office work is entirely to blame. Nursing remains almost 90 percent female. Nursing shifts are typically rigid, often set far in advance. While hours on a given shift can be grueling, they're also a known quantity.

    但女性的职业选择并不支持 “办公室工作该负全责” 的说法。护理行业从业者中女性占比近 90%，其班次通常固定且提前安排。尽管单班工时可能繁重，但至少是可预知的。

    rigid
    adj. 严格的
    grueling
    adj. 折磨人的，把人累垮的
  7. 07

    Obstetricians were required to be on call at all hours to deliver babies. It was once believed that mothers who care for their own children simply couldn't do the job. But as those initial female doctors entered the job market, many patients preferred them. They used the market power to organize into group practices and whoever was on call when the patient went into labor would deliver the baby.

    产科医生曾需 24 小时待命处理分娩事宜，人们一度认为，身为母亲的女性根本无法胜任这份工作。但当首批女性产科医生进入职场后，许多患者更倾向于选择她们。这些女性医生借助市场影响力组建联合诊所，患者分娩时，由当时待命的医生负责接生。

    obstetrician
    n. 妇产科医生
  8. 08

    Today, over 60 percent of OBs are female. Your move, consulting.

    如今，超过 60% 的产科医生是女性。咨询行业，该你们行动了。

  9. 09

    Men don’t have to choose between being successful and being a man, but women were and still are expected to make such choices. ——Margaret Atwood

    男人不必在“成功”和“做一个男人”之间做选择，而女性在过去和现在，都需要在“成功”和“做一个女人”之间做这样的取舍。

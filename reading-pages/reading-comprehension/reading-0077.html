阅读理解

02-2014天津，B

学习、阅读与教育方式

A world-famous Canadian author,Margaret Atwood,has created the world's first long-distance signing device(装置),the LongPen.

After many tiring book-signing tours from city to city,Atwood thought there must be a better way to do them.She hired some technical experts and started her own company in 2004.Together they designed the LongPen.Here's how it works:The author writes a personal message and signature on a computer tablet(手写板)using a special pen.On the receiving end,in another city,a robotic arm fitted with a regular pen signs the book.The author and fan can talk with each other via webcams(网络摄像机)and computer screens.

Work on the LongPen began in Atwood's basement(地下室).At first,they had no idea it would be as hard as it turned out to be.The device went through several versions,including one that actually had smoke coming out of it.The inventing finally completed,test runs were made in Ottawa,and the LongPen was officially launched at the 2006 London Book Fair.From here,Atwood conducted two transatlantic book signings of her latest book for fans in Toronto and New York City.

The LongPen produces a unique signature each time because it copies the movement of the author in real time.It has several other potential applications.It could increase credit card security and allow people to sign contracts from another province.The video exchange between signer and receiver can be recorded on DVD for proof when legal documents are used.

“It's really fun,”said the owner of a bookstore,who was present for one of the test runs.“Obviously you can't shake hands with the author,but there are chances for a connection that you don't get from a regular book signing.”

The response to the invention hasn't been all favorable.Atwood has received criticism from authors who think she's trying to end book tours.But she said,“It will be possible to go to places that you never got sent to before because the publishers couldn't afford it.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Why did Atwood decide to invent the LongPen?

  • A.To set up her own company.
  • B.To win herself greater popularity.
  • C.To write her books in a new way.
  • D.To make book signings less tiring.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

1.D　推理判断题。由文章第二段第一句可知,经过许多次奔波于不同城市间的令人疲惫的巡回签名售书活动后,Atwood认为一定有一种更好的签售的办法。由此可推测,Atwood决定发明LongPen的目的就是让这种签售活动稍微轻松一点。故选择D项。

2How does the LongPen work?

  • A.It copies the author's signature and prints it on a book.
  • B.It signs a book while receiving the author's signature.
  • C.The webcam sends the author's signature to another city.
  • D.The fan uses it to copy the author's signature himself.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2.B　推理判断题。由第二段第四、五句可知LongPen的工作原理:作者用一支专用笔在电脑的手写板上写上个人信息和签名,另一个城市的接收方用配备了常规笔的机器手臂在书上签名。故选择B项。

3What do we know about the invention of the LongPen?

  • A.It has been completed but not put into use.
  • B.The basement caught fire by accident.
  • C.Some versions failed before its test run.
  • D.The designers were well-prepared for the difficulty.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　推理判断题。由第三段第三句The device went through several versions,including one that actually had smoke coming out of it.可知,LongPen在获得成功之前经历过数次失败。故选择C项。

4How could the LongPen be used in the future?

  • A.To draft legal documents.
  • B.To improve credit card security.
  • C.To keep a record of the author's ideas.
  • D.To allow author and fan to exchange videos.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4.B　细节理解题。由第四段中It has several other potential applications.It could increase credit card security and...可知,LongPen还有很多潜在的功能,比如说提高信用卡的安全性。故选择B项。

5What could be inferred from Paragraphs 5 and 6?

  • A.Atwood doesn't mean to end book tours.
  • B.Critics think the LongPen is of little use.
  • C.Bookstore owners don't support the LongPen.
  • D.Publishers dislike the LongPen for its high cost.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

5.A　推理判断题。根据最后一段最后一句Atwood所说的内容可推断出,她并没有打算结束她的巡回签名售书活动。故选择A项。

阅读难度参考：CSE 6

有钱有闲又健康，这届退休老人正带火全球旅游

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Boomers Are Wealthy, Healthy and Driving a Global Travel Boom

    婴儿潮一代：富足、健康，正掀起全球旅游热潮

    boomer
    n. 婴儿潮一代（baby boomer的简称，指二战后，1946–1964年间出生的一代人）
  2. 02

    After Jean Chow’s husband and mother died within six months of each other, she needed to get away.

    半年内丈夫与母亲相继离世后，琼・周需要出去走走。

  3. 03

    The retired mother and grandmother booked a 21-day tour on a private plane and visited six countries. The trip, one of two private-plane trips offered by Road Scholar, cost $93,000.

    这位退休的妈妈，也是奶奶，预订了一次为期21天的私人飞机旅行，游历了六个国家。该行程是老年游学机构 Road Scholar 推出的两个私人飞机旅行项目之一，费用为 93000 美元。

  4. 04

    Baby boomers collectively have more wealth than any other living generation, and they are splurging on travel. They have more free time and are in better shape than their parents and grandparents. That is reshaping travel on several fronts.

    婴儿潮一代的整体财富高于当今其他任何一代人，他们在旅行上的投入也相当阔绰。相比父辈与祖辈，他们拥有更多闲暇时间，身体状况也更好。这正从多个方面重塑旅游业。

    reshape
    v. 重新塑造，彻底改变（暗示一种结构性的、方向性的转变）
    collectively
    adv. 集体地，共同地
  5. 05

    Boomers want adventure, but also want nice accommodations, good food, smooth connections and fewer baggage hassles. A recent survey by AARP found that, regardless of income, boomers shop for bargains. Many use AI to find the best travel deals and use loyalty discounts.

    婴儿潮一代追求冒险，但同时也要住得舒服、吃得讲究、交通顺畅、行李少添麻烦。美国退休人员协会（AARP）近期一项调查发现：无论收入高低，婴儿潮一代都会精打细算。不少人还会借助人工智能查找最划算的旅行方案，同时还会使用会员折扣来省钱。

    hassle
    n. 麻烦，繁琐，讨人嫌的小事
    accommodation
    n. 住宿
  6. 06

    Victor Medina, founder of Palante Wealth Advisors, works with his clients to create buckets of funds, including a “great life” bucket for things like travel. He says clients are increasingly intentional about travel. Rather than spend a week in a nice hotel in Europe, they book an Airbnb in a more remote area to immerse themselves in a country and culture.

    帕兰特财富顾问的创始人维克多·梅迪纳在为客户做财务规划时，会帮他们把资金分成几个不同的“账户”，包括一个专门用于旅行等体验的账户，他称之为“美好生活”账户。他表示，客户在选择旅行时越来越有目的性。与其在欧洲的高级酒店住一周，他们宁愿在更偏远的地方订一间爱彼迎民宿，把自己完全沉浸到这个国家和文化中。

    intentional
    adj. 有意图的，刻意为之的
  7. 07

    Plenty of boomers are happy to indulge. For them, Road Scholar began its private-plane trips in 2019, allowing people to see several of the world’s wonders in one trip.

    大量婴儿潮一代愿意尽情享受旅行。针对这类人群，Road Scholar 于 2019 年推出私人飞机旅行项目，让人们一趟旅程就能看遍多处世界奇观。

    indulge
    v. 放纵，沉溺，尽情享受
  8. 08

    “The boomer ethos is how much time I have left and what is important to me,” says James Moses. “They want to do as much as they can in a reasonable time frame.”

    詹姆斯・摩西说：“婴儿潮一代人的核心观念是，我还剩下多少时光，什么对我才最重要。他们希望在尚可把握的时间范围内，尽可能多地去体验。”

    ethos
    n. 精神特质，核心价值观

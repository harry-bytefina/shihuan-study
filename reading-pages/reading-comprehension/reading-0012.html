04-2024天津春，C

睡眠、运动与身心健康

Humans have evolved (进化) to spend long periods doing physical exercise. But why doesn't everyone enjoy exercising? The complexity of the human brain is to blame.

Evolving an ability doesn't automatically mean we'll want to use it. While physical exercise isn't that bad, it's still typically unpleasant. It has to be: you're pushing your body to its physical limits, which leads to significant discomfort.

Another issue is that the human brain is highly sensitive to wasted effort. Studies have shown that it calculates the effort required for actions and tends to stop us from squandering vital resources on fruitless efforts, like walking over 30 km for a handful of berries.

The thing is, regular exercise to get “in shape” requires constant and considerable effort—all for gradual progress and uncertain rewards. So, your brain's tendency to ask, “Is it worth it?” will be hard to quieten. This particular feature also means we typically prefer things which offer minimum effort for maximum reward.

Thankfully, the human brain is a complex organ. It isn't ruled by its more primitive drives. While many species' thought processes are limited to “Food, eat it!”, “Danger, run!”, “Pain, avoid!”, we've evolved beyond that. Our brains can form multiple long-term goals and ambitions. We can imagine a desirable future scenario(情况), figure out how we'd achieve it, and do just that. Or at least work toward it. This directly impacts how our brain processes motivation and willpower. It makes us capable of delayed gratification(满足): we can recognize that rejecting a reward now can lead to a greater reward later, and act accordingly.

So how does the brain process motivation? The self-discrepancy(自我差异) theory suggests we have several “selves” active in our minds at any given time: our “actual” self, our “ideal” self, and our “ought” self. Your “actual” self is how you are right now. Your “ideal” self is what you want to be. And your “ought” self is the self that does what you ought to be doing to become your “ideal” self. So, if your “ideal” self is a professional footballer, and your “actual” self isn't, your “ought” self is the one that spends a lot of time training, exercising, and getting better at football.

As far as your brain is concerned, there are processes that discourage exercise, and processes that encourage it. Ideally, you'll end up putting more weight on the latter than the former.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What can be learnt from the first two paragraphs?

  • A.Some people are born physically weaker than others.
  • B.Some people dislike driving themselves too hard physically.
  • C.Most people have the habit of doing physical exercise.
  • D.Most people do physical exercise for comfort and pleasure.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1. B　主旨要义题。文章首段提出了“But why doesn't everyone enjoy exercising?(但是为什么不是每个人都喜欢锻炼?)”的问题。随后作者给出了答案“The complexity of the human brain is to blame.(人类大脑的复杂性是罪魁祸首)”,这为后续的解释做了铺垫。第二段中提及体育锻炼通常是不愉快的(typically unpleasant),因为它会将身体推向极限,导致明显的不适(leads to significant discomfort)。B项中driving...too hard physically和原文pushing...body to its physical limits是同义表达,故选B。

2Which is closest in meaning to the underlined word “squandering” in Paragraph 3?

  • A.Conserving.
  • B.Mixing.
  • C.Misusing.
  • D.Sharing.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2. C　推测词义题。画线词后like walking over 30 km for a handful of berries是对fruitless efforts(无效努力)的举例说明,此处可借助这个例子来理解squandering的含义。walking over 30 km for a handful of berries描述了为了少量浆果而行走超过30千米的行为,这种行为的投入(行走超过30千米)与产出(少量浆果)不成正比,是一种对资源(体力等资源)的浪费。第三段第一句“Another issue is that the human brain is highly sensitive to wasted effort.”指出人类的大脑对浪费精力非常敏感。画线词所在句中的it指代前一句话中的human brain。由此可推知,人类大脑往往会阻止我们把重要的资源滥用在无效努力上。故选C。

3What is the author's purpose in writing Paragraph 4?

  • A.To raise a question.
  • B.To provide a definition.
  • C.To make a comparison.
  • D.To further explain a point.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D　写作意图题。题干询问写作意图,首先要理解该段落的主要信息,然后需要判断该段落是在提问、下定义、作比较还是进一步解释前面提到的观点。回答这一题需要回顾前文的内容,看这段与之前内容的逻辑关系。第三段提出人脑对无效努力很敏感,会阻止我们将重要资源浪费在无效努力上(stop us from squandering vital resources on fruitless efforts)。第四段提出常规锻炼塑形需要持续且大量的努力(constant and considerable effort),而回报却是不确定的。基于以上原因,大脑会不自主产生“这样做值得吗?”的疑问。这种特性使得人们通常倾向于选择那些能用最小努力获取最大回报的事情(prefer things which offer minimum effort for maximum reward)。第四段从锻炼的投入、大脑的反应以及普遍偏好三方面详细阐述了第三段首句提到的“人类大脑对无效努力高度敏感(the human brain is highly sensitive to wasted effort)”这一观点。故选D。

4According to the author,how are humans different from many other species?

  • A.Humans are able to picture a favorable future.
  • B.Humans benefit more from painful experiences.
  • C.Humans care more about immediate rewards.
  • D.Humans are skilled at avoiding dangers.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4. A　细节理解题。根据第五段中的“While many species' thought processes are limited...we've evolved beyond that.”以及“We can imagine a desirable future scenario”可知,人类的进化已经超越了满足果腹之需、本能地躲避危险以及下意识避免疼痛,人类能够按自己的期待勾画未来。A项中picture a favorable future与原文的imagine a desirable future scenario是同义表达,故选A。

5According to the self-discrepancy theory,who presents the most active “ought” self?

  • A.A footballer dreaming of becoming a movie star.
  • B.A student eating potato chips while watching TV.
  • C.A professional dancer performing at a theatre.
  • D.A swimmer training to win an Olympic medal.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

5. D　推理判断题。根据题干关键词self-discrepancy和“ought”self定位在倒数第二段。“自我差异”理论提出了头脑中有“实际”自我(“actual”self)、“理想”自我(“ideal”self)和“应该”自我(“ought”self)。作者通过举例子来阐释这三个概念:如果“理想”自我是成为一名专业的足球运动员,而“实际”自我并不是,那么“应该”自我则花费时间训练从而更擅长足球,即为目标持续努力。由此推断,D项(一个为了赢得奥运奖牌而训练的游泳运动员)表现出了最积极的“应该”自我。 故选D。

外刊延伸

阅读难度参考：CSE 9

“救命，我可能是个传统主妇”：一个职业女性的厨房自白

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    When I heard that Netflix was rebooting “Little House on the Prairie,” I was hopeful, if a little wary. The “Little House” reboot arrives in the midst of a tradwife moment.

    当得知网飞将要翻拍《草原上的小木屋》时，我心怀期待，却也略带警惕。这场《草原上的小木屋》的翻拍热潮，恰逢传统主妇风潮盛行的当下。

    tradwife
    n. 传统全职妻子，传统家庭主妇（traditional wife的简称）
    wary
    adj. 谨慎的，提防的，有所防备的
  2. 02

    As a woman who’s always believed that my life purpose is synonymous not with my fecundity or my homemaking prowess but with my artistic ambitions, I’m decidedly not the audience for this kind of content.

    我一直坚信，自己的人生价值不在于生育能力，也不在于持家本领，而在于艺术理想。因此，我显然绝不是这类内容的受众。

  3. 03

    But maybe it was the moment when I was slicing strawberries and scooping homemade coconut-almond granola into my son’s Greek yogurt that a startling revelation dawned on me. I’m definitely not a tradwife — but maybe I’m a little tradwife-curious? Maybe I have a few latent tradwife tendencies?

    但就在我切着草莓、把自制的椰香杏仁格兰诺拉麦片舀进儿子的希腊酸奶里的那一刻，我突然有了一个惊人的感悟。我绝对不是一个传统主妇——但我会不会对这种生活心生向往？我身上会不会潜藏着一些传统主妇的特质？

    latent
    adj. 潜在的，潜伏的，尚未显露的
  4. 04

    How can I renounce the regressive side of tradwives while blissfully kneading my own milk bread dough? How can I deny the appeal of the Hannah Neelemans of the world when my own household habits help balance my workaholism, keep me present and mindful in my body?

    我该如何一边满心愉悦地揉制牛奶面包面团，一边摒弃传统主妇观念中保守落后的一面？当我的居家日常能够克制我的工作狂倾向，让我专注当下、身心沉静时，我又怎能否认汉娜·尼勒曼这类生活博主的独特吸引力呢？

    workaholism
    n. 工作狂倾向
  5. 05

    So, like many others, I’m learning to reassess the value of so-called grandma hobbies, those frontier-adjacent activities that — surprise, surprise — don’t also require you to be a religious zealot or an antisuffragist.

    所以，和很多人一样，我开始重新评估那些所谓“奶奶辈爱好”的价值。那些贴近拓荒时代的生活活动，说来意外，它们并不需要你成为宗教狂热者，也不需要你反对女性选举权。

    antisuffragist
    n. 反对妇女选举权的人
  6. 06

    I imagine other people find this same kind of contemplation in sewing, or knitting, or mending. So even if I don’t live the full tradwife lifestyle — for anyone else’s consumption, or my own — I can still venerate those tasks that remind me that who I am meant to be is fluid and multitudinous. It can be expressed in how I stir a wooden spoon through water and flour to make the tangzhong that is the starter for my son’s favorite milk bread, and also in a poem I might write, born of my fascination with the same word.

    我想，其他人也能在缝纫、编织、缝补这类小事中获得同样的沉思与治愈。所以，即便我不会为了取悦他人或满足自我，完全复刻传统主妇的生活方式，我依然敬重这些琐事。它们让我明白，人的自我定位是流动且多元的。这份自我，既可以体现在我手持木勺、搅拌水和面粉，制作出儿子最爱的牛奶面包所需的烫种里，也可以体现在我提笔写下的诗句中，而这首诗正源于我对同一个词汇的着迷。

    tangzhong
    n. 汤种（烘焙术语）
    contemplation
    n. 深思，沉思，静心观想
    venerate
    v. 尊崇，崇尚

    句子结构

    It can be expressed in how I stir a wooden spoon through water and flour / to make the tangzhong that is the starter for my son’s favorite milk bread, / and also in a poem I might write, / born of my fascination with the same word.

    in how I stir … 和 in a poem … 同受 can be expressed 支配。第一个 in 后接 how 从句；第二个 in 后是名词短语，I might write 修饰 poem，省略了作 write 宾语的关系词。born of … 补充交代这首诗何以写成。

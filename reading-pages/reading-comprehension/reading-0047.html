阅读理解

04-2019天津夏，D

学习、阅读与教育方式

Would you BET on the future of this man? He is 53 years old. Most of his adult life has been a losing struggle against debt and misfortune. A war injury has made his left hand stop functioning, and he has often been in prison. Driven by heaven-knows-what motives, he determines to write a book.

The book turns out to be one that has appealed to the world for more than 350 years. That former prisoner was Cervantes, and the book was Don Quixote(《堂吉诃德》). And the story poses an interesting question:why do some people discover new vitality and creativity to the end of their days, while others go to seed long before?

We've all known people who run out of steam before they reach life's halfway mark. I'm not talking about those who fail to get to the top. We can't all get there. I'm talking about people who have stopped learning on growing because they have adopted the fixed attitudes and opinions that all too often come with passing years.

Most of us, in fact, progressively narrow the variety of our lives. We succeed in our field of specialization and then become trapped in it. Nothing surprises us. We lose our sense of wonder. But, if we are willing to learn, the opportunities are everywhere.

The things we learn in maturity seldom involve information and skills. We learn to bear with the things we can't change. We learn to avoid self-pity. We learn that however much we try to please, some people are never going to love us—an idea that troubles at first but is eventually relaxing.

With high motivation and enthusiasm, we can keep on learning. Then we will know how important it is to have meaning in our life. However, we can achieve meaning only if we have made a commitment to something larger than our own little egos(自我),whether to loved ones, to fellow humans, to work, or to some moral concept.

Many of us equate(视……等同于)“commitment”with such “caring”occupations as teaching and nursing. But doing any ordinary job as well as one can is in itself an admirable commitment. People who work toward such excellence—whether they are driving a truck, or running a store—make the world better just by being the kind of people they are. They've learned life's most valuable lesson.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1The passage starts with the story of Cervantes to show that _____ .

  • A.loss of freedom stimulates one's creativity
  • B.age is not a barrier to achieving one's goal
  • C.misery inspires a man to fight against his fate
  • D.disability cannot stop a man's pursuit of success
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　推理判断题。根据第一段可知,塞万提斯历经磨难,在53岁时才开始写《堂吉诃德》,根据第二段最后一句可知,塞万提斯的故事引出了一个有趣的问题:为什么有些人在生命晚年发现新的生命力和创造性,而其他人则在很久之前就失去活力了?由此可见B项符合题意,意为“年龄并不是实现个人目标的障碍”。

2What does the underlined part in Paragraph 3 probably mean?

  • A.End one's struggle for liberty.
  • B.Waste one's energy taking risks.
  • C.Miss the opportunity to succeed.
  • D.Lose the interest to continue learning.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　猜测词义题。根据第三段最后一句“I'm talking about people who have stopped learning on growing...”可知,作者谈到的是停止学习的人,故D项“失去继续学习的兴趣”符合题意。

3What could be inferred from Paragraph 4?

  • A.Those who dare to try often get themselves trapped.
  • B.Those who tend to think back can hardly go ahead.
  • C.Opportunity favors those with a curious mind.
  • D.Opportunity awaits those with a cautious mind.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　推理判断题。由本段内容可知,我们在某个领域取得成功后就会被困在里面,从而失去了好奇心。由最后一句“But, if we are willing to learn, the opportunities are everywhere.”可知,但是如果我们愿意学习,机会便无处不在,故C项(机会青睐那些有好奇心的人)为正确答案。

4What does the author intend to tell us in Paragraph 5?

  • A.A tough man can tolerate suffering.
  • B.A wise man can live without self-pity.
  • C.A man should try to satisfy people around him.
  • D.A man should learn suitable ways to deal with life.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　推理判断题。第五段首句指出“我们在成熟期所学习的东西很少涉及信息和技能”,结合下文中的三句话“We learn to...We learn to...We learn that...”可知,本段主要说明了我们应该学习的东西,故D项符合本段内容,意为“一个人应该学习应对生活的适当方式”。

5What is the author's purpose in writing the passage?

  • A.To provide guidance on leading a meaningful adult life.
  • B.To stress the need of shouldering responsibilities at work.
  • C.To state the importance of generating motivation for learning.
  • D.To suggest a way of pursuing excellence in our lifelong career.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

5.A　推理判断题。由全文可知,作者通过塞万提斯创作《堂吉诃德》的例子阐述了学习的重要性,学习让机会无处不在,学习让生活有意义,由此可知A项(就如何过上有意义的成年生活提供指导)符合作者的写作目的。

小米汽车工厂门票，为何成了虎妈们的必争之地？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Tiger Moms Battle for the Hottest Ticket in China: a Tour of a Factory Floor

    虎妈争抢中国最火门票：一次工厂车间之旅

  2. 02

    When Yang Xingyue heard that tech company Xiaomi was offering free tours of its electric-vehicle plant, she dropped everything and applied for tickets.

    当杨星月（Yang Xingyue，音译）听说科技公司小米向公众开放其电动汽车工厂的免费参观名额时，她立刻放下手头的一切去抢票。

  3. 03

    The odds weren't good. Influencers looking for a perfect photo op competed against tiger moms hoping that a glimpse of the robot-powered assembly lines might inspire their kids to pursue tech careers. More than a hundred thousand people entered the lottery for about two hundred tours in December and January, making the EV plant harder to get into than some competitive colleges.

    中签概率微乎其微。网红和宝妈势均力敌，前者想要拍出“完美打卡照”，而后者则希望让孩子亲眼看看机器人装配线，从而激励他们未来投身到科技行业。去年12月至今年1月，大约200个参观名额吸引了超过10万人报名抽签，这让进电动车工厂参观变得比考入一些名牌大学还要难。

    lottery
    抽签分配名额；参观资格靠随机抽取，文中没有购买彩票的意思。
    odds
    成功的可能性；the odds weren't good 指报名者多、中签希望小。
    glimpse
    短暂的一瞥；a glimpse of the assembly lines 指让孩子亲眼看看装配线。
  4. 04

    Yang, a car aficionado, was selected for a tour last March. During the one-hour tour, she saw hundreds of automated robotic arms and machines on the factory floor. According to Xiaomi, the plant churns out a new car every 76 seconds. "It was a very advanced industrial factory," she said. "I thought, 'Oh, so this is how cars are made.'"

    杨星月是名汽车发烧友，去年3月她幸运中签，获得了参观机会。在那一小时的参观中，她在车间看到了数百个自动化机械臂和各类设备。据小米方面的数据，这座工厂平均每76秒就能下线一辆新车。“这是一座非常先进的工业工厂，”她说，“我当时想，‘噢，原来车是这么造出来的。’”

    aficionado
    对某事有浓厚兴趣的人；a car aficionado 是汽车发烧友。
    automated
    自动化的；这里修饰能按程序运作的机器和机械臂。
  5. 05

    That's one reason industrial tourism is taking over China. Beijing is drumming up national pride in its high-tech manufacturing. Chinese companies, meanwhile, are looking for novel ways to connect with customers in the country's uber-competitive market. People are flocking to factory tours hosted by EV maker NIO, dairy manufacturer Mengniu, brewer Tsingtao and others.

    这正是“工业旅游”在中国迅速兴起的原因之一。政府正在大力激发人们对高科技制造业的民族自豪感。与此同时，在竞争异常激烈的国内市场下，中国企业也在寻找与消费者连接的新模式。越来越多的人涌向蔚来汽车（NIO）、蒙牛乳业（Mengniu）、青岛啤酒（Tsingtao）等企业主办的工厂参观活动。

    flocking
    成群涌向；flock to factory tours 突出许多人都去参观工厂。
    novel
    新颖的；novel ways 是企业与消费者建立联系的新办法，novel 在此是形容词。
  6. 06

    One mom who scored a slot last month said seeing her daughter's eyes light up in the factory made the trip from their home in neighboring Hebei province worth it.

    一位上个月抢到名额的母亲说，当她看到女儿在工厂里眼睛发亮的那一刻，便觉得从邻近的河北省赶来的一切奔波都值了。

    slot
    一个可获得的名额；scored a slot 是抢到了一次参观机会。
  7. 07

    "The best education is letting children see the power of technology with their own eyes," she wrote on social-media platform Xiaohongshu. "It's more effective than saying 'study hard' a hundred times."

    “最好的教育，是让孩子亲眼看见科技的力量。”她在社交平台小红书上写道，“这比说一百遍‘好好学习’都管用。”

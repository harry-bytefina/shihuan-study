06-2024新课标Ⅱ，B

文化遗产、艺术与跨文化

Do you ever get to the train station and realize you forgot to bring something to read? Yes, we all have our phones, but many of us still like to go old school and read something printed.

Well, there's a kiosk(小亭) for that. In the San Francisco Bay Area, at least.

“You enter the fare gates(检票口) and you'll see a kiosk that is lit up and it tells you can get a one-minute, a three-minute, or a five-minute story,” says Alicia Trost, the chief communications officer for the San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit—known as BART. “You choose which length you want and it gives you a receipt-like short story.”

It's that simple. Riders have printed nearly 20,000 short stories and poems since the program was launched last March. Some are classic short stories, and some are new original works.

Trost also wants to introduce local writers to local riders. “We wanted to do something where we do a call to artists in the Bay Area to submit stories for a contest,” Trost says. “And as of right now, we've received about 120 submissions. The winning stories would go into our kiosk and then you would be a published artist.”

Ridership on transit(交通) systems across the country has been down the past half century, so could short stories save transit?

Trost thinks so.

“At the end of the day all transit agencies right now are doing everything they can to improve the rider experience. So I absolutely think we will get more riders just because of short stories,” she says.

And you'll never be without something to read.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Why did BART start the kiosk program?

  • A.To promote the local culture.
  • B.To discourage phone use.
  • C.To meet passengers' needs.
  • D.To reduce its running costs.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C　细节理解题。本题问BART开设小亭项目的原因。第二段第一句“Well, there's a kiosk for that.”提示了答案。句中的that指代上文内容。根据第一段内容可知,BART开设小亭项目是为了满足没有带读物进火车站,但又想阅读纸质读物的乘客的需求。故选C。

2How are the stories categorized in the kiosk?

  • A.By popularity.
  • B.By length.
  • C.By theme.
  • D.By language.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2. B　细节理解题。根据第三段中的“you can get a one-minute, a three-minute, or a five-minute story”以及“You choose which length you want”可知,阅读小亭里的故事是根据长度(length)分类的。故选B。

3What has Trost been doing recently?

  • A.Organizing a story contest.
  • B.Doing a survey of customers.
  • C.Choosing a print publisher.
  • D.Conducting interviews with artists.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

3. A　细节理解题。根据第五段中的“We wanted to do something where we do a call to artists in the Bay Area to submit stories for a contest”以及“And as of right now, we􀆳ve received about 120 submissions.”可知,Trost正在号召旧金山湾区的艺术家们提交作品参加比赛,目前已收到约120份作品。所以,Trost最近在组织一场故事创作比赛。故选A。

4What is Trost's opinion about BART's future?

  • A.It will close down.
  • B.Its profits will decline.
  • C.It will expand nationwide.
  • D.Its ridership will increase.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4. D　推理判断题,考查Trost对BART的未来的看法。根据倒数第二段中的“I absolutely think we will get more riders just because of short stories”可知,Trost认为他们会因短篇故事而得到更多乘客。由此可推知,Trost认为BART的客流量(ridership)会增加。故选D。  重点词汇　original adj.独创的;起初的　submit v.提交

失踪20年的画作重现人间：AI追查系统立大功

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    A painting went missing 20 years ago. Police tracked it down with the help of AI

    一幅画作在20年前失窃。警方借助AI将它找回。

  2. 02

    During their routine visit to the annual antiquity fair in Parma, Italy, in October 2024, police officers busily snapped photos of the artworks as they walked between the stands. It wasn't art appreciation – it was detective work.

    2024年10月，警员在意大利帕尔马一年一度的古董博览会上例行巡查，穿梭于各个展位之间，不停地给艺术品拍照。不过，他们此行并非为了赏画，而是在执行一项侦查工作。

  3. 03

    Just a couple of hours later, though, the police system flagged one of the snapshots with an alert signal: a painting of a young, fair-skinned woman wearing aquamarine and red, holding up a half-unravelled parchment scroll.

    然而，仅数小时后，警方系统就对其中一张快照发出警报：画中是一名肤色白皙的年轻女子，身着水蓝色与红色衣裙，手举一卷展开了一半的羊皮纸卷轴。

    scroll
    n. 卷轴
    half-unravelled
    adj. 半展开的
    aquamarine
    n. 蓝绿色；adj. 蓝绿色的
  4. 04

    While Lt Col Salvatore Rapicavoli's team of the police unit for the Protection of Cultural Heritage of Italy has specialised in tracking down stolen artworks nationwide for decades, Rapicavoli says that the novel, tailor-made artificial intelligence system they were testing at the fair has since allowed the team to detect stolen art more quickly and helped them to spot details beyond what the human eye can normally see. Crucially, it can match hidden visual fingerprints in the works – pairing similarities between shades of colour and rough silhouettes.

    数十年来，意大利文化遗产保护警察部队的萨尔瓦托雷・拉皮卡沃利中校（Lt Col Salvatore Rapicavoli）带领的团队一直在全国范围内追查被盗艺术品。拉皮卡沃利表示，他们当时在集市上测试的这套全新定制的AI系统，如今已帮助团队更快发现被盗艺术品，还能捕捉到人眼通常难以察觉的细节。重要的是，它可以比对艺术品中隐藏的视觉“指纹”，也就是配对色彩深浅与大致轮廓，找出彼此相似的作品。

    crucially
    adv. 关键的是
  5. 05

    A manual search for a painting of a "woman with a scroll" in the stolen artworks database wouldn't have surfaced the painting stolen 20 years earlier. It had apparently then been painted over to appear less recognisable – the lamb now the scroll, and the halo and the palm branch erased to turn the Saint into a young pagan woman instead.

    如果在被盗艺术品数据库里手动检索 “持卷轴女子” 这幅画，根本搜不到这幅20年前就被盗的作品。这幅画当时显然被人重新涂盖过，就是为了让它难以辨认：原画中的羔羊被改成了卷轴，光环与棕榈枝都被抹除，一位圣女就被改画成了一名年轻的异教女子。

    erase
    v. 抹除
    manual
    adj. 手工的，人力的
  6. 06

    Still, AI isn't a silver bullet against art theft. For one, there are still many technical issues to iron out. And not to forget that the same AI tools helping investigators can also help the criminals, allowing them to manipulate an image, change a frame, digitally repair damaged areas on a painting, and, fabricate false provenance documents or create realistic synthetic objects that don't actually exist.

    但AI并不是打击艺术品盗窃的万能良方。首先，还有不少技术问题有待解决。另外，不能忽视的是，这套助力调查人员的AI工具，同样可能被犯罪分子利用：他们可以借此篡改图像、更换画框、对画作破损处做数字修复，还能伪造来源证明文件，或是生成看似逼真、实际上根本不存在的合成品。

    fabricate
    v. 凭空捏造（带有明确的欺骗意图）
    manipulate
    v. 操纵，篡改

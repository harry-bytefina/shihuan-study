Job for You!

Family Alliance Meal Delivery

Family Alliance is looking for people who can deliver hot Chinese meals one to two times a week for people with disabilities in north suburban Fairfield County.

All applicants must have a driver's license and access to a car. Gas will be covered for each delivery.

Working hours:10 a.m. to 1 p.m. Tuesdays and Fridays

For more information, contact Charles Chen at cchen@famd.com

Or call 740-6668988

Lancaster Design

We provide full furnishings, like donated kitchen equipment and furniture items, for the new homes of people who were once homeless.

We need people who can work at our warehouse (仓库) sorting and packing items to be delivered to families, repair donated furniture and more.Candidates will be matched with a task based on skill set.

Interested? Submit your application to www.lancasterdesign.org

Or call 740-6119735

Lancaster Weekly

Would you like the opportunity to earn extra cash close to home? If so we have a vacancy in our team which would suit you.Both adults and youngsters aged 13+ are required to deliver our newspaper.You may have to cover some distance.Bicycles are available if needed.

Working hours:8 a.m. to 10 a.m. Saturdays

For more information

Call 740-6538360

Parkview Nursing Home

Caregivers are wanted.We seek people who are patient and caring, and who have experience and interest in working with older adults.

As part of the package, initial and follow-up training sessions are provided and accommodation is available if required.

For more details, visit us at Unit 3 Park View Lane

Or contact Ellen White at 740-6968399

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Which job requires a driver's license?

  • A.Delivering meals.
  • B.Delivering newspapers.
  • C.Working at the warehouse.
  • D.Working at the nursing home.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1.A　细节理解题。根据Family Alliance Meal Delivery中All applicants must have a driver's license and access to a car.(所有的申请人都必须有驾照并能使用汽车。)可知答案选A。

2Which of the following is TRUE about the Lancaster Design job?

  • A.Applicants are expected to design furniture.
  • B.Work is arranged according to one's skill set.
  • C.It involves collecting furniture from homes.
  • D.It favors applicants who are homeless.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2.B　细节理解题。Lancaster Design广告中有一句Candidates will be matched with a task based on skill set.(候选人将根据技能分配任务。),故选B。

3A middle-school student is most likely to be hired by _____ .

  • A.Family Alliance Meal Delivery
  • B.Lancaster Design
  • C.Lancaster Weekly
  • D.Parkview Nursing Home
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　细节理解题。在Lancaster Weekly广告中有这句Both adults and youngsters aged 13+ are required to deliver our newspaper.(成人和13岁以上的青少年都要递送我们的报纸。),故选C。

4Which number should you call if you want to work with the elderly?

  • A.740-6668988.
  • B.740-6119735.
  • C.740-6538360.
  • D.740-6968399.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　细节理解题。 Parkview Nursing Home广告的主要工作内容为照顾老人,联系电话为740-6968399,故选D。 难句分析　原句:We seek people who are patient and caring, and who have experience and interest in working with older adults. 分析:本句中两个who引导定语从句,修饰people。 译文:我们寻找有耐心,有爱心,有与老年人一起工作的经验和兴趣的人。

写封邮件要1小时，中年人不敢说出口的健忘症危机

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    More Americans Are Dealing With Memory Decline at Work

    越来越多美国职场人正在应对记忆力衰退的困扰

  2. 02

    Kerry Dennis was in her mid 50s, managing a 200-person team at Fidelity Investments, when she began having trouble keeping details straight. A simple email took an hour to compose.

    克里·丹尼斯（Kerry Dennis）50多岁时，在富达投资集团管理着一支200人的团队，就在那时，她开始难以理清工作细节。写一封简单的邮件，竟要花上一小时。

    compose
    撰写。compose an email 指组织措辞写出一封邮件。
  3. 03

    During a call with her boss to review a campaign she had been working on for months, Dennis said her mind went blank. She went on short-term disability and was diagnosed with mild cognitive impairment, which can lead to dementia. Although her mom, uncle and grandmother had Alzheimer's, she pushed those thoughts to the back of her head and continued working for about four years after symptoms surfaced.

    在一次和上司通话、复盘她筹备了好几个月的一个项目时，丹尼斯说自己的大脑突然一片空白。她后来开始休“短期伤病假”，还被诊断出患有轻度认知障碍——这种病症有可能发展为痴呆症。尽管她的母亲、舅舅和外婆都患有阿尔茨海默病，但她一直刻意不去想这件事，在出现症状后仍坚持工作了大约4年。

    impairment
    功能受损。mild cognitive impairment 指轻度认知障碍。
    dementia
    痴呆症。文中说轻度认知障碍可能发展成这种病症。
    disability
    因病伤而无法正常工作。go on short-term disability 指暂时停止工作，进入短期伤病给付期。
    surface
    浮出；开始显现。symptoms surfaced 指症状开始被察觉。
  4. 04

    She seemed too young for such a memory problem, a widely held assumption. But age-related diseases are a workplace reality, says Katie Evans, chief programs and mission engagement officer for the Alzheimer's Association.

    她看起来还很年轻，不像是记忆力会出问题的人，这是人们普遍的看法。但阿尔茨海默病协会“首席项目与使命参与官”凯蒂·埃文斯（Katie Evans）表示，与年龄相关的疾病已成为职场中的一种现实。

  5. 05

    More people are or will be working with some memory lapse or cognitive decline, including those in their 50s. That, in part, reflects an aging workforce. About 62% of older adults work full time, up from 47% in 1987. Functional memory declines naturally with age, while dementia risk increases with age.

    现在或将来，会有越来越多的人，在记忆开始出现差错、认知能力有所下降的情况下继续工作，其中也包括50多岁的人。这在一定程度上反映了劳动力老龄化的趋势。目前约有62%的老年人从事全职工作，而1987年这一比例仅为47%。随着年龄增长，人的功能性记忆会自然衰退，患痴呆症的风险则会上升。

    lapse
    一时的差错或中断。memory lapse 指一时记不起某件事。
  6. 06

    It's not clear how many people are working with dementia. People are reluctant to acknowledge cognitive decline or dementia and primary-care doctors often miss the conditions.

    目前尚不清楚有多少人在患有痴呆症的情况下坚持工作。人们不愿承认自己出现认知衰退或痴呆症状，而家庭医生也常常漏诊这类病症。

    reluctant
    不情愿的。这里说一些员工不愿承认自己有认知衰退的迹象。
  7. 07

    About half of the workers with dementia were too afraid to tell their boss of their condition. The fear is especially acute for those supporting a family and too young for Social Security and Medicare.

    在患有痴呆症的员工中，大约一半人不敢告诉上司自己的病情。对于需要养家糊口、年龄又还没有到可以领取社会保障金和老年医疗保险福利的人来说，这种恐惧尤为强烈。

    acute
    强烈、尖锐的。the fear is acute 指一些既要养家、又没到领福利年龄的人特别不敢向上司坦白病情。
  8. 08

    The encouraging news: Many people continue to work in earlier stages of dementia, which can last two to four years. Flexible work schedules, technology-based reminders and modified roles help, as do supportive bosses.

    情况也并非全然悲观：许多人在痴呆症早期阶段仍能继续工作，这一阶段可能会持续2-4年。灵活的工作时间、基于技术手段的提醒、岗位职能的调整，以及给予下属支持的上司，都能提供帮助。

03-2022全国乙，C

AI、脑科学与新技术

Can a small group of drones(无人机) guarantee the safety and reliability of railways and, at the same time, help railway operators save billions of euros each year? That is the very likely future of applying today's “eyes in the sky” technology to making sure that the millions of kilometres of rail tracks and infrastructure(基础设施) worldwide are safe for trains on a 24/7 basis.

Drones are already being used to examine high⁃tension electrical lines. They could do precisely the same thing to inspect railway lines and other vital aspects of rail infrastructure such as the correct position of railway tracks and switching points. The more regularly they can be inspected, the more railway safety, reliability and on⁃time performance will be improved. Costs would be cut and operations would be more efficient(高效) across the board.

That includes huge savings in maintenance costs and better protection of railway personnel safety. It is calculated that European railways alone spend approximately 20 billion euros a year on maintenance, including sending maintenance staff, often at night, to inspect and repair the rail infrastructure. That can be dangerous work that could be avoided with drones assisting the crews' efforts.

By using the latest technologies, drones could also start providing higher⁃value services for railways, detecting faults in the rail or switches, before they can cause any safety problems. To perform these tasks, drones for rail don't need to be flying overhead. Engineers are now working on a new concept: the rail drones of the future. They will be moving on the track ahead of the train, and programmed to run autonomously. Very small drones with advanced sensors and AI and travelling ahead of the train could guide it like a co⁃pilot. With their ability to see ahead, they could signal any problem, so that fast⁃moving trains would be able to react in time.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What makes the application of drones to rail lines possible?

  • A.The use of drones in checking on power lines.
  • B.Drones' ability to work at high altitudes.
  • C.The reduction of cost in designing drones.
  • D.Drones' reliable performance in remote areas.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1.A　细节理解题。由第二段第一、二句Drones are already being used to examine high⁃tension electrical lines. They could do precisely the same thing to inspect railway lines and other vital aspects of rail infrastructure... (无人机已经被用来检查高压电线。它们完全可以做同样的事情来检查铁路线和铁路基础设施的其他重要方面……)可知,无人机在检查高压电线方面的应用使它们在铁路线方面的应用成为可能。

2What does “maintenance” underlined in paragraph 3 refer to?

  • A.Personnel safety.
  • B.Assistance from drones.
  • C.Inspection and repair.
  • D.Construction of infrastructure.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　猜测词义题。由第三段第二句尤其是sending maintenance staff和to inspect and repair the rail infrastructure可知,派遣人员是为了检查和维修铁路基础设施,因此maintenance表示“检查和维修”。

3What function is expected of the rail drones?

  • A.To provide early warning.
  • B.To make trains run automatically.
  • C.To earn profits for the crews.
  • D.To accelerate transportation.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

3.A　细节理解题。由最后一段最后一句With their ability to see ahead, they could signal any problem, so that fast⁃moving trains would be able to react in time.可知,铁路无人机有望具备预警的功能。

4Which is the most suitable title for the text?

  • A.What Faults Can Be Detected with Drones
  • B.How Production of Drones Can Be Expanded
  • C.What Difficulty Drone Development Will Face
  • D.How Drones Will Change the Future of Railways
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　主旨要义题。通读全文可知,本文介绍了一种可能很快就能应用的铁路无人机,并说明了它们可以保障铁路安全并能大量节省铁路维护费用。由此可知,文章主要说明无人机会如何改变铁路的未来,故以此为题目最合适。

烦人经济，正在吞噬你的钱包

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The ‘Annoyance Economy’ Is More Than Just Annoying

    “烦人经济”可不只是烦人而已

  2. 02

    Everyone knows the feeling of being stuck on hold, or spending time clicking through an endless series of pages to cancel a subscription, or screening spam calls, or trying to change a flight. These inconveniences make life less pleasant, but they are easy enough to write off as small concerns.

    人人都体会过这样的感受：打个电话需要排队等待接听；取消订阅需要没完没了地点击页面；各种骚扰电话需要一一屏蔽；试图改签航班却屡屡碰壁。这些不便固然令人不快，但也很容易被当作无关紧要的小事，一笑置之。

    subscription
    n. 订阅，订购
  3. 03

    It turns out that such time-sucking, tedious tasks have a meaningful economic impact. The accumulated cost of what some refer to as the “annoyance economy” adds up to $165 billion a year in lost time and wasted money for American families, according to a new report from Neale Mahoney, a Stanford economist.

    然而事实是，这类耗时费力、令人厌倦的琐事，会产生可观的经济影响。根据斯坦福大学经济学家尼尔·马霍尼（Neale Mahoney）的一份最新报告，这些被称为“烦人经济”的现象，日积月累下来代价巨大，美国家庭因此遭受的时间损失和金钱浪费价值高达每年1650亿美元。

    tedious
    adj. 枯燥的，让人厌烦的
  4. 04

    Some of the inconveniences plaguing American consumers are a result of clunky processes and outdated regulation. But some are, Mr. Mahoney said, “intentionally designed” to take advantage of people. And the rise of artificial intelligence chatbots that pose as real people is advancing the capacity for companies to mislead and target consumers.

    困扰美国消费者的一些不便，源于繁琐的流程以及过时的监管制度。但马霍尼指出，但其中一部分干扰是“刻意设计”出来的，目的就是占消费者便宜。而伪装成真人的聊天机器人的兴起，正在进一步提升企业误导和精准锁定消费者的能力。

    plague
    v. 长期困扰，不断折磨
    capacity
    n. 能力
    clunky
    adj. 老旧的，笨重的，卡顿的；（象声词）金属碰撞的“咣当”声
  5. 05

    Intentional or not, trapping consumers can pay off for corporations: companies that make it harder to cancel subscriptions see revenues tick up 14 to 200 percent. Beyond wasting money and time, logistical blocks can even affect health: A 2019 study found that almost a quarter of respondents had forgone or delayed health care because of administrative tasks.

    无论是否刻意为之，将消费者“困住”确实能为企业带来回报：那些让取消订阅变得更困难的公司，其收入会增长 14% 到 200%。除了浪费金钱和时间，这些流程上的障碍甚至会影响健康：2019 年的一项研究发现，近四分之一的受访者曾因手续繁琐而放弃或推迟就医。

    logistical
    adj. 流程上的，操作层面的
  6. 06

    The consistent inconveniences that people face in their day-to-day lives “become a reminder about how the system is stacked against them,” Mr. Mahoney reflected. Public distaste could help spur lawmakers to mandate more consumer-friendly policies and companies to dial back fees and the like.

    马霍尼先生表示，人们在日常生活中反复遭遇的这些不便，会不断提醒他们：整个系统是如何对他们不利的。而公众的不满情绪，或许会推动立法者制定更有利于消费者的政策，也促使企业减少各种收费与限制。

    spur
    v. 刺激，推动；马刺（骑马时踢马用的小金属刺）
    distaste
    n. 反感，厌恶
  7. 07

    This will take some work, he suggested, but it is possible. “We put people on the moon 50 years ago,” he said. “We should be able to figure out spam texts and phone calls.”

    他认为，这一改变确实需要付出努力，但并非不可能。“50年前我们就能把人送上月球，”他说，“我们也理应能解决垃圾短信和骚扰电话的问题。”

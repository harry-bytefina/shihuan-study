03-2021全国乙，C

生态、气候与城市自然

You've heard that plastic is polluting the oceans—between 4.8 and 12.7 million tonnes enter ocean ecosystems every year. But does one plastic straw or cup really make a difference? Artist Benjamin Von Wong wants you to know that it does. He builds massive sculptures out of plastic garbage, forcing viewers to re⁃examine their relationship to single⁃use plastic products.

At the beginning of the year, the artist built a piece called “Strawpocalypse,”a pair of 10⁃foot⁃tall plastic waves, frozen mid⁃crash. Made of 168,000 plastic straws collected from several volunteer beach cleanups, the sculpture made its first appearance at the Estella Place shopping center in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

Just 9% of global plastic waste is recycled. Plastic straws are by no means the biggest source(来源) of plastic pollution, but they've recently come under fire because most people don't need them to drink with and, because of their small size and weight, they cannot be recycled. Every straw that's part of Von Wong's artwork likely came from a drink that someone used for only a few minutes.Once the drink is gone,the straw will take centuries to disappear.

In a piece from 2018,Von Wong wanted to illustrate(说明) a specific statistic:Every 60 seconds, a truckload's worth of plastic enters the ocean. For this work, titled “Truckload of Plastic,” Von Wong and a group of volunteers collected more than 10,000 pieces of plastic, which were then tied together to look like they'd been dumped(倾倒)from a truck all at once.

Von Wong hopes that his work will also help pressure big companies to reduce their plastic footprint.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What are Von Wong's artworks intended for?

  • A.Beautifying the city he lives in.
  • B.Introducing eco⁃friendly products.
  • C.Drawing public attention to plastic waste.
  • D.Reducing garbage on the beach.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1.C　推理判断题。根据第一段中“He builds massive sculptures out of plastic garbage, forcing viewers to re⁃examine their relationship to single⁃use plastic products.”可知Von Wong把塑料废物制成了雕塑,让看到雕塑的人重新审视他们和一次性塑料产品之间的关系,让人们关注塑料污染。

2Why does the author discuss plastic straws in paragraph 3?

  • A.To show the difficulty of their recycling.
  • B.To explain why they are useful.
  • C.To voice his views on modern art.
  • D.To find a substitute for them.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2.A　推理判断题。根据第三段第一句“Just 9% of global plastic waste is recycled.”及第二句的“because of their small size and weight, they cannot be recycled”可知,全球只有9%的塑料废品被回收利用,吸管因小而且轻不能被回收利用。本段体现了塑料吸管的回收难度。

3What effect would “Truckload of Plastic” have on viewers?

  • A.Calming.
  • B.Disturbing.
  • C.Refreshing.
  • D.Challenging.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3.B　推理判断题。根据倒数第二段第一句“In a piece from 2018, Von Wong wanted to illustrate a specific statistic: Every 60 seconds, a truckload's worth of plastic enters the ocean.”可知,Von Wong在2018年的一件作品中,想说明每60秒就有一卡车的量的塑料被倒入海洋。这种景象会让人们感到震惊和担忧。

4Which of the following can be the best title for the text?

  • A.Artists' Opinions on Plastic Safety
  • B.Media Interest in Contemporary Art
  • C.Responsibility Demanded of Big Companies
  • D.Ocean Plastics Transformed into Sculptures
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　主旨要义题。依照说明文特点,主旨要义一般在第一段或最后一段体现。根据第一段最后一句话“He builds massive sculptures out of plastic garbage, forcing viewers to re⁃examine their relationship to single⁃use plastic products.”可知Von Wong把塑料垃圾制成艺术品,唤醒人们的环保意识。文章围绕这一点展开说明,故D项作为标题最合适。

水源危机下，伊朗面临迁都困局

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    How Did a City of 10 Million People Nearly Run Out of Water?

    一座千万人口城市何以濒临断水绝境？

  2. 02

    This month Tehran, a city of 10 million people, was questioning its viability. After six years of drought and the driest autumn in over 50 years, the reservoirs that supply the city fell to around 10 percent of capacity. City residents faced rolling water cuts and pleas from officials to limit washing.

    本月（2025年12月），拥有千万人口的德黑兰，其存续能力遭到质疑。历经六年干旱，又遭遇五十多年来最为干旱的秋季后，整座城市的供水水库蓄水量已降至总库容的 10% 左右。市民们既要面对分时段轮流停水的困扰，还需听从官方呼吁，限制用水。

    reservoir
    n. 蓄水池
    plea
    n. 恳求（带呼吁感）
    drought
    n. 旱灾
    viability
    n. 存活能力
  3. 03

    Thankfully, rain eventually fell, easing immediate pressure on water supplies. But the city came too close to disaster.

    所幸，降雨最终姗姗而至，暂时缓解了供水的燃眉之急。但这座城市，离灾难只有一步之遥。

  4. 04

    The drought conditions in Iran are in part a result of climate change: Much of the Middle East, including Iran, is warming twice as fast as the global average. Other factors may contribute even more to the crisis: rapid urbanization, growing population and rising water use. Tehran's population has risen from 700,000 during the 1940s to around 10 million today.

    伊朗的干旱状况，部分源于气候变化的影响：包括伊朗在内的中东大部分地区，升温速度是全球平均水平的两倍。还有其他因素，对这场危机的推动作用或许更为显著 —— 快速的城市化进程、持续增长的人口数量，以及攀升的用水量。德黑兰的人口已从 20 世纪 40 年代的 70 万，增长至如今的约 1000 万。

  5. 05

    Such rapid growth is unsustainable, Iran's president Masoud Pezeshkian has said, which is why he suggested the best course of action was to move the country's capital.

    伊朗总统马苏德・佩泽希齐扬（Masoud Pezeshkian）表示，如此迅猛的增长态势难以为继，所以他才提议将迁都作为最佳的后续行动方案。

    unsustainable
    adj. 无法维持的；不可持续的
  6. 06

    Relocating a capital demands tens of billions of dollars of investment. Iran is already cash strapped by sanctions, inflation and competing social needs. But such an effort would absorb scarce resources that might otherwise be directed toward addressing the underlying water crisis: getting serious about water management, pricing, agriculture's overuse and urban growth.

    迁都需要投入数百亿美元的巨额资金。而伊朗早已因制裁、通货膨胀以及各项民生需求的挤压，陷入了资金短缺的困境。而更关键的问题在于，迁都之举会占用本就稀缺的资源，而这些资源原本可以用于解决更根本的缺水危机：比如认真推进水资源管理、调控水价、整治农业用水浪费，以及管控城市扩张。

  7. 07

    Cities have come and gone throughout history. Many of the greatest urban centers of the past were eventually undone in part by failures to manage water.

    城市在历史长河中兴衰更替。许多昔日辉煌的大都市，最终走向衰落，部分原因正是源于水资源管理的失败。

    undone
    adj. 被毁灭的、被破坏的
  8. 08

    Like all species, humans depend on equilibrium with the natural world. Our ingenuity allows us to bend the rules — but not forever. In the long run, the bank always wins.

    与世间万物一样，人类也依赖于与自然的平衡共生。人类的智慧固然能让我们突破规则的限制 ——但不会永远如此。长远来看，庄家（大自然）才是永恒的赢家。

    ingenuity
    独创性、机智
    equilibrium
    n. 均衡、平衡

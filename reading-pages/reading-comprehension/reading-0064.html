学习、阅读与教育方式

Career guidance specialists in John Adams High School can provide information about Career & Technical Education classes, assist you in making wise decisions for your future, and help you find opportunities in: certificate programs, apprenticeship(学徒)programs and four-year colleges that best fit your needs.

Each of the following two-year programs offers three high school credits per semester and requires a formal application.

Business Cooperative Experiences

This is a program for students who have a career goal in business or office work.Students spend one period each day learning advanced skills related to the students' career objective in business.Students will be employed at a training station and will work each week at a business in the local community.

Important facts:

●　Computer applications

●　Work a total of 540 hours on earn credit

●　Transportation not provided

Business Technology Lab

This program will prepare students for careers in business and industry and for working with interactive media.Students will learn multimedia, web design, and photo design skills by using state-of-the-art(最先进的)equipment and software.This course will provide students with the tools to seek jobs or continue studying at a post-secondary school.

Important facts:

●　Available at Clay High School

●　Two-hour course

Automotive(汽车的)Services Technology

The program includes classroom instruction and lab activities for the students to learn entry-level automotive skills and basic automotive operation and service.Students will gain skills and training in auto repair that can lead to entry-level jobs, or post-secondary education.

Important facts:

●　Classes held at Ivy Tech

●　Transportation provided

Building Trades Technology

In this program, students, under the leadership of a qualified instructor, will build a house.It gives students basic skills needed to enter the building trades, or to continue study of construction in an apprentice program, or a post-secondary school.

Important facts:

●　Three-hour course

●　Transportation provided

●　Work boots and helmet needed

抗议浪潮席卷多国，欧洲年轻人为何感觉没有未来？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Student Protests Mushroom Across Europe, Exposing Bleak Views of the Future

    欧洲各地学生抗议蔓延，透露出对未来的悲观看法

    bleak
    adj. （形容境况）惨淡的，毫无希望的；（形容景色）荒凉的、凄凉的
  2. 02

    Young Spaniards and Portuguese have pitched tents in their countries’ capitals to protest high rents. French and Italian students have marched en masse for better school conditions in demonstrations marred by looters.

    西班牙和葡萄牙的年轻人为了抗议高昂的房租，在各自国家的首都搭起了帐篷。法国和意大利的学生为了改善校园条件而大规模游行，但示威活动中混入了抢劫者。

    looter
    n. 趁火打劫者，打砸抢烧商店的人
  3. 03

    These are among the young Europeans who have taken to the streets in recent days in at least eight countries across the continent. Their demands vary according to the context and the government, but a common thread unites them all: They fear for their future.

    近日欧洲大陆至少有八个国家的年轻人走上街头抗议，上述这些人正是他们中的成员。他们的诉求因具体情况和政府背景而有所不同，但他们有一点是共通的：他们对自己的未来感到担忧。

    vary
    v. 有差异，不同
    context
    n. 具体的环境、情况
  4. 04

    “The no-future generation is back,” Sacha Cossi, 27, a concert stagehand, said on Tuesday as he sat with a group of other young Spaniards under a low tarp in the tent encampment in central Madrid.

    “‘没有未来的一代’又回来了，”27岁的音乐会舞台工作人员萨沙·科西（Sacha Cossi）周二说道。当时，他正与一群西班牙年轻人一起坐在马德里市中心一个帐篷营地里低矮的防水布下。

    stagehand
    n. 舞台工作人员
  5. 05

    Governments of all ideological stripes — a right-wing leader in Italy, a left-wing one in Spain, a moderate in France — are all struggling to provide a plausible answer. Europe’s young increasingly feel left out of the picture.

    各种意识形态的政府——意大利的右翼领导人、西班牙的左翼领导人、法国的温和派领导人——都在挣扎着给出一个说得过去的答案。欧洲的年轻人越来越觉得自己被排除在外。

    plausible
    adj. 貌似行得通的，看起来似乎站得住脚的
    stripe
    n. 种类，类型；条纹
  6. 06

    Whoever takes power across Europe in the coming months will have few easy offerings for young people. They will face excruciating decisions about what public services to fund with declining resources in an environment marked by economic headwinds, including inflation, rising bond yields and soaring fuel prices.

    无论未来几个月欧洲各国由谁掌权，他们能轻易为年轻人拿出的政策方案都寥寥无几。他们将面临艰难的选择：在经济逆风的环境下——包括通货膨胀、债券收益率上升和燃料价格飙升，要把日益减少的资源用于资助哪些公共服务。

    inflation
    n. 通胀
  7. 07

    “My dream would be to have a job that I love,” said Salomé Regnaud, a 15-year-old student who has been protesting for better school conditions in France. “Not to have to look at my expenses every month and ask myself, ‘Will I be able to do this?’ or ‘Can I afford to buy that?’”

    “我的梦想就是能拥有一份热爱的工作，”15岁的学生萨洛梅·雷诺（Salomé Regnaud）说道，她一直在法国为改善学校条件而抗议。“不必每个月都要紧盯着花销，问自己‘我能做这个吗？’或者‘我买得起那个吗？’”

HABITAT RESTORATION TEAM

Help restore and protect Marin’s natural areas from the Marin Headlands to Bolinas Ridge. We’ll explore beautiful park sites while conducting invasive(侵入的) plant removal, winter planting, and seed collection. Habitat Restoration Team volunteers play a vital role in restoring sensitive resources and protecting endangered species across the ridges and valleys.

GROUPS

Groups of five or more require special arrangements and must be confirmed in advance. Please review the List of Available Projects and fill out the Group Project Request Form.

AGE, SKILLS, WHAT TO BRING

Volunteers aged 10 and over are welcome. Read our Youth Policy Guidelines for youth under the age of 15.

Bring your completed Volunteer Agreement Form. Volunteers under the age of 18 must have the parent/guardian approval section signed.

We’ll be working rain or shine. Wear clothes that can get dirty. Bring layers for changing weather and a raincoat if necessary.

Bring a personal water bottle, sunscreen, and lunch.

No experience necessary. Training and tools will be provided. Fulfills(满足) community service requirements.

1What is the aim of the Habitat Restoration Team?

  • A.To discover mineral resources.
  • B.To develop new wildlife parks.
  • C.To protect the local ecosystem.
  • D.To conduct biological research.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C　细节理解题。本题问生物栖息地修复工作组的目标(aim)是什么。根据第一段中的Help restore and protect Marin’s natural areas以及play a vital role in restoring sensitive resources and protecting endangered species across the ridges and valleys可知,生物栖息地修复工作组的目标是保护当地的生态系统。故选C。

2What is the lower age limit for joining the Habitat Restoration Team?

  • A.5.
  • B.10.
  • C.15.
  • D.18.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2. B　细节理解题。本题问加入生物栖息地修复工作组的最低年龄。根据AGE, SKILLS, WHAT TO BRING部分中的“Volunteers aged 10 and over are welcome.”可知,10岁及以上的志愿者都可以参加,所以最低年龄是10岁。故选B。

在秋天，有一种浪漫叫做“落叶不扫”

词汇梳理背景补充
  1. 01

    This Fall, Let's Stop Fighting the War on Leaves

    这个秋天，停止对落叶“宣战”吧

  2. 02

    Every autumn, as leaves turn from green to auburn and float to the ground, we turn into a nation of outdoor undertakers. There will be raking, and pile-making, and paper sacks stuffed with leaves, arrayed along curbs like defensive bulwarks.

    每到秋天，当树叶由绿色变成红褐色，轻轻飘落在大地上，全国民众就会集体化身“户外殡葬师”。大家用耙子清扫落叶，再把叶子堆成一堆，然后将塞满落叶的纸袋整齐地排列在路边，宛如一道道防御堡垒。

    array
    v. 把......排成一排;（军事上）配置兵力、排兵布阵
    undertaker
    n. 在殡仪馆工作的人（负责处理逝者尸体、安排丧礼细节等）
  3. 03

    I first encountered this strange ritual as a child, shortly after my family arrived in Connecticut from the Soviet Union in 1989. America's war on leaves made no sense to me then, and it makes no sense to me now.

    我第一次见识到这种奇特仪式的时候还是个孩子，那是在1989年，我刚随家人从苏联（the Soviet Union）搬家，来到了康涅狄格州（Connecticut）。彼时，美国的“落叶大战”在我看来毫无道理，如今依旧如此。

    ritual
    n. 老规矩
  4. 04

    Healthy soil requires "moisture and heat and warmth," says Annabel Renwick, a native plant curator at the Sarah P. Duke Gardens. Leaves provide all three, whether you leave them where they fall, scatter them in perennial beds or put them in a compost pile.

    莎拉·杜克花园（the Sarah P. Duke Gardens）的本土植物策展人安娜贝尔·伦尼克（Annabel Renwick）表示，健康的土壤需要“湿度、热能和温暖”，无论是让落叶留在原地，或是把它们撒到多年生植物的花坛里，还是把它们作为堆肥集中在一起，落叶都能为土壤提供所需的三种要素。

    句子结构

    Leaves provide all three, / whether you leave them where they fall, / scatter them in perennial beds / or put them in a compost pile.

    主句说「落叶能提供这三点」，all three 要回到上一句找，说的是水分和保暖方面的条件。whether … or … 引出三种处理方式，意思是「不管采用哪一种」。scatter 和 put 共用从句的主语 you；where they fall 只交代第一种方式的地点，即落叶原来掉下来的地方。

  5. 05

    Why do so many of us spend money and effort sweeping all that fecundity away? "I think it's habit," Renwick said. For many people, a lawn with leaves unblown or unraked offends their sensibilities. It suggests a household that lacks rules, or plays by rules other than the ones most of us have been inculcated with.

    为何我们中如此多人要费钱费力，就为了把这孕育生机的落叶一扫而净呢？“我觉得只是习惯使然。”伦尼克说。对许多人而言，看到草坪上有落叶没被吹扫或耙拢，就会让他们感到不舒服。它意味着这家人要么是没规矩，要么是遵守的规矩和大部分人被灌输的不一样。

    fecundity
    n. 生命孕育力，繁殖能力
    inculcate
    v.（向某人）灌输（信念或想法，通过一遍遍的重复）
  6. 06

    I've come to think that inertia is among the most pernicious forces that can work on a society or an individual. We keep doing the same thing because the cost—emotional, cultural—of doing something new is simply too high. We sink into habits we never really meant to adopt, habits we know are not making us happy or healthy.

    我渐渐意识到，惯性是作用于社会或个人的最有害力量之一。我们持续采取同一种做法，只是因为无论在情感还是文化层面，采取一种新做法的代价都太高了。我们深陷其中的那些习惯，都是无意之间养成的，而我们也知道，这些习惯对于我们的幸福和健康都没有任何好处。

    inertia
    n. 物体移动过程中的惯性；没动力没兴趣，惰性，摆烂
  7. 07

    Whatever else leaves are, they are real. As they change color, wither and flame out, they remind us of the beauty of life, and of life's impermanence.

    无论落叶还象征着什么，它们都是真实的存在。从色彩渐变、枯萎到凋零，它们都在提醒着我们，去看到生命之美，去直面人生无常。

    impermanence
    n. 无常；暂时性
  8. 08

    A drop of existential awareness may even make us a more humane society. After all, we too are leaves, facing inevitable autumn. A carpet of leaves teems with color and life, an ocean of reds and browns. Don't worry about the spiders. Dive right in.

    多一点存在主义的意识，甚至可能让我们的社会更有人情味。毕竟，我们每个人也都和树叶一样，终将面对生命中不可避免的“秋日”。一地落叶交织出斑斓色彩与蓬勃生机，宛如一片红褐相间的海洋。不要担心落叶里可能藏着蜘蛛，一头扎进去吧。

    existential
    adj. 存在主义的

背景补充

「把落叶留下」留在哪里？

杜克植物园会把落在草坪上的叶子移到邻近林地或覆盖区，既让草坪透光，又让叶片在园内分解。原文谈的 leave the leaves 并不是要求所有落叶原地不动，关键是因地处理，别把落叶一律当作垃圾运走。

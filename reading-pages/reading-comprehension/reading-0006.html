阅读理解

06-2025天津春，B

文化遗产、艺术与跨文化

I first encountered tai chi in Ottawa's Chinatown. Men and women gathered in loose formation. Their actions combined to achieve a meditative(冥想的) dance. I was amazed by its beauty and grace. It was like watching a water lily opening.

Yet I couldn't see the point. I could achieve my fitness goals from exercise classes, swimming and cycling—and moving so slowly didn't seem to build muscle, my objective for all activities. I chose only to admire this martial art(武术) but kept my distance.

Years later, a recommendation from my doctor brought me to a training group for tai chi beginners led by a caring instructor. I had thought that my goal would be to master the 108 moves. Technically right, but wrong on so many counts.

The class was full of lovely people who glided effortlessly, seemingly never confused about where to move next. They assured me that they had been beginners once too, and that I would be able to remember all the moves within three or four years. Three to four years! Graduate degrees took less time. But I could see they were right. Commit and improve: That was the only path.

Another realization came to me when I attended an advanced class. We began with foundation exercises, and almost immediately, the instructor singled me out for instruction. To be singled out was an honour, but I was mortified. All the others in the class stopped and turned to watch me while the instructor corrected my positioning. “Don't bend,” he said, and I repeated the movement. “Straighten your back,” he urged, and on it went—probably for only a few minutes, but it seemed like hours.

I couldn't say,“Yeah, I get it.” I had to show that I did. I concentrated as I had never done before, listening to what the instructor was saying, watching his movement and then willing my body to move in imitation. I was unlearning and learning, overcoming awkwardness to focus. I realized that I was learning a new way of being in the world—truly listening and open to correction.

I don't know that I will ever master the 108 moves. What I do know is that I have found an activity in my life where change and growth are not just possible but expected, even when my stubborn(固执的) personality tendencies re-emerge at every turn.

Gradually, I have become that lily unfolding, awoken not by sunlight and warmth but by this gentle martial art.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What was the author's first impression of tai chi?

  • A.Pleasant to the eye but not of much use.
  • B.Too slow to be regarded as a martial art.
  • C.Helpful in accomplishing fitness goals.
  • D.Mysterious and difficult to master.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1. A　细节理解题。根据前两段中amazed by its beauty and grace和didn't seem to build muscle, my objective for all activities可知,作者认为太极拳赏心悦目,但是没有太多用处,达不到健身长肌肉的目标。故选A。

2In the beginners' class, the author realized that mastering tai chi required _____ .

  • A.discipline
  • B.leadership
  • C.persistence
  • D.teamwork
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2. C　推理判断题。根据第三段和第四段中的to master the 108 moves和“to remember all the moves within three or four years. Three to four years! Graduate degrees took less time.”可推知,学习太极拳是一件长时间的事情,需要坚持不懈(persistence)。故选C。

3What does the underlined word “mortified” in Paragraph 5 probably mean?

  • A.Angered.
  • B.Confused.
  • C.Disappointed.
  • D.Embarrassed.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D　推测词义题。由“To be singled out was an honour, but I was mortified.”可知,作者虽觉得被单独指导是一种荣幸,但紧接着用but转折,说明实际感受与“荣幸”相反。再根据“All the others in the class stopped and turned to watch me”以及第六段中的awkwardness可推知,作者被教练单独指导,成为众人焦点,会感到尴尬、难堪。故选D。

4How did the author respond to the instructions in the advanced class?

  • A.She denied her weaknesses and carried on.
  • B.She stayed focused and followed attentively.
  • C.She worked hard and displayed her talent.
  • D.She opened up her mind and sought advice.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4. B　细节理解题。根据第六段中“I concentrated as I had never done before”以及truly listening and open to correction可知,作者聚精会神地聆听教练的指令,改进自己的动作。故选B。

5Which of the following can be concluded from the author's experience?

  • A.Tai chi empowers people to improve.
  • B.Tai chi keeps the balance of the body.
  • C.Tai chi fires people's passion for sports.
  • D.Tai chi facilitates healthy relationships.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

5. A　主旨要义题。根据第六段中的“I realized that I was learning a new way of being in the world”以及第七段中的“change and growth are not just possible but expected”可知,作者在练习太极拳的过程中,身心得到提升,实现了自我蜕变和成长。故选A。

“旅行黑话”大更新：三种颠覆你想象的新玩法

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Land Snorkeling? Townsizing? A User's Guide to the Latest Travel Lingo.

    陆地浮潜？小镇一游？最新旅行术语使用指南。

  2. 02

    For your next trip, have you considered townsizing? What about choosing a detour destination? And maybe it's time to give land snorkeling a try.

    你的下一次旅行，是否考虑“小镇一游”（townsizing）？或者选择一个“绕道而行的目的地”（detour destination）？又或许是时候尝试一下“陆地浮潜”（land snorkeling）了。

    detour
    绕道。detour destination 指可以顺路绕去玩的地点。
  3. 03

    If the terms sound odd, you might need a travel translator. Agencies, online platforms and sometimes travelers themselves have been flexing their creative language skills by labeling trends in travel — some new, some tried-and-true (like bleisure, a trip that combines business with leisure time).

    如果这些词语听上去很奇怪，那你可能需要一位旅行术语翻译官。旅行社、旅行在线平台，有时甚至是旅行者自己，都在发挥着自己的创造性语言技能，给旅行的各种趋势贴上标签——有些标签是新的，另一些则经久不衰（比如商务休闲旅行bleisure——一种将出差和休闲结合在一起的行程）。

    flex
    展示自己的本事。flex creative language skills 指给旅行方式起新名字，显显造词本领。
    tried-and-true
    经过尝试、证明确实好用的。这里指早已有人实践的旅行概念。

    句子结构

    Agencies, online platforms and sometimes travelers themselves / have been flexing their creative language skills / by labeling trends in travel — some new, some tried-and-true (like bleisure, a trip that combines business with leisure time).

    Agencies、online platforms 和 travelers 是并列主语，共用 have been flexing。by labeling 说明这些人怎样展示造词本领。破折号后两个 some 都代指 trends；a trip … 则是 bleisure 的同位语，解释缩合词。

  4. 04

    Detour destinations are "less well-known and less crowded than tourist hot spots". They can be destinations on their own or side trips from a bigger target. This practical two-for-one trip concept bundles an appealing neighbor with the closest major airport destination.

    “绕道而行的目的地”是指那些“比热门景点更不为人所知、人也更少”的地方。它们可以是独立的目的地，也可以是较大城市的周边景点。这种“一箭双雕”的实用旅行概念，将迷人的周边景点和临近的主要机场城市绑定在了一起。

    bundle
    把几项东西打包在一起。这里是把大城市和临近的小地方排进同一趟行程。
  5. 05

    Your next vacation is townsized if instead of big-city buzz you opt for small-town vibes in easygoing destinations.

    如果你放弃了大城市的喧嚣，选择了轻松随意的小镇风情，那你的下一次旅行就是“小镇一游”。

    opt
    选择。opt for small-town vibes 指宁愿去小镇感受当地气氛。
  6. 06

    Coined by the artists Clyde Aspevig and Carol Guzman, Land snorkeling in the context of a walk (which could even be in a city) is paying attention to where you are, not necessarily where you're going. It encourages a focus on minute details, the way you might hover over a reef when snorkeling. "When you snorkel, you don't go with a destination in mind but go with the water and let the visuals take over," said Ms. Guzman. "You wander and you wonder," Mr. Aspevig added.

    “陆地浮潜”一词由艺术家克莱德·阿斯佩维格（Clyde Aspevig）和卡罗尔·古兹曼（Carol Guzman）提出，是指在散步时（甚至可以是在城市中散步），关注你置身何处，而不一定要关注你去往何处。它鼓励人们关注微小的细节，就像浮潜时在礁石上徘徊一样。古兹曼说：“在你浮潜时，你脑中并没有特定的目的地，你随水流而动，让眼前的景致主导一切。”阿斯佩维格补充道：“你徜徉其中，惊叹不已。”

    hover
    停留在某处上方。这里是画出浮潜者停在礁石上方细看的动作。
    minute
    微小的。minute details 是细微之处；这个形容词重音在第二音节。
    coin
    创造一个新词或新说法。coined by 指这个说法由谁首创。

阅读理解

07-2021全国甲，C

学习、阅读与教育方式

When I was 9, we packed up our home in Los Angeles and arrived at Heathrow, London on a gray January morning. Everyone in the family settled quickly into the city except me. Without my beloved beaches and endless blue⁃sky days, I felt at a loss and out of place. Until I made a discovery.

Southbank, at an eastern bend in the Thames, is the center of British skateboarding, where the continuous crashing of skateboards left your head ringing. I loved it. I soon made friends with the local skaters. We spoke our own language. And my favorite: Safe. Safe meant cool. It meant hello. It meant don't worry about it. Once, when trying a certain trick on the beam(横杆), I fell onto the stones, damaging a nerve in my hand, and Toby came over, helping me up: Safe, man. Safe. A few minutes later, when I landed the trick, my friends beat their boards loud, shouting: “Safe!Safe!Safe!”And that's what mattered—landing tricks, being a good skater.

When I was 15, my family moved to Washington. I tried skateboarding there, but the locals were far less welcoming. Within a couple of years, I'd given it up.

When I returned to London in 2004, I found myself wandering down to Southbank, spending hours there. I've traveled back several times since, most recently this past spring. The day was cold but clear; tourists and Londoners stopped to watch the skaters. Weaving (穿梭)among the kids who rushed by on their boards, I found my way to the beam. Then a rail⁃thin teenager, in a baggy white T⁃shirt, skidded(滑)up to the beam. He sat next to me. He seemed not to notice the man next to him. But soon I caught a few of his glances. “I was a local here 20 years ago,” I told him. Then, slowly, he began to nod his head. “Safe, man. Safe.”

“Yeah,” I said. “Safe.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What can we learn about the author soon after he moved to London?

  • A.He felt disappointed.
  • B.He gave up his hobby.
  • C.He liked the weather there.
  • D.He had disagreements with his family.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1.A　细节理解题。根据第一段倒数第二句话Without my beloved beaches and endless blue⁃sky days, I felt at a loss and out of place.可知,作者搬到伦敦后在生活上感到失落和不适。

2What do the underlined words“Safe!Safe!Safe!”probably mean?

  • A.Be careful!
  • B.Well done!
  • C.No way!
  • D.Don't worry!
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2.B　猜测词义题。根据第二段的第五、六、七、八句And my favorite: Safe...It meant don't worry about it.可知,safe一词对于作者来说有正面、积极的含义。再结合画线单词所在句子的描述可知当作者完成了一次滑板动作后,同伴们都大喊“Safe! Safe! Safe!”来表示祝贺或鼓励作者,认为他动作完成得很棒,故选B。

3Why did the author like to spend time in Southbank when he returned to London?

  • A.To join the skateboarding.
  • B.To make new friends.
  • C.To learn more tricks.
  • D.To relive his childhood days.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　推理判断题。根据倒数第二段第四句Weaving among the kids who rushed by on their boards, I found my way to the beam.可知作者去南岸回味儿时的乐趣。

4What message does the author seem to convey in the text?

  • A.Children should learn a second language.
  • B.Sport is necessary for children's health.
  • C.Children need a sense of belonging.
  • D.Seeing the world is a must for children.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

4.C　推理判断题。文章第一段写作者小时候搬家到伦敦后感到失落和不适。第二段写自己在南岸找到了和当地孩子们一起滑滑板的乐趣。第三段讲述自己搬到美国华盛顿后又失去了那种乐趣。倒数第二段提到多年后回到伦敦南岸,回味童年乐趣,在那里作者有归属感(a sense of belonging)。

外刊延伸

阅读难度参考：CSE 9

都在抢减肥针？营养学家：别忽略了餐桌上最朴素的“瘦身力”

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Is it possible to lose weight on an 'Ozempic' diet?

    采用“Ozempic”饮食法真能减肥吗？

  2. 02

    The emergence of weight-loss drugs including Ozempic has turned the medical world upside down in recent years.

    近年来，以Ozempic为代表的减肥药物问世，彻底颠覆了医学界。

    emergence
    出现；the emergence of weight-loss drugs 指这类药物问世。
  3. 03

    Glucogon-like peptide 1 (GLP-1) agonists have proved effective in helping obese people lose weight by reducing the users' appetite, so they eat less. But alongside the hype, there have also been claims that we can easily mimic the drugs' effects with the foods we eat.

    胰高血糖素样肽-1（GLP-1）受体激动剂已被证实能有效帮助肥胖人群减重，其原理是通过降低使用者的食欲，减少进食量。但在一片炒作叫好之下，也有人声称，我们只需通过日常饮食，就能轻松模拟这类药物的效果。

    mimic
    模仿；mimic the drugs’ effects 是指复现药物作用，文章用疑问句探讨食物能否做到。
    agonists
    激动剂；药理学上指能激活相应受体的一类物质。
    hype
    夸大的宣传或热议；alongside the hype 提醒读者把市场热度和证据分开。
  4. 04

    But can any diet or individual food or drink really come close to the effects of Ozempic?

    但有没有哪种饮食方式，或某一种食物、饮料，真能达到接近 Ozempic 的效果？

  5. 05

    The new generation of weight-loss drugs mimic a process that naturally occurs in our bodies every day. When we eat, our guts produce the hormone GLP-1, which increases insulin levels in our blood, reduces the liver's sugar production, slows digestion and reduces appetite.

    新一代减肥药物模拟的，是我们身体每天自然发生的生理过程。当我们进食时，肠道会分泌GLP-1激素，这种激素能提高血液中的胰岛素水平、减少肝脏的糖生成、减缓消化速度，同时降低食欲。

  6. 06

    GLP-1 hormones are "master regulators" of our body's whole metabolic process, says Chris Damman, gastroenterologist and clinical associate professor at the University of Washington.

    华盛顿大学胃肠病学家、临床副教授克里斯·达曼（Chris Damman）表示，GLP-1激素是人体整个代谢过程的“主导调节因子”。

    metabolic
    新陈代谢的；metabolic process 统称机体转化和利用物质、能量的过程。
  7. 07

    The two main elements of our diet that are associated with GLP-1 are fibre and polyphenols. When we eat foods rich in fibre and polyphenols, these components are converted into fatty acids, which stimulate the production of GLP-1. Foods high in fibre include nuts, legumes, fruit and vegetables, while polyphenols can also be found in fruit, vegetables and nuts.

    饮食中与GLP-1分泌相关的两大核心成分是膳食纤维和多酚。当我们摄入富含膳食纤维和多酚的食物时，这些成分会转化为脂肪酸，进而刺激GLP-1的产生。高纤维食物包括坚果、豆类、水果和蔬菜，而多酚也同样存在于水果、蔬菜和坚果中。

    polyphenols
    多酚类化合物；文中说它们存在于水果、蔬菜和坚果等食物中。
    legumes
    豆类；在文中与 nuts、fruit 和 vegetables 并列，属于食物分类。
  8. 08

    Ultimately, a diet that encourages natural production of GLP-1 is full of whole foods, including fruit, vegetables, legumes and nuts, says Damman. That people could be discovering this by attempting to mimic the effects of weight-loss drugs is an irony not lost on some nutritional scientists, including Damman.

    达曼指出，归根结底，能促进身体自然分泌GLP-1的饮食，核心是各类天然食物，比如水果、蔬菜、豆类和坚果。包括达曼在内的部分营养学家认为，人们需要通过“模拟减肥药物效果”才发现这一饮食原理，本身就颇具讽刺意味。

    irony
    具有反差意味的事；文末的反差是，人们想模仿新药，结果重新关注常见的天然食物。

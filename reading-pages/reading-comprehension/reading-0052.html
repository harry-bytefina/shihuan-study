AI、脑科学与新技术

Fire Prevention Information

The University of Adelaide employs a full-time staff of fire prevention professionals. They inspect all campus buildings and test and maintain all sprinkler(喷水灭火装置)systems, fire alarms, and fire extinguishers(灭火器). They also provide educational programs on fire safety in the residence halls. Whenever you move to a new area, you should locate the fire alarm pull stations and the two exits nearest your room.

Fire Alarms

The floors of all campus buildings are equipped with manual(手动的)fire alarm systems which include fire alarm pull stations and pipes. Most are also equipped with automatic fire alarm systems consisting of heat detectors, smoke detectors and sprinklers. For your safety, never tamper with(胡乱摆弄)these systems. False fire alarms are illegal and may lead to imprisonment.

Fire Drills

A fire drill will be conducted in your residence hall every semester. During a fire drill, please do the following:

·Take your room key and ID, close and lock the door to your room.

·Exit immediately from the nearest emergency exit; do not use a lift.

·Meet outside of your residence hall and wait for further instructions.

Fire Extinguishers

Fire extinguishers are located on each floor and in each apartment. Use a fire extinguisher only if you have been trained to do so. Irresponsible use of a fire extinguisher can create a dangerous situation for other residents and could result in damage to personal property. Misuse of a fire extinguisher will result in fines.

Smoke Detectors

A smoke detector is on the ceiling in your room. Some buildings also have heat detectors on the ceilings. Do the following to ensure the safe operation of your smoke detector:

·If your smoke detector is working properly, the red light should be on. If the red light is not blinking(闪动),contact residence hall staff immediately.

·Do not cover or block your smoke detector in any way.

·If a smoke detector sets off an alarm and there is no fire or smoke, inform your hall staff.

1What is the main duty of the fire prevention professionals?

  • A.To provide part-time jobs for students.
  • B.To lead the students to the nearest exits.
  • C.To check and maintain fire prevention equipment.
  • D.To train teachers to be fire prevention professionals.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1.C　细节理解题。题干句意:预防火灾的专业人员的主要职责是什么?根据第一部分中的第二句话(他们巡查所有的校园建筑,检验并维护所有的喷水灭火装置、火警报警器和灭火器)可知,C项(检查并维护火灾预防设备)描述的就是他们的主要职责。A项:为学生提供兼职工作。B项:带领学生到最近的通道。D项:把老师培训成预防火灾的专业人员。这三项在文章中均没有提及。

2What do the automatic fire alarm systems include?

  • A.Pipes and smoke detectors.
  • B.Smoke detectors and sprinklers.
  • C.Fire alarm pull stations and pipes.
  • D.Sprinklers and fire alarm pull stations.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2.B　细节理解题。题干句意:自动火警报警系统包括什么?根据题干中的the automatic fire alarm systems直接定位到第二部分中的第二句话(大多数校园大楼内还配备了自动火警报警系统,包括热探测器、烟雾探测器和喷水灭火装置),可知答案为B项。A项中的 “pipes”不属于自动火警报警系统。C项中的两个都不属于自动火警报警系统。D项中的“fire alarm pull stations”不属于自动火警报警系统。

  • D.wait for instructions in the hall
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　细节理解题。题干句意:在火灾演练中,学生们应该　　　。根据第三部分中的前两个黑点提示部分(带上钥匙和你的身份证,关上并锁上你的房间门。从离你最近的紧急出口马上离开,不要用电梯),可知,这时候学生们应该关上门马上离开,这与C项描述一致。A项:迅速跑向电梯。这与“不要用电梯”恰好相反。B项:在最近的通道集合。让你从这里马上离开,而不是在这里集合。D项:在宿舍楼里等候指令。由第三部分中的第三个黑点后的内容可知,是让你离开宿舍楼在外面等候指令。

Anthropic冲刺史上最大IPO，招股书却警告AI可能终结世界？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Anthropic’s I.P.O. Prospectus Spells Out a Future of Promise and Peril

    Anthropic 的招股说明书描绘了一个充满机遇又暗藏危机的未来

    prospectus
    n. 招股书
    peril
    n. （偏书面）重大威胁
  2. 02

    For years, Dario Amodei, a founder of the start-up Anthropic, has been painted as the pre-eminent doomsayer of artificial intelligence, often making sweeping statements about how the technology could wreak havoc on the world. On Monday, as Dr. Amodei presented his company’s paperwork for what could be the biggest ever initial public offering on Wall Street, he painted a more nuanced picture: Even with its many risks, A.I. has the potential to bring about untold benefits to humanity.

    多年来，初创公司 Anthropic 的创始人达里奥・阿莫迪（Dario Amodei）一直被视作人工智能领域首屈一指的末日预言者。他时常发表笼统的论断，称这项技术可能给世界带来浩劫。周一，阿莫迪博士提交了公司上市申报文件，这有望成为华尔街史上规模最大的首次公开募股（IPO）。在这份文件里，他描绘了一幅更周全的图景：尽管人工智能存在诸多风险，但它也有潜力为人类带来难以估量的福祉。

    doomsayer
    n. 末日预言者
    untold
    adj. 数不清的；未讲述的
    anthropic
    adj. 与人类有关的（本文Anthropic为公司名）
  3. 03

    From the very top of the founder’s letter — a kind of mission statement typical of many I.P.O. prospectuses — Dr. Amodei and his six co-founders set the stakes. “We believe that AI is one of the most consequential technologies in human history.”

    在创始人信函开篇——这类信函相当于招股说明书的“使命宣言”，阿莫迪博士与另外六位联合创始人就把利害摆上台面：“我们认为，在人类历史上，像AI这样影响深远的技术，屈指可数。”

    consequential
    adj. 会产生重大后果的，影响深远的，举足轻重的
  4. 04

    Though the prospectus is front-loaded with sweetness and light, the paperwork has all the hallmarks of Anthropic’s anxieties. In page after page, the company lists the many risks that A.I. poses to its business — and society.

    虽说这份招股书开篇满是美好愿景，但文件中处处流露出 Anthropic 标志性的忧虑。公司一页页逐条列明了人工智能给其业务乃至整个社会带来的诸多风险。

  5. 05

    No I.P.O. prospectus from Anthropic would have been complete without Dr. Amodei’s biggest concern, and what some might call the ultimate “risk factor” — a disclosure companies are required to make to investors about issues that could adversely affect their business. “Sufficiently advanced AI systems could, if not developed, deployed, and operated with appropriate safeguards, oversight, and security measures, pose catastrophic or existential risks to humanity.”

    任何一份Anthropic的招股说明书，若没包含阿莫迪博士最担忧的问题，都是不完整的。有人称之为终极 “风险因素”，即 上市公司必须向投资者披露的有可能对经营造成负面影响的事项。“足够先进的人工智能系统，如果不能在研发、落地与运行过程中配套恰当的防护机制、监管与安全措施，将给人类带来灾难性或生死攸关的风险。”

    catastrophic
    adj. 灾难性的
  6. 06

    Put more simply: Yes, Anthropic believes its technology could bring about conditions for human flourishing the likes of which have never been seen. The company also believes it could bring about the end of the world.

    简单来说就是：是的，Anthropic 确信AI技术能够创造出人类前所未见的局面，让人类社会走向繁荣；但这家公司也承认，它也可能招致世界末日。

    flourishing
    n. 兴旺，繁荣

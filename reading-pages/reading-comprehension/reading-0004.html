太空、地球系统与科学探索

Science serves as a powerful tool for unlocking the mysteries of the universe, but understanding its limitations is essential for its effective application. There are occasions where I have used the handle of a knife as a hammer(锤子), but the result would have been better if I'd had a more suitable tool at hand. As far as science goes, it is really good at testing things that are testable, but not so for those that are not.

We can do, and have done, an impressive amount with our brains. But there are limits. Sometimes these limits go away if we keep at it for long enough—we just need better facilities and experiments to get the answer. Breaking new ground in modern science this way can be costly. Next-generation supercomputers or incredibly large telescopes are expensive, yet these may be required to find answers to some of the unsolved mysteries of the universe.

Sometimes the limits we encounter in trying to unlock the nature of the universe are cognitive(认知的). Think about this: human DNA is only about 1.2 percent different from that of chimps(黑猩猩). Chimps are smart, no question. But could you teach them advanced mathematics? What if our DNA were another 1.2 percent further evolved than it is? What might our brains be capable of then? The level of abstract thinking might be unimaginable.

Sometimes the limits we hit are fundamental. There are laws of nature we may never be able to understand, however advanced our brains might become. There are experiments we might never be able to perform. We may never be able to test what caused the universe to be created, and what caused the cause of the universe being created. This is where science may never break through.

For something to be considered scientific, it must, by definition, be testable. There is a problem here: it may not need to be testable right now,but it must be testable at some point in the future by experiment. If an idea is untestable, that doesn't mean it is wrong. It means it is untestable for now. These untestable ideas also happen to be some of the most interesting ones, probably because they've puzzled humanity for centuries.

1Why does the author mention “knife” and “hammer” in Paragraph 1?

  • A.To demonstrate how tools can be used creatively.
  • B.To highlight consequences of using a wrong tool.
  • C.To show the necessity of keeping a handy tool within reach.
  • D.To stress the need for the right tool to achieve desired results.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

1. D　写作意图题。根据第一段中“the result would have been better if I'd had a more suitable tool at hand”可知,作者在第一段中举“刀”和“锤子”的例子是为了强调需要使用恰当的工具才能达到预期的效果,故选D。

2What is often required in breaking new ground in science?

  • A.Broader science education.
  • B.More advanced facilities for experiments.
  • C.Deeper understanding of the brain power.
  • D.More investment in next-generation scientists.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2. B　细节理解题。根据第二段中“we just need better facilities and experiments to get the answer”可知,在科学领域开拓创新时通常需要更好的设备和实验,故选B。

3How does the author assess human beings in terms of their cognitive capacity?

  • A.They are just 2.4% away from true abstract thinking.
  • B.They are slightly smarter than other intelligent beings.
  • C.They are yet to evolve further to learn more about the universe.
  • D.They are good at solving problems with advanced mathematics.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3. C　推理判断题。根据第三段内容可知,作者假设若人类DNA再进化1.2%,其抽象思维能力可能达到无法想象的高度,以此揭示当前人类大脑的认知水平可能从根本上限制了人类对宇宙的理解。由此可推断,作者认为人类在认知能力方面还需要进一步进化,以便了解更多关于宇宙的知识,故选C。

4What message does Paragraph 4 convey?

  • A.Some puzzles about the universe are way beyond scientific exploration.
  • B.Experimental research lays solid foundations for space technology.
  • C.Boundaries of science can be pushed back with determined efforts.
  • D.Limitations of science may result from insufficient testing.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4. A　主旨要义题。作者在第四段指出某些自然法则可能永远无法被理解,某些实验可能永远无法进行,以及宇宙起源相关问题可能永远无法通过科学实验验证。因此作者在第四段主要想表达的是有些关于宇宙的难题远远超出了科学探索的范畴。故选A。

5What has the author added to the definition of a scientific idea?

  • A.Correct ideas are testable.
  • B.Untestable ideas can be true.
  • C.Some scientific ideas may never be testable.
  • D.An idea must be testable to be seen as correct.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

5. B　主旨要义题。根据最后一段中“If an idea is untestable, that doesn't mean it is wrong. It means it is untestable for now.”以及全文内容可知,作者认为人类的认知能力仍有局限,未经证实的想法也有可能是正确的,故选B。

与耐克CEO的24小时：他如何奋力收复体坛江山

词汇梳理背景补充
  1. 01

    24 Hours With Nike's C.E.O. as He Races to Win Back the Sports World

    跟访耐克CEO的24小时：看他如何争分夺秒，力图收复体育失地

  2. 02

    In recent years, Nike lost its iron grip on the sports world, which had driven the company's sales and defined its brand.

    近年来，耐克失去了对体育世界的铁腕掌控，而这种掌控曾经驱动了公司的销售，也定义了耐克这个品牌。

  3. 03

    Relationships became strained with some of the leagues, teams, athletes and retailers that Nike worked with, amid complaints the company was neglecting them. Major League Baseball players complained about the quality of its jerseys.

    一些与耐克有合作关系的体育联盟、球队、运动员以及零售商，与耐克的关系变得紧张，他们纷纷抱怨自己被这家公司忽视了。美国职业棒球大联盟的球员甚至抱怨耐克的球衣质量不佳。

    strained
    紧张的。relationships became strained 指合作关系因不满而变得不融洽。
    jersey
    球衣。这里指职业棒球运动员收到的比赛队服。
  4. 04

    Mr. Hill, 62, is trying to restore those connections through his own style of corporate diplomacy, racing around the globe to meet with some of the sports world's most influential executives and athletes.

    62岁的希尔（Mr. Hill）正试图以他独特的“商业外交”风格，重建这些关系。他马不停蹄地飞遍全球，去见体育界那些最具影响力的高管和运动员。

    diplomacy
    用协商和沟通处理关系。corporate diplomacy 指高管通过当面沟通修复合作。
  5. 05

    Mr. Hill arrived at F.C. Barcelona's facility. The manager of the women's soccer team, spoke with him on the practice pitch as the team finished training. Barcelona's team is one of Nike's critical deals, giving it a strong presence among the world's most prestigious squads.

    希尔抵达了巴塞罗那足球俱乐部的训练基地。在训练结束后，女队主教练在训练场边与他交谈。巴萨球队是耐克至关重要的一个合作伙伴，能够让耐克在全球最负盛名的顶级球队中保持强大的存在感。

    prestigious
    地位高、受人尊敬的。这里形容有声望的顶级球队。
    pitch
    球场。the practice pitch 指训练用的足球场。
  6. 06

    He walked to another field to meet Pablo Gavira, known as Gavi, a star on the Barcelona men's team, and used the bits of Spanish he had picked up over the years to quiz the 21-year-old midfielder about his custom blue Nike boots. Mr. Hill tried to glean technical insights straight from one of the best young players in the world. How is the toe room? Is there proper traction on the surface?

    希尔走到另一块场地，去见巴塞罗那男队的明星球员巴勃罗·加维拉（Pablo Gavira），也就是大家熟知的加维（Gavi）。他用这些年学来的几句西班牙语，询问这位21岁的中场球员对自己的定制款蓝色耐克球鞋感受如何。希尔想要从这位世界顶级的年轻球员那里，挖掘出最一线的技术反馈。鞋头空间怎么样？这双鞋在场地上的抓地力够不够？

    traction
    抓地力。这里指球鞋鞋底踩在场地上时能否防滑。
    glean
    从零散信息中一点点获取。这里是从球员的回答里收集产品反馈。
    quiz
    仔细询问。quiz sb about sth 在这里是向球员认真追问球鞋的使用感受。

    句子结构

    He walked to another field to meet Pablo Gavira, known as Gavi, a star on the Barcelona men's team, / and used the bits of Spanish he had picked up over the years / to quiz the 21-year-old midfielder about his custom blue Nike boots.

    He walked 和 (he) used 是主句的两个并列谓语，中间的人名、别名和身份都在介绍同一个人。he had picked up … 修饰 the bits of Spanish，省略了关系词；had picked up 早于当场 used 的动作。

  7. 07

    Then there is Michael Jordan.

    还有迈克尔·乔丹（Michael Jordan）。

  8. 08

    "I texted him on his birthday," Mr. Hill said while scrolling through his phone for evidence.

    希尔说：“我在他（指乔丹）生日时给他发了短信。”他一边说，一边在手机上翻找短信作为证明。

  9. 09

    Mr. Jordan, 63, said in an email that Mr. Hill had been a longtime supporter of the brand and that he is what Nike needed. "What sets Elliott apart is his ability to build trust — with athletes, partners and people on the ground around the world."

    63岁的乔丹在一封邮件中表示，希尔一直是品牌的长期支持者，并且他就是耐克所需要的那个人。“埃利奥特（Elliott）的过人之处，在于他能与各方建立信任——无论是运动员、合作伙伴，还是全球各地的一线工作人员。”

背景补充

球鞋的抓地力为什么要问场地？

原文里问的 traction 是鞋底在场地上的抓地力。天然草坪、偏软的泥草地和人工草皮所需的鞋底和鞋钉设计不同。希尔问加维「在这块场地上」的脚感，就是想把品牌与球队的合作，落实到球员能直接检验的产品细节。

Need a Job This Summer?

The provincial government and its partners offer many programs to help students find summer jobs. The deadlines and what you need to apply depend on the program.

Not a student?Go to the government website to learn about programs and online tools available to help people under 30 build skills, find a job or start businesses all year round.

Jobs for Youth

If you are a teenager living in certain parts of the province, you could be eligible(符合条件)for this program, which provides eight weeks of paid employment along with training.

Who is eligible:Youth 15-18 years old in select communities(社区).

Summer Company

Summer Company provides students with hands⁃on business training and awards of up to $3,000 to start and run their own summer businesses.

Who is eligible:Students aged 15-29, returning to school in the fall.

Stewardship Youth Ranger Program

You could apply to be a Stewardship Youth Ranger and work on local natural resource management projects for eight weeks this summer.

Who is eligible:Students aged 16 or 17 at time of hire, but not turning 18 before December 31 this year.

Summer Employment Opportunities(机会)

Through the Summer Employment Opportunities program, students are hired each year in a variety of summer positions across the Provincial Public Service, its related agencies and community groups.

Who is eligible:Students aged 15 or older. Some positions require students to be 15 to 24 or up to 29 for persons with a disability.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What is special about Summer Company?

  • A.It requires no training before employment.
  • B.It provides awards for running new businesses.
  • C.It allows one to work in the natural environment.
  • D.It offers more summer job opportunities.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　细节理解题。Summer Company标题下第一句提到“Summer Company给学生提供实际操作的商业培训和高达3,000美元的夏季自我创业奖励”,这一点是其他项目没有的,故B项正确(为创业者提供奖励)。

2What is the age range required by Stewardship Youth Ranger Program?

  • A.15-18.
  • B.15-24.
  • C.15-29.
  • D.16-17.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　细节理解题。Stewardship Youth Ranger Program标题下第二段中提到符合条件的学生年龄是16或17岁,并且在今年12月31日以前不满18岁。由此可知答案为D项。

3Which program favors the disabled?

  • A.Jobs for Youth.
  • B.Summer Company.
  • C.Stewardship Youth Ranger Program.
  • D.Summer Employment Opportunities.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　细节理解题。根据题干中的the disabled可定位到Summer Employment Opportunities部分最后一句话(有些职位要求学生年龄15到24岁,有残疾的可以放宽到29岁)。由此可知答案为D项。

顶尖收入女性的另类成功法则

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Surprising Lessons From Talking With America's Highest-Earning Women

    与美国最高收入女性对话得到的意外启示

  2. 02

    It's practically gospel that you need a thick skin to succeed in business, especially if you belong to an underrepresented group. Ditto for the notion that self-doubt holds you back.

    若想在职场取得成功，必须拥有厚脸皮，这几乎已经是一条金科玉律，对于少数群体而言尤其如此。同样，自我怀疑会阻碍你的发展，这一观点也已被广泛接受。

    gospel
    n. 被人深信不疑的真理
    underrepresented
    adj. 未被充分代表的
    ditto
    n. 同样如此
  3. 03

    Yet women who make at least $775,000 a year are less likely than others to describe themselves as tough or thick-skinned. They are also less likely to view themselves as unique or even unusual, according to a study by Emily Riley, a digital marketing executive and a former research director.

    然而，根据数字营销高管兼前研究主管艾米丽·莱利（Emily Riley）的一项研究，那些年收入不低于77.5万美元的女性，反而比其他人更少将自己描述为“坚强”或“脸皮厚”。她们也更少认为自己独一无二，哪怕是与众不同。

  4. 04

    "The number-one reason that women get into the 1% is drive," Riley says. "They just stick with it." Notably, stick-to-itiveness isn't the same as thick skin.

    莱利说：“这些女性能够跻身收入前 1%最核心的原因是驱动力，她们始终坚持不懈。”值得注意的是，坚持不懈和厚脸皮并不是一回事。

    stick-to-itiveness
    n. 坚持到底的能力，持续做下去的劲儿
  5. 05

    Nancy Marzouk felt she didn't get the respect she deserved as a sales executive in previous jobs, and she didn't shrug it off.

    南希・马祖克（Nancy Marzouk）曾在之前的工作中担任销售主管，当时她觉得自己没有得到应有的尊重，但她并没有对此一笑置之。

  6. 06

    This was 15 or so years ago, when Marzouk was in her late 30s and already earning about $800,000 a year.

    这大概是15年前的事了，当时将近 40 岁的马祖克，年收入已经达到了80万美元左右。

  7. 07

    Roughly 99% of us are thinking: I'd like to be disrespected to the tune of $800,000 a year.

    我们中大概99%的人都会想：如果一年能赚 80 万美元，我也甘愿被这样“不尊重”。

    the tune of
    达到……之多（强调数量很大）
  8. 08

    But these 1-percenters are wired differently. They're like elite athletes who use slights to fuel their pursuits of even greater achievements. She founded her own company, and has run it for more than a decade.

    但这些收入前1%的人，思维方式不太一样。她们就像顶尖运动员一样，把别人的轻视当作动力，激励自己追求更高的成就。后来她创办了自己的公司，并经营了十多年。

    slight
    n. 轻视，怠慢，冒犯
    fuel
    v. 推动，激发，助长
  9. 09

    It is striking how few of the women in Riley's survey believe they possess some kind of special talent.

    令人惊讶的是，在莱利的调查中，认为自己拥有某种特殊天赋的女性竟如此之少。

    possess
    v. 拥有（某种品质、能力或特征）
  10. 10

    "I definitely don't think, nor did I ever think, 'I am irreplaceable,'" says Lisa Davis. "For women, we're proving our value many times because we're looking to get that seat at the table. So taking that for granted and thinking, 'I'm irreplaceable'? That never would enter my mind."

    莉萨・戴维斯（Lisa Davis）说：“我绝对不会这么想，也从来没有想过‘我是不可替代的。’ 对女性而言，我们需要一次次证明自己的价值，只为在职场获得一席之地。所以理所应当地认为自己‘无可替代’？这种想法从来都不会出现在我的脑海里。”

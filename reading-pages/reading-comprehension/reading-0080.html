学习、阅读与教育方式

Guide to Stockholm University Library

Our library offers different types of studying places and provides a good studying environment.

Zones

The library is divided into different zones.The upper floor is a quiet zone with over a thousand places for silent reading,and places where you can sit and work with your own computer.The reading places consist mostly of tables and chairs.The ground floor is the zone where you can talk.Here you can find sofas and armchairs for group work.

Computers

You can use your own computer to connect to the wi-fi specially prepared for notebook computers;you can also use library computers,which contain the most commonly used applications,such as Microsoft Office.They are situated in the area known as the Experimental Field on the ground floor.

Group-study Places

If you want to discuss freely without disturbing others,you can book a study room or sit at a table on the ground floor.Some study rooms are for 2-3 people and others can hold up to 6-8 people.All rooms are marked on the library maps.

There are 40 group-study rooms that must be booked via the website.To book,you need an active University account and a valid University card.You can use a room three hours per day,nine hours at most per week.

Storage of Study Material

The library has lockers for students to store course literature.When you have obtained at least 40 credits(学分),you may rent a locker and pay 400 SEK for a year's rental period.

Rules to be Followed

Mobile phone conversations are not permitted anywhere in the library.Keep your phone on silent as if you were in a lecture and exit the library if you need to receive calls.

Please note that food and fruit are forbidden in the library,but you are allowed to have drinks and sweets with you.

村上春树：每次动笔，我都不知道下一句会写什么

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    When Haruki Murakami sits down to write, he has no idea what’s going to happen.

    当村上春树（Haruki Murakami）坐下来写作时，他完全不知道接下来会发生什么。

  2. 02

    This seems like an astonishing admission for a seasoned novelist, particularly for a global literary icon who has written more than 40 books and sold tens of millions of copies in dozens of languages.

    对于一位经验丰富的小说家而言——尤其是这位世界级的文学偶像已经出版过四十多部作品、作品被译成数十种语言、销量高达数千万册——这番坦白听来着实令人惊讶。

    icon
    有代表性的知名人物；literary icon 指在文学领域有广泛影响力的人。
    seasoned
    经验丰富的；seasoned novelist 是写作阅历深的小说家。
    admission
    坦白、承认；指村上直言自己下笔前不知道故事会怎样发展。
  3. 03

    “I don’t have any plan, I’m just writing, and while I’m writing, strange things happen very naturally, very automatically,” Murakami said. “Every time I write fiction, I go into another world — maybe you can call it subconsciousness — and anything can happen in that world,” he continued. “I see so many things there, then I come back to this real world and I write it down.”

    “我没有任何计划，我就只是去写，写的过程中，奇妙的事情会自然而然地、像是自动触发一般发生，”村上说，“每次写小说，我都会去到另一个世界——或许你可以称之为‘潜意识’——在那个世界里，凡事皆有可能，”他继续道，“我在那里看到了许多景象，然后我再回到这个现实世界，并把它们记录下来。”

    subconsciousness
    潜意识；村上用它形容写作时进入的、感觉不受自觉计划支配的世界。
  4. 04

    Murakami doesn’t regard himself as a masterly prose stylist or a brilliant storyteller. In his telling, his one unique skill is his ability to travel between worlds and report back.

    村上并不觉得自己是技艺精湛的文字高手或者多么出色的故事讲述者。按他的说法，他唯一的过人之处，就是能够穿梭于不同世界并将所见所闻带回来。

    masterly
    技艺精湛的；masterly prose stylist 指擅长驾驭文字的高手。
    prose
    散文体文字；prose stylist 说的是写作风格，不限于中文文体里的散文。
  5. 05

    For about as long as he’s been writing, Murakami has also translated books from English into Japanese, including works by 20th-century giants like Raymond Chandler, J.D. Salinger and Raymond Carver. “I’m using a different part of my brain,” he said. “You can learn so many things from the work of translation. You can be in other people’s shoes.”

    几乎从他开始写作起，村上就一直在把英语作品翻译成日语，其中包括雷蒙德·钱德勒（Raymond Chandler）、J.D.塞林格（J.D. Salinger）和雷蒙德·卡佛（Raymond Carver）等20世纪文学巨匠的作品。 “我用的是大脑的不同区域，”他说。“你能从翻译工作中获益良多，它让你学会站在别人的立场，设身处地地思考。”

  6. 06

    Lately, Murakami has been happily enmeshed in his usual routine, waking up early to write, doing chores like washing dishes and ironing, and running. “I don’t know how many more novels I can write,” he said. “I’m feeling I’ll be able to do more, because writing fiction is so wonderful, it’s just like exploring myself. Even when I got old, there’s still space to explore.”

    最近，村上一直沉浸式地享受他的生活日常——早起写作，做做家务，像是洗洗碗、熨熨衣服一类的，再去跑跑步。“我不知道自己还能写多少本小说，”他说，“不过我觉得我还能写下去，因为小说写作太美妙了，就像是在探索自我。即便步入暮年，内心深处仍有未竟之地。”

    enmeshed
    深深融入；happily enmeshed in his usual routine 强调他习惯并享受规律生活。
    ironing
    熨烫衣物；和 washing dishes 并列，是村上日常做的家务之一。

Washington, D.C. Bicycle Tours

Cherry Blossom Bike Tour in Washington, D.C.

Duration:3 hours

This small group bike tour is a fantastic way to see the world⁃famous cherry trees with beautiful flowers of Washington, D.C. Your guide will provide a history lesson about the trees and the famous monuments where they blossom. Reserve your spot before availability—and the cherry blossoms—disappear!

Washington Capital Monuments Bicycle Tour

Duration:3 hours(4 miles)

Join a guided bike tour and view some of the most popular monuments in Washington, D.C. Explore the monuments and memorials on the National Mall as your guide shares unique facts and history at each stop. Guided tour includes bike, helmet, cookies and bottled water.

Capital City Bike Tour in Washington, D.C.

Duration:3 hours

Morning or Afternoon, this bike tour is the perfect tour for D.C. newcomers and locals looking to experience Washington, D.C. in a healthy way with minimum effort. Knowledgeable guides will entertain you with the most interesting stories about Presidents, Congress, memorials, and parks. Comfortable bikes and a smooth tour route(路线)make cycling between the sites fun and relaxing.

Washington Capital Sites at Night Bicycle Tour

Duration:3 hours(7 miles)

Join a small group bike tour for an evening of exploration in the heart of Washington, D.C. Get up close to the monuments and memorials as you bike the sites of Capitol Hill and the National Mall. Frequent stops are made for photo taking as your guide offers unique facts and history. Tour includes bike, helmet, and bottled water. All riders are equipped with reflective vests and safety lights.

暴力抓捕马杜罗之后，美国要怎么“接管”委内瑞拉？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    After Maduro Ouster, Trump Takes On the Risks of Governing Venezuela

    赶马杜罗下台后，特朗普将承担起接管委内瑞拉的风险​

  2. 02

    With Nicolás Maduro out of power, the Trump administration is racing to assemble an interim governing structure for Venezuela.

    尼古拉斯·马杜罗（Nicolás Maduro）倒台后，特朗普政府正加紧为委内瑞拉搭建一个临时的治理架构。​

    interim
    adj. 过渡的，临时的
    assemble
    v. 组建，搭建
  3. 03

    President Trump made the prospect of setting up a transitional government in Venezuela appear relatively easy, telling reporters on Saturday that billions in oil money would be used to rebuild the industry and make Venezuelans rich. He said he would personally oversee the next stage in the country's history after Maduro was unseated in an audacious nighttime raid by American commandos. Otherwise, Trump offered few details.

    特朗普总统将在委内瑞拉组建过渡政府的前景说得相对容易，他周六向记者表示，委内瑞拉数十亿美元的石油收入将用于重振该国石油产业，还能让委内瑞拉民众富裕起来。他还称，在美国突击队大胆的夜间突袭致使马杜罗下台后，他本人将亲自监督这个国家的历史进入下一阶段。除此之外，特朗普并未透露更多细节。​

    prospect
    n. 前景
    raid
    n. 突然袭击
    unseat
    罢免、使下台（oust）
  4. 04

    Administering a country of 28 million twice the size of California will be enormously complicated.

    要治理一个2800万人口、面积相当于两个加利福尼亚州的国家，实则极其复杂。​

    administer
    v. 治理，管理
  5. 05

    The U.S. engaged in a costly 20-year nation-building experiment in Afghanistan that ended in failure and has had a checkered past of fomenting violence and instability by toppling other leaders in Latin America during the Cold War.

    美国曾在阿富汗进行过一场耗资巨大、耗时20年的“建国实验”，最终以失败收场。冷战时期，美国还曾在拉美地区多次推翻他国领导人，煽动暴力冲突与局势动荡，留下了一段充满争议的历史。​

    costly
    adj. 昂贵的；代价高昂、损失惨重的
    foment
    v. 挑起、激起事端
  6. 06

    Some Democratic lawmakers have also vocally condemned Trump. "I lived through the consequences of an illegal war sold to the American people with lies," Sen. Ruben Gallego, (D., Ariz.), a former Marine who deployed to Iraq, wrote on social media.

    部分美国民主党议员也直言不讳地谴责了特朗普。亚利桑那州民主党参议员鲁本·加列戈（Ruben Gallego）曾是一名海军陆战队队员，并有过派驻伊拉克的经历。他在社交媒体上写道：“我亲身经历过一场非法战争带来的恶果，那场战争就是用谎言包装后兜售给美国民众的。”​

  7. 07

    Trump said in his press conference that he could work with Venezuela's vice president, Delcy Rodríguez. Yet hours later, she sharply condemned the capture of Maduro and demanded the U.S. free him.

    特朗普在新闻发布会上称，他可以与委内瑞拉副总统德尔西·罗德里格斯（Delcy Rodríguez）展开合作。然而就在数小时后，罗德里格斯便强烈谴责美方抓捕马杜罗的行为，并要求美国立即释放马杜罗。​

  8. 08

    She called the U.S. operation against Maduro "barbaric" and said the regime was ready to defend Venezuela. "Never again will we be slaves, never again will we be a colony of any empire," she said.

    她称美国针对马杜罗的行动十分“野蛮”，同时表示委内瑞拉现政权已准备好保卫国家。她说：“我们绝不会再做奴隶，绝不会再成为任何帝国的殖民地。”​

文化遗产、艺术与跨文化

Pali Overnight Adventures offers children and teens exciting experiences this summer. From broadcasting to street art, these are just 4 of the 17 highly unique camps being offered.

Broadcasting Camp

Become the next star reporter, news writer, director or producer. While running every aspect of our own news station, kids and their fellow campers will create and host a broadcast airing each night at dinner for the entire camp. Every night it goes on the web, keeping parents and the world informed of the happenings at Pali.

Secret Agent Camp

In the movie Mission Impossible, Tom Cruise made being a secret agent seem like the coolest job ever. Campers who sign up for the 2⁃week secret agent camp can get to know about the life of real secret agents by learning strategies and military skills on the paintball field.

Culinary Camp

If your child enjoys being in the kitchen, then the culinary camp is definitely the right fit. Campers learn technical skills of roasting, frying and cutting, as well as some recipes that they can take home and share with their families.

Street Art Camp

This camp takes creative license to an entirely new level. Campers will share their colorful ideas and imagination with each other and work together to visualize, sketch and paint with non⁃traditional techniques to create the coolest mural(壁画) which will be displayed in public for all to see.

1How many camps does Pali Overnight Adventures offer this summer?

  • A.2.
  • B.4.
  • C.17.
  • D.21.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1.C　细节理解题。根据第一段中“From broadcasting to street art, these are just 4 of the 17 highly unique camps being offered.”可知,从广播到街头艺术,这只是17个非常独特的营地中的4个。由此可知有17个度假营。

2What will campers do at the Broadcasting Camp?

  • A.Create a website.
  • B.Run a news station.
  • C.Meet a star reporter.
  • D.Hold a dinner party.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2.B　细节理解题。根据第二段中“While running every aspect ... for the entire camp.”可知,度假营营员在广播营会经营一家新闻站。

3Which camp will attract children who are interested in cooking?

  • A.Broadcasting Camp.
  • B.Secret Agent Camp.
  • C.Culinary Camp.
  • D.Street Art Camp.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　细节理解题。根据倒数第二段“If your child enjoys being in the kitchen,...with their families.”可知,Culinary Camp会吸引对烹饪感兴趣的孩子。

阅读难度参考：CSE 8

穿什么样的羽绒服，暴露了你是什么样的人

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Once upon a time a puffer was a functional garment for very cold weather activities, the grown-up equivalent of a toddler's snowsuit, made mostly for enduring winter and its related sports.

    曾几何时，羽绒服只是一种专为严寒户外活动设计的功能性服装，相当于儿童防寒连体服的成人版，主要用来熬过寒冬，以及进行各类冬季运动。

    endure
    When something "endures," it continues for a long time, even when things are difficult / when it’s hard to continue.
    a functional garment
    We use this to describe clothing designed mainly for a practical purpose, not just for style or fashion.
  2. 02

    By contrast, the classic tailored overcoat or Crombie coat — wool, tweed or cashmere, single-breasted, usually reaching to the knee or mid-thigh — was one of those garments that, like a sensible leather shoe, signaled adulthood and one's ascent into the workforce.

    相比之下，经典剪裁大衣或克龙比大衣，往往采用羊毛、粗花呢或羊绒面料，单排扣设计，衣长通常及膝或及大腿中部，这类大衣就像一双得体的皮鞋，是象征成年、跻身职场的标志性服饰。

    tweed
    Tweed is a thick, rough, warm fabric often used for jackets, coats, and skirts.
    tailored
    "Tailored" describes clothing that is carefully shaped or adjusted to fit a person's body well.
  3. 03

    When office dress codes relaxed and comfort clothing took over the world, the overcoat increasingly seemed like an anachronism.

    随着职场着装规范日渐宽松，舒适型服饰风靡全球，传统大衣反而愈发显得不合时宜。

    anachronism
    An anachronism is something that feels out of place in time—either too old-fashioned or from the wrong period.
  4. 04

    By then, the puffer, like so many other once banal garments, had been appropriated by the hip-hop world, making it a feature of the urban landscape rather than the outdoor rec world. Puffer specialists like Moncler and Canada Goose, sensing opportunity, began framing themselves and their down coats less as sporty outerwear and more as fashion statements.

    也就是从那时起，和许多曾经平平无奇的服饰一样，羽绒服被嘻哈圈接纳并重新定义，成了都市街头的潮流单品，而非只属于户外休闲场景。盟可睐（Moncler）、加拿大鹅（Canada Goose）等羽绒服专业品牌嗅到商机，开始将自家的羽绒外套重新定位——不再只是运动户外服饰，更是彰显时尚态度的宣言。

    appropriated by
    When something is "appropriated by" a group, it means that group takes it and uses it, often in a new or different way from the original purpose.
    sensing opportunity
    We say someone is "sensing opportunity" when they see or notice a chance to succeed, improve, or gain something before others see it.
  5. 05

    All of which means that at this point the question is not where you can wear a puffer — you can wear it anywhere, including any kind of office — but rather what kind of puffer should you wear? Because not all puffers are created equal.

    种种转变带来的结果是，如今的问题早已不是“羽绒服能穿去什么场合”，它几乎百搭，各种职场都能穿，而是“你该选什么样的羽绒服”？毕竟，羽绒服和羽绒服之间，差别可大着呢。

    not all
    This is a phrase we use to say that things may look similar, but their quality, or maybe their function, is actually different.
  6. 06

    The first thing to consider is whether you go with a puffer or a parka. A puffer is generally a more quilted, bulky coat. A parka is longer, sleeker and a little tougher.

    首先要考虑的是，选短款羽绒服还是派克服。前者通常绗缝纹路更明显，版型偏蓬松；后者衣长更长，线条更利落，版型也更挺括。

  7. 07

    Then you need to think about color. Increasingly, the sports world has embraced ever brighter shades. Typically conservative colors like black, dark gray or even olive keep the mind in overcoat territory.

    其次要考虑颜色。运动圈如今越来越偏爱鲜艳的亮色；而黑色、深灰乃至橄榄绿这类经典的保守色系，则能让羽绒服穿出传统大衣的沉稳气场。

  8. 08

    Whatever you choose, remember, one of the most adult decisions you can make is to "dress appropriately for the cold."

    无论你最终怎么选，都请记住：一个人能做出的最“成熟”的决定之一，就是在寒冷的时候，穿得足够保暖。

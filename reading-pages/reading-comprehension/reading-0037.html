02-2020天津夏，B

成长、韧性与心理调适

“They tell me that you'd like to make a statue(塑像) of me—is that correct, Miss Vinnie Ream?”

The deep, gentle voice helped calm the nervous girl. Asking a favor of the President of the United States was no casual matter, especially for a seventeen-year-old girl.

“Yes, sir,” she replied, her dark eyes meeting his. “I wouldn't have dared to ask you, but my teacher, Mr. Mills, says I am ready. I plan to make it in an admirable manner.”

President Lincoln smiled. “Painters, sculptors—they've all tried to make the best of this ordinary face, but I'm afraid there's not much hope. What did you have in mind, Miss Ream? A bust(半身像)?”

Before Vinnie could say yes, the President hurried on, a shade of apology in his voice. “Of course—I shouldn't have asked. A full-length pose would be much too big a project for a young woman your size.”

Vinnie's face turned red. She realized she looked like a child, with her tiny figure. “Small does not mean weak, sir,” she defended herself. “I was born in the country of Wisconsin. I've driven teams of horses and carried water. Making a full-length clay(粘土) figure would not exhaust my strength—and that is what I intend to do!”

The President's eyes brightened at her show of spirit. “Sorry, madam, I have underestimated you as I didn't know your background.”

But his smile faded as he rubbed his beard with bony fingers, in thought. “Miss Ream,” he sighed, “I'd like to let you do it, but as you know, we are in the middle of a war. How could I possibly take the time to pose for a sculpture now? I hardly have a minute to myself.”

Vinnie glanced around and noted the size of his office. “I work quickly,” she said. Her voice was soft but confident as she pointed to the corner near the windows. “If I were to bring my clay here and work for three hours every afternoon, I could complete most of the project while you are at your desk.”

The President seemed to consider her idea seriously. He got up and shook Vinnie's hand warmly, “I've heard that you are a talented young woman, and I have found you charming and intelligent as well. I cannot make my decision immediately, but you will hear from me soon.”

The very next day, Vinnie received an invitation from the President.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What gave Vinnie confidence to make her request of President Lincoln?

  • A.Her aggressive personality.
  • B.Mr. Mills's encouraging remark.
  • C.President Lincoln's gentle voice.
  • D.Her interest in a challenging job.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　细节理解题。根据文章第三段Vinnie的回答 “I wouldn't have dared to ask you,but my teacher, Mr. Mills, says I am ready. I plan to make it in an admirable manner.” 可知B项(Mills老师鼓励的话语)正确。

2How did President Lincoln first respond to Vinnie's request?

  • A.Pleased.
  • B.Thrilled.
  • C.Regretful.
  • D.Doubtful.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　细节理解题。根据文章第四段林肯的回答“Painters, sculptors—they've all tried to make the best of this ordinary face, but I'm afraid there's not much hope.”以及第五段中“Of course—I shouldn't have asked. A full-length pose would be much too big a project for a young woman your size.”可知林肯的话里充满了疑问,他认为对这么小的女孩子来说这个工程太大。故答案为D项(怀疑的)。

3Vinnie confirmed her ability to make a full-length statue by highlighting _____ .

  • A.her experience from other projects
  • B.her innocent childhood in the country
  • C.the heavy labor she had done before
  • D.the skill she picked up in Wisconsin
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　细节理解题。根据文章第六段Vinnie的回答中“I've driven teams of horses and carried water. Making a full-length clay figure would not exhaust my strength—and that is what I intend to do!”可知,女孩子曾经做过体力活,她有自信能做好雕像。故C项(她以前做过的体力活)正确。

4Vinnie wanted to choose the corner near the windows to _____ .

  • A.achieve effects of natural lighting
  • B.keep all her tools within easy reach
  • C.observe the President at a right angle
  • D.avoid disturbing the President's work
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　推理判断题。根据文章倒数第三段可以推断出, Vinnie工作时坐在角落里是为了不影响林肯工作,故答案为D项(避免打扰总统的工作)。

5What message does the story convey?

  • A.A strong-willed soul can reach his goal.
  • B.Experience helps to promote excellence.
  • C.Ups and downs make one strong.
  • D.Devotion requires enthusiasm.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

5.A　推理判断题。文章讲述了Vinnie想给林肯塑造雕像,这件事对一个十七岁的女孩子来说实属不易, 但是在老师的鼓励下,她向总统提出了请求,用自己的经验和周到的考虑一步步打消了总统的疑虑并得到了总统的应允。 由此可以总结出整篇文章传递了只要有坚强的意志就会成功的信息,故答案为A项(有志者事竟成)。

中东局势动荡，普通人该如何投资？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Best Playbook for Investing During a War Is Usually Doing Nothing

    战争时期的最佳投资策略，往往是按兵不动

    playbook
    n.（商业、投资语境）应对策略，操作指南；（体育术语）战术手册
  2. 02

    There is chaos in the Middle East and global markets have been oscillating wildly. But rather than panic about what the Iran war may be doing to your investments, try to forget about all of it. That, in a nutshell, is the standard long-term investing playbook for times of crisis.

    中东局势陷入混乱，全球市场随之剧烈震荡。不过，与其恐慌于伊朗冲突是否会给你的投资造成冲击，不如索性将这一切抛诸脑后。简而言之，这正是危机时期标准的长期投资心法。

    oscillate
    v. 振荡，来回摆动
  3. 03

    Academic wisdom suggests simply aiming for an average market return over the long haul is a sensible approach - and, probably, the best strategy for most people. So if you already have set up your portfolio appropriately, using cheap, diversified index funds to hold the entire stock and bond markets in a reasonable proportion, there is no reason to make abrupt changes - and plenty of reasons to avoid acting hastily.

    学界共识认为，以长线视角追求市场平均回报率本就是靠谱选择——对绝大多数人而言，这或许也是最优策略。因此，如果你早已合理搭建好投资组合，通过低成本、多元化的指数基金，用明智合理的比例配置好了全市场股票与债券资产，便无需做出仓促调整——你甚至有充分的理由不做贸然行动。

    sensible
    adj. 靠谱的
    hastily
    adv. 仓促、草率地
  4. 04

    The logic here is sound, but I readily admit that it's not foolproof. Economic recessions are bad for nearly everyone, and certainly for the markets, and if the crisis in the Persian Gulf sets off a recession, it could conceivably take a long time for a full recovery.

    这一逻辑自然站得住脚，但我必须坦言，它并非万无一失。经济衰退几乎对所有人都构成冲击，对市场更是如此，倘若这次波斯湾危机引发经济衰退，市场完全复苏很可能需要相当漫长的时日。

    readily
    adv. 毫不犹豫地
    foolproof
    adj. 万无一失的；防蠢，防傻瓜
    conceivably
    adv. 可以想见地
  5. 05

    Ideally, you have already put aside enough money in safe places — like federally insured bank accounts, Treasury bills and high-quality money-market funds — to ride out a storm. Within weeks or months, the markets usually rebound, and people who stay the course end up prospering.

    理想状态下，你早已在安全渠道储备了充足资金，足以安然挺过风暴——比如联邦保险银行账户、国库券以及优质货币市场基金。一般在数周或数月之内，市场就会反弹，那些坚守原有策略的投资者，最终收获回报。

    prosper
    v. 茁壮成长，兴旺发达
  6. 06

    But the world is moving into strange new places and it would be wise to prepare for further departure from past norms. Hold more bonds and cash than usual, if you are worried. Hope for peace and prosperity but prepare for the horrors of war.

    然而，世界正步入前所未有的诡谲境地。为进一步偏离过往常态做好应对准备，方为明智之举。如果你忧心忡忡，那就相较往常增持债券与现金类资产。总之，既要期许和平与繁荣，也要为战争带来的严峻风险未雨绸缪。

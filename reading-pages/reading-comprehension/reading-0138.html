03-2023全国乙，C

食物、农业与地方身份

What comes into your mind when you think of British food? Probably fish and chips, or a Sunday dinner of meat and two vegetables. But is British food really so uninteresting? Even though Britain has a reputation for less-than-impressive cuisine, it is producing more top class chefs who appear frequently on our television screens and whose recipe books frequently top the best seller lists.

It’s thanks to these TV chefs rather than any advertising campaign that Britons are turning away from meat-and-two-veg and ready-made meals and becoming more adventurous in their cooking habits. It is recently reported that the number of those sticking to a traditional diet is slowly declining and around half of Britain’s consumers would like to change or improve their cooking in some way. There has been a rise in the number of students applying for food courses at UK universities and colleges. It seems that TV programmes have helped change what people think about cooking.

According to a new study from market analysts, 1 in 5 Britons say that watching cookery programmes on TV has encouraged them to try different food. Almost one third say they now use a wider variety of ingredients(配料) than they used to， and just under 1 in 4 say they now buy better quality ingredients than before. One in four adults say that TV chefs have made them much more confident about expanding their cookery knowledge and skills， and young people are also getting more interested in cooking. The UK's obsession(痴迷) with food is reflected through television scheduling. Cookery shows and documentaries about food are broadcast more often than before. With an increasing number of male chefs on TV, it's no longer “uncool” for boys to like cooking.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What do people usually think of British food?

  • A.It is simple and plain.
  • B.It is rich in nutrition.
  • C.It lacks authentic tastes.
  • D.It deserves a high reputation.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1. A推理判断题。根据题干关键词think of British food可定位到第一段。根据“Probably fish and chips, or a Sunday dinner of meat and two vegetables. But is British food really so uninteresting?...less-than-impressive cuisine...”可知，当被问到能想到什么英国食物时，人们可能会想到炸鱼和薯条，或者是星期天的晚餐——肉和两种蔬菜。这些是很乏味、平庸的。这表明人们通常认为英国食物是简单清淡的。故选A。

2Which best describes cookery programmes on British TV?

  • A.Authoritative.
  • B.Creative.
  • C.Profitable.
  • D.Influential.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2. D推理判断题。根据题干关键词cookery programmes on British TV可定位到第二段。根据thanks to these TV chefs、becoming more adventurous、change or improve their cooking in some way以及a rise in the number of students applying for food courses可知，本段说明烹饪节目对英国人产生了各种影响。根据本段最后一句中的“helped change what people think about cooking”可知，烹饪节目是有影响力的（Influential），有助于改变英国人对烹饪的看法。故选D。

3Which is the percentage of the people using more diverse ingredients now?

  • A.20%.
  • B.24%.
  • C.25%.
  • D.33%.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D 细节理解题。根据题干关键词using more diverse ingredients now可定位到第三段第二句。根据“Almost one third say they now use a wider variety of ingredients than they used to”可知，几乎三分之一（33%）的人说他们现在使用更多种类的配料。故选D。

4What might the author continue talking about?

  • A.The art of cooking in other countries.
  • B.Male chefs on TV programmes.
  • C.Table manners in the UK.
  • D.Studies of big eaters.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4. B 推理判断题，考查对后续内容的预测。解答这类试题时要注意认真研读最后一段内容，尤其是最后的句子。根据“With an increasing number of male chefs on TV, it’s no longer ‘uncool’ for boys to like cooking.”可知，随着电视上男厨师越来越多，男孩们喜欢烹饪不再是一件不酷的事情了。由此可推断出，接下来很可能是写有关男厨师的内容。故选B。 长难句 Even though Britain has a reputation for less-than-impressive cuisine, it is producing more top class chefs who appear frequently on our television screens and whose recipe books frequently top the best seller lists. 译文 尽管英国以烹饪平庸著称，但它正在培养出更多的顶级厨师，他们经常出现在我们的电视屏幕上，他们的烹饪书经常高居畅销书排行榜榜首。 分析Even though引导让步状语从句，who appear frequently on our television screens和whose recipe books frequently top the best seller lists为并列的定语从句，修饰先行词chefs。 重点词汇 reputation n.名誉；名声campaign n.活动adventurous adj.有冒险精神的；大胆开拓的analyst n.分析者documentary n.纪录片

星巴克中国“卖身”，昔日“咖啡一哥”为何低头？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Starbucks to Sell Stake in China Business

    星巴克将出售其中国业务股份

    stake
    n. 股份，股权；利害关系 v. 下注
  2. 02

    Starbucks is selling a majority stake in its China business to Boyu Capital, a deal that brings an outside partner to oversee its second-largest market.

    星巴克（Starbucks）已同意将中国业务的多数股权出售给博裕资本（Boyu Capital），这项交易将引入一个外部合作伙伴来负责运营星巴克的第二大市场。

    oversee
    v. 监督
  3. 03

    The coffee giant said Monday that Boyu will hold up to a 60% stake in its retail operations in China, while Starbucks will retain 40% and continue to own and license its brand and intellectual property.

    这家咖啡巨头周一表示，博裕资本将持有其在华零售业务至多60%的股权，而星巴克将保留40%的股权，并继续拥有其品牌和知识产权并进行授权。

    license
    v. 商业特许，授权
  4. 04

    Starbucks said its deal would value Boyu's stake in the operation at $4 billion. The company pegged the total value of the business at more than $13 billion, including an upfront investment by Boyu, Starbucks's retained stake, and licensing payments due over the next decade or more.

    星巴克表示，这项交易对博裕资本所持股份的估值为40亿美元。星巴克对其中国业务的总估值超过130亿美元，其中包括博裕资本的前期投资，星巴克保留的股份，以及未来十年或更长时间内应支付给星巴克的品牌授权费。

    upfront
    adj. 款项预付的；开诚布公的 adv. 预付
  5. 05

    The company had 8,011 stores in China as of Sept. 28. It said it aims to expand its China business to up to 20,000 locations over time with its Boyu partnership.

    截至9月28日，星巴克在中国拥有8011家门店。该公司表示，其目标是通过与博裕资本的合作，逐步将中国门店数量扩大到20000家。

    as of
    截至……（by）
  6. 06

    The Seattle chain was an early entrant to China's burgeoning consumer market, opening its first cafe in the country in 1999. Howard Schultz, Starbucks's longtime leader, oversaw the chain's expansion from China's biggest cities into smaller municipalities, adding hundreds of new locations a year. Schultz personally cultivated relationships in the country, which executives predicted would one day become Starbucks's biggest market.

    这家总部位于西雅图的连锁咖啡店，是较早进入中国高速增长消费市场的外国品牌，1999年就在中国内地开设了第一家门店。星巴克的长期领导者霍华德·舒尔茨（Howard Schultz）主导了品牌从中国最大城市向中小城市的扩张，每年新增数百家门店。舒尔茨还亲自培养在中国的人脉关系，高管们曾预测，中国有朝一日会成为星巴克最大的市场。

    cultivate
    v. 精心种植；悉心维护（关系）
    municipality
    n. 市级行政单位
    chain
    n. 连锁店，连锁企业
  7. 07

    Covid-19 dealt a blow to Starbucks's China operations, driving sales lower and directing Chinese consumers to seek cheaper options. And Starbucks's success brought on homegrown rivals, including Luckin Coffee, which raced to open locations centered on mobile ordering and has since surpassed Starbucks to become the country's biggest coffee chain by both sales and units.

    不过，新冠疫情对星巴克的中国业务造成重创，导致销售额下降，并促使中国消费者寻求更便宜的选择。星巴克的成功也催生了包括瑞幸咖啡（Luckin Coffee）在内的中国本土竞争对手，瑞幸以移动端点单为核心迅速开设门店，此后在销售额和门店数量上都超过了星巴克，成为中国最大的咖啡连锁品牌。

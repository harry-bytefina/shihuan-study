05-2021全国甲，A

生态、气候与城市自然

Take a view, the Landscape(风景)Photographer of the Year Award, was the idea of Charlie Waite, one of today's most respected landscape photographers. Each year, the high standard of entries has shown that the Awards are the perfect platform to showcase the very best photography of the British landscape. Take a view is a desirable annual competition for photographers from all corners of the UK and beyond.

Mike Shepherd

(2011)

Skiddaw in Winter

Cumbria, England

It was an extremely cold winter's evening and freezing fog hung in the air. I climbed to the top of a small rise and realised that the mist was little more than a few feet deep, and though it was only a short climb, I found myself completely above it and looking at a wonderfully clear view of Skiddaw with the sun setting in the west. I used classical techniques, translated from my college days spent in the darkroom into Photoshop, to achieve the black⁃and⁃white image(图像).

Timothy Smith

(2014)

Macclesfield

Forest

Cheshire, England

I was back in my home town of Macclesfield to take some winter images. Walking up a path through the forest towards Shutlingsloe, a local high point, I came across a small clearing and immediately noticed the dead yellow grasses set against the fresh snow. The small pine added to the interest and I placed it centrally to take the view from the foreground right through into the forest.

1Who would most probably enter for Take a view?

  • A.Writers.
  • B.Photographers.
  • C.Painters.
  • D.Tourists.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　细节理解题。根据第一段最后一句 “Take a view is a desirable annual competition for photographers from all corners of the UK and beyond.”可知,Take a view是为英国乃至全世界摄影师举行的年度比赛,即参加Take a view比赛的是摄影师们。

2What do the works by Shepherd and Smith have in common?

  • A.They are winter images.
  • B.They are in black and white.
  • C.They show mountainous scenes.
  • D.They focus on snow⁃covered forests.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2.A　细节理解题。根据表格中“It was an extremely cold winter's evening and freezing fog hung in the air.”以及“I was back in my home town of Macclesfield to take some winter images.”可知,他们的作品都是冬天的摄影作品。

3Where can the text be found?

  • A.In a history book.
  • B.In a novel.
  • C.In an art magazine.
  • D.In a biography.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　推理判断题。根据第一段的内容可知,这篇文章在介绍Take a view这个一年一度的摄影比赛,由此可推知文章应该可以在艺术杂志上找到。

求财、求爱、求晴天：海外年轻人掀起一股玄学消费热

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Love, luck and magic: Why are young people turning to online witches?

    爱情、好运与魔法：为什么年轻人开始求助于网络女巫？

    witch
    n. 女巫，这里指时下涌现的占卜师、灵性顾问和神秘学服务者等。
  2. 02

    Love or luck, take your pick. All yours with a simple click.

    爱情还是好运，任你挑选。轻点一下，全都归你。

  3. 03

    Or at least that's the promise being made by so-called "Etsy witches", who claim to cast spells for everything from good fortune to good weather.

    至少，所谓的“Etsy女巫”是这样承诺的。她们声称可以为各种愿望施法，从带来好运到求个好天气，无所不包。

  4. 04

    And it seems there's a growing appetite for a little magic. Online retailer Etsy says searches for weather spells and witches have significantly increased this year.

    人们似乎越来越渴望得到一点魔法。电商平台Etsy表示，今年平台上对于天气魔法和女巫的搜索量显著增加。

    appetite
    n. 对某件事的强烈兴趣、需求
  5. 05

    Ellecia Cahill, from Erskine, describes herself as a "mystic" and says there's something her customers are particularly keen on: abundance spells — in other words, a little help attracting money, success and opportunities.

    来自厄斯金的埃莉西亚・卡希尔（Ellecia Cahill）自称是一名 “神秘主义者”，她说有一类服务格外受顾客青睐：富足魔法，换句话说，就是能帮助人们吸引金钱、成功和机会的魔法。

    mystic
    n. 从事神秘学和灵性实践的人
  6. 06

    Whether they can actually deliver is another question entirely—in fact, Etsy rules say spells must be marketed as for entertainment purposes only.

    她们究竟能不能真正做到所承诺的事情，完全是另一个问题。事实上，Etsy规定，魔法服务必须被标注为仅供娱乐。

    deliver
    v. 实现承诺、交出预期的结果
  7. 07

    For Cahill, it's not all about doing business. She says that a lot of younger people feel lonely, and she wants to be a "safe space" for people, and to give them comfort.

    对卡希尔来说，这不完全是一门生意。她说，很多年轻人感到孤独，她希望为他们提供一个“安全空间”，给他们一些安慰。

  8. 08

    "As humans we need to believe in something that is bigger than ourselves," she said.

    “作为人类，我们需要相信某种比自身更大的存在，”她说道。

  9. 09

    "I think a lot of the time people know the answer they want, but are just looking for a bit of reassurance. Even if magic isn't real, if it helps someone feel more confident, what is the harm?"

    “我觉得，很多时候，人们其实知道自己想要什么答案，只是在寻找一点确认和安慰。即使魔法并不存在，如果它能够让一个人更有信心，又有什么关系呢？”

    reassurance
    n. 能消除疑虑的话或行动；安慰，慰藉
  10. 10

    Researcher Alexandria Arthur-Hastie is a sociologist of religion at Leeds Beckett University. She believes the rise of online witches is part of a broader shift away from traditional religion and towards more "personalised forms of spirituality".

    研究者亚历山德里亚·阿瑟-哈斯蒂（Alexandria Arthur-Hastie）是利兹贝克特大学的宗教社会学家。她认为，网络女巫的兴起，是一场更广泛的社会转变的一部分：人们正在逐渐远离传统宗教，转向更加“个人化的灵性形式”。

    spirituality
    n. 人对于精神世界、生命意义以及超越个人的存在所进行的探索；精神性；灵性
  11. 11

    "People go online, they're looking for solutions to these life problems, they're looking for a balm to soothe, really," she said.

    她说，“人们上网寻找这些生活问题的解决办法。说到底，他们是在寻找一剂可以舒缓心绪的良药。”

    soothe
    v. 安抚（焦虑、不安）
    balm
    n. 能够安慰人、缓解痛苦的事物

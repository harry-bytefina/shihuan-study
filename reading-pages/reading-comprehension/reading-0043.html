学习、阅读与教育方式

Studying a subject that you feel pointless is never a fun or easy task. If you're studying history, asking yourself the question “why is history important” is a very good first step. History is an essential part of human civilization. You will find something here that will arouse your interest, or get you thinking about the significance of history.

History grounds us in our roots. History is an important and interesting field of study, and learning the history of our home country can give us a deeper, more meaningful glimpse (一瞥) into our ancestral pasts, and how we got to where we are today. Many people feel like they need a sense of cultural belonging, which is something that studying your roots and being open⁃minded to the evolution of your culture can provide.

History enriches our experience. Reading history is an amazing experience because it enables us to reflect on the social and economic life of the people living long time ago. According to the experts, problems faced by people regardless of the past and present are the same. With the information about the ancestors, one can become more experienced in handling challenges of life.

History makes us more empathetic (具有共情能力的). Studying history can give us insight (洞察力) into why our culture does certain things, and how the past has shaped it into what we know now. It also provides a rather strong foundation for empathy across cultures. Fear and hate for others is usually caused by ignorance (无知). We're scared of the things that we don't understand. History has the potential to break down those boundaries by offering us insight into entire worlds that would otherwise be foreign to us.

History can inspire us to learn more. What's fantastic about history is the way it broadens our horizons. It's almost impossible to learn about one historical period without having dozens of questions about related concepts. Study the 19th century England, and you might catch a glimpse of Charles Dickens' Oliver Twist. Look up Charles Dickens, and you might learn a thing or two about realism. Or maybe you end up switching your attention away from novels, and discover the history of romantic poets in England. It can go anywhere, and there is something in there for absolutely anybody.

The value of history cannot be underestimated. We don't have to live in the past, but we can definitely do better by learning from it and using the lessons learnt to lead more meaningful lives.

1The underlined part “where we are today” in Para. 2 probably means _____ .

  • A.the turning point in our history
  • B.the present state of our nation
  • C.the location of our homeland
  • D.the total area of our country
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

[语篇解读] 本文开头引出话题:为什么历史重要,然后分四方面论述学历史的好处,最后说明学历史的意义——我们可以通过学习历史并利用学到的教训过更有意义的生活。 deeper, more meaningful glimpse into our ancestral pasts, and how we got to where we are today”可知，研究我们祖国的历史可以让我们更深入、更有意义地了解我们祖先的过去以及我们是如何走到今天的，故选B。

2According to the experts, why is history useful for people to handle challenges of life?

  • A.The problems at present are similar to those in the past.
  • B.Ancient people laid economic foundations for people today.
  • C.The current challenges of life were predicted by the ancestors.
  • D.People living long time ago knew more about how to solve problems.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2. A推理判断题。根据第三段中的“According to the experts, problems faced by people regardless of the past and present are the same.（专家认为，无论过去和现在，人们面临的问题是一样的）”可知选A（现在遇到的问题和过去是相似的）。

3What can be concluded from Para. 4?

  • A.It is difficult to get rid of cultural barriers.
  • B.People are willing to accept foreign cultures.
  • C.Cultural conflicts in history are difficult to ignore.
  • D.History helps us improve our cross⁃cultural awareness.

答案：D

3. D主旨大意题。根据“History makes us more empathetic.”和“History has the potential to break down those boundaries by offering us insight into entire worlds that would otherwise be foreign to us.”可知选D（历史帮助我们提高跨文化意识）。

  • A.Charles Dickens contributes much to British literature

  • D.studying history can arouse people's interest in other fields

学制“缩水”的大学，为何反而是更好的教育？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    American universities can’t afford complacency. Tuition is too high, standards are too low and administrators emphasize experience over academics.

    美国各大高校切不可安于现状。学费居高不下，学术标准太低，校方愈发重视校园体验，却轻视专业教学。

    complacency
    自满、安于现状；作者用它批评大学面对高学费和教学问题仍不改革。
  2. 02

    That makes Virginia and Ohio’s venture to develop three-year bachelor’s degree programs good news. A 90-credit bachelor’s degree (instead of the standard 120) would reduce waste and save families about 25% in tuition.

    在此背景下，弗吉尼亚州与俄亥俄州试点推出三年制学士学位项目，这不失为一则好消息。该方案将学士学位学分要求降至 90 学分（常规标准为 120 学分），这样能够减少资源浪费，帮助家庭节省约 25% 的学费开支。

    venture
    带有探索性质的新项目；指两个州试行三年制本科的举措。
  3. 03

    For too long, universities have increased tuition, which is now more than $100,000 a year at some schools. But the four-year model isn’t untouchable. Colleges make money when more students stay longer. Tuition, housing, dining, fees, athletics, facilities and campus amenities all depend on keeping students enrolled in a costly ecosystem. Three-year degrees would disrupt that by putting students’ interests ahead of institutions’.

    长期以来，各大高校不断上调学费，部分院校年度总花费已突破十万美元。然而四年制学位体系并非不可撼动。学生在校就读时间越长，学校获利越多：学费、住宿费、餐费、各类杂费、体育项目、基础设施以及校园配套服务，整套高成本运转体系，都依靠持续留住在校生维持。三年制学位模式将打破这一格局，把学生的切身利益置于院校营收之上。

    amenities
    方便生活的配套设施；campus amenities 指为学生生活提供便利的校园设施。
    enrolled
    已注册在读的；keeping students enrolled 指让学生继续留校修读。
  4. 04

    We need to stop thinking of college as a time to find oneself and recast it as a period of intellectual development.

    我们应当摒弃 “大学是寻找自我的阶段” 这种固有观念，重新将大学定位为心智培育、知识精进的成长时期。

    recast
    重新界定；recast A as B 是把大学重新定位为发展智力的阶段。
  5. 05

    Universities should streamline general-education requirements, trim unnecessary electives and make students declare their majors by the end of freshman year. Students don’t need more politicized course requirements that displace core classes. They need writing, history, math, science, economics, literature and civic knowledge.

    高校应当精简通识教育要求，砍掉不必要的选修课，并要求学生在大一结束前确定专业方向。学生不需要充斥意识形态倾向的课程挤占核心专业课；他们真正需要写作、历史、数学、科学、经济学、文学以及公民通识类课程。

    streamline
    精简流程或要求；文中指删去冗余的通识教育要求。
    electives
    选修课程；trim unnecessary electives 是削减不必要的选修课。
  6. 06

    Academic calendars also need reform. Month-long winter breaks and three month summer vacations are outdated customs based on historical agrarian calendars. Calendar-year schedules would benefit students who want to enter the workforce sooner.

    校历制度同样亟待改革。长达一个月的寒假与三个月的暑假，是沿袭农耕时代旧历的过时传统。采用自然学年制能够帮助希望尽早就业的学生缩短求学周期。

    agrarian
    与农业生产有关的；agrarian calendars 指按农事安排的旧历法。
  7. 07

    Undergraduates can complete bachelor’s degrees in three years if colleges become leaner and more mission-focused. The goal isn’t less education, it’s less waste.

    倘若高校精简冗余环节、回归办学核心使命，本科生完全可以在三年内完成学士学位。这一改革追求的不是缩减教育内容，而是杜绝无谓消耗。

11-2020全国Ⅲ，C

志愿服务、社区与包容

With the young unable to afford to leave home and the old at risk of isolation(孤独), more families are choosing to live together.

The doorway to peace and quiet, for Nick Bright at least, leads straight to his mother⁃in⁃law: she lives on the ground floor, while he lives upstairs with his wife and their two daughters.

Four years ago they all moved into a three⁃storey Victorian house in Bristol—one of a growing number of multigenerational families in the UK living together under the same roof. They share a front door and a washing machine, but Rita Whitehead has her own kitchen, bathroom, bedroom and living room on the ground floor.

“We floated the idea to my mum of sharing a house,” says Kathryn Whitehead. Rita cuts in:“We spoke more with Nick because I think it's a big thing for Nick to live with his mother⁃in⁃law.”

And what does Nick think? “From my standpoint, it all seems to work very well. Would I recommend it? Yes, I think I would.”

It's hard to tell exactly how many people agree with him, but research indicates that the numbers have been rising for some time. Official reports suggest that the number of households with three generations living together had risen from 325,000 in 2001 to 419,000 in 2013.

Other varieties of multigenerational family are more common. Some people live with their elderly parents; many more adult children are returning to the family home, if they ever left. It is said that about 20% of 25⁃34⁃year⁃olds live with their parents, compared with 16% in 1991. The total number of all multigenerational households in Britain is thought to be about 1.8 million.

Stories like that are more common in parts of the world where multigenerational living is more firmly rooted. In India, particularly outside cities, young women are expected to move in with their husband's family when they get married.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Who mainly uses the ground floor in the Victorian house in Bristol?

  • A.Nick.
  • B.Rita.
  • C.Kathryn.
  • D.The daughters.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　细节理解题。第三段提到Rita在一楼有她自己的厨房、浴室、卧室和客厅,由此可知Rita主要用一楼的设施,故选B。

2What is Nick's attitude towards sharing the house with his mother⁃in⁃law?

  • A.Positive.
  • B.Carefree.
  • C.Tolerant.
  • D.Unwilling.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2.A　推理判断题。根据第五段可知,Nick对于和岳母共同生活感到非常满意,故选择A项(积极的)。

3What is the author's statement about multigenerational family based on?

  • A.Family traditions.
  • B.Financial reports.
  • C.Published statistics.
  • D.Public opinions.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　推理判断题。由第六段的官方报告(Official reports)提供的数据可知C项(发布的统计数据)正确。

4What is the text mainly about?

  • A.Lifestyles in different countries.
  • B.Conflicts between generations.
  • C.A housing problem in Britain.
  • D.A rising trend of living in the UK.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　主旨要义题。文章主要讲的是越来越多的英国人开始选择多代同住的生活方式,故D选项(英国一种兴起的生活趋势)正确。

战火未歇，伊朗到底能不能参加世界杯？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    What happens if Iran does not play at the World Cup?

    如果伊朗不参加世界杯，会发生什么？

  2. 02

    Following the conclusion of the final qualification playoffs, we now know the 48 teams who will play at the 2026 World Cup in North America. Or do we?

    随着最后一轮世界杯预选赛附加赛尘埃落定，我们如今已经确定了征战 2026 年北美世界杯的 48 支参赛队伍。但，事实果真如此吗？

    qualification
    n. 资格；资历；合格条件
  3. 03

    Questions have continued to swirl around Iran’s participation ever since United States and Israeli military strikes on the Persian country began in February.

    自今年 2 月美国与以色列对该波斯国家（伊朗）发动军事打击以来，伊朗能否参赛的疑问便一直不绝于耳。

    swirl
    v. 旋转；转圈
  4. 04

    The Iranian men’s national team qualified comfortably last year, and is scheduled to play all three of its Group G games in the U.S. — against New Zealand (June 15, at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles), Belgium (June 21, also at SoFi) and Egypt (June 26, at Lumen Field in Seattle).

    伊朗男足去年轻松拿到世界杯入场券，并按计划将在美国比完全部三场G组小组赛：6 月 15 日在洛杉矶索菲体育场对阵新西兰，6 月 21 日同样在该场馆迎战比利时，6 月 26 日在西雅图流明球场对战埃及。

    comfortably
    adv. 轻松地、不费力地
  5. 05

    But Iran’s sports minister, Ahmad Donyamali, told state TV last month the team“certainly”cannot participate in the tournament,“given that this corrupt (U.S.) regime has assassinated our leader”. A day later, U.S. President Donald Trump wrote on social media that,“I really don’t believe it is appropriate (for Iran to) be there, for their own life and safety”.

    但伊朗体育部长艾哈迈德・多尼亚马利（Ahmad Donyamali）上月对国家电视台表示，鉴于美国这个腐败政权杀害了伊朗领导人，伊朗队肯定不会参加本届赛事。一天后，美国总统特朗普在社交媒体发文称，出于伊朗球员自身的生命安全，伊朗队并不适合来这里参赛。

    assassinate
    v. 刺杀；暗杀
    tournament
    n. 体育联赛
    appropriate
    adj. 合适的、适宜的
  6. 06

    FIFA would essentially have two broad options if Iran withdraws, either by choice or force. FIFA could cancel its three group games and tweak rules to treat Group G as a three-team section, or it could replace Iran with another country’s national team.

    无论伊朗是主动退出还是被迫无法参赛，国际足联大体有两种应对方案：取消伊朗的三场小组赛，微调赛制将 G 组改为三支球队的小组；或是安排其他国家队顶替伊朗参赛。

    tweak
    v. 稍作修改，改进
  7. 07

    Finding a replacement, however, would be complicated by timing. The war could last weeks or months yet — nobody knows. Iran, as a country and a team, might not have clarity on World Cup participation any time soon. Any withdrawal would therefore be relatively last-minute.

    不过，受时间因素制约，寻找替补球队十分复杂。这场冲突可能持续数周甚至数月，谁也说不准。伊朗本国及伊朗国家队，短期内都无法确定能否参加世界杯。因此任何退出决定，都很可能会发生在最后时刻。

    clarity
    n. 清晰；明确

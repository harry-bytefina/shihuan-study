04-2015课标全国Ⅰ，D

食物、农业与地方身份

Conflict is on the menu tonight at the cafe La Chope.This evening,as on every Thursday night,psychologist Maud Lehanne is leading two of France's favorite pastimes,coffee drinking and the“talking cure”.Here they are learning to get in touch with their true feelings.It isn't always easy.The customers—some thirty Parisians who pay just under $2(plus drinks)per session—are quick to intellectualize(高谈阔论),slow to open up and connect.“You are forbidden to say‘one feels,’or‘people think’,”Lehanne told them.“Say ‘I think,’‘Think me’.”

A cafe society where no intellectualizing is allowed?It couldn't seem more un-French.But Lehanne's psychology cafe is about more than knowing oneself:It's trying to help the city's troubled neighborhood cafes.Over the years,Parisian cafes have fallen victim to changes in the French lifestyle—longer working hours,a fast-food boom and a younger generation's desire to spend more time at home.Dozens of new theme cafes appear to change the situation.Cafes focused around psychology,history,and engineering are catching on,filling tables well into the evening.

The city's“psychology cafes”,which offer great comfort,are among the most popular places.Middle-aged homemakers,retirees,and the unemployed come to such cafes to talk about love,anger,and dreams with a psychologist.And they come to Lehanne's group just to learn to say what they feel.“There's a strong need in Paris for communication,”says Maurice Frisch,a cafe La Chope regular who works as a religious instructor in a nearby church.“People have few real friends.And they need to open up.”Lehanne says she'd like to see psychology cafes all over France.“If people had normal lives,these cafes wouldn't exist,”she says.“If life weren't a battle,people wouldn't need a special place just to speak.”But then,it wouldn't be France.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What are people encouraged to do at the cafe La Chope?

  • A.Learn a new subject.
  • B.Keep in touch with friends.
  • C.Show off their knowledge.
  • D.Express their true feelings.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

1.D　细节理解题。根据第一段第三句Here they are learning to get in touch with their true feelings.以及本段最后一句Lehanne所说的话中“Say‘I think,’‘Think me’.”可以看出Lehanne鼓励来咖啡店的顾客表达自己的真实情感。故D项符合文章内容。

2How are cafes affected by French lifestyle changes?

  • A.They are less frequently visited.
  • B.They stay open for longer hours.
  • C.They have bigger night crowds.
  • D.They start to serve fast food.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2.A　推理判断题。根据第二段中的Over the years,Parisian cafes have fallen victim to changes in the French lifestyle—longer working hours,a fast-food boom and a younger generation's desire to spend more time at home.可知,这些年以来,巴黎的咖啡店都成为法国人的生活方式发生改变的受害者,如法国人工作时间变长,非常喜欢快餐,年轻一代更渴望待在家里面(而不愿去咖啡店)。故A项符合文章内容。

3What are theme cafes expected to do?

  • A.Create more jobs.
  • B.Supply better drinks.
  • C.Save the cafe business.
  • D.Serve the neighborhood.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　推理判断题。根据第二段倒数第二句Dozens of new theme cafes appear to change the situation.可知,许多新的主题咖啡店的出现是为了改变法国咖啡店的现状,故C项正确。

4Why are psychology cafes becoming popular in Paris?

  • A.They bring people true friendship.
  • B.They give people spiritual support.
  • C.They help people realize their dreams.
  • D.They offer a platform for business links.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4.B　推理判断题。最后一段指出中年家庭主妇、退休人员、失业人员会到这样的“心理咖啡店”向心理学家畅谈自己的爱情、愤怒和梦想,“心理咖啡店”会给他们巨大的安慰,因而是最受欢迎的地方之一。故B项符合文章意思。

降薪跳槽成常态？1/4 美国人已妥协

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Hoping for a Big Raise This Year? Adjust Your Expectations

    今年还在期待大幅加薪？调整一下你的预期

  2. 02

    Ann Schliemann made six figures as a project manager a couple of years ago. Her next job paid $90,000. Now she is interviewing for $80,000 positions after being laid off in December.

    几年前，安·施利曼（Ann Schliemann）做项目经理时，年薪尚有六位数。但她的下一份工作，薪水降到了9万美元。去年12月她被解雇了，如今她正在面试薪资8万美元的岗位。

  3. 03

    While some Americans are still upping their earnings, federal data show overall wage growth has been slowing since the second half of 2022. More than a quarter of people who recently switched jobs took pay cuts, and 16.3% settled for lateral moves, according to ZipRecruiter.

    虽然有一部分美国人的收入仍在上涨，但联邦数据显示，自2022年下半年以来，整体薪资增速已持续放缓。根据ZipRecruiter的数据，近期换工作的人中，超过四分之一接受了降薪，还有16.3%的人选择了平级跳槽。

    lateral
    横向的、同级别的；lateral moves 指从一个岗位换到级别相当的岗位。
  4. 04

    With more applicants for fewer job openings, many of us are being forced to adjust our expectations around pay.

    随着开放招聘的职位减少而求职者增多，许多人都被迫调整对薪资的预期。

  5. 05

    It's debatable whether AI efficiencies are as responsible for layoffs and salary deflation as some executives claim. Automation can be a convenient cover to reverse postpandemic overhiring—not to mention an attempt to signal that a business is on the cutting edge.

    人工智能带来的效率提升，是否如一些高管所言，是裁员和薪资缩水的罪魁祸首，这一点还尚无定论。而且自动化可能只是扭转疫情后过度招聘的一个便利借口——更不用说这也是一种标志着企业处于技术前沿的手段。

    debatable
    尚可争辩的、没有定论的；指裁员和薪资下行能否归因于 AI。
    deflation
    下降、缩水；salary deflation 说的是薪酬承压，不是整个经济的通货紧缩。
    overhiring
    招聘过多；postpandemic overhiring 指疫情后招聘人数超出实际需要。
  6. 06

    Job seekers aren't the only ones feeling downward pressure on salaries. People who are employed can't assume the customary pay bump is coming this year. At the same time, bonuses are shrinking, and fewer employees are receiving them, according to payroll software provider ADP.

    感受到薪资下行压力的，不是只有求职者。在职人员也不能默认今年的加薪会如期到来。与此同时，根据薪资软件提供商ADP的数据，奖金正在缩水，拿到奖金的员工正在减少。

    customary
    惯常的；customary pay bump 指平常预期会有的那次加薪。
    payroll
    工资发放管理；payroll software 用于处理薪酬和相关记录。
  7. 07

    It's a commentary on how bosses view their charges at the moment. Managers are focused on retaining key players; just about everyone else is replaceable.

    这也反映出了老板们当下对员工的态度。管理者们只关心留住核心员工；而其他几乎所有人都是可以替代的。

    charges
    由某人负责管理的人；their charges 在这里指经理手下的员工，不是费用。
  8. 08

    This should prompt us to think differently about job hopping. Sizable raises have traditionally been primary motivators; security may now be the main reason to make a change. Some workers use the term "lily padding" to describe career moves that are more about finding safe landing spots, or learning skills to become more employable, than about scoring bigger paychecks.

    我们应该因此开始用不同的视角看待跳槽这件事。大幅涨薪一直以来都是跳槽的主要动力；而如今，寻求职业稳定性或许已成为人们跳槽的主要原因。一些职场人士用“踩莲叶式跳槽”来形容这种职业变动——其目的更多在于寻找安全的落脚点，或是学习新技能以提升就业竞争力，而非单纯追求更高的薪资。

    employable
    更有就业竞争力的；学习新技能是为了让自己更容易被雇用。

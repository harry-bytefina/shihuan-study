01-2025天津夏，A

生态、气候与城市自然

Welcome to our university's Botanic Garden—plan your visit, and find out about what we do!

You can either pay at the Ticket Office on arrival, or book your tickets online. Please note: Tickets cannot be used on any date other than the one they were booked for and are non-refundable(不可退款的).

Guidelines for Visitors

The Garden is an active research facility and a living museum with a collection of over 8,000 plants.

Please bear in mind some simple rules for your visit:

Only trained working dogs are permitted to enter the Garden.

Children must stay with a grown-up at all times.

Stick strictly to the paths and lawns(草坪).

Do not climb the trees.

Please do not pick any part of a growing plant.

Cycling and ball games are not permitted in the Garden.

Guided Tours in the Garden

With over 8,000 plant species and a wonderful range of gardens and plantings to enjoy, there is something for everyone at the Garden, and many different aspects to appeal to a wide variety of interest groups.

◆Free Drop-in Weekend Tours

Enjoy a free-of-charge Garden tour with one of our expert guides, taking in the seasonal highlights of the Garden.

Our weekend tours are available every Sunday at 11:30 a.m. and 2 p.m. throughout the year. During the months of January and February these tours focus on the Winter Garden, Snowdrops and general winter interest.

The free tours last one hour and start from the Fountain, near the Main Lawn.

◆Group Tours

Our experienced volunteer guides will help you enjoy the highlights and provide some of the background history and development of this amazing garden.

These 90-minute, pre-bookable tours are ideal for adult groups wishing to make the most of their visit to our 40-acre site. Please visit the Group Guided Tours page for more details.

1What should a potential visitor take note of before booking a ticket to the Garden?

  • A.Whether one can afford the visit.
  • B.Whether the Garden website is accessible.
  • C.Whether one can accept the ticket-booking policy.
  • D.Whether the tickets are still available at the Ticket Office.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C　细节理解题。根据题干关键词take note of可定位在文章第二段的“Please note: Tickets cannot be used on any date other than the one they were booked for and are non-refundable.(请注意:门票仅可在预订的日期使用,且不可退款)”。由此可知,游客在订票前要先确认是否可以接受该订票政策。故选C。

2A kid is allowed to visit the Garden when they are _____ .

  • A.well trained
  • B.with a bicycle
  • C.accompanied by an adult
  • D.guided by their working dog
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2. C　细节理解题。根据题干关键词A kid可定位在第一个小标题Guidelines for Visitors下的“Children must stay with a grown-up at all times.”。由此可知,孩子在参观过程中要有一名成年人陪伴。故选C。

3What behaviour is acceptable in the Garden?

  • A.Picking a flower.
  • B.Climbing a tree.
  • C.Playing a ball game.
  • D.Walking on the lawns.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D　细节理解题。根据题干关键词behaviour is acceptable可定位至第一个小标题Guidelines for Visitors下的6条规定。由“Stick strictly to the paths and lawns.”可知,游客可以在草坪上走。故选D。

4Where do the free drop-in weekend tours start?

  • A.Snowdrops.
  • B.The Fountain.
  • C.The Main Lawn.
  • D.The Winter Garden.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4. B　细节理解题。根据题干关键词free drop-in weekend tours start可定位在文章第八段“The free tours last one hour and start from the Fountain, near the Main Lawn.”。由此可知,游览从主草坪附近的喷泉开始。故选B。

5What special service can visitors enjoy on the group tour?

  • A.The company of professional guides.
  • B.The explanation of how the Garden works.
  • C.The extended visit in and around the Garden.
  • D.The introduction to the birth and growth of the Garden.

答案：D

5. D　细节理解题。根据第九段“Our experienced volunteer guides will...provide some of the background history and development of this amazing garden.”可知,导游会提供有关这座神奇植物园的历史背景和发展历程。the background history and development与D项中的the birth and growth相对应,故选D。

    El Niño Is the Next Risk Hanging Over the Global Economy

    厄尔尼诺，成为笼罩全球经济的下一大风险

  2. 02

    The expected return of the El Niño weather pattern, supercharged by climate change, is shaping up to be the next test for a global economy already strained by the fallout from the Iran war.

    受气候变化加剧影响，厄尔尼诺气候现象或将再度来袭；本就因伊朗战争后遗症承压的全球经济，即将迎来新一轮考验。

    strain
    v. 使承受压力
    supercharge
    v. 加强、增进（原指给发动机增压）
    fallout
    n. 不良影响、后果（原指核爆后的放射性坠尘）

    句子结构

    The expected return of the El Niño weather pattern, / supercharged by climate change, / is shaping up to be the next test / for a global economy already strained by the fallout from the Iran war.

    主语中心词是 return，真正的谓语是 is shaping up。supercharged by … 补充说明气候变化对厄尔尼诺的影响；strained by … 紧跟 economy，说明全球经济已经因什么承压。两处都是过去分词，但所说明的对象不同。

  3. 03

    El Niño occurs every few years when trade winds of the tropical Pacific weaken and the ocean warms up. It typically lasts up to a year, peaking around year-end, and tends to bring dryness and heat to much of Asia and wet weather to various places, including the Gulf Coast.

    厄尔尼诺现象每隔几年便会出现一次。当热带太平洋的信风减弱、海水升温时，便会形成这一气候模式。该现象通常持续一年左右，年末达到峰值，往往令亚洲大部分地区干旱酷热，同时为包括美国墨西哥湾沿岸在内的多个地区带来更多降雨。

  4. 04

    Forecasters in the U.S. and elsewhere say El Niño is highly likely to form this year. Very hot ocean temperatures mean it has the potential to be a severe one, but it’s too early to say.

    美国及其他地区的气象预报专家表示，今年极有可能形成厄尔尼诺。由于当前海洋温度异常偏高，本次厄尔尼诺或强度剧烈，但目前下定论为时过早。

    forecaster
    n. 预测专家、预报者

    In energy, the uncertainty is compounding the big unknown of when the Strait of Hormuz reopens, said BNP Paribas commodities strategist Jason Ying. He’s watching for extra demand for liquefied natural gas in Asia to power air conditioners as temperatures rise. That could leave Europe short in the coming months.

    法国巴黎银行（BNP Paribas）大宗商品策略师杰森・英（Jason Ying）表示，能源市场本就因霍尔木兹海峡何时恢复通航充满变数，厄尔尼诺带来的不确定性更是雪上加霜。随着气温上升，亚洲地区为满足空调用电需求，可能会增加液化天然气（LNG）的进口。这意味着未来几个月，欧洲天然气供应可能更加紧张。

    compound
    v. 使加重、使恶化

    他说："原本运往欧洲储气库的液化天然气货船，可能会改道驶向亚洲。这意味着欧洲将在更低的天然气库存基础上迎接冬季。"

背景补充

厄尔尼诺为什么不只影响太平洋？

厄尔尼诺是热带太平洋海洋和大气共同变化的一个阶段。通常从东向西吹的信风减弱时，赤道中东太平洋海温会比平常偏高。随之改变的降雨和大气环流，会把影响传到别的地区。读到文章谈农作物或物价时，要区分两层：海温和环流会变，某一地区的收成或价格是否受影响，还取决于当地条件。

10-2019课标全国Ⅲ，B

文化遗产、艺术与跨文化

For Western designers, China and its rich culture have long been an inspiration for Western creative.

“It's no secret that China has always been a source(来源) of inspiration for designers,” says Amanda Hill, chief creative officer at A+E Networks, a global media company and home to some of the biggest fashion(时尚) shows.

Earlier this year, the China Through A Looking Glass exhibition in New York exhibited 140 pieces of China-inspired fashionable clothing alongside Chinese works of art, with the aim of exploring the influence of Chinese aesthetics(美学) on Western fashion and how China has fueled the fashionable imagination for centuries. The exhibition had record attendance, showing that there is huge interest in Chinese influences.

“China is impossible to overlook,” says Hill.“Chinese models are the faces of beauty and fashion campaigns that sell dreams to women all over the world, which means Chinese women are not just consumers of fashion—they are central to its movement.” Of course, not only are today's top Western designers being influenced by China—some of the best designers of contemporary fashion are themselves Chinese.“Vera Wang, Alexander Wang, Jason Wu are taking on Galliano, Albaz, Marc Jacobs—and beating them hands down in design and sales,” adds Hill.

For Hill, it is impossible not to talk about China as the leading player when discussing fashion.“The most famous designers are Chinese, so are the models, and so are the consumers,” she says.“China is no longer just another market; in many senses it has become the market. If you talk about fashion today, you are talking about China—its influences, its direction, its breathtaking clothes, and how young designers and models are finally acknowledging that in many ways.”

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What can we learn about the exhibition in New York?

  • A.It promoted the sales of artworks.
  • B.It attracted a large number of visitors.
  • C.It showed ancient Chinese clothes.
  • D.It aimed to introduce Chinese models.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　细节理解题。根据文章第三段中的 The exhibition had record attendance(展览的出席人数达到新的纪录)可知,B项(展览吸引了大量的参观者)正确。

2What does Hill say about Chinese women?

  • A.They are setting the fashion.
  • B.They start many fashion campaigns.
  • C.They admire super models.
  • D.They do business all over the world.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2.A　推理判断题。根据文章第四段可知,Hill认为中国女性不仅仅是时尚的消费者——她们是时尚运动的核心。因此,A项(中国女性开创时尚)符合题意。

3What do the underlined words “taking on” in paragraph 4 mean?

  • A.learning from
  • B.looking down on
  • C.working with
  • D.competing against
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　猜测词义题。根据上下文可知,此处表示Vera Wang, Alexander Wang, Jason Wu 在和Galliano, Albaz, Marc Jacobs竞争,而且在设计和销量上很容易地打败了他们。 根据语境可知,这里taking on的意思是竞争,因此D项(与……竞争)正确。

4What can be a suitable title for the text?

  • A.Young Models Selling Dreams to the World
  • B.A Chinese Art Exhibition Held in New York
  • C.Differences Between Eastern and Western Aesthetics
  • D.Chinese Culture Fueling International Fashion Trends
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　主旨要义题。本文第一段讲了中国及其文化是西方设计界的灵感来源之一,第二段和第三段进一步解释了中国文化对西方时尚界的影响。第四段介绍了中国女性对时尚界的影响。最后一段,Hill总结了中国在国际时尚界的巨大影响。因此D项是本文最合适的标题。

近期名画频繁失窃，大盗为何专盯欧洲博物馆？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    A Detective’s Perspective on the Rapid Rise in Art Theft

    一名侦探对艺术品盗窃案激增的看法

  2. 02

    On Sept. 8, thieves in southern France broke into Impressionist painter Pierre-Auguste Renoir’s last home, now a museum, and escaped with two of his paintings. In August, unmasked men absconded with a 200-carat platinum and diamond necklace from Vienna’s Museum of Applied Arts. Those daring crimes followed the infamous 2025 raid at the Louvre, where men disguised as construction workers made off with French crown jewels.

    9月8日，法国南部的一群窃贼闯入印象派画家皮埃尔-奥古斯特·雷诺阿（Pierre-Auguste Renoir）的最后居所（现为博物馆），盗走两幅画作后逃逸。8月，几名未戴面具的男子从维也纳应用艺术博物馆盗走了一条200克拉的铂金镶钻项链。这些胆大妄为的罪行紧跟2025年卢浮宫那起臭名昭著的盗窃案——当时，几名伪装成建筑工人的男子盗走了法国王室王冠上的珠宝。

    abscond
    v. 秘密潜逃、躲避法律责任或监管
    raid
    n. 犯罪突袭
  3. 03

    Coincidence? No, “it’s a real uptick,” says the art detective Robert King Wittman, a former FBI agent.

    是巧合吗？不，“这确实是一次明显的抬头，”艺术犯罪侦探、前联邦调查局探员罗伯特·金·维特曼（Robert King Wittman）说道。

    uptick
    n. 小幅上涨（来自早期股票市场打字实时显示股价）
  4. 04

    “Criminals are going after soft targets, and museums are much softer targets than banks and even some jewelry stores,” which frequently employ armed security guards, he says…

    “犯罪分子专挑‘软目标’下手，而博物馆比银行、甚至比一些珠宝店要‘软’得多，”他说，后者通常会雇佣持枪保安。

  5. 05

    It’s no accident that most cases are taking place in Europe. A lot of the museums are historic houses, and it’s very difficult to rewire internet technology.

    大多数案例发生在欧洲，这并非偶然。许多博物馆都是历史建筑，要重新布线以适配互联网技术非常困难。

    rewire
    v. 重新布线
  6. 06

    “On top of that,” he added, “the European Union has open borders. So, you have easy escape routes, lack of armed guards, lack of electronic surveillance and electronic response.”

    “除此之外，”他补充道，“欧盟实行开放边境政策，因此，逃跑路线容易获得，武装警卫不足，电子监控和电子响应也缺乏。”

  7. 07

    Mr Wittman has concluded that the perpetrators are “terrible businessmen” who overestimate their potential returns.

    维特曼先生得出结论：这些作案者是“糟糕的商人”，他们高估了潜在的回报。

  8. 08

    Famous paintings are difficult to monetize. Criminals are often forced to hold on to precious paintings for years until they can unload them, possibly to shady art dealers who try to sell them to unsophisticated buyers who don’t know, or don’t care, about provenance.

    名画很难变现。罪犯往往被迫将这些珍贵画作囤积数年，直到能将其脱手为止——接手的往往是一些来路不明的画商，他们试图将画作转卖给对作品来源一无所知，或者漠不关心的外行买家。

    unsophisticated
    adj. 没见过世面的、不懂行的
    monetize
    v. 把不是钱的东西变成钱
    provenance
    n. 来源，出处（词源法语）
    unload
    v. 脱手，卸货
  9. 09

    Penalties for art theft are usually low. Unless they use firearms, culprits are usually spared steep sentences.

    艺术品盗窃的量刑通常很轻。除非动用了枪支，作案者通常不会被判处重刑。

  10. 10

    Museums have to improve their electronic security systems; vet, train and check on all of their employees and volunteers; and make sure their operations manuals are updated and circulated every year.

    博物馆必须改进其电子安防系统；必须对所有员工和志愿者进行背景审查、培训及定期考核；并确保操作手册每年更新、下发到人。

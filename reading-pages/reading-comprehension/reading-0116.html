01-2021新高考Ⅰ，A

城市、交通与公共空间

Rome can be pricey for travelers, which is why many choose to stay in a hostel(旅社). The hostels in Rome offer a bed in a dorm room for around ＄25 a night, and for that, you’ll often get to stay in a central location(位置) with security and comfort.

Yellow Hostel

If I had to make just one recommendation for where to stay in Rome, it would be Yellow Hostel. It’s one of the best-rated hostels in the city, and for good reason. It’s affordable, and it’s got a fun atmosphere without being too noisy. As an added bonus， it’s close to the main train station.

Hostel Alessandro Palace

If you love social hostels, this is the best hostel for you in Rome. Hostel Alessandro Palace is fun. Staff members hold plenty of bar events for guests like free shots, bar crawls and karaoke. There’s also an area on the rooftop for hanging out with other travelers during the summer.

Youth Station Hostel

If you’re looking for cleanliness and a modern hostel, look no further than Youth Station. It offers beautiful furnishings and beds. There are plenty of other benefits, too;it doesn’t charge city tax; it has both air conditioning and a heater for the rooms; it also has free Wi-Fi in every room.

Hotel and Hostel Des Artistes

Hotel and Hostel Des Artistes is located just a 10minute walk from the central city station and it’s close to all of the city’s main attractions. The staff is friendly and helpful, providing you with a map of the city when you arrive, and offering advice if you require some. However, you need to pay 2 euros a day for Wi-Fi.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What is probably the major concern of travelers who choose to stay in a hostel?

  • A.Comfort.
  • B.Security.
  • C.Price.
  • D.Location.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C细节理解题。根据首段第一句（罗马对旅行者来说是很昂贵的，这就是很多人选择住在旅社的原因）可知选择住在旅社的旅客最关心的是价格。

2Which hotel best suits people who enjoy an active social life?

  • A.Yellow Hostel.
  • B.Hostel Alessandro Palace.
  • C.Youth Station Hostel.
  • D.Hotel and Hostel Des Artistes.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2. B细节理解题。根据Hostel Alessandro Palace下的第一句“If you love social hostels, this is the best hostel for you in Rome.（如果你喜欢社交旅社，这是罗马最适合你的旅社。）”可知选择B项。

3What is the disadvantage of Hotel and Hostel Des Artistes?

  • A.It gets noisy at night.
  • B.Its staff is too talkative.
  • C.It charges for Wi-Fi.
  • D.It’s inconveniently located.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3. C细节理解题。根据Hotel and Hostel Des Artistes下的最后一句“However, you need to pay 2 euros a day for Wi-Fi.”可知，Wi-Fi收费是它的缺点。 长难句 The staff is friendly and helpful, providing you with a map of the city when you arrive, and offering advice if you require some. 译文 工作人员很友好，乐于助人，当你到达时为你提供该市地图，如果你需要一些建议，（他们）可以提供。 分析 providing you with a map of the city when you arrive, and offering advice if you require some是现在分词短语作状语，providing...和offering...是并列关系。

十三万人失踪，墨西哥却豪掷重金办世界杯

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    World Cup Puts Mexico's Cartel Crisis on the Global Stage

    世界杯让墨西哥的毒枭危机暴露在世界注目之下

  2. 02

    In this upscale, leafy suburb of Guadalajara, where manicured lawns sit near high-end shopping centers, 89 bags of human remains have been found in the past year, dumped in ravines or dug up from unmarked graves.

    在瓜达拉哈拉（Guadalajara）一个绿树成荫的高档郊区，修剪整齐的草坪紧邻着高端购物中心，过去一年里，这里发现了89袋人类遗体，它们有的被丢弃在山谷里，有的则从无名坟墓中被挖掘发现。

    manicured
    adj. 像护理指甲那样精心打理过的
  3. 03

    A few miles from those macabre discoveries, soccer fans will pour into the volcano-shaped Akron Stadium, just outside Guadalajara, for the first of four World Cup matches that will be played there, beginning Thursday.

    距离那些令人毛骨悚然的遗体发掘出仅几英里之遥，足球迷们将涌入位于瓜达拉哈拉郊外、形似火山的阿克伦体育场，观看将于周四拉开帷幕的四场世界杯比赛中的首场比赛。

    macabre
    adj. 阴森可怖的，通常和死亡相关
  4. 04

    Four months after a spasm of cartel violence paralyzed Guadalajara and the surrounding state of Jalisco, Mexican authorities are mounting a multimillion-dollar security dragnet to convince the world the tournament is safe. Guadalajara is particularly sensitive, located in the namesake state of the Jalisco New Generation Cartel—one of the biggest, most violent organized-crime groups in the country.

    在贩毒集团的暴力活动导致瓜达拉哈拉及周边的哈利斯科州（Jalisco）陷入瘫痪四个月后，墨西哥当局正展开一项耗资数百万美元的安全排查行动，以向全世界证明本届赛事是安全的。瓜达拉哈拉的局势尤为敏感，因为它位于“哈利斯科新生代贩毒集团”名字的来源地哈利斯科州——该组织是墨西哥境内规模最大、最为暴力的有组织犯罪集团之一。

    mount
    vt. 开展、组织、发动
    dragnet
    n. 警方的抓捕行动、收网行动
  5. 05

    More than 130,000 people are missing across Mexico—most of them kidnapped or killed by cartels, sometimes in cooperation with law enforcement. Their relatives are plastering posters with photos of them outside World Cup sites, and they will take part in protests that threaten to disrupt access to the Estadio Azteca in Mexico City for Thursday's opening game.

    墨西哥全国有超过13万人下落不明——其中大多数人被贩毒集团绑架或杀害，有时这些集团还会与执法部门勾结。失踪者亲属在世界杯赛场外张贴印有亲人照片的海报，并计划参加抗议活动，这可能导致周四开幕战当天前往墨西哥城阿兹特克体育场的交通受阻。

    plaster
    v. 使贴紧、粘紧
  6. 06

    For the families of Mexico's missing, the millions spent on high-tech stadium shields are an insult.

    对于墨西哥失踪者的家属来说，花在高科技体育场安保系统上的数百万资金，简直是一种侮辱。

    shield
    n. 保护，防护
    insult
    n. 侮辱，辱骂
  7. 07

    "There's a lack of resources and of will," said Jaime Aguilar, a volunteer with Search Warriors of Jalisco, which looks for clandestine graves across the state. "The resources are there. With the World Cup, we are seeing so much squandering of money to meet FIFA guidelines."

    “资源欠缺，意愿也不足。”哈利斯科州“搜寻勇士”组织的一名志愿者海梅·阿吉拉尔（Jaime Aguilar）说道。该组织致力于在全州范围内搜寻秘密墓地。“资源其实是有的。在世界杯期间，我们看到大量资金被挥霍，只是为了满足国际足联的标准。”

    squander
    v. 浪费，挥霍

睡眠、运动与身心健康

Race walking shares many fitness benefits with running, research shows, while most likely contributing to fewer injuries. It does, however, have its own problem.

Race walkers are conditioned athletes. The longest track and field event at the Summer Olympics is the 50⁃kilometer race walk, which is about five miles longer than the marathon. But the sport's rules require that a race walker's knees stay straight through most of the leg swing and one foot remain in contact(接触) with the ground at all times. It's this strange form that makes race walking such an attractive activity, however, says Jaclyn Norberg, an assistant professor of exercise science at Salem State University in Salem, Mass.

Like running, race walking is physically demanding, she says. According to most calculations, race walkers moving at a pace of six miles per hour would burn about 800 calories(卡路里) per hour, which is approximately twice as many as they would burn walking, although fewer than running, which would probably burn about 1,000 or more calories per hour.

However, race walking does not pound the body as much as running does, Dr. Norberg says. According to her research, runners hit the ground with as much as four times their body weight per step, while race walkers, who do not leave the ground, create only about 1.4 times their body weight with each step.

As a result, she says, some of the injuries associated with running, such as runner's knee, are uncommon among race walkers. But the sport's strange form does place considerable stress on the ankles and hips, so people with a history of such injuries might want to be cautious in adopting the sport. In fact, anyone wishing to try race walking should probably first consult a coach or experienced racer to learn proper technique, she says. It takes some practice.

越来越多夫妻，把“全职带娃补偿”写进了“婚前协议”

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Trigger Clause More Couples Want in Their Prenups

    越来越多情侣希望在婚前协议中加入的“触发条款”

    prenup
    n. 婚前协议
  2. 02

    From her perch in Midtown Manhattan, divorce attorney Lisa Zeiderman has seen every imaginable marital horror story. Near the top of the list: Divorces where the spouse who stays home with the children is left with very little after decades of marriage.

    在位于曼哈顿中城的办公室里，离婚律师丽莎·扎伊德曼（Lisa Zeiderman）见识过各种各样你能想象到的婚姻恐怖故事。其中最恐怖的莫过于：当数十年的婚姻走到尽头，留在家中抚养孩子的一方最终落得几乎一无所有。

    perch
    n. 居高临下的位置、有利的观察点
  3. 03

    But over the last couple of years, she is increasingly being asked to craft prenuptial agreements that include a "leaving the workforce" trigger. It activates if one spouse decides to stay at home and give up a career.

    但过去几年中，越来越多的人找她起草包含“退出职场”触发条款的婚前协议。如果婚姻中的一方决定照顾家庭并放弃事业，该条款就会生效。

    activate
    v.（预先订立的条款在特定条件满足后）使处于休眠状态的机制、法律条款等转入生效状态；激活
    craft
    v. 精心起草
    leave the workforce
    全面而长期地退出劳动力市场
  4. 04

    Anna Morgenstern, 40 years old, and Zach Spiess, 45, used an online platform to create their own agreements. The trigger they agreed on ensured the working spouse—whoever it would be—would provide additional financial support if one of them left the workforce, except in the case of infidelity.

    40岁的安娜·莫根施特恩（Anna Morgenstern）和45岁的扎克·斯皮斯（Zach Spiess）选择使用一个在线平台来创建自己的婚前协议。他们商定的触发条款约定，如果其中一方退出职场，继续工作的另一方——无论具体是谁——将为对方提供额外的财务支持，除非存在婚姻不忠行为。

    ensure
    v. 确保……发生
  5. 05

    "It was a good opportunity to get a view into how he thinks about our future," Morgenstern said. The process became a kind of financial planning exercise.

    “这是一次很好的机会，让我了解他如何看待我们的未来，” 莫根施特恩说道。整个过程某种程度上变成了一次财务规划演习。

  6. 06

    One way to structure the provision is to have the working spouse set aside money that would reflect the stay-at-home parent's previous salary, as well as 3% to 5% annual raises. Or the working spouse could contribute regularly to an investment account that goes solely to the other parent in a divorce.

    构建该条款的方式之一是让工作的配偶预留一笔资金，该资金应反映全职在家一方先前的薪水外加每年3%至5%的涨幅。或者，工作的配偶可以定期向一个投资账户存钱，一旦离婚，该账户的资金将完全归另一方所有。

    provision
    n. 条款
    contribute
    v. 与他人一道支付（部分或全部）资金，以划入共同基金或用于共同目的（to pay along with others towards a common fund or for a common purpose）

学习、阅读与教育方式

Marian Bechtel sits at West Palm Beach's Bar Louie counter by herself, quietly reading her e⁃book as she waits for her salad. What is she reading?None of your business!Lunch is Bechtel's “me” time. And like more Americans, she's not alone.

A new report found 46 percent of meals are eaten alone in America. More than half(53 percent)have breakfast alone and nearly half(46 percent)have lunch by themselves. Only at dinnertime are we eating together anymore, 74 percent, according to statistics from the report.

“I prefer to go out and be out. Alone, but together, you know?”Bechtel said, looking up from her book. Bechtel, who works in downtown West Palm Beach, has lunch with coworkers sometimes, but like many of us, too often works through lunch at her desk. A lunchtime escape allows her to keep a boss from tapping her on the shoulder. She returns to work feeling energized. “Today, I just wanted some time to myself,”she said.

Just two seats over, Andrew Mazoleny, a local videographer, is finishing his lunch at the bar. He likes that he can sit and check his phone in peace or chat up the barkeeper with whom he's on a first⁃name basis if he wants to have a little interaction(交流). “I reflect on how my day's gone and think about the rest of the week,”he said. “It's a chance for self⁃reflection. You return to work recharged and with a plan.”

That freedom to choose is one reason more people like to eat alone. There was a time when people may have felt awkward about asking for a table for one, but those days are over. Now, we have our smartphones to keep us company at the table. “It doesn't feel as alone as it may have before all the advances in technology,”said Laurie Demeritt, whose company provided the statistics for the report.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What are the statistics in paragraph 2 about?

  • A.Food variety.
  • B.Eating habits.
  • C.Table manners.
  • D.Restaurant service.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　细节理解题。根据第二段可知,本段中出现的统计数字都体现了人们吃饭的习惯,所以选择B项。

2Why does Bechtel prefer to go out for lunch?

  • A.To meet with her coworkers.
  • B.To catch up with her work.
  • C.To have some time on her own.
  • D.To collect data for her report.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　细节理解题。根据第三段的最后一句的Today, I just wanted some time to myself可知答案为C项。

3What do we know about Mazoleny?

  • A.He makes videos for the bar.
  • B.He's fond of the food at the bar.
  • C.He interviews customers at the bar.
  • D.He's familiar with the barkeeper.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　细节理解题。根据题干中的人名Mazoleny可知答案应在第四段寻找。根据第四段的第二句可知,他和服务员很熟,故答案为D项。on a first⁃name basis意为“很熟络”。

4What is the text mainly about?

  • A.The trend of having meals alone.
  • B.The importance of self⁃reflection.
  • C.The stress from working overtime.
  • D.The advantage of wireless technology.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4.A　主旨要义题。文章最后一段出现了关键词eat alone,第三段和第四段举了两个人独自吃饭的例子。整篇文章都是围绕着eat alone展开的,与A项中的having meals alone相匹配,故答案为A项。

悉尼邦迪海滩惊魂：枪口之下，选择挺身而出的平民

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Australia's Complacency and the Bondi Beach Shooting

    澳大利亚的安逸自满与邦迪海滩枪击案

    complacency
    n. 麻痹大意，缺乏警觉
  2. 02

    Australians like to believe that some places are beyond politics, beyond hatred, beyond violence. Bondi Beach, a sun-washed stretch of sand in Sydney, is one of them. That illusion was shattered on Sunday afternoon.

    澳大利亚人总是相信有些地方是超越政治、远离仇恨、杜绝暴力的。就比如悉尼那片洒满阳光的邦迪海滩（Bondi Beach），便是其中之一。但这种幻象，在周日下午被彻底击碎。

    shatter
    v. 粉碎
    illusion
    n. 幻象
  3. 03

    At least 16 people, including a 12-year-old girl, were murdered while gathering to celebrate Hanukkah, a Jewish festival that centers on survival against the odds. The attack didn't occur in a dark alley or at the margins of society but in daylight at one of Australia's most famous public spaces. This was no mere crime. It was a national reckoning.

    至少有16人遇害，其中包括一名12岁的女孩。当时，人们正聚集在一起庆祝光明节（Hanukkah）——这是一个以“在逆境中存活”为核心主题的犹太节日。这起袭击并非发生在阴暗的小巷或社会的边缘地带，而是于光天化日之下，发生在澳大利亚最著名的公共场所之一。这不只是一桩普通犯罪，而是一次全国性的“审判时刻”。

  4. 04

    Since Oct. 7, 2023, Jewish Australians have reported a sharp rise in harassment, intimidation, vandalism and threats at schools, universities, workplaces and public spaces. Antisemitic graffiti had already appeared in Bondi in the weeks before the attack. The violence didn't emerge from nowhere.

    自2023年10月7日以来，澳大利亚的犹太群体报告称，在学校、大学、工作场所及公共空间中，他们所遭遇的骚扰、恐吓、破坏他人财物和暴力威胁事件急剧增加。在袭击发生前几周，邦迪海滩一带就已出现反犹涂鸦。这场暴力并非凭空而来。

    harassment
    n. 骚扰
    intimidation
    n. 恐吓
  5. 05

    Political violence grows when hostility is normalized, when legitimate grievances are dismissed, and when those targeted are told, explicitly or implicitly, that their fear is exaggerated or politically inconvenient. The pattern is familiar: Elites hesitate to name antisemitism clearly for fear of inflaming tensions, only to discover that ambiguity emboldens extremists rather than restraining them.

    当敌意被常态化，当合理诉求被忽视，当遭受针对的群体被直接或通过种种暗示告知——他们的恐惧被视为“反应过度”，或是在政治上“不合时宜”，政治暴力便会滋生。而这样的模式并不陌生——精英阶层因担心激化紧张局势而迟迟不愿明确点出反犹主义，最终就会发现，含糊其辞只会助长极端分子的气焰，而非遏制他们。

    legitimate
    adj. 正当的，合理的
    restrain
    v. 以强制力来约束、制止
  6. 06

    The cost of inaction isn't borne by Jewish Australians alone. When any group is made unsafe in public life, the promise of equal citizenship is weakened for everyone. Antisemitism corrodes the civic foundations on which plural societies depend.

    不作为的代价并非仅由澳大利亚犹太群体承担。当任何一个群体在公共生活中失去安全感，那么面向所有人的平等公民权承诺都会被削弱。反犹主义正在侵蚀多元社会赖以维系的公民根基。

    corrode
    v. 损害，侵蚀
  7. 07

    Bondi Beach should never have been the scene of terror. That it was—during Hanukkah, in daylight—is a grave warning. Australia can choose complacency, or it can choose courage.

    邦迪海滩本不该成为任何恐袭事件的发生地。可它偏偏在光明节期间、在朗朗白昼下发生了——这是一个严峻的警告。澳大利亚可以选择继续自满，也可以选择拿出勇气。

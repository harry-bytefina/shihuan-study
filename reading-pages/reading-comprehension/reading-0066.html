阅读理解

07-2017天津春，C

学习、阅读与教育方式

Debra Avery has been a horse lover since she was a little girl in San Diego.Her family couldn't afford a horse of its own, so Debra borrowed rides at the local horse-riding center, gaining the confidence only found atop a horse.Years later, Debra remembered those early days, and the lessons she learned with the horses at the riding center, and wanted to help other young women get the benefits of connecting with horses.“I always wanted to run a horse program for girls like me,” says Debra.“I wanted to help them gain the courage to be around a horse and to care for a horse.”

One day at the Los Angeles Horse-riding Center, as Debra rode beside fellow rider Judith Hopkins, she mentioned an inheritance(遗产)from her father-in-law and her desire to use it to serve disadvantaged girls.Judith had the same dream—and a name for the organization.

They founded Taking the Reins (TTR) in 1998.The organization serves more than 400 young women per year in after-school, weekend, and summer programs that feature horse riding and care, gardening and even competitive horse showing.Most participants live below the poverty line and often have not had reliable housing for more than six months at a time.Few have previous horse experience.

TTR uses farm and ranch experiences to teach responsibility, leadership, teamwork, and self-confidence.Director Dr. Jane Haven says, “A large number of our girls are somewhat autistic(孤独症的).I can't count how many of them have found their first social friends thanks to sharing this hobby and activity.Horses bring them out in such a wonderful way.”

Jane also sees members of TTR program excel in school—100 percent of young women involved for four years or more have graduated from high school.Ninety-nine percent of those participants have gone on to attend four-year colleges.

Debra sees a more obvious impact—after a few weeks in the TTR program, the young women sit a little higher in their saddles(马鞍).“There's just something about sitting up on the back of a horse that gives them a boost of confidence.”

1Debra's early life was greatly influenced by _____ .

  • A.her family
  • B.horse riding
  • C.school lessons
  • D.her fellow rider
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B细节理解题。由首段第二句中的 gaining the confidence only found atop a horse可知，是骑马使她获得了自信，从而改变了她早年的生活。

2What programs does TTR offer?

  • A.Horse riding and showing.
  • B.Horse care and farming.
  • C.Pet care and gardening.
  • D.Weekend hiking and camping.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2.A细节理解题。第三段第二句中的 programs that feature horse riding and care, gardening and even competitive horse showing可知答案为A。

3Who was the organization founded for?

  • A.Homeless and lonely women.
  • B.Hard-working school girls.
  • C.Female autistic patients.
  • D.Poor horse-loving girls.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D细节理解题。由第一段中Debra早年的经历及她的话“I always wanted to run a horse program for girls like me"可知选D。

4In what way has TTR changed its members' life?

  • A.They have been given financial aid.
  • B.They have achieved academic excellence.
  • C.They have grown into professional horse riders.
  • D.They have been provided with reliable housing.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4.B细节理解题。由倒数第二段可知，百分之百的成员都中学毕业，百分之九十九的成员进入四年制大学。故答案为B。

5What can we learn from the last paragraph?

  • A.TTR is a profit-making organization.
  • B.TTR program lasts a few weeks.
  • C.The girls' confidence grows quickly.
  • D.The girls can ride taller horses.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

5.C细节理解题。由尾段可知，这些女孩子在几周内就获得了自信，故答案为C。

拒绝苹果，不留硅谷，Kimi创始人杨植麟的回国豪赌赢了吗？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The 33-Year-Old AI Hotshot Taking Silicon Valley Swagger Back to Beijing

    这位33岁的AI红人把硅谷派头带回了北京

    hotshot
    n. 神人，高手
    swagger
    v. 大摇大摆地走
  2. 02

    Many of China's brightest computer engineering students come to the U.S. for its educational opportunities and often stay, shaping tech culture from teaching positions at prestigious schools or senior roles at companies big and small. Yang Zhilin wanted to go home. His Ph.D. adviser at Carnegie Mellon University—renowned computer scientist Ruslan Salakhutdinov—thought it was a bad idea. "That's not what I would have done in his shoes," Salakhutdinov said.

    中国最聪明的那批计算机工程学生，很多都为了教育机会来美国，而且往往会留在美国，通过名校教职或大大小小公司的高管职位塑造着这里的科技文化。杨植麟却想回国。他在卡内基梅隆大学的博士导师，著名计算机科学家鲁斯兰·萨拉赫丁诺夫（Ruslan Salakhutdinov），认为这是个坏主意。“换作是我，我不会这么做，”萨拉赫丁诺夫说。

    renowned
    adj. 著名的，享有盛誉的
  3. 03

    Yang's gamble is starting to pay off. The 33-year-old is the co-founder and chief executive of Moonshot AI, one of China's top AI labs. His startup's latest private funding round values it at $50 billion, and confidentially submitted an application for an initial public offering to Hong Kong's stock exchange.

    杨植麟的这场豪赌正开始获得回报。这位33岁的年轻人是中国顶尖AI实验室之一月之暗面的联合创始人兼首席执行官。他的初创公司最新一轮私募融资估值达500亿美元，并已秘密向香港交易所提交了首次公开募股申请。

    gamble
    n. 赌注
  4. 04

    He's infusing his Beijing-based startup with Western flair. Moonshot eschews titles and hierarchy, a relatively unusual practice at a Chinese company and one Yang sees as essential to get rid of red tape. "Direct communication" is his personal motto. Moonshot's roughly 300 employees sometimes wear slippers in the office and step into conference rooms named after bands and musicians including Led Zeppelin, Metallica, Queen, Prince and Bob Dylan.

    他正为这家总部位于北京的初创公司注入了西方气质。月之暗面摒弃头衔和层级，这在中国公司里相对少见，而杨植麟认为这对消除官僚流程至关重要。“直接沟通”是他的个人信条。月之暗面的约300名员工有时会在办公室里穿着拖鞋，走进以乐队和音乐人命名的会议室，包括齐柏林飞艇（Led Zeppelin）、金属乐队（Metallica）、皇后乐队（Queen）、王子乐队（Prince）和鲍勃·迪伦（Bob Dylan）。

    eschew
    v.（出于某种原则）刻意避开；摒弃
    flair
    n. 格调，腔调，气质
  5. 05

    The adoption of Silicon Valley swagger in China is part of a fascinating cultural exchange happening in both places. Meanwhile, some American startups, racing to build the next generation of trillion-dollar companies, have also embraced the "9-9-6" schedule—a grueling timetable of working from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., six days a week—that has its origins in China.

    在中国采用硅谷派头，是两地正在发生的一场有趣的文化交流的一部分。与此同时，一些美国初创公司为了竞相打造下一代万亿美元公司，也接受了“996工作制”——一种早九点到晚九点，一周六天的折磨人的残酷时间表，它起源于中国。

    embrace
    v. 欣然接受
    grueling
    adj. 累得人脱一层皮的，使人精疲力尽的
  6. 06

    Of all of Salakhutdinov's Chinese national students, only two have elected to go back to China — Yang being one of them. "He never even did a proper job search,"he said.

    在萨拉赫丁诺夫的所有中国籍学生中，只有两人选择回中国——杨植麟是其中之一。“他甚至没有正经找过工作，”他说。

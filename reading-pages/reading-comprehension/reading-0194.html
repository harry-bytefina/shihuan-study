11-2018课标全国Ⅲ，C

文化遗产、艺术与跨文化

While famous foreign architects are invited to lead the designs of landmark buildings in China such as the new CCTV tower and the National Center for the Performing Arts, many excellent Chinese architects are making great efforts to take the center stage.

Their efforts have been proven fruitful. Wang Shu, a 49⁃year⁃old Chinese architect, won the 2012 Pritzker Architecture Prize—which is often referred to as the Nobel Prize in architecture—on February 28. He is the first Chinese citizen to win this award.

Wang serves as head of the Architecture Department at the China Academy of Art(CAA). His office is located at the Xiangshan campus(校园) of the university in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province. Many buildings on the campus are his original creations.

The style of the campus is quite different from that of most Chinese universities. Many visitors were amazed by the complex architectural space and abundant building types. The curves(曲线) of the buildings perfectly match the rise and fall of hills, forming a unique view.

Wang collected more than 7 million abandoned bricks of different ages. He asked the workers to use traditional techniques to make the bricks into walls, roofs and corridors. This creation attracted a lot of attention thanks to its mixture of modern and traditional Chinese elements(元素).

Wang's works show a deep understanding of modern architecture and a good knowledge of traditions. Through such a balance, he had created a new type of Chinese architecture, said Tadao Ando, the winner of the 1995 Pritzker Prize.

Wang believes traditions should not be sealed in glass boxes at museums. “That is only evidence that traditions once existed,”he said.

“Many Chinese people have a misunderstanding of traditions. They think tradition means old things from the past. In fact, tradition also refers to the things that have been developing and that are still being created,”he said.

“Today, many Chinese people are learning Western styles and theories rather than focusing on Chinese traditions. Many people tend to talk about traditions without knowing what they really are,”said Wang.

The study of traditions should be combined with practice. Otherwise, the recreation of traditions would be artificial and empty, he said.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Wang's winning of the prize means that Chinese architects are _____ .

  • A.following the latest world trend
  • B.getting international recognition
  • C.working harder than ever before
  • D.relying on foreign architects
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　推理判断题。根据第二段第一句(他们的努力已被证明是富有成效的。)和最后一句(他是第一个获得这一奖项的中国公民。)可推知,王澍获奖意味着中国的建筑设计师正在获得世界的认可,故选B项。

2What impressed visitors to the CAA Xiangshan campus most?

  • A.Its hilly environment.
  • B.Its large size.
  • C.Its unique style.
  • D.Its diverse functions.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　细节理解题。根据第四段的内容,尤其是最后一句(这些建筑物的曲线与山的起伏完美搭配,形成了独特的风景。)可知答案为C项。

3What made Wang's architectural design a success?

  • A.The mixture of different shapes.
  • B.The balance of East and West.
  • C.The use of popular techniques.
  • D.The harmony of old and new.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　推理判断题。根据第五段最后一句(由于现代元素与中国传统元素的结合,这一作品吸引了很多关注。)可知答案为D项。

4What should we do about Chinese traditions according to Wang?

  • A.Spread them to the world.
  • B.Preserve them at museums.
  • C.Teach them in universities.
  • D.Recreate them in practice.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　细节理解题。根据最后一段可知,对传统的学习应该和实践相结合,否则对传统的重新创造就会虚假又空洞,由此可知答案为D项。

泡泡玛特季度销售锐减：热潮退去，谁还真的爱Labubu？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    He spent $50,000 on Labubus but now the craze is dying

    他曾为拉布布豪掷5万美元，但如今这股热潮正在退去

    craze
    n. 一时的狂热，风靡一时的热潮
  2. 02

    For years, Labubu dolls seemed unstoppable. Collectors young and old energetically snapped up "blind boxes" - sealed, mystery packages containing the quirky monsters.

    过去几年，拉布布玩偶的热度似乎势不可挡。收藏爱好者，无论老少，都争相抢购“盲盒”——这些密封包装的未知盒子里藏着各款造型古怪的小怪兽。

    energetically
    adv. 充满活力地，积极热情地
    unstoppable
    adj. 无法阻挡的
    quirky
    adj. 古怪的、奇特的（带有趣、讨人喜欢的意味）
  3. 03

    The hype around the creatures convinced a mega-fan from North Carolina, who goes by the name Ensito, to leave his tech job in 2024 to create Labubu content full-time. Ensito has spent more than $50,000 to build his collection of more than 400 Labubus. But in recent months, Ensito's once-viral posts about the furry monsters have struggled. "Those videos used to be able to hit 10,000 really quickly but I'm struggling to do that now."

    拉布布热潮让一位来自美国北卡罗来纳州、名为恩西托（Ensito）的狂热粉丝在2024年辞去科技行业的工作，开始全职制作拉布布相关内容。如今，他已经花费超过5万美元，收藏了400多个拉布布玩偶。但近几个月来，恩西托关于这些小毛怪、一度爆火的帖子数据表现并不理想。“以前，那些视频的播放量轻轻松松就能破万，但现在很难做到。”他说。

    mega-fan
    n. 超级粉丝，铁杆粉丝
    hype
    n. 炒作，热潮，狂热追捧
  4. 04

    It's a sign that the craze which made parent company Pop Mart worth more than US toy giants Hasbro and Mattel put together may be running out of steam. Pop Mart recently reported that global sales in the three months to the end of September had fallen by as much as 50% on the previous year.

    这释放出一个信号：一度让母公司泡泡玛特的市值超过美国两家玩具巨头孩之宝和美泰市值总和的那股热潮，或许正在失去动力。泡泡玛特近期财报显示，截至今年9月底的第三季度，其全球销售额同比下滑幅度高达50%。

  5. 05

    Labubu has been Pop Mart's most successful line of toys. For Ensito, it is clear the dolls' exclusivity helped fuel the craze. "Now it doesn't feel that way. I walk into a Pop Mart and there's a whole series of Labubus sitting there," he says.

    拉布布一直是泡泡玛特最成功的玩具系列。对恩西托来说，玩偶的稀缺性显然大大助推了这股热潮。“现在那种感觉完全没有了。走进一家泡泡玛特门店，一整个系列的拉布布就摆在那里。”他说。

    exclusivity
    n. 独有性，专属性（此处指产品的稀缺性）
  6. 06

    Experts say one of Pop Mart's biggest challenges is creating enduring characters. Unlike Disney, which sells a story before it sells a toy, Pop Mart is working in reverse, says Ashley Dudarenok of China research firm ChoZan. "The real test for next year is whether Pop Mart can make people love a character without a celebrity holding it."

    专家指出，泡泡玛特面临的一个最大难题，是如何打造拥有长久生命力的角色。中国研究机构ChoZan的阿什利・杜达雷诺克（Ashley Dudarenok）表示，迪士尼是先讲好故事、再售卖玩具，而泡泡玛特的路子恰恰相反。“明年真正的考验在于：泡泡玛特能否在不靠名人带货的情况下，依然让人们发自内心地喜欢一个角色。”

    enduring
    adj. 持久、经久不衰的（用来形容一种能够经受时间考验、长期存在的事物）

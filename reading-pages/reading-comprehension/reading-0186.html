03-2018课标全国Ⅰ，C

文化遗产、艺术与跨文化

Languages have been coming and going for thousands of years, but in recent times there has been less coming and a lot more going. When the world was still populated by hunter⁃gatherers, small, tightly knit(联系)groups developed their own patterns of speech independent of each other. Some language experts believe that 10,000 years ago, when the world had just five to ten million people, they spoke perhaps 12,000 languages between them.

Soon afterwards, many of those people started settling down to become farmers, and their languages too became more settled and fewer in number. In recent centuries, trade, industrialisation, the development of the nation⁃state and the spread of universal compulsory education, especially globalisation and better communications in the past few decades, all have caused many languages to disappear, and dominant languages such as English, Spanish and Chinese are increasingly taking over.

At present, the world has about 6,800 languages. The distribution of these languages is hugely uneven. The general rule is that mild zones have relatively few languages, often spoken by many people, while hot, wet zones have lots, often spoken by small numbers. Europe has only around 200 languages;the Americas about 1,000;Africa 2,400;and Asia and the Pacific perhaps 3,200, of which Papua New Guinea alone accounts for well over 800. The median number(中位数) of speakers is a mere 6,000, which means that half the world's languages are spoken by fewer people than that.

Already well over 400 of the total of 6,800 languages are close to extinction(消亡), with only a few elderly speakers left. Pick, at random, Busuu in Cameroon(eight remaining speakers), Chiapaneco in Mexico(150), Lipan Apache in the United States(two or three) or Wadjigu in Australia(one, with a question⁃mark):none of these seems to have much chance of survival.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What can we infer about languages in hunter⁃gatherer times?

  • A.They developed very fast.
  • B.They were large in number.
  • C.They had similar patterns.
  • D.They were closely connected.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　推理判断题。根据第一段第二句(当世界人口仍然由采猎者构成的时候,小的、联系紧密的群体彼此独立地形成了自己的讲话模式。)可知当时应该形成了多种多样的语言,故答案为B项(它们的数量很大)。

2Which of the following best explains “dominant” underlined in paragraph 2?

  • A.Complex.
  • B.Advanced.
  • C.Powerful.
  • D.Modern.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　猜测词义题。根据画线词所在句子中的“such as English, Spanish and Chinese are increasingly taking over”可推知dominant的意思是“占统治地位的,强大的”,故答案为C项(Powerful)。

3How many languages are spoken by less than 6,000 people at present?

  • A.About 6,800.
  • B.About 3,400.
  • C.About 2,400.
  • D.About 1,200.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3.B　推理判断题。根据第三段第一句(目前世界上大约有6,800种语言。)和第三段最后一句(……这意味着世界的语言中有一半的语言,其使用人数不足6,000。)可知,答案为B项(6,800×1/2=3,400)。

4What is the main idea of the text?

  • A.New languages will be created.
  • B.People's lifestyles are reflected in languages.
  • C.Human development results in fewer languages.
  • D.Geography determines language evolution.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

4.C　主旨要义题。作者在首段首句介绍了本文的主题,即语言几千年来一直变化不定,但在最近时期,其数量越来越少。然后进一步解释造成这种现象的原因是社会的发展,故答案为C项。

这种水果为什么成了全球饮品界的新顶流？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Trending beverages have gone bananas. Banana is in matcha. Banana is in seltzer, soft and hard. Banana is in dairy-free creamer. Banana is in canned water.

    一股“香蕉热”席卷了网红饮品界：抹茶里兑入了香蕉，气泡水（包括无酒精版和含酒精版）里有香蕉，奶精里含香蕉，甚至罐装水里也加入了香蕉。

    seltzer
    苏打水、气泡水；文中提到带香蕉味的有酒精和无酒精气泡饮品。
  2. 02

    And, despite its relatively low protein content, banana is now synonymous with liquid gains at Starbucks. Last fall, the company introduced banana protein cold foam. Not to be outflavored, Dunkin’ released its own line of banana syrups and foams in March, calling banana “the flavor of the season” in a press release.

    尽管香蕉蛋白质含量较低，却不妨碍它在星巴克成为“液体蛋白”的代名词。去年秋天，星巴克推出了香蕉蛋白奶盖。为了在口味上不被比下去，邓肯咖啡也在今年3月推出了自己的香蕉糖浆和奶泡系列，并在新闻稿中称香蕉是“当季风味”。

    synonymous
    紧密联系在一起的；be synonymous with 在这里说到一种成分就让人想到某类饮品。
    syrups
    调味糖浆；指咖啡店用来为饮品加上香蕉风味的浓稠液体。
  3. 03

    Leonid Yuffa, the owner of 20 locations of Dazbog Coffee in Colorado, would go as far as to call it the next pumpkin spice. When you sip one of his cafe’s seasonal banana lattes, “it feels like you’re on vacation,” he said.

    在科罗拉多州拥有20家门店的Dazbog Coffee老板列奥尼德·尤法（Leonid Yuffa），直言香蕉会是下一个“南瓜香料”。他说，啜一口店里的季节限定香蕉拿铁，“就是度假的味道”。

  4. 04

    That familiarity makes bananas ripe for experimentation, experts say. “It’s one of the last nonpolarizing foods in a polarizing world,” said Rob Smithson, a founder of Banagua, a banana water company. “Every demographic, every age group, every culture all over the world loves bananas.”

    专家表示，正是这种熟悉感让香蕉成为创新试验的理想对象。“香蕉是这个充满分歧的世界里为数不多、不会引发争议的食物之一，”香蕉水公司 Banagua 的创始人之一罗布·史密森（Rob Smithson）说，“不分人群、年龄和文化背景，全世界都爱香蕉。”

    demographic
    按年龄、收入等特征划分的人群；every demographic 强调受众不局限于某一群体。
    ripe
    适合某事、条件已成熟的；ripe for experimentation 说香蕉味适合拿来开发新品，不是说香蕉很熟。
    nonpolarizing
    不容易让人意见两极化的；指香蕉味普遍容易被接受。
  5. 05

    More than 139 million metric tons of them are grown worldwide each year, making bananas the most-produced, most-consumed fresh fruit globally. They’re reliably predictable, tasting the same in schools, delis, airports and Walmart stores, where they are the most popular grocery item sold. And bananas are a foundational ingredient in vintage desserts like sundaes and puddings. “In the past, banana seemed like a retro play,” said Sarah Sanneh, an owner of Pies ’n’ Thighs in Brooklyn, where the banana cream pie is the top seller. “But banana water is from the year 3005.”

    全球每年种植的香蕉超过1.39亿公吨，使之成为世界上产量最高、卖得最好的鲜食水果。香蕉的味道稳定可靠：无论在学校、熟食店、机场，还是沃尔玛门店，尝到的香蕉基本都是一个味儿。而在沃尔玛，香蕉是销量最高的杂货商品。此外，香蕉还是圣代、布丁等经典复古甜点的基础食材。“过去，香蕉似乎代表一种复古风潮，”位于布鲁克林的餐厅 Pies ’n’ Thighs 主理人之一莎拉·桑尼（Sarah Sanneh）说，这家餐厅销量最高的菜品就是香蕉奶油派，“而如今，香蕉水可是来自3005年的东西。”

    delis
    熟食店；报道说无论在熟食店、机场还是超市，香蕉的味道都很稳定。
    retro
    复古的；a retro play 指用传统香蕉甜品唤起熟悉感的一种产品做法。

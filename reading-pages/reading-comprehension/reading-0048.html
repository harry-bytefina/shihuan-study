05-2019天津春，A

学习、阅读与教育方式

Welcome to University of Waikato. Our mission is to create a welcoming environment in which residents feel comfortable, safe and a sense of belonging.

Student Village and Waikato Apartments

Waikato Accommodation Services offers two types of residential accommodation: Student Village and Waikato Apartments.

Student Village consists of fifteen independent units. Each contains five bedrooms, toilet facilities, and a shared kitchen with a fridge and cupboard. There is also a games room and a TV room.

Waikato Apartments are situated in a threelevel apartment building. Each level has four apartments containing six bedrooms, toilet facilities, and a kitchen. Each level has a shared area with a TV, a study desk and easy chairs.

Security and Safety

Campus Security is available to assist 24 hours, 7 days a week. If you see anything suspicious, please call Campus Security on 0800863800.

Please ensure that you have your own insurance for your personal belongings as Waikato Accommodation Services takes no responsibility for any theft or damage to personal belongings.

Housekeeping and Maintenance

Housekeeping staff service all shared areas. Residents are responsible for keeping their rooms, kitchens and shower rooms clean and tidy.

Maintenance staff ensure necessary repairs to facilities are made. If your room or other shared area needs maintenance, please inform the Accommodation Office. Maintenance staff may be required to enter your room to tend to a maintenance request—this will be prearranged.

Termination（终止）of Residence and Checking Out

You are required to pay accommodation fees for the full period of the residency contract. At the end of your contract you must arrange a time with the office staff to check out. A room inspection will take place and you will be responsible for payment for any damage or missing items. If you fail to check out in this manner, the staff will hold an inspection in your absence and assess whether any payment is required.

1What facilities are available in both Student Village and Waikato Apartments?

  • A.Kitchen and TV.
  • B.Study desk and fridge.
  • C.Cupboard and study room.
  • D.Games room and toilet facilities.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1. A细节理解题。根据Student Village and Waikato Apartments部分中的Student Village consists of...and a shared kitchen with a fridge and cupboard. There is also a games room and a TV room.及Waikato Apartments...a kitchen. Each level has a shared area with a TV, a study desk and easy chairs.可知，A选项正确。

2What should you do when you suspect that someone is destroying school facilities?

  • A.Report to the insurance company.
  • B.Contact Campus Security.
  • C.Inform the assistant teacher.
  • D.Call Waikato Accommodation Services.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2. B细节理解题。根据Security and Safety部分中的If you see anything suspicious, please call Campus Security on 0800863800.（如果您发现任何可疑之处，请拨打0800863800联系校园保安部门）可知，B选项正确。

3The responsibility of the maintenance staff includes .

  • A.making plans for Accommodation Office
  • B.monitoring the use of kitchen facilities
  • C.fixing a broken window
  • D.cleaning shower rooms
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3. C推理判断题。根据Housekeeping and Maintenance部分中的Maintenance staff ensure necessary repairs to facilities are made.（维修人员确保对设施进行必要的维修）可推知，修一扇坏了的窗户属于维修人员的职责。故C选项正确。

4If you can't check out in the required way, the staff will .

  • A.ask you to pay higher accommodation fees
  • B.assess whether an inspection is necessary
  • C.still carry out the room inspection

答案：C

4. C细节理解题。根据Termination of Residence and Checking Out部分中的If you fail to check out in this manner, the staff will hold an inspection in your absence and assess whether any payment is required.（如果你未能以这种方式退房，工作人员将在你不在时进行检查，并评估是否需要交付一些费用）可知，如果你不能按照要求办理退房手续，工作人员仍会对你的房间进行检查。故C选项正确。

5Which of the following is the passage probably taken from?

答案：D

外刊延伸

阅读难度参考：CSE 7

心理学最新研究：想要过得好，先学会自嘲

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    When it's good (and when it's bad) for you to laugh at yourself

    自嘲什么时候是好事，什么时候是坏事？

  2. 02

    Why do some schoolchildren become outcasts?

    为什么有些孩子在学校里会被孤立？

    outcast
    n. 被孤立的人
  3. 03

    In the 1990s, psychologists set out to investigate why some kids were excluded by their peers. They discovered two archetypes. Some of the rejected children were bullying and aggressive. Others were meek and subservient. Both shared one quality: an inability to take a joke.

    上世纪九十年代，心理学家曾着手研究一个问题：为什么有些孩子会被同伴排挤？他们发现了两种典型。被排挤的孩子里，有一些霸道、好斗、爱欺负人；另一些则温顺怯懦、逆来顺受。这两类孩子有一个共同点：开不起玩笑。

    meek
    adj. 温顺的；怯懦的
    inability
    n. 没有能力
    subservient
    adj. 顺从的；谄媚的
  4. 04

    If you really hate people laughing at you, you could be a gelotophobe – someone with an acute sensitivity to laughter. "You might be sitting in a cafe and joking with your friends, and someone might approach you and ask, why did you laugh at me?" says René Proyer, psychology professor at Martin Luther University Halle-Wittenberg in Germany.

    如果你特别讨厌别人笑话你，那你有可能是个“恐笑者”——对笑声极度敏感的人。“你可能正坐在咖啡馆里，和朋友们有说有笑，这时有个人走过来问你：你们刚才为什么笑我？”德国马丁·路德哈勒-维滕贝格大学的心理学教授勒内·普罗耶（René Proyer）如是说。

    gelotophobe
    n. 恐笑的人
  5. 05

    There is no one factor explaining why one might become a gelotophobe, but Proyer says that a very strict and punishing style of parenting is one risk factor. Another is an early traumatic experience of having been laughed at.

    一个人之所以会变成“恐笑者”，并没有单一的原因。但普罗耶教授说，非常严苛、经常惩罚的养育风格，是风险因素之一。另一个风险因素，是早年被人嘲笑的创伤经历。

  6. 06

    There also seems to be a cultural dimension. East Asian countries generally have higher levels of gelotophobia than Western nations, something researchers attribute to those belonging to collectivistic cultures being less likely to single themselves out for attention, or to engage in humour as a coping mechanism. In China, for example, people are more likely to associate humour with "experts", i.e. comedians, than everyday citizens.

    “恐笑症”中似乎还有一层文化的维度。东亚国家的“恐笑”程度，普遍高于西方国家。研究者把这一点归因于：身处集体主义文化的人，更不愿意突显自己、吸引注意，也不太会把幽默当作一种应对方式。比如在中国，人们更容易把幽默和“专业人士”（也就是喜剧演员）联系在一起，而不是和普通人联系在一起。

    collectivistic
    adj. 集体主义的
  7. 07

    Like the children unable to take teasing, gelotophobes tend to experience worse psychological and interpersonal outcomes.

    和那些经不起取笑的孩子一样，“恐笑者”在心理健康和人际关系上，结局也往往更差。

  8. 08

    The opposite trait is gelotophilia, the joy of being laughed at by others, which has been linked to stronger social connectedness. One way this can manifest is in "playfulness", the tendency to take oneself and the world lightly.

    与“恐笑”相反的特质，是“乐于被笑”——以被别人笑话为乐，这种特质和更强的社会连接感有关。它的一种表现，就是“玩心”，一种对自己、对世界都举重若轻的倾向。

    gelotophilia
    n. 乐于被笑
    playfulness
    n. 玩心
  9. 09

    Proyer carried out a study where he tested whether it was possible to increase playfulness in participants using simple activities over the course of a week. For example, setting aside 10 minutes each evening to write down the three most playful things that happened during the day.

    普罗耶做过一项研究：花一周的时间，利用一些简单的练习，能不能提升参与者的玩心？比如，每天晚上留出十分钟，写下当天发生的最好玩的三件事。

  10. 10

    So what was the most playful thing that happened to you today? Write it down and laugh yourself silly.

    那么，今天发生在你身上的、最好玩的一件事是什么？写下来，把自己笑个够。

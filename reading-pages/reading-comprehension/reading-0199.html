04-2017课标全国Ⅰ，D

睡眠、运动与身心健康

A build-it-yourself solar still(蒸馏器) is one of the best ways to obtain drinking water in areas where the liquid is not readily available. Developed by two doctors in the U.S. Department of Agriculture, it's an excellent water collector. Unfortunately, you must carry the necessary equipment with you, since it's all but impossible to find natural substitutes. The only components required, though, are a 5'×5' sheet of clear or slightly milky plastic, six feet of plastic tube, and a container—perhaps just a drinking cup—to catch the water. These pieces can be folded into a neat little pack and fastened on your belt.

To construct a working still, use a sharp stick or rock to dig a hole four feet across and three feet deep. Try to make the hole in a damp area to increase the water catcher's productivity. Place your cup in the deepest part of the hole. Then lay the tube in place so that one end rests all the way in the cup and the rest of the line runs up—and out—the side of the hole.

Next, cover the hole with the plastic sheet, securing the edges of the plastic with dirt and weighting the sheet's center down with a rock. The plastic should now form a cone(圆锥体) with 45-degree-angled sides. The low point of the sheet must be centered directly over, and no more than three inches above, the cup.

The solar still works by creating a greenhouse under the plastic. Ground water evaporates(蒸发) and collects on the sheet until small drops of water form, run down the material, and fall off into the cup. When the container is full, you can suck the refreshment out through the tube, and won't have to break down the still every time you need a drink.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What do we know about the solar still equipment from the first paragraph?

  • A.It's delicate.
  • B.It's expensive.
  • C.It's complex.
  • D.It's portable.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

1.D　细节理解题。根据第一段最后两句,尤其是尾句These pieces can be folded into a neat little pack and fastened on your belt.可知该装置是可折叠的、便携的(portable),故答案为D项。

2What does the underlined phrase “the water catcher”in paragraph 2 refer to?

  • A.The tube.
  • B.The still.
  • C.The hole.
  • D.The cup.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2.B　猜测词义题。根据第二段前两句可知,画线短语指代的是该段首句中的a working still,故答案为B项。

3What is the last step of constructing a working solar still?

  • A.Dig a hole of a certain size.
  • B.Put the cup in place.
  • C.Weight the sheet's center down.
  • D.Cover the hole with the plastic sheet.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　细节理解题。本题问制作蒸馏器的最后一个步骤是什么。根据第二、三段可知四个选项的正确顺序是A→B→D→C,故答案为C项。

4When a solar still works, drops of water come into the cup from _____ .

  • A.the plastic tube
  • B.outside the hole
  • C.the open air
  • D.beneath the sheet
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　细节理解题。本题问蒸馏器工作时水滴从何处进入杯子。根据尾段前两句可知该装置是利用蒸发到塑料薄片上的水分凝聚成水滴,最后滴落到杯子中,故答案为D项。

外刊延伸

这届家长的唯一愿望：“我只想坐在车里刷会手机”

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    A Parent's Only Wish: 'I Just Want to Sit in My Car and Scroll'

    为人父母后唯一的心愿：“我只想坐在车里刷会儿手机”

  2. 02

    Matt Ferrante used to pull into his driveway after work and scroll on his phone for a few minutes before going inside.

    马特·费兰特（Matt Ferrante）以前下班回家，把车停进自家车道后，会先在车里刷几分钟手机，再进屋。

    scroll
    v. 滑动屏幕来浏览内容
  3. 03

    That stopped when his wife caught him on their Ring camera.

    直到有一天，他的妻子从门铃摄像头里把他抓了个正着，这个习惯就此戛然而止。（译者注：Ring是美国智能门铃初创公司，后被亚马逊收购。）

  4. 04

    Ferrante, a father of two who works in film production in Los Angeles, has found a workaround: He now parks on the street, out of the Ring cam's view.

    费兰特是两个孩子的父亲，在洛杉矶从事影视制作，如今他找到了一个变通方法：他把车停在街边，这样就能避开门铃摄像头的视线。

  5. 05

    Carscrolling has become a favorite little luxury for exhausted parents, who cherish the routine as a buffer between hectic workdays and whatever awaits them at home.

    “车里刷手机”（Carscrolling）已成为疲惫不堪的父母们钟爱的一种“小奢侈”。他们格外珍惜这个习惯，把它看作是忙碌工作日与家庭琐事之间的缓冲时光。

    hectic
    adj. 繁忙的
    carscrolling
    n. 车内刷屏，车里刷手机
    buffer
    n.（本文隐喻） 起缓冲作用的人或物；减震器，缓冲器
  6. 06

    Most American households have two working parents, so once they're home there's plenty to do and little opportunity to tune out. Mom and dad are opting to scroll in secret lest they be accused of being hypocritical for telling their children to get off their screens.

    大多数美国家庭都是“双职工”，因此父母只要一进门，就有处理不完的杂事，几乎没有片刻喘息。于是，他们选择偷偷在车里刷手机，也是为了避免在教育孩子远离屏幕时，被指责为双标。

    hypocritical
    adj. 虚伪的，伪善的，言行不一，双标
  7. 07

    Adam Olson, a father of four in Tampa, Fla., where he runs a hospice foundation, sits in the car and reads the news on his phone while his wife and kids shop.

    佛罗里达州坦帕市的亚当·奥尔森（Adam Olson）是四个孩子的父亲，在当地经营着一家临终关怀基金会。每当妻子和孩子们去购物时，他就会坐在车里用手机看新闻。

  8. 08

    He's on the fence about whether carscrolling is helpful or harmful.

    对于“车里刷手机”到底是有益还是有害，奥尔森摇摆不定。

  9. 09

    "It's possible that technology has fractured our attention so thoroughly that we need these stolen moments in our cars," he said. "On the other hand, it could be that technology has stolen the last refuges where we used to accept boredom."

    “也许技术已经彻底粉碎了我们的注意力，以至于我们需要在车里偷来这片刻安宁，”他说，“但另一方面，也可能是技术偷走了我们曾经用来接受枯燥的最后避风港。”

    fracture
    v. 使分裂，使破裂
    refuge
    n. 避难所，庇护所
  10. 10

    Hannah Weisman, a clinical psychologist in Seattle who broke her own carscrolling habit last year, warns against doomscrolling. "Unless someone has carefully curated their social feed to give them only cute animals, most of us are getting alarming content and are actually increasing our stress by scrolling," she said.

    西雅图临床心理学家汉娜·魏斯曼（Hannah Weisman）去年戒掉了“车里刷手机”的习惯，她提醒人们要警惕“末日刷屏”。她指出：“除非一个人精心筛选过自己的社交推送，只看可爱动物这类内容，否则大多数人刷到的都是令人焦虑的信息，这实际上只会让压力越来越大。”

    doomscrolling
    n. 末日刷屏（持续滚动浏览负面新闻的行为）

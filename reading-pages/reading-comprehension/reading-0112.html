Children’s Discovery Museum General Information about Group Play

Pricing

Group Play＄7/person

Scholarships

We offer scholarships to low-income schools and youth organizations, subject to availability. Participation in a post-visit survey is required.

Scholarships are for Group Play admission fees and/or transportation. Transportation invoices(发票）must be received within 60 days of your visit to guarantee the scholarship.

Group Size

We require one chaperone（监护人） per ten children. Failure to provide enough chaperones will result in an extra charge of ＄50 per absent adult.

Group Play is for groups of 10 or more with a limit of 35 people. For groups of 35 or more, please call to discuss options.

Hours

The Museum is open daily from 9:30 am to 4:30 pm.

Group Play may be scheduled during any day or time the Museum is open.

Registration Policy

Registration must be made at least two weeks in advance.

Register online or fill out a Group Play Registration Form with multiple date and start time options.

Once the registration form is received and processed, we will send a confirmation email within two business days.

Guidelines

  • Teachers and chaperones should model good behavior for the group and remain with students at all times.
  • Children are not allowed unaccompanied in all areas of the Museum.
  • Children should play nicely with each other and exhibits.
  • Use your indoor voice when at the Museum.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What does a group need to do if they are offered a scholarship?

  • A.Prepay the admission fees.
  • B.Use the Museum's transportation.
  • C.Take a survey after the visit.
  • D.Schedule their visit on weekdays.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C细节理解题。根据Scholarships部分第一段第二句“Participation in a postvisit survey is required.”可知，想要获得奖学金，就要参与参观后调查。故选C。

2How many chaperones are needed for a group of 30 children to visit the Museum?

  • A.One.
  • B.Two.
  • C.Three.
  • D.Four.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2. C细节理解题。根据Group Size部分中的“We require one chaperone per ten children.”可知，每十个孩子需要一个监护人，所以30个孩子需要3个监护人。故选C。

3What are children prohibited from doing at the Museum?

  • A.Using the computer.
  • B.Talking with each other.
  • C.Touching the exhibits.
  • D.Exploring the place alone.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D细节理解题。根据Guidelines部分中的“Children are not allowed unaccompanied in all areas of the Museum.”可知，儿童不得在无人陪伴的情况下进入博物馆的任何区域，所以单独探索是被禁止的。故选D。

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The Classic Movie That Was Nearly Destroyed by a Single Line of Code

    那部差点被一行代码毁掉的经典电影

  2. 02

    “Toy Story”has been a part of our lives for so long that fully grown adults who saw the original as kids will be seeing “Toy Story 5” this weekend with their own kids.

    《玩具总动员》陪伴我们走过了漫长的岁月，许多小时候看过第一部电影的人如今已成年，他们这个周末会带上自己的孩子一起观看《玩具总动员5》。

  3. 03

    But long before a cowboy, a space ranger and a cantankerous spud went up against a tablet, the real people behind “Toy Story 2” fought their own battle with tech—and they almost lost the entire movie.

    然而，早在牛仔、太空骑警和脾气暴躁的土豆与平板电脑展开较量之前，《玩具总动员2》的幕后团队就曾与技术展开过一场较量——而且他们差点就输掉了整部电影。

    cantankerous
    adj. 脾气暴躁、爱发牢骚的
    spud
    n. 土豆（俚语，原指挖土豆的铲子）
  4. 04

    It was a battle that began with this string of code：/bin/rm -r -f *

    这场较量始于这一串代码：/bin/rm -r -f *

  5. 05

    This command to wipe out the files as fast as possible nuked “Toy Story 2” in the time it takes to play “You’ve Got a Friend in Me.” Nobody knew who was responsible.

    这条旨在以最快速度清除文件的指令，在播完主题曲《我是你好朋友》（You’ve Got a Friend in Me）的短短两分钟里，就抹掉了《玩具总动员2》的全部数据。没有人知道是谁干的。

    nuke
    v. 彻底摧毁
    wipe
    v. 擦、擦拭；n. 湿巾、卸妆巾
  6. 06

    When Pixar employees went looking for the lost data, they discovered that the backup systems were not working properly.

    当皮克斯的员工着手寻找丢失的数据时，他们发现备份系统不能正常运行。

  7. 07

    Galyn Susman, the film’s supervising technical director, and a technical director named Oren Jacob realized that she had a backup of “Toy Story 2” at home. Once a week after her parental leave, Susman lugged the hulking computer to the office and downloaded the latest version of the film so she could keep working after bedtime.

    影片的首席技术主管盖林·苏斯曼（Galyn Susman）和技术总监奥伦·雅各布（Oren Jacob）意识到，苏斯曼家里有一份《玩具总动员2》的备份。休完育儿假后，她每周都会把那台笨重的电脑搬回办公室，下载电影的最新版本，以便在孩子入睡后继续工作。

    parental
    adj. 父母的
    hulking
    adj. 庞大笨重的
  8. 08

    They raced to Susman’s home, loaded the computer in her station wagon, smothered the machine in pillows and blankets—and buckled the seat belt. The computer made it to Pixar’s campus, flickered to life and brought Woody and Buzz back from the dead.

    他们立刻赶到苏斯曼的家中，把电脑搬上她的旅行车，用枕头和毯子把机器裹得严严实实——还给它系好安全带。这部电脑最终顺利抵达皮克斯园区，开机亮灯，胡迪和巴斯光年就此起死回生。

    smother
    v. 用枕头、被子层层包裹严实
    load
    v. 搬运、装车
  9. 09

    Eli, Susman’s son, is the only baby credited in the movie who also helped save the movie.

    苏斯曼的儿子伊莱（Eli）是唯一一名在片尾被署名、且在现实中参与“拯救”了这部电影的宝宝。

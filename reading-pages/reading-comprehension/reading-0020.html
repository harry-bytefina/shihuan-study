阅读理解

08-2023天津春，D

学习、阅读与教育方式

Most people with good sense would accept that we can and should learn from accidental failures. It would be impossible to progress in anything, after all, without taking the occasional misstep. And by understanding how we slipped, we can avoid falling in the future.

Few would advocate making intentional mistakes, however. Yet consciously erring(犯错) can promote deeper understanding and better recall. The phenomenon is known as the derring effect—coming from “deliberate(故意的) erring”—and when applied properly, it may bring benefits in many unexpected areas of life.

In one experiment carried out at the National University of Singapore, participants were given the task of learning concepts on a difficult subject. For some terms, they simply copied out the correct definition; for others, they were asked to first add an error in their description of the term before correcting the mistake.

Naturally, you would expect the addition of the errors to have increased unwanted confusion. Yet the exact opposite was true: the participants who made deliberate errors learned about twice as much as the people who simply copied out the correct definitions.

The derring effect could be applied in other situations. So a music teacher may find the addition of deliberate errors could help a student remember the right musical notes. Such a playful approach could fuel their creativity for composition, if the student looks for ways to develop those wrong notes into something more attractive. It is encouraging to discover that by readily accepting our errors and wisely placing ourselves in the way of being wrong, we can in fact overcome weaknesses and rise stronger.

The derring effect could be useful for many other challenges too. If you enjoy cooking, for example, you may faithfully follow a recipe without questioning the instructions. But why not try to break away from those habits and deliberately do the “wrong” thing for a change, and see where your derring takes you? If you are painting, meanwhile, you could relax one of the constraints (限制) that you usually put on your work and see what you produce.

At worst, you will have refreshed and deepened your knowledge of the rules you normally apply, so that you can be even more effective next time. At best, you may just find that you have discovered something completely new and unexpected, through a flash of inspiration that you would have missed with perfectionism. Either way, your apparent missteps will have moved you a little closer to true mastery.

    Mahjong Swept Summer Camp This Year. Is Canasta Next?

    今年，麻将风靡夏令营。接下来会是卡纳斯塔吗？

  2. 02

    Samson Derham and his bunkmates at Camp Harlam typically pick rock-wall climbing or free swim as their Saturday elective activity. This summer, 10-year-old Derham convinced friends to join him in the rec hall for mahjong lessons.

    在哈拉姆夏令营（Camp Harlam），萨姆森·德汉姆（Samson Derham）和他的室友通常会选择攀岩或自由游泳作为周六的选修活动。而今年夏天，10岁的德汉姆说服了朋友们和他一起去休息室上麻将课。

    bunkmate
    同住一间营房的伙伴。bunk 是营房里常见的上下铺。
    elective
    选修项目。这里指夏令营里孩子可以自行选择的活动。
  3. 03

    His friends were skeptical. He wanted them to sit inside and learn a tile game?

    他的朋友们对此表示怀疑。他竟然想让他们坐在屋里学一种棋牌游戏？

    skeptical
    心存怀疑的。这里说孩子们一开始怀疑麻将是否好玩。

    Soon they were hooked. Since Camp Harlam introduced mahjong lessons as an elective this summer, "word spread like wildfire" among campers, said Ellie Tepper Schulman, who is associate director of the overnight camp, as well as its resident mahjong instructor. She said campers and staff alike were playing the game at "all hours," adding: "It's the thing to do."

    不过很快，大家都迷上了麻将。哈拉姆夏令营今年夏天将麻将课程开设为选修课后，该营地住宿部副主任（也是驻营麻将教练）埃莉·特珀·舒尔曼（Ellie Tepper Schulman）表示，消息在营员中“像野火一样迅速传开”。她说，营员和工作人员都在“随时随地”打麻将，并补充道：“这已经成了大家最热衷的活动。

    hooked
    着迷的。这里说孩子们一学就喜欢上了，常常想再打。
    resident
    常驻的。resident instructor 是平时就在营地任教的老师。

    句子结构

    Since Camp Harlam introduced mahjong lessons as an elective this summer, / "word spread like wildfire" among campers, / said Ellie Tepper Schulman, / who is associate director of the overnight camp, as well as its resident mahjong instructor.

    Since 从句给出消息传开的时间起点。word spread like wildfire 指消息迅速传开；said Ellie Tepper Schulman 是后置的报道语，所以引语里的 word 与 said 无关。末尾 who 补出说话人的两种身份。

    It isn't just Camp Harlam. Across the Northeast and New England camp corridor, summer camps have hired mahjong instructors to lead multiday clinics and are incorporating play into daily schedules.

    这不仅仅发生在哈拉姆夏令营。在整个东北部和新英格兰地区的夏令营聚集地，各夏令营都聘请了麻将教练来主持为期数天的培训课程，并将麻将活动纳入了每日日程中。

    incorporate
    把某项内容纳入既有安排。这里是把打麻将排进每日活动表。
    clinic
    面向某项技能的集中训练课。这里是持续几天的麻将教学，并非诊所。
  6. 06

    Wendy Siegel, owner and director (and resident mahjong instructor) of Tyler Hill Camp in Wayne County, Penn., said: "There's something about sitting around a table, talking and using their brains that kids are looking for. They want to do something that has stood the test of time for generations."

    宾夕法尼亚州韦恩县泰勒山夏令营的老板兼主任（也是驻营麻将教练）温迪·西格尔（Wendy Siegel）表示：“围坐在桌边，一边聊天一边动脑子，这种体验正是孩子们所追求的。他们希望参加的活动已经受了几代人的时间考验。

  7. 07

    Last year Siegel taught the oldest boys at camp canasta, another game popular among retirees. "Every time I saw them they wanted to play canasta." she said.

    去年，西格尔教营地里年纪最大的男孩们玩卡纳斯塔，这是另一款在退休人群中很受欢迎的游戏。“每次见到他们，他们都想玩卡纳斯塔，”她说。

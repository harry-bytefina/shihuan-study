阅读理解

06-2022新高考Ⅱ，B

学习、阅读与教育方式

We journalists live in a new age of storytelling, with many new multimedia tools. Many young people don’t even realize it’s new. For them, it’s just normal.

This hit home for me as I was sitting with my 2yearold grandson on a sofa over the Spring Festival holiday. I had brought a children’s book to read. It had simple words and colorful pictures—a perfect match for his age.

Picture this: my grandson sitting on my lap as I hold the book in front so he can see the pictures. As I read, he reaches out and pokes(戳) the page with his finger.

What’s up with that? He just likes the pictures, I thought. Then I turned the page and continued. He poked the page even harder. I nearly dropped the book. I was confused: Is there something wrong with this kid?

Then I realized what was happening. He was actually a stranger to books. His father frequently amused the boy with a tablet computer which was loaded with colorful pictures that come alive when you poke them. He thought my storybook was like that.

Sorry, kid. This book is not part of your high-tech world. It’s an outdated, lifeless thing. An antique. Like your grandfather. Well, I may be old, but I’m not hopelessly challenged, digitally speaking. I edit video and produce audio. I use mobile payment. I’ve even built websites.

There’s one notable gap in my new-media experience, however: I’ve spent little time in front of a camera, since I have a face made for radio. But that didn’t stop China Daily from asking me last week to share a personal story for a video project about the integration of Beijing, Tianjin and Hebei province.

Anyway, grandpa is now an internet star—two minutes of fame! I promise not to let it go to my head. But I will make sure my 2yearold grandson sees it on his tablet.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What do the underlined words “hit home for me” mean in paragraph 2?

  • A.Provided shelter for me.
  • B.Became very clear to me.
  • C.Took the pressure off me.
  • D.Worked quite well on me.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1. B猜测词义题。第一段中作者提出看法——对许多年轻人来说，新的多媒体工具是很正常的，然后叙述了自己的一次经历予以佐证——孙子听作者读故事时伸出手指猛戳书页，作者开始以为孩子出了状况，后来明白他把书当作了习以为常的平板电脑。画线短语所在句子是文章的过渡句，其主语This指代上文提到的这一看法。结合第二段内容可推知，春节假期，作者坐在沙发上给两岁的孙子读故事的时候，非常明确地感受到了这一点。

2Why did the kid poke the storybook?

  • A.He took it for a tablet computer.
  • B.He disliked the colorful pictures.
  • C.He was angry with his grandpa.
  • D.He wanted to read it by himself.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2. A细节理解题。根据第五段内容尤其是最后一句“He thought my storybook was like that.”可知这个孩子把书当成了平板电脑。

3What does the author think of himself?

  • A.Socially ambitious.
  • B.Physically attractive.
  • C.Financially independent.
  • D.Digitally competent.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3. D推理判断题。根据第六段中“Well, I may be old, but I'm not hopelessly challenged...even built websites.”可知作者认为自己虽然老了，但从数字角度来说，自己还没有遭遇令人绝望的挑战。从作者列举的自己编辑视频、制作音频、使用移动支付、甚至建立了网站等事情可知，作者认为自己在数码科技方面还是很有能力的。

4What can we learn about the author as a journalist?

  • A.He lacks experience in his job.
  • B.He seldom appears on television.
  • C.He manages a video department.
  • D.He often interviews internet stars.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4. B推理判断题。根据文章第七段内容尤其是该段中的“There’s one notable gap in my new-media experience, however: I’ve spent little time in front of a camera, since I have a face made for radio.”可知，作者作为记者，很少在镜头前露面，并将其原因归结为他的脸是为电台而生的（不适合出镜）。

    The year China's famous road-tripping 'auntie' found freedom

    中国著名的自驾阿姨找到自由的这一年

    road-tripping
    adj. 自驾游的
  2. 02

    Sixty-year-old Chinese grandmother Su Min had no intention of becoming a feminist icon.

    苏敏，这名60岁的中国外婆无意成为女性主义偶像。

    icon
    n. 代表人物，行业标杆
  3. 03

    She was only trying to escape her abusive husband when she hit the road in 2020 in her white Volkswagen hatchback with a rooftop tent and her pension.

    她只不过是想逃离虐待自己的丈夫，于是在2020年带着屋顶帐篷和自己的退休金，开着一辆白色大众掀背式轿车上路了。

    hatchback
    n. 掀背车
    abusive
    adj. （言语、精神、生理上）虐待的
  4. 04

    Over the next four years and 180,000 miles, the video diaries she shared of her adventures, while detailing decades of pain, earned her millions of cheerleaders online. They called her the "road-tripping auntie" as she inadvertently turned into a hero for women who felt trapped in their own lives.

    在接下来的4年和18万英里的旅程中，苏敏通过视频日记的形式分享了自己经历的种种冒险，同时详细描述了数十年间自己遭遇的痛苦，这为她在网上赢得了数百万的支持者。大家亲切地称苏敏为“自驾阿姨”，因为对那些感到自己被生活所困的女性们来说，苏敏无意中成为了她们的英雄。

    detail
    v. 详细描述
    cheerleader
    n. 支持者，摇旗呐喊者
    inadvertently
    adv. 不经意地
  5. 05

    In her new life on the road, Su Min's duty is to herself. Although she drives alone, she never seems lonely. Her audience travels with her to places they never knew they would long for. To her millions of followers, Su Min offers comfort and hope.

    在这段旅途的新生活中，苏敏只用对她自己负责。虽然是独自驾车，但她看起来一点也不孤独。苏敏的视频观众与她一起，前往她们从未想过自己会渴望的远方。对于她的数百万关注者而言，苏敏带给了大家慰藉和希望。

  6. 06

    Su Min keeps planning more trips and hopes to one day travel abroad. She's worried about overcoming language barriers, but is confident her story will resonate around the world — as it has in China. "I would like to say that no matter what environment you are in, you must be good to yourself. Learn to love yourself, because only when you love yourself can the world be full of sunshine."

    苏敏一直在计划更多的旅行，并且期待有一天能够踏出国门。虽然担心自己能否克服语言障碍，但她相信自己的故事会在世界各地引起共鸣，就像在中国一样。“我想说的是，无论身处何种环境，你都务必要善待自己。学会去爱自己吧，因为只有当你爱自己，这个世界才会充满阳光。”

    resonate
    v. 引发共鸣，获得认同

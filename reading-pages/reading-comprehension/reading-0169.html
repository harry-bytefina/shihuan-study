动物行为与动物认知

When “Rise of the Planet of the Apes” was first shown to the public last month, a group of excited animal activists gathered on Hollywood Boulevard. But they weren't there to throw red paint on fur⁃coat⁃wearing film stars. Instead, one activist, dressed in a full⁃body monkey suit, had arrived with a sign praising the filmmakers:“Thanks for not using real apes(猿)!”

The creative team behind“Apes”used motion⁃capture(动作捕捉)technology to create digitalized animals, spending tens of millions of dollars on technology that records an actor's performance and later processes it with computer graphics to create a final image(图像). In this case, one of a realistic⁃looking ape.

Yet “Apes” is more exception than the rule. In fact, Hollywood has been hot on live animals lately. One nonprofit organization, which monitors the treatment of animals in filmed entertainment, is keeping tabs on more than 2,000 productions this year. Already, a number of films, including “Water for Elephants,” “The Hangover PartⅡ”and “Zookeeper,” have drawn the anger of activists who say the creatures acting in them haven't been treated properly.

In some cases, it's not so much the treatment of the animals on set in the studio that has activists worried; it's the off⁃set training and living conditions that are raising concerns. And there are questions about the films made outside the States, which sometimes are not monitored as closely as productions filmed in the States.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Why did the animal activists gather on Hollywood Boulevard?

  • A.To see famous film stars.
  • B.To oppose wearing fur coats.
  • C.To raise money for animal protection.
  • D.To express thanks to some filmmakers.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

1.D　细节理解题。根据文章第一段第三句的Thanks for not using real apes!可知D项正确。

2What does paragraph 2 mainly talk about?

  • A.The cost of making “Apes.”
  • B.The creation of digitalized apes.
  • C.The publicity about “Apes.”
  • D.The performance of real apes.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2.B　主旨要义题。根据第二段内容可知,电影《猩球崛起》的创意团队运用动作捕捉技术创造出了数字化的猿。B项符合本段主旨,故为正确答案。

3What does the underlined phrase “keeping tabs on” in paragraph 3 probably mean?

  • A.Listing completely.
  • B.Directing professionally.
  • C.Promoting successfully.
  • D.Watching carefully.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　猜测词义题。根据本段第三句可知,一个非营利的组织监控在电影娱乐中动物的待遇,故D项为正确答案。

4What can we infer from the last paragraph about animal actors?

  • A.They may be badly treated.
  • B.They should take further training.
  • C.They could be traded illegally.
  • D.They would lose popularity.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4.A　推理判断题。 根据文章最后一段第一句可知,动物演员在摄影棚外的训练以及它们的生活条件引发担忧,即动物们可能受到虐待,故A项正确。

谁往鸟巢里塞烟头？答案比你想的更离谱

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Why Some Birds Seem to Be Developing a Cigarette Habit

    为何一些鸟类似乎养成了“吸烟”的习惯

  2. 02

    Darwin's finches in the Galápagos, house finches in Mexico and song thrushes in New Zealand have all developed a curious habit: They put cigarette butts in their nests. Some songbirds in Britain are even nesting in outdoor ashtrays.

    加拉帕戈斯的达尔文雀、墨西哥的家雀以及新西兰的歌鸫都养成了一个奇怪的习惯：它们会把烟头放进鸟巢里。英国的一些鸣禽甚至直接在室外的烟灰缸里筑巢。

    cigarette butts
    "Cigarette butts" are the small pieces left over after a cigarette has been smoked.
    a curious habit
    We can describe something as "a curious habit" when it is an unusual or slightly strange behaviour that someone does regularly.
  3. 03

    A new study adds evidence for why urban birds have picked up this preference, at least in one species: The toxins in tobacco may keep parasites at bay in the nests of blue tits, colorful birds that are found across Europe.Cigarette butts contain about 4,000 chemical compounds. These compounds could ward off pests that harm birds and their offspring.

    城市鸟类为何会形成这种偏好？一项新研究为此提供了证据，这一证据至少在某一种鸟类身上得到了验证：烟草中的毒素或许有助于驱除蓝山雀（一种遍布欧洲的彩色鸟类）鸟巢中的寄生虫。烟头中含有约4000种化学化合物。这些化合物或许能驱赶那些危害鸟类及其后代的害虫。

    parasite
    n. A "parasite" is a living thing that lives on or inside another living thing and takes food or nutrients from it.
    offspring
    n. "Offspring" means the young or babies of people or animals.
    ward off
    "To ward off something" means to stop something harmful or unpleasant from reaching you or affecting you.
    at bay
    We use "at bay" when something harmful or unwanted is being kept under control, so it does not cause problems.
  4. 04

    Blue tits are cavity nesters, building nests in natural hollows or human-built boxes. Their nests are also prime habitat for bloodsucking parasites. So, when it came to cigarette butts and nesting in outdoor ashtrays, the researchers wanted to know whether blue tits could benefit from the pesticidal impacts of tobacco.

    蓝山雀是穴居鸟类，会在天然树洞或人工制作的鸟巢箱中筑巢。它们的巢穴也是吸血寄生虫的主要栖息地。既然研究涉及了烟头以及在户外烟灰缸中筑巢的问题，研究人员便想了解，蓝山雀是否能从烟草的杀虫作用中获益。

    bloodsucking
    adj. "Bloodsucking" describes insects or animals that feed by taking blood from other animals or even people.
    prime
    adj. When we use prime as an adjective often with a place, it means that place is the best, or most important, in the best position.
  5. 05

    A team of researchers studied blue tits in urban parkland and forest. They monitored the health of 99 birds hatched in three different types of nest boxes. The researchers analyzed the parasite populations of all the nest material. The invaders were most numerous in natural nests and nearly absent from nests with the sterilized materials. In the butt nests, the parasites were slightly fewer than in the natural nests.

    一个研究团队对城市公园和森林中的蓝山雀进行了研究。研究人员监测了99只在三种不同类型鸟巢箱中孵化的鸟类的健康状况。同时他们分析了不同材质的巢穴中，寄生虫的数量。在天然巢中，寄生虫的数量最多。在材料经过了消毒的巢穴中，寄生虫几乎不见踪影。而在放有烟头的巢穴中，寄生虫的数量略少于天然巢。

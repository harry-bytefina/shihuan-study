02-2019天津夏，B

学习、阅读与教育方式

I must have always known reading was very important because the first memories I have as a child deal with books. There was not one night that I don’t remember Mom reading me a storybook by my bedside. I was extremely inspired by the elegant way the words sounded.

I always wanted to know what my mom was reading. Hearing Mom say, “I can’t believe what’s printed in the newspaper this morning,”made me want to grab it out of her hands and read it myself. I wanted to be like my mom and know all of the things she knew. So I carried around a book, and each night, just to be like her, I would pretend to be reading.

This is how everyone learned to read. We would start off with sentences, then paragraphs, and then stories. It seemed an unending journey, but even as a six-year-old girl I realized that knowing how to read could open many doors. When Mom said, “The C-A-N-D-Y is hidden on the top shelf,”I knew where the candy was. My progress in reading raised my curiosity, and I wanted to know everything. I often found myself telling my mom to drive more slowly, so that I could read all of the road signs we passed.

Most of my reading through primary, middle and high school was factual reading. I read for knowledge, and to make A’s on my tests. Occasionally, I would read a novel that was assigned, but I didn’t enjoy this type of reading. I liked facts, things that are concrete. I thought anything abstract left too much room for argument.

Yet, now that I’m growing and the world I once knew as being so simple is becoming more complex, I find myself needing a way to escape. By opening a novel, I can leave behind my burdens and enter into a wonderful and mysterious world where I am now a new character. In these worlds I can become anyone. I don’t have to write down what happened or what technique the author was using when he or she wrote this. I just read to relax.

We’re taught to read because it’s necessary for much of human understanding. Reading is a vital part of my life. Reading satisfies my desire to keep learning. And I’ve found that the possibilities that lie within books are limitless.

答案：B

学校活动里的精致妈妈，正在逼死普通妈妈

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    The other day, I was at a school function for one of our kids. Everyone looked great. Like, really great. Alarmingly great. Fit, stylish, with long, cascading hair, their faces smooth, their teeth white, their makeup modern and ﬂawless.

    前几天，我参加了我们家一个孩子所在学校举办的家长活动。在场的每位妈妈都光彩照人，每个人都真的很美，美到让人心生警觉。她们身材紧致、穿搭时髦，留着如瀑布般垂坠的长发，脸庞光滑、牙齿洁白，妆容精致新潮，无可挑剔。

  2. 02

    Long gone are the days of the stereotypically harried mom, the woman racing out the door with her children in tow, her shirt stained with tomato sauce, her clothes woefully out of style.

    那种刻板印象中整日手忙脚乱的妈妈形象，早已成为过去。拖着孩子匆匆出门，衬衫上沾着番茄酱、穿着十分老土的女人，再也不是典型的妈妈形象。

    stereotypically
    adv. 带有刻板印象地
    harried
    adj. 被事情追着跑的、被多重任务围剿的，手忙脚乱的，焦头烂额的
  3. 03

    It's hard to pinpoint exactly when this shift began—the advent of social media, the explosion of stay-young-forever ﬁxes like Botox and injectables and now GLP-1s—but since the early 2000s, the image of mom as an androgynous, overwhelmed blob has done a complete 180. We're ﬁrmly in the "hot mom" era.

    我们很难确切地说出这一转变始于何时——是社交媒体的兴起，还是“永葆青春”的方法遍地开花，比如肉毒杆菌、各类医美注射剂，还有如今的GLP-1减肥药？但自21世纪初起，那种“性别特征不明显、疲惫不堪、形象模糊”的妈妈模样，已经发生了180度大转变。我们已然全面迈入了“精致妈妈”的时代。

    advent
    n. （重要事情的）到来
    pinpoint
    v. 准确指出
    injectables
    n. 泛指各种注射类医美产品
  4. 04

    I'm not arguing that this is necessarily a bad thing for mom-kind. The previous version of moms-as-hobbits reeked of sexism and was also mostly inaccurate. It's great to feel empowered and sexy and physically ﬁt into middle age—whatever ﬂoats your boat. But instead of that being one option, it's become the default expectation, part of the modern mom's endless to-do list.

    我并非想说，追求精致对妈妈一族而言一定是件坏事。此前那种把妈妈比作霍比特人的说法，充满了性别歧视，且大多与现实不符。人到中年，仍能活得充满能量、性感迷人、身材健美，这当然很棒——只要你开心就好。可如今，这种状态不再是一种选项，而是成了一种默认的期待，成了现代妈妈们无穷无尽的待办清单上，又一项必须完成的任务。

    default
    adj. 默认的；n. (电脑系统设置里的）默认选项
  5. 05

    Thankfully, there's a growing cohort bucking against the idea of aesthetic perfection.

    万幸的是，一个日益壮大的群体正挺身而出，反抗这种追求极致外在美的理念。

    aesthetic
    adj. 美学的，外表的
  6. 06

    Kate Auletta, the editor in chief of the website Scary Mommy, which covers all things motherhood, says, "Between work, household stuff, kids' sports and everything else, I don't have the time or patience to look any sort of way. There's too much other s—to worry about in life and in the world. I just can't be bothered anymore."

    内容涵盖一切母婴话题的网站“Scary Mommy”总编辑凯特·奥莱塔（Kate Auletta）直言：“要在工作、家务、孩子的体育活动和其他琐事之间连轴转，我根本没时间也没耐心去把自己打扮成哪种样子。生活里和这个世界上有太多其他烂事儿要操心了。我真的顾不上了。”

    bother
    v. 给别人添麻烦

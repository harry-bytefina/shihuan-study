Monthly Talks at London Canal Museum

Our monthly talks start at 19:30 on the first Thursday of each month except August. Admission is at normal charges and you don't need to book. They end around 21:00.

November 7th

The Canal Pioneers, by Chris Lewis. James Brindley is recognized as one of the leading early canal engineers.He was also a major player in training others in the art of canal planning and building. Chris Lewis will explain how Brindley made such a positive contribution to the education of that group of early“civil engineers”.

December 5th

Ice for the Metropolis, by Malcolm Tucker. Well before the arrival of freezers, there was a demand for ice for food preservation and catering. Malcolm will explain the history of importing natural ice and the technology of building ice wells, and how London's ice trade grew.

February 6th

An Update on the Cotswold Canals, by Liz Payne. The Stroudwater Canal is moving towards reopening. The Thames and Severn Canal will take a little longer. We will have a report on the present state of play.

March 6th

Eyots and Aits—Thames Islands, by Miranda Vickers. The Thames has many islands. Miranda has undertaken a review of all of them. She will tell us about those of greatest interest.

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Gold Price Hits $4,000 an Ounce as Record-Breaking Climb Continues

    金价突破每盎司4000美元，创纪录涨势持续

  2. 02

    Gold surpassed $4,000 a troy ounce for the first time, signalling an investor rush into alternative assets at a time of concern about the outlook for the U.S. economy and its place in the world.

    黄金价格首次突破每金衡盎司4000美元。这一现象表明，在美国经济前景及其全球地位引发担忧的背景下，投资者正纷纷涌入替代性资产领域。

    surpass
    v. 超越（常指跨过某个重要的基准线）
  3. 03

    The price of the precious metal has surged this year more than it did during some of America's biggest crises. Rising more than 50%, futures' run-up in 2025 has outpaced rallies during the pandemic and the 2007–09 recession. Not since the inflationary shock of 1979 has gold catapulted so much higher in a year.

    今年以来，这种贵金属的价格涨幅，已经超过美国历史上多场重大危机时期的水平。2025年，黄金期货价格暴涨逾50%，涨幅不仅超过了新冠疫情期间，还超过了2007至2009年经济衰退时期。自1979年通胀冲击以来，黄金价格还从未在一年之内出现过如此大幅的上涨。

    surge
    v. 骤增，激增
    rally
    n.&v.（金融语境）上涨，V型回升、反弹；游行集会；多拍相持球
  4. 04

    Trump's attempt to reorder global trade has buoyed prices and upended growth forecasts. White House pressure on the Federal Reserve to lower interest rates is threatening the independence of one of the financial system's bulwarks. The U.S. dollar by one measure had its weakest first half of the year in more than five decades.

    特朗普试图颠覆全球贸易秩序的举动推高了金价，也扰乱了经济增长预期。白宫向美联储施压要求降息的行为，正威胁着这一金融体系支柱机构的独立性。按某项指标衡量，美元在今年上半年的表现为50多年来最疲弱。

    upend
    v. 颠覆
    reorder
    v. 重新构建（新体系）
    bulwark
    n. 支柱性的保障
    buoy
    v. 支撑，使稳定在高位不下沉；提振，助推……上涨；n. 浮标
  5. 05

    The latest leg of the rally began in August, when Fed Chair Jerome Powell signalled that the central bank was ready to begin cutting interest rates in an economy with low unemployment and above-target inflation.

    金价的最新一波上涨行情始于8月，当时美联储主席杰罗姆·鲍威尔（Jerome Powell）表示，在失业率处于低位且通胀率高于目标水平的经济环境下，央行已准备好开始降息。

  6. 06

    Central banks around the world, especially in countries that have strained relationships with the West, have gobbled up gold for more than a decade, helping the precious metal surpass the euro as the world's second-largest reserve asset this year.

    全球各国央行，尤其是那些与西方关系紧张的国家央行，十多年来一直在大量购买黄金，这一趋势推动黄金在今年超越欧元，成为全球第二大储备资产。

  7. 07

    Some on Wall Street are beginning to warn that gold prices may be nearing a peak and are due for a correction. Bank of America analysts note that since the 1860s, gold's multiyear runs higher have consistently been followed by dramatic busts.

    不过，华尔街部分人士开始发出警告，金价或已逼近顶点，可能即将出现回调。美国银行（Bank of America）分析师指出，自19世纪60年代以来，黄金价格在多年上涨的行情之后总是伴随着大幅下跌。

    bust
    n. 泡沫破裂，价格崩盘

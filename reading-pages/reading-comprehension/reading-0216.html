Opera at Music Hall:1243 Elm Street. The season runs June through August, with additional performances in March and September. The Opera honors Enjoy the Arts membership discounts. Phone:241-2742. http://www.cityopera.com.

Chamber Orchestra:The Orchestra plays at Memorial Hall at 1406 Elm Street, which offers several concerts from March through June. Call 723-1182 for more information. http://www.chamberorch.com.

Symphony Orchestra:At Music Hall and Riverbend. For ticket sales, call 381-3300. Regular season runs September through May at Music Hall and in summer at Riverbend. http://www.symphony.org/home.asp.

College Conservatory of Music(CCM):Performances are on the main campus(校园)of the university, usually at Patricia Cobbett Theater. CCM organizes a variety of events, including performances by the well-known LaSalle Quartet, CCM's Philharmonic Orchestra, and various groups of musicians presenting Baroque through modern music. Students with I.D. cards can attend the events for free. A free schedule of events for each term is available by calling the box office at 556-4183. http://www.ccm.uc.edu/events/calendar.

Riverbend Music Theater:6295 Kellogg Ave. Large outdoor theater with the closest seats under cover(price difference). Big name shows all summer long!Phone:232-6220. http://www.riverbendmusic.com.

莎士比亚的《麦克白》，怎么成了会计课上的财务造假案例？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    What 'Macbeth' Can Teach Us about Avoiding Financial Fraud

    关于如何规避金融欺诈，《麦克白》能教会我们什么？

  2. 02

    As an accounting professor, I teach my students about some of the great financial scandals. I want them to see how those massive frauds came about, and how to avoid those same ethical mistakes.

    身为一名会计学教授，我向学生讲授一些重大的财务丑闻。我希望他们看清这些巨额欺诈是如何发生的，以及怎样避免犯下同类道德层面的错误。

  3. 03

    To really drive the point home, I give them an example centuries older than Enron: "Macbeth."

    为了把道理讲透，我给学生举了一个比安然事件早数百年的例子：《麦克白》。

  4. 04

    Like so many executives who plunge into fraud, Macbeth isn't confused about right and wrong. He knows the immorality of murder as well as any CFO knows accounting rules. That is the first step down the slope to fraud: a person who sees the ethical line clearly and crosses it anyway.

    和许多深陷欺诈泥潭的高管一样，麦克白并非分不清是非。他清楚谋杀是不道德的，正如首席财务官一样，熟知会计准则。这正是滑向欺诈深渊的第一步：一个人明明清楚道德底线，却依然选择越界。

    plunge
    v. 突然且无法控制地跳入、跌落、下坠
    slope
    n. 文中指逐步滑向造假的过程；斜坡
  5. 05

    The second step comes when Lady Macbeth urges her husband on. She gets personal. In today's C-suites, Lady Macbeth is someone in authority who frames hesitation as a character flaw. This person says, "I need someone on this who can actually get it done."

    第二步始于麦克白夫人对丈夫的不断怂恿。她对麦克白发起人身攻击。放到如今的企业高管层，“麦克白夫人” 就是手握权力、把犹豫定性为性格缺陷的人。这类人会说：“这件事我需要一个能真正办成事的人来负责。”

  6. 06

    The third step is the easiest to miss. After Duncan is dead, Macbeth orders Banquo's killing without needing Lady Macbeth's help at all. In the frauds I teach, that issue comes up again and again: Fudge one quarter's numbers, and you usually have to fudge the next set. Ultimately, it reaches a point where there is no going back.

    第三步最容易被忽视。邓肯遇害之后，麦克白根本不需要麦克白夫人的推波助澜，便亲自下令杀害班柯。在我讲授的各类欺诈案例中，这种情况屡见不鲜：一旦篡改一个季度的数据，往往就不得不继续篡改下一期。最终，事态发展到无法挽回的地步。

    fudge
    v. （为了达到预定目的）操纵、扭曲（数据、账目或事实等）；弄虚作假、虚构
  7. 07

    So, how do executives stop themselves before they make that first mistake?

    那么，高管该如何在犯下第一个错误前及时止损？

  8. 08

    They should lay out all the lessons not to do it. It makes their objections real and permanent. Second, they shouldn't make a decision in a pressure situation. Any choice that makes you genuinely hesitate gets a 24-hour delay and one conversation with someone who has no stake in the outcome. Beyond all that, executives must stop treating discomfort with a decision as a problem to manage. Discomfort is very often the most accurate signal in the room.

    首先，他们应当把所有前车之鉴都罗列出来，让反对理由变得真实、确定。其次，不要在高压场景下做决定。任何让你发自内心迟疑的选择，都要推迟 24 小时，并与一位与结果毫无利害关系的人士进行一次交谈。除此之外，高管们不要把对决策的不安当成需要去克服的麻烦。不安感往往是当下最准确的信号。

    stake
    n. 利害关系
    discomfort
    n.（身体或精神层面）不安、局促、苦恼
    genuinely
    adv. 真正地，由衷地
    manage
    v.（为了达到预定目的）改变、操纵（某种破坏性因素）；处理、应对……（降低影响）
    permanent
    adj. 永久的，持久的

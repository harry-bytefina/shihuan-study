04-2022全国乙，D

AI、脑科学与新技术

The Government's sugar tax on soft drinks has brought in half as much money as Ministers first predicted it would generate, the first official data on the policy has shown.

First announced in April, 2016, the tax which applies to soft drinks containing more than 5g of sugar per 100ml, was introduced to help reduce childhood obesity(肥胖). It is believed that today's children and teenagers are consuming three times the recommended level of sugar, putting them at a higher risk of the disease.

Initially the sugar tax was expected to make ￡520m a year for the Treasury. However, data of the first six months showed it would make less than half this amount. At present it is expected to generate ￡240m for the year ending in April 2019, which will go to school sports.

It comes after more than half of soft drinks sold in shops have had their sugar levels cut by manufacturers(制造商) so they can avoid paying the tax. Drinks now contain 45 million fewer kilos of sugar as a result of manufacturers' efforts to avoid the charge, according to Treasury figures. Since April drinks companies have been forced to pay between 18p and 24p for every litre of sugary drink they produce or import, depending on the sugar content.

However, some high sugar brands, like Classic Coca Cola, have accepted the sugar tax and are refusing to change for fear of upsetting consumers. Fruit juices, milk⁃based drinks and most alcoholic drinks are free of the tax, as are small companies manufacturing fewer than 1m litres per year.

Today's figures, according to one government official, show the positive influence the sugar tax is having by raising millions of pounds for sports facilities(设施) and healthier eating in schools. Helping the next generation to have a healthy and active childhood is of great importance, and the industry is playing its part.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Why was the sugar tax introduced?

  • A.To collect money for schools.
  • B.To improve the quality of drinks.
  • C.To protect children's health.
  • D.To encourage research in education.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1.C　细节理解题。由第二段第一句中的the tax...was introduced to help reduce childhood obesity可知,征收该税是为了帮助减少儿童肥胖,从而保护孩子们的健康。

2How did some drinks companies respond to the sugar tax?

  • A.They turned to overseas markets.
  • B.They raised the prices of their products.
  • C.They cut down on their production.
  • D.They reduced their products' sugar content.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　细节理解题。由第四段第一句It comes after more than half of soft drinks sold in shops have had their sugar levels cut by manufacturers so they can avoid paying the tax. (在此之前,超过一半的在商店销售的软饮料已经被制造商降低了糖含量,这样他们就可以避免纳税)可知,有些公司通过降低产品的含糖量来应对糖税。

3From which of the following is the sugar tax collected?

  • A.Most alcoholic drinks.
  • B.Milk⁃based drinks.
  • C.Fruit juices.
  • D.Classic Coke.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

3.D　细节理解题。由倒数第二段中的some high sugar brands, like Classic Coca Cola, have accepted the sugar tax (一些高糖品牌,如经典可口可乐,已经接受了糖税) 和Fruit juices, milk⁃based drinks and most alcoholic drinks are free of the tax, as are small companies manufacturing fewer than 1m litres per year. (果汁、以牛奶为主原料的饮料和大多数含酒精的饮料免税,年产量不足100万升的小公司也是如此)可知,糖税是从经典可口可乐这样的产品中收取的。

4What can be inferred about the adoption of the sugar tax policy?

  • A.It is a short⁃sighted decision.
  • B.It is a success story.
  • C.It benefits manufacturers.
  • D.It upsets customers.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4.B　推理判断题。由最后一段中的show the positive influence the sugar tax is having (显示了糖税正在产生的积极影响)和Helping the next generation to have a healthy and active childhood is of great importance, and the industry is playing its part. (帮助下一代拥有一个健康和活跃的童年是非常重要的,这个行业正在发挥作用)可知,糖税政策是成功的。

疯狂的白银：涨得比黄金猛，跌得比黄金狠

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Silver Prices Are Surging Even Faster Than Gold

    白银价格飙升速度甚至超过黄金

    surge
    v. 急剧增加（soar）
  2. 02

    Silver has been on a breakneck rally for much of the past year, more than doubling in price in 2025 before accelerating in the first few weeks of this year. Prices crossed $100 an ounce for the first time last week, and have set fresh records in the days since.

    过去一年中的大部分时间里，白银价格都处于“狂飙”状态：2025年已翻了一倍不止，今年头几周涨势更是进一步加快。上周，银价首次突破每盎司100美元，并在随后几天不断刷新历史纪录。

    rally
    n. 价格回升及上涨期
    breakneck
    adj. 极快的
  3. 03

    Some of the momentum is explained by investors seeking safe places to park their money during trade tensions, geopolitical conflicts, and worries about inflation and rising debt. Gold is generally a go-to haven asset in times of turmoil, but silver's outsize gains have made it more prominent than usual.

    贸易摩擦、地缘政治冲突，叠加通胀与债务高企的担忧，促使投资者纷纷寻找资金避险渠道，这是白银价格走高的部分原因。动荡时期，黄金向来是首选的避险资产，但白银此次的超额收益使其受关注度远超以往。

    turmoil
    n. 动荡；混乱；剧烈的不安定的状态
    haven
    n. 避风港（risk-off）
  4. 04

    "Silver is behaving like 'gold squared' or 'gold on steroids,'" Citi analysts wrote in a note Tuesday. They recently raised their silver price forecast to $150 an ounce in the next three months.

    花旗（Citi）分析师周二在一份报告中写道：“白银的表现就像‘黄金的平方’，或是‘打了类固醇的黄金’。” 该机构近期将未来三个月的白银价格预期上调至每盎司150美元。

  5. 05

    Silver is a smaller market than gold, with a broader variety of players, which can make it prone to bigger swings. And it has a wider range of industrial uses than gold. Silver is a key material in solar panels, data centers, electric vehicles, electronics and defense equipment. That reliance means that volatility in silver prices can reverberate across the economy.

    与黄金相比，白银市场规模更小、参与者类型更为多元，这使得白银价格更容易出现大幅波动。同时，白银的工业用途远比黄金广泛，它是太阳能板、数据中心、电动汽车、电子产品和国防设备中的关键材料。由于诸多行业对白银的依赖，其价格波动可能在整个经济体系中产生连锁反应。

    volatility
    n.（宏观）波动性，不稳定程度
    reverberate
    v. 余波不断，产生连锁反应
  6. 06

    Silver is also important to artificial intelligence technology. Its ability to conduct electricity has made the metal "much more valuable" to A.I. infrastructure spending as the technology evolves. Soaring copper prices have also been linked to the metal’s utility for data centers and other A.I. infrastructure.

    白银对人工智能技术的发展也至关重要。随着该技术的不断进步，白银优异的导电性能使其在人工智能基础设施投资中的价值“显著提升”。铜价飙升也与其在数据中心等人工智能基础设施中的应用有关。

    evolve
    v. 渐进式改善
  7. 07

    Some forecasters have warned that the rally in silver is running ahead of itself. Silver ranked highest for "bubblelike asset dynamics" in a recent ranking of stocks, commodities and cryptocurrencies by Bank of America, just ahead of gold.

    也有一些预测人士警告称，白银价格此轮上涨过快，已经脱离基本面。在美国银行（Bank of America）近期对股票、大宗商品和加密货币进行的一项排名中，白银在“类泡沫资产动态”这一指标上位居第一，略高于黄金。

Journey Back in Time with Scholars

Classical Provence(13 days)

Journey through the beautiful countryside of Provence, France, with Prof. Ori Z. Soltes. We will visit some of the best-preserved Roman monuments in the world. Our tour also includes a chance to walk in the footsteps of Van Gogh and Gauguin. Fields of flowers, tile-roofed(瓦屋顶)villages and tasty meals enrich this wonderful experience.

Southern Spain(15 days)

Spain has lovely white towns and the scent(芳香)of oranges, but it is also a treasury of ancient remains including the cities left by the Greeks, Romans and Arabs. As we travel south from Madrid with Prof. Ronald Messier to historic Toledo, Roman Mérida and into Andalucia, we explore historical monuments and architecture.

China's Sacred Landscapes(21 days)

Discover the China of “past ages,”its walled cities, temples and mountain scenery with Prof. Robert Thorp. Highlights(精彩之处)include China's most sacred peaks at Mount Tai and Hangzhou's rolling hills, waterways and peaceful temples. We will wander in traditional small towns and end our tour with an exceptional museum in Shanghai.

Tunisia(17 days)

Join Prof. Pedar Foss on our in-depth Tunisian tour. Tour highlights include the Roman city of Dougga, the underground Numidian capital at Bulla Regia, Roman Sbeitla and the remote areas around Tataouine and Matmata, unique for underground cities. Our journey takes us to picturesque Berber villages and lovely beaches.

阅读难度参考：CSE 9

英国新房窗户越做越小，元凶居然是高龄老奶奶？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Britain Has a Tiny-Window Epidemic, and Old Ladies Are to Blame

    英国盛行小窗设计，这一现象竟归咎于老年女性

  2. 02

    In a country that can go months without seeing the sun, windows have long been a key element in construction. Traditional British architecture is characterized by the majesty of Elizabethan halls with their palatial glass, the elegance of Georgian townhouses and their sash windows or the charm of Tudor cottages with their trademark diamond casements.

    英国时常数月不见阳光，窗户历来是建筑设计的重要部分。英国传统建筑风格鲜明：伊丽莎白时代的宅邸配有气派的大面积玻璃窗，恢弘大气；乔治王朝联排住宅的推拉窗尽显雅致；都铎式小屋则以标志性的菱形格窗独具韵味。

    palatial
    宫殿般宏伟的；palatial glass 形容传统大厅里气派的玻璃窗。
    sash
    承托玻璃的活动窗框；sash windows 指靠窗框滑动来开启的窗户。
  3. 03

    Today, a small, older lady should be able to reach every corner of that window for it to be built.

    如今，建筑窗户的设计要求，必须保证身形娇小的老年女性能够触到窗户的每一处位置。

  4. 04

    Guidance from the British Standards Institution on window safety recommends that windows in any new building should accommodate 95% of the U.K. adult population in terms of reach. Scottish recommendations are even more explicit, saying windows “should be cleanable from inside by 95% of the elderly female population, without the need for stretching.”

    英国标准协会发布的窗户安全指引建议，所有新建筑的窗户，要让英国 95% 的成年人伸手可及。苏格兰的相关要求则更为具体，明确窗户 “需保证 95% 的老年女性在室内无需探身踮脚，即可完成擦拭”。

    accommodate
    顾及、满足……的需要；这里指新窗的设计要让绝大多数成人够得到。
    explicit
    明确、说得具体的；苏格兰的建议直接写明应能从室内清洁且不用伸展身体。
  5. 05

    Critics say it’s a prime example of overregulation in the U.K., where following such guidance to the letter, rather than the spirit, generates ugly and ill-proportioned buildings.“You won’t get any attractive buildings by following the regulations,”said Stephen Bayley, chairman of the Royal Fine Art Commission Trust, a British charity that advises on building design.

    批评者认为这是英国监管过度的典型。不少建筑商死守条文、偏离初衷，造出的建筑外观难看、比例失调。英国建筑设计咨询机构皇家美术委员会信托基金会主席斯蒂芬・贝利（Stephen Bayley）表示：“一味遵从这些规定，根本打造不出美观的建筑。”

    overregulation
    管得过严或过细；批评者认为把设计标准照字面执行限制了建筑设计。
    ill-proportioned
    比例失调的；这里指窗户尺寸与建筑立面之间不协调。
  6. 06

    From a design perspective, great buildings have an inspiring effect and affect people’s mood, he said. Having small windows makes buildings, and therefore their surroundings, less charming.

    他表示，从设计角度而言，出色的建筑能陶冶人心、影响心情。狭小的窗户会让建筑及周边环境失去美感。

  7. 07

    “It is difficult to execute such a thing with aesthetic finesse,”said Bayley.

    贝利说道：“依照这类标准设计，很难兼顾建筑的美学格调。”

    finesse
    精巧的处理手法；aesthetic finesse 指既顾及实用要求又处理好外观比例的本领。
  8. 08

    The guidance for window sizing is a recommendation rather than a rule, which means builders in most of the U.K. don’t actually need to adhere to it. Indeed, the British Standards guidance itself states that “in some building types and circumstances other values are appropriate.”

    这套窗户尺寸标准仅为参考建议，并非强制法规，也就是说，英国大部分地区的建筑商本不必严格执行。英国标准协会也在指引中注明：“针对部分建筑类型与特殊场景，可采用其他合理标准。”

    adhere
    遵守、坚持遵循；adhere to it 中的 it 指窗户尺寸建议。

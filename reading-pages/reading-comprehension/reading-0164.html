The Lake District Attractions Guide

Dalemain Mansion & Historic Gardens

History, Culture & Landscape(景观). Discover and enjoy 4 centuries of history, 5 acres of celebrated and award⁃winning gardens with parkland walk. Owned by the Hasell family since 1679, home to the International Marmalade Festival. Gifts and antiques, plant sales, museums & Mediaeval Hall Tearoom.

Open:29 Mar-29 Oct, Sun to Thurs.

Tearoom, Gardens & Gift Shop:10.30-17.00(16.00 in Oct).

House:11.15-16.00(15.00 in Oct).

Town:Pooley Bridge & Penrith

Abbot Hall Art Gallery & Museum

Those viewing the quality of Abbot Hall's temporary exhibitions may be forgiven for thinking they are in a city gallery. The impressive permanent collection includes Turners and Romneys and the temporary exhibition programme has Canaletto and the artists from St Ives.

Open: Mon to Sat and Summer Sundays. 10.30-17.00 Summer. 10.30-16.00 Winter.

Town:Kendal

Tullie House Museum & Art Gallery

Discover, explore and enjoy award⁃winning Tullie House, where historic collections, contemporary art and family fun are brought together in one impressive museum and art gallery. There are four fantastic galleries to visit from fine art to interactive fun, so there's something for everyone!

Open:High Season 1 Apr-31 Oct: Mon to Sat 10.00-17.00, Sun 11.00-17.00.

Low Season 1 Nov-31 Mar: Mon to Sat 10.00-16.30, Sun 12.00-16.30.

Town: Carlisle

Dove Cottage & The Wordsworth Museum

Discover William Wordsworth's inspirational home. Take a tour of his Lakeland cottage, walk through his hillside garden and explore the riches of the collection in the Museum. Visit the shop and relax in the café. Exhibitions, events and family activities throughout the year.

Open: Daily, 09.30-17.30(last admission 17.00).

Town: Grasmere

英国向“外放一族”宣战：100英镑能买回车厢的安静吗？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    People Are So Rude': Can Britain Shhhh Headphone Dodgers on Trains?

    “人们实在太没礼貌了”：英国能否制止火车上那些不戴耳机外放的乘客？

  2. 02

    Many Londoners would agree that there is a set of unwritten rules for travelers on the city's Underground and iconic red buses. Eye contact is largely a no-go. Interactions are kept at a minimum, with exceptions for cute babies and dogs.

    很多伦敦人都会认同，在伦敦地铁以及标志性红色巴士上，乘客之间存在一套不成文的规矩。尽量不要进行眼神接触。人际交流降到最低限度，可爱的婴儿和狗狗除外。

    unwritten
    没有成文但普遍默认的；unwritten rules 指公交车上靠大家自觉遵守的礼节。
    iconic
    有代表性、辨识度很高的；iconic red buses 指一看就让人想到伦敦的红色公交车。
  3. 03

    But in recent years, one long-held convention, that riders should keep their electronic devices quiet, has appeared to be crumbling — in many places around the world — as some travelers scroll videos or play music without headphones.

    但近些年，一项长久以来的惯例 —— 乘客应当让电子设备保持静音 —— 正在瓦解，这种现象在全球多地都有出现：部分旅客刷视频、放音乐却不佩戴耳机。

    convention
    社会惯例；指乘车时把电子设备调成静音的旧规矩。
    crumbling
    逐步瓦解；这里比喻原有的乘车礼节正在失效。
  4. 04

    The government is urging passengers to use headphones on trains or risk being asked to leave the train or face a fine of up to 100 pounds (about $134). The reality of how that will be enforced remains unclear, however.

    英国政府呼吁乘客在列车上使用耳机，违者可能被要求下车，或面临最高 100 英镑（约合 134 美元）的罚款。不过这项规定究竟如何落地执行，目前仍不明确。

    enforced
    被实际执行的；报道区分了公布罚款措施和让乘客遵守措施。
  5. 05

    Patricia Foley has lived in London her whole life and said that she always felt that people using public transportation were polite. In recent years, though, she feels that has changed. "People are so rude now, and I am not sure why," she said. "But how do you implement it? They are not going to spend money on people patrolling this."

    帕特里夏・福利一辈子住在伦敦，她说过去一直觉得乘坐公共交通的人都很有礼貌。但近些年，她感觉情况变了。“现在的人太没礼貌了，我也不清楚原因。” 她说，“但这项规定该怎么落实？政府不会花钱安排人员专门巡查这件事。”

    patrolling
    巡查；受访者怀疑交通部门会不会安排人员在车厢里逐处检查。
  6. 06

    Geoff Newnham said that he found music blaring from people's phones irritating and that he was in favor of the idea of fines "because it gets the message out there." But he, too, was skeptical it could work. "I think the reality is that enforcing those fines is going to be very difficult, and it's probably just going to cause more issues," he said.

    杰夫・纽纳姆表示，手机外放的音乐让他十分烦躁，他支持罚款的想法，“因为这样能把信息传递出去”。但他同样对政策效果持怀疑态度。“现实情况是，执行罚款会非常困难，甚至可能引发更多矛盾。” 他说道。

    skeptical
    持怀疑态度的；受访者赞同罚款的出发点，却怀疑这个办法能否落实。
    blaring
    响亮刺耳地播放；music blaring from phones 强调外放的声音大到打扰别人。
  7. 07

    "I don't think it's going to be effective; it's nonsense," Emma Hawkins, 57, said of the government's plans. "I'd rather they spend more time dealing with other crime."

    57 岁的艾玛・霍金斯谈及政府这项计划时表示：“我觉得这办法没用，纯属空谈。我宁愿他们多花精力处理其他犯罪问题。”

English Market Towns to Visit in the UK

English market towns come in many shapes and sizes. Each has a personality shaped by the goods and services produced and traded for centuries. But each town has more to do than shop.

Hereford, Herefordshire

Hereford has remained a lively market town since 1189. Skirting the town square, you'll find lovely shops, eateries, and the Black and White House Museum. The Hereford Cathedral is the most impressive building in town. It's also home to an ancient library. One of the four original copies of the Magna Carta is displayed there.

Ludlow, Shropshire

Ludlow is known as the Foodie Center of England. Butcher shops, greengrocers, bakeries, and cheese shops line the town square. Bordering the square, the Ludlow Castle is a “must explore” medieval stronghold. The three-day Ludlow Food Festival is held each September.

Shrewsbury, Shropshire

Getting to Shrewsbury Town Center from London is challenging but worth the anxiety. The River Severn has a significant turn through town, almost making an island of Shrewsbury Town Center. The shape creates a perfect market where goods could be shipped and received using the river as a highway. Flowers are everywhere—hanging baskets, window boxes, and planters—just what you imagine in an attractive English market town.

Mevagissey, Cornwall

Even if you haven't been to the small fishing village Mevagissey, you've probably seen it in a movie or British TV show. The working harbor(港口) took shape in 1774. Fishermen go out to sea daily and sell their fish in harbor-side markets. Don't leave the harbor without a traditional Cornish pie. It's delicious.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Where can you find an original copy of the Magna Carta?

  • A.In the Ludlow Castle.
  • B.In the Foodie Center of England.
  • C.In the Hereford Cathedral.
  • D.In the Black and White House Museum.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1. C　细节理解题。根据题干关键信息an original copy of the Magna Carta可定位到第一个小标题下最后一句。根据“One of the four original copies of the Magna Carta is displayed there.”可知,在赫里福德大教堂可以看到《大宪章》的原稿。故选C。

2What is a feature of Shrewsbury Town Center?

  • A.It's situated near a big island.
  • B.It's almost surrounded by water.
  • C.It's known for its flower festival.
  • D.It's easily accessible from London.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

2. B　细节理解题。根据Shrewsbury, Shropshire部分中的“The River Severn has a significant turn through town, almost making an island of Shrewsbury Town Center.”可知,塞汶河在什鲁斯伯里镇中心有一个大转弯,几乎使镇中心成为一个岛屿,这意味着它几乎被水环绕。故选B。

3What does the author suggest visitors do in Mevagissey?

  • A.Try the Cornish pie.
  • B.Watch a British TV show.
  • C.Go fishing in the sea.
  • D.Take pictures of the harbor.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

3. A　细节理解题。根据题干关键词Mevagissey可定位到最后一段。根据倒数第二句“Don't leave the harbor without a traditional Cornish pie.”可知,作者建议去梅瓦吉西渔村的游客品尝康沃尔馅饼。故选A。

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Louvre Heist Leaves France Asking How It Could Have Happened

    卢浮宫大劫案引发法国自省：为何会发生这样的事？

    heist
    n. 劫案（常用于新闻与传媒语境，尤指大型、有预谋的盗窃抢劫）
  2. 02

    The morning after thieves broke into the Louvre Museum in broad daylight, making off with its crown jewels, an embarrassed nation is asking itself a question: How could France let this happen?

    窃贼在光天化日之下闯入卢浮宫，并盗走其镇馆珠宝。次日清晨，整个法国陷入尴尬，国民纷纷自问：法国怎么会让这样的事发生？

  3. 03

    "One thing is certain, it's that we have failed," said Justice Minister Gérald Darmanin, who was once the country's top cop. The theft, he said, succeeded in "giving France a deplorable image."

    “有一点可以肯定，我们失败了。”司法部长杰拉尔德·达尔马宁表示。他曾担任法国最高警衔官员，并称此次失窃“成功给法国蒙上了一层可悲的形象”。

    deplorable
    adj. 非常糟糕的，极度恶劣的
  4. 04

    Some museum staff are placing the blame on years of staffing cuts that have left the Louvre with tough choices over where to position a limited number of security guards and surveillance cameras. The burglars' decision to conduct the heist at 9:30 a.m. local time—right after the museum opened its doors—left museum security juggling competing demands: the evacuation of a large crowd and the need to protect works.

    部分博物馆工作人员将责任归咎于多年来的人员缩减。这一举措使得卢浮宫在部署数量有限的安保人员和监控摄像头时，不得不面临艰难抉择。窃贼选择在当地时间上午9点30分——也就是博物馆刚开门时——实施盗窃，这让博物馆安保陷入两难：既要疏散大量人群，又要保护馆藏文物。

    evacuation
    n. 疏散，转移
    juggle
    v. 杂耍时同时抛接多个球；同时应付多件事
  5. 05

    Finding public funds for any overhaul of the Louvre is going to be a challenge. The fiscal strain has fueled a profound political crisis that has led to the departure of four different prime ministers in just over a year.

    寻找公共资金对卢浮宫进行全面改造，都将是一项挑战。财政压力已引发严重的政治危机，在短短一年多时间里，已有四任总理因此下台。

    overhaul
    n. 大规模整修或改革
    profound
    adj. 深刻的，极度的
    departure
    n. 离职，离任
  6. 06

    The longer France struggles to pull out of the fiscal spiral, the more it risks losing priceless works in heists that French officials say are increasingly well-organized. Historic jewelry are hard to sell intact, French officials say, because potential buyers will know they are stolen. But that doesn't stop the thieves from breaking down the jewels and selling off the stones.

    法国在财政漩涡中挣扎的时间越长，其珍贵文物在盗窃案中失窃的风险就越高。法国官员表示，此类盗窃案的组织性正变得越来越强。他们指出，具有历史价值的珠宝很难完整出售，因为潜在买家会识别赃物。但这并不能阻止窃贼将珠宝拆解，单独出售宝石。

  7. 07

    "The subject of museums and their vulnerability is old," said Culture Minister Rachida Dati. " For 40 years, there was no interest in securing these great museums."

    “博物馆及其安全漏洞问题由来已久。”文化部长拉希达·达蒂表示，“40年来，各方对这些大型博物馆的安保问题始终缺乏关注。”

    vulnerability
    n. 脆弱性

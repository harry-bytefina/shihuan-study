阅读理解

02-2015天津，B

志愿服务、社区与包容

Whether in the home or the workplace,social robots are going to become a lot more common in the next few years.Social robots are about to bring technology to the everyday world in a more humanized way,said Cynthia Breazeal,chief scientist at the robot company Jibo.

While household robots today do the normal housework,social robots will be much more like companions than mere tools.For example,these robots will be able to distinguish when someone is happy or sad.This allows them to respond more appropriately to the user.

The Jibo robot,arranged to ship later this year,is designed to be a personalized assistant.You can talk to the robot,ask it questions,and make requests for it to perform different tasks.The robot doesn't just deliver general answers to questions;it responds based on what it learns about each individual in the household.It can do things such as reminding an elderly family member to take medicine or taking family photos.

Social robots are not just finding their way into the home.They have potential applications in everything from education to health care and are already finding their way into some of these spaces.

Fellow Robots is one company bringing social robots to the market.The company's“Oshbot”robot is built to assist customers in a store,which can help the customers find items and help guide them to the product's location in the store.It can also speak different languages and make recommendations for different items based on what the customer is shopping for.

The more interaction the robot has with humans,the more it learns.But Oshbot,like other social robots,is not intended to replace workers,but to work alongside other employees.“We have technologies to train social robots to do things not for us,but with us,”said Breazeal.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1How are social robots different from household robots?

  • A.They can control their emotions.
  • B.They are more like humans.
  • C.They do the normal housework.
  • D.They respond to users more slowly.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1.B　推理判断题。根据第二段内容可知,如今家庭机器人可以做日常家务,但是社交类机器人则更像是人类的朋友,而不仅仅是工具。例如,这些机器人可判断人的不同心情,从而做出不同的反应。由此可推知与家庭机器人相比,这些机器人更像人类。

2What can a Jibo robot do according to Paragraph 3?

  • A.Communicate with you and perform operations.
  • B.Answer your questions and make requests.
  • C.Take your family pictures and deliver milk.
  • D.Obey your orders and remind you to take pills.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　细节理解题。根据第三段内容可知,你可以和这种机器人说话,问它问题,要求它去执行各种任务,这种机器人不仅可以给出问题的一般答案,而且还会根据不同的家庭成员作出不同的反应,如提醒老人吃药或者拍全家福等。D项中“服从命令”和“提醒吃药”都是这种机器人可以胜任的。

3What can Oshbot work as?

  • A.A language teacher.
  • B.A tour guide.
  • C.A shop assistant.
  • D.A private nurse.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　细节理解题。由倒数第二段的“...is built to assist customers in a store...”可知,Oshbot可以作为售货员,帮助顾客找到他们所需要的商品,并且还能根据顾客需要提出建议。

4We can learn from the last paragraph that social robots will _____ .

  • A.train employees
  • B.be our workmates
  • C.improve technologies
  • D.take the place of workers
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

4.B　细节理解题。根据最后一段第二句可知,设计这种机器人不是用来替代工人的,而是和其他工人一起工作的,因此它们会成为我们的同事。

5What does the passage mainly present?

  • A.A new design idea of household robots.
  • B.Marketing strategies for social robots.
  • C.Information on household robots.
  • D.An introduction to social robots.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

5.D　主旨要义题。概括全文可知,本文主要介绍了社交类机器人,如为家庭提供人性化服务的Jibo robot以及可作为售货员的Oshbot。 评析　本题难度适中。做主旨要义题时切不可“以偏概全”,应该从文章整体的角度进行概括总结。本文第一段为主题段,social robots为文章中心词。注意C项中的household robots不同于social robots。

冰柜变烤箱，数百万欧元食物尽毁：法国超市遭遇史无前例的黑客攻击

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Fridges in French supermarkets hit by cyber attack

    法国多家超市的冰箱遭网络攻击

  2. 02

    Hundreds of French supermarkets have been hit by a cyber attack that caused their fridges and freezers to heat up, spoiling fresh and frozen food estimated to be worth millions of euros.

    法国数百家超市遭到网络攻击，导致冰箱和冰柜温度升高，造成价值数百万欧元的生鲜和冷冻食品变质。

    spoil
    使变质。这里指柜内温度升高后，冷鲜和冷冻食品坏掉。
  3. 03

    Hackers gained control of the software that regulates the temperatures of the refrigeration units, forcing some to warm to 50C overnight, according to one supermarket chain affected. The chief executive of Coopérative U said several of its supermarkets had been hit by the "extremely serious and unprecedented" attack.

    据一家受影响的连锁超市透露，黑客控制了调节冷藏设备温度的软件，导致部分冷藏设备在一夜之间升温至50摄氏度。Coopérative U的首席执行官表示，该公司旗下多家超市遭到了“极其严重且史无前例”的袭击。

    unprecedented
    前所未有的。受影响超市的负责人用它形容这次事件。
    regulate
    调节。这里说软件原本用来把冷柜温度保持在设定范围。
    refrigeration
    冷藏、制冷。refrigeration units 是超市里维持低温的设备。
  4. 04

    More than 100 of the chain's supermarkets were targeted, while 180 stores from Intermarché and its Netto brand were affected, with a spokesman saying that another 100 shops were urgently disconnected from the internet to prevent the same thing happening to them.

    该连锁超市旗下的100多家门店遭到攻击，而Intermarché及其旗下Netto品牌的180家门店也受到波及。一名发言人表示，另有100家门店已被紧急断网，防止遭遇同样的情况。

    disconnect
    断开连接。这里指一些门店主动把相关设备与互联网断开。
  5. 05

    Other major food retailers including Carrefour, were also hit by the attack, which targeted software provided by Danish engineering firm Danfoss. The software allows retailers to manage their cold units remotely.

    包括家乐福（Carrefour）在内的其他主要食品零售商也受到了攻击。此次攻击的目标是丹麦工程公司丹佛斯（Danfoss）提供的软件，使用该软件的零售商可远程管理冷藏设备。

  6. 06

    Normally, supermarket freezers go through a short period of defrosting overnight, in which temperatures rise slightly. But the hackers ordered the units to heat up and stay hot.

    通常情况下，超市的冷柜会在夜间进行短暂的除霜，期间温度会略有上升。但黑客却强制这些冰柜升温并保持高温。

    defrost
    除霜。文中说冷柜正常除霜时温度也会暂时回升。
  7. 07

    The full scale of the attack is unclear, but figures supplied by individual chains suggest about 400 stores were affected out of 15,000 or so overall in France. As a result of the attack, managers arriving at branches of several supermarket chains on Monday morning were hit by a wave of hot air from the freezers. In some places there were even small fires.

    此次攻击的受害总数尚不明确，但根据各连锁店提供的数据，在法国总计约15,000家的超市门店中，有400家左右受到了攻击。此次攻击发生之后，周一早上，当经理们到达各家连锁超市门店，纷纷被冰柜中喷射出的热浪击中。有些地方甚至发生了小型火灾。

  8. 08

    European Food Commissioner Christophe Hansen condemned the hack in a post on X and added: "the weaponisation of food has become a reality."

    欧盟食品专员克里斯托夫·汉森（Christophe Hansen）在X平台的一篇帖子中谴责了这次攻击，并补充道：“将食品武器化已成为现实。”

    condemn
    公开严厉谴责。这里指欧盟官员出面表态批评。
    weaponisation
    把某种事物当作打击手段。这里是官员对食品遭破坏的说法。

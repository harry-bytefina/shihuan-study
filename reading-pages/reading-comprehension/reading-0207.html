12-2017课标全国Ⅲ，D

城市、交通与公共空间

The Intelligent Transport team at Newcastle University have turned an electric car into a mobile laboratory named “DriveLAB” in order to understand the challenges faced by older drivers and to discover where the key stress points are.

Research shows that giving up driving is one of the key reasons for a fall in health and well-being among older people, leading to them becoming more isolated(隔绝) and inactive.

Led by Professor Phil Blythe, the Newcastle team are developing in-vehicle technologies for older drivers which they hope could help them to continue driving into later life.

These include custom-made navigation(导航) tools, night vision systems and intelligent speed adaptations. Phil Blythe explains:“For many older people, particularly those living alone or in the country, driving is important for preserving their independence, giving them the freedom to get out and about without having to rely on others.”

“But we all have to accept that as we get older our reactions slow down and this often results in people avoiding any potentially challenging driving conditions and losing confidence in their driving skills. The result is that people stop driving before they really need to.”

Dr Amy Guo, the leading researcher on the older driver study, explains: “The DriveLAB is helping us to understand what the key stress points and difficulties are for older drivers and how we might use technology to address these problems.”

“For example, most of us would expect older drivers always go slower than everyone else but surprisingly, we found that in 30mph zones they struggled to keep at a constant speed and so were more likely to break the speed limit and be at risk of getting fined. We're looking at the benefits of systems which control their speed as a way of preventing that.”

“We hope that our work will help with technological solutions(解决方案) to ensure that older drivers stay safer behind the wheel.”

1What is the purpose of the DriveLAB?

  • A.To explore new means of transport.
  • B.To design new types of cars.
  • C.To find out older drivers' problems.
  • D.To teach people traffic rules.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

1.C　推理判断题。 短文第一段就明确指出,纽卡斯尔大学智能交通工具研究团队把一辆电动汽车变成了一个移动实验室,其目的是了解老年人驾车所面临的各种挑战,并找到老年人的重要压力点。文章第六段中Amy Guo博士进一步解释说DriveLAB有助于他们了解老年驾车者的重要压力点和所面临的各种困难。由此可判断出C项为正确选项。

2Why is driving important for older people according to Phil Blythe?

  • A.It keeps them independent.
  • B.It helps them save time.
  • C.It builds up their strength.
  • D.It cures their mental illnesses.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2.A　推理判断题。由短文第四段可知,对于很多老年人,特别是独自生活或生活在乡下的老人来说,驾车在他们保持独立自主、不依靠别人就能自由到外面活动方面至关重要。依据上述信息,可判断出正确选项为A。

3What do researchers hope to do for older drivers?

  • A.Improve their driving skills.
  • B.Develop driver-assist technologies.
  • C.Provide tips on repairing their cars.
  • D.Organize regular physical checkups.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3.B　推理判断题。第一个信息点在短文第三段,该段说纽卡斯尔大学研究团队正在为老年驾驶者研发车载技术,希望这些技术能帮助老年人延长驾龄。第二个信息点在最后一段的“technological solutions to ensure that older drivers stay safer behind the wheel”。由以上信息可知,B项正确。

4What is the best title for the text?

  • A.A New Model Electric Car
  • B.A Solution to Traffic Problems
  • C.Driving Services for Elders
  • D.Keeping Older Drivers on the Road
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　主旨要义题。文章主要介绍了科研人员通过把一辆电动汽车改装成汽车实验室来研究如何帮助老年人解决在开车时遇到的问题,以便延长他们独立生活的时间,提高他们的生活质量。

职场博主吹捧的“高能动性”，正在批量制造差劲的人

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    All the Worst People Seem to Want to Be 'High Agency'

    所有最差劲的人似乎都想拥有“高主体性/能动性”

  2. 02

    I first noticed the phrase when it cropped up in conversations among my friends, as a dichotomy: Were we "high agency" or "low agency"? Was inertia or timidity keeping us in a city, a job or a relationship? Or were we the captains of the ships of our own lives?

    我头一次听见这个说法是朋友们聊天时，它以一种二元对立的标签出现：我们是“高主体性（能动性）”还是“主体性（能动性）”的人？我们是出于惰性或胆怯才留在某个城市、某份工作，或某段关系中吗？还是说我们是自己人生真正的主宰呢？

    inertia
    n. 懒惰
    timidity
    n. 胆怯
  3. 03

    The basic idea of "agency" has long been theorized and debated in philosophy, in relation to free will and the human capacity for action. It caught on in Silicon Valley. And then "high agency" wormed its way out of tech and into the broader lexicon, cycling through viral X threads, LinkedIn posts, and podcasts with self-help leanings.

    “主体性（能动性）”这一基本概念在哲学领域早已被广泛探讨和辩论，其核心在于自由意志与人类的行动能力。这个概念最先在硅谷火起来。后来，“高主体性（能动性）”从科技圈悄悄扩散出来，渗透进了大众话语体系：在X的热门讨论里、在领英的帖子里、在教你自我提升的播客里，不停地反复出现。

    theorize
    v. 理论化，提出理论
  4. 04

    The slow, incremental buildup of a life and a career, the accumulation of savings and experience — this seems to hold less appeal to younger generations. And why would it? The future feels volatile and the rewards of labor are unequal, even absurd. Why not have a little agency and bet it all on red?

    生活与事业的缓慢建造，积蓄与经验的积累——这些对年轻一代似乎已不再具有吸引力。这是为何呢？未来充满变数，劳动的回报不仅不公平，甚至十分荒诞。何不掌握一点主动权，来一场豪赌呢？

    volatile
    adj. 变化无常的，动荡不定
    absurd
    adj. 荒谬的
  5. 05

    The valorization of "high agency" is emblematic of a moment when risk-taking is overvalued. Agency is about action, but it tells us nothing of direction. To valorize agency without also emphasizing its purpose allows us to ignore harder questions like: How do I live a good life? And what about the collective good?

    对“主体性（能动性）”的推崇，恰恰反映了当下社会对冒险行为的过度推崇。主体性（能动性）关乎行动，却未能揭示行动的方向。如果只是推崇主体性（能动性）而忽视其目的，会让我们忽略更棘手的问题，例如：我该如何过上美好的人生？而集体的福祉又是什么？

    valorization
    n. 歌颂、赞扬
    overvalue
    v. 高估
  6. 06

    People often refer to "agency" as an A.I.-proof trait. And yet, ironically, we also talk about A.I. in terms of agency. A.I. can act—without its own direction, but with incredible efficiency and effectiveness. Maybe this is the endpoint of all this "high agency": constant hamster wheels of action, unmoored from any values, no compass to be found.

    人们常将“主体性（能动性）”视为一种“人工智能无法拥有”的特质。然而，颇具讽刺意味的是，我们谈论人工智能时，却也常以“主体性（能动性）”为参照。虽然缺乏方向，但人工智能能够行动，而且是以惊人的效率和效果行动。或许，这正是所谓“高主体性（能动性）”的终极归宿：如同永不停歇的仓鼠轮一直保持行动，不锚定任何价值观，也没有任何指引方向的罗盘。

    unmoored
    adj. 没有锚点，漂泊无依的

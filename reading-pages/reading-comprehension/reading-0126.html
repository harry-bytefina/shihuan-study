阅读理解

03-2020新高考Ⅰ，C

学习、阅读与教育方式

In the mid1990s, Tom Bissell taught English as a volunteer in Uzbekistan. He left after seven months, physically broken and having lost his mind. A few years later, still attracted to the country, he returned to Uzbekistan to write an article about the disappearance of the Aral Sea.

His visit, however, ended up involving a lot more than that. Hence this book, Chasing the Sea：Lost Among the Ghosts of Empire in Central Asia, which talks about a road trip from Tashkent to Karakalpakstan, where millions of lives have been destroyed by the slow drying up of the sea. It is the story of an American travelling to a strange land, and of the people he meets on his way：Rustam, his translator, a lovely 24-year-old who picked up his colorful English in California, Oleg and Natasha, his hosts in Tashkent, and a string of foreign aid workers.

This is a quick look at life in Uzbekistan, made of friendliness and warmth, but also its darker side of society. In Samarkand, Mr Bissell admires the architectural wonders, while on his way to Bukhara he gets a taste of police methods when suspected of drug dealing. In Ferghana, he attends a mountain funeral (葬礼) followed by a strange drinking party. And in Karakalpakstan, he is saddened by the dust storms, diseases and fishing boats stuck miles from the sea.

Mr Bissell skillfully organizes historical insights and cultural references, making his tale a well-rounded picture of Uzbekistan, seen from Western eyes. His judgment and references are decidedly American, as well as his delicate stomach. As the author explains, this is neither a travel nor a history book, or even a piece of reportage. Whatever it is, the result is a fine and vivid description of the purest of Central Asian traditions.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What made Mr Bissell return to Uzbekistan?

  • A.His friends’ invitation.
  • B.His interest in the country.
  • C.His love for teaching.
  • D.His desire to regain health.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

1. B细节理解题。根据第一段第三句(几年后，他仍然眷恋着这个国家，于是他重返乌兹别克斯坦来写一篇关于咸海消失的文章)可知，他依然对这个国家充满了感情和兴趣，这种力量让他又来到了这里。故选B项。

2What does the underlined word “that” in paragraph 2 refer to?

  • A.Developing a serious mental disease.
  • B.Taking a guided tour in Central Asia.
  • C.Working as a volunteer in Uzbekistan.
  • D.Writing an article about the Aral Sea.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2. D猜测词义题。由第一段最后一句可知，他回乌兹别克斯坦是为了写一篇关于咸海消失的文章。由第二段第一句（然而，他的访问最终涉及的远不止那件事）可知，画线单词指的是写一篇关于咸海的文章。故选D项。

3Which of the following best describes Mr Bissell’s road trip in Uzbekistan?

  • A.Romantic.
  • B.Eventful.
  • C.Pleasant.
  • D.Dangerous.
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3. B推理判断题。第三段描述了他领略了Samarkand的建筑奇迹、在去往Bukhara的路上被警察怀疑贩毒、在Ferghana参加了一个葬礼、在Karakalpakstan遇到了沙尘暴等经历。由这些经历可推断出他的这次旅途是多姿多彩的(eventful)。故选B项。

4What is the purpose of this text?

  • A.To introduce a book.
  • B.To explain a cultural phenomenon.
  • C.To remember a writer.
  • D.To recommend a travel destination.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

4. A推理判断题。本文第一段写了Bissell先生回到乌兹别克斯坦的目的，中间介绍了他写的这本书，最后表明：这不是一本游记，也不是一本历史书。不管它是什么，它都形象地描述了中亚地区的传统。由此推断出作者写这篇文章就是为了介绍一本书。故选A项。 长难句 Mr Bissell skillfully organizes historical insights and cultural references, making his tale a well-rounded picture of Uzbekistan, seen from Western eyes. 译文 Bissell先生巧妙地组织了历史见解和文化借鉴，使他的故事从西方人的角度全面展现了乌兹别克斯坦。 分析 本句是一个简单句。making...是现在分词短语作状语；seen...是过去分词短语作状语。

美国今日中期选举，加州会诞生"全球首例"亿万富豪税吗？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    Are California voters about to impose 'world's first' tax on billionaires?

    impose
    v. 推行，强制施行
  2. 02

    Few places on the planet have as many ultra-wealthy residents as California, a US state known for glamour, coastal mega-mansions, and being home to the rich and famous. Now voters are about to decide whether the mega-rich among them- including media mogul Oprah Winfrey, Meta head Mark Zuckerberg, Skims cofounder and reality television star Kim Kardashian, and filmmaker George Lucas - should hand over billions of dollars to pay for improvements to healthcare.

    地球上很少有地方像加州这样居住着如此众多的超级富豪。这个美国州以奢华、海湾豪宅以及富豪名流云集而闻名。如今，加州选民即将投票决定：是否应该让他们当中的超级富豪——包括传媒大亨奥普拉·温弗瑞（Oprah Winfrey）、Meta掌门人马克·扎克伯格（Mark Zuckerberg）、Skims联合创始人兼真人秀明星金·卡戴珊（Kim Kardashian），以及电影制作人乔治·卢卡斯（George Lucas）——拿出数十亿美元，为改善医疗保障提供资金。

    glamour
    n. 浮华，光环 ；魅力
  3. 03

    Proposition 40 is aimed at levying a one-time 5% tax on the net worth of all of California's billionaires.

    第40号提案旨在对加州所有亿万富豪的净资产征收一次性5%的税。

    levy
    v. 征收
    proposition
    n. 提议，建议；（哲学领域）指需要证明的观点、命题
  4. 04

    The vote is also offering regular residents a voice on an issue that has roiled politics for decades. The state has the largest share of homeless people in the nation and one of the highest wealth gaps.

    这次投票也让普通民众有机会对一个几十年来持续搅动加州政坛的议题表达自己的意见。加州拥有全美比例最高的无家可归者，同时也是全美贫富差距最大的地区之一。

    roil
    v. 搅动，使翻滚
  5. 05

    Those who back the measure have argued it helps ensure that the ultra-wealthy are contributing their fair share and will help backfill gaps in the state's budget.

    支持这项提案的人认为，这有助于确保超级富豪承担自己应尽的那份税收责任，同时也能填补州政府预算中的资金缺口。

  6. 06

    While the ballot measure has many supporters - including some billionaires like Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang - it has faced fierce opposition from a variety of figures, from billionaires to the state's liberal governor.

    尽管这项公投提案有不少支持者——其中包括英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）这样的亿万富豪——但它也遭到了来自各方人士的强烈反对，反对者既有亿万富豪，也包括加州自由派的州长。

  7. 07

    California Governor Gavin Newsom, whose tenure is coming to an end, is perhaps the most notable political leader opposing it. He has voiced concerns that the measure could cause the wealthy to leave the state and drive out their crucial income tax revenue.

    即将结束任期的加州州长加文·纽森（Gavin Newsom），或许是反对该措施的最引人注目的政治领袖。他曾表达担忧，认为这项提案可能导致富豪离开加州，并使该州失去他们所贡献的大量所得税收入。

    tenure
    n. 任期
    notable
    adj. 显著的、突出的
  8. 08

    Senator Bernie Sanders of Vermont, a vocal champion against income inequality, has campaigned in support of Prop 40. "They have all the wealth, they got all the power, and who do you think you are challenging them? Well, I think on November 3 they're going to learn a very powerful lesson."

    佛蒙特州联邦参议员伯尼·桑德斯（Bernie Sanders）一直是反对收入不平等的坚定发声者，他也公开支持40号提案。“他们拥有所有的财富，掌握着所有的权力。你们以为自己是谁，竟然敢挑战他们？好吧，我想，到了11月3日，他们会得到一个深刻的教训。”

    vocal
    adj. 直言不讳的，畅所欲言的

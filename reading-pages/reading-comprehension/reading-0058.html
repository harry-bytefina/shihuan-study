阅读理解

07-2018天津春，C

学习、阅读与教育方式

If you were bringing friends home to visit, you could show them the way. You know the landmarks—a big red house or a bus-stop sign. But what if you were swimming in the middle of the Atlantic Ocean? Could you still find your way home? A loggerhead turtle（海龟）could.

According to Dr. Ken Lohmann, loggerheads have a magnetic（磁力的）sense based on Earth's magnetic field. It helps them locate the best spots for finding food and their home beaches.

Scientists already know that several other animals, such as whales and honeybees, can detect（探测到）magnetic fields. The difference between them and loggerheads, however, is the way they learn to use their magnetic sense. Young whales and honeybees can learn from adults. Loggerheads are abandoned as eggs.

As newborn loggerheads have no adults to learn from, what helps them figure out how to use their magnetic sense? Lohmann thinks one of the cues was light on the sea.

Baby loggerheads hatch only at night. However, a small amount of light reflects off the ocean. The light makes that region brighter. Heading toward the light helps them get quickly out to sea, where they can find food. Lohmann tested whether newborn loggerheads use this light source to set their magnetic “compasses”（罗盘）. He and his team put some newborns in a water tank and recorded which way they swam. Around the tank, the scientists created a magnetic field that matched the Earth's. They set a weak light to the east of the magnetic field. Then they let the newborns go.

At first, the newborns swam toward the light. After the scientists turned off the light, the turtles that had seen the light in the east always swam toward east. When the researchers reversed（颠倒）the magnetic field, these turtles turned around and swam toward the new “east”.

This and the follow-up experiments all showed that loggerheads use light from the outside world to set their magnetic “compasses” and then remember the “correct” direction. If a turtle hatches on a brightly-lit beach, that would damage its magnetic sense forever and make survival hard for the turtle.

Lohmann's work has led others to protect the habitat of this endangered species. Yet many questions about these creatures remain unanswered, and researchers have a lot to study.

1Loggerheads and whales differ in the way they .

  • A.bring up their young
  • B.recognize landmarks
  • C.detect magnetic fields
  • D.learn to find directions
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

1. D细节理解题。由题干中的关键词Loggerheads、whales、differ定位到文中第三段第二句“The difference between them and loggerheads, however, is the way they learn to use their magnetic sense.”可知选D。learn to use their magnetic sense与learn to find directions表达的是同一个意思。

2What is needed for newborn loggerheads to set their magnetic sense?

  • A.Weak light reflected off the ocean.
  • B.Help from adult loggerheads.
  • C.Bright sunlight from the sky.
  • D.Food in warmer waters.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

2. A细节理解题。由第四段的内容并结合第五段的第二句和第五句以及第七段首句可知选A。

3In the experiment, after the newborns' magnetic sense was set, their moving direction was determined by .

  • A.the light
  • B.the magnetic field
  • C.other unknown factors
  • D.the light and the magnetic field
查看答案与讲解

答案：B

3. B推理判断题。由文中提到的实验并结合倒数第二段首句“This and the follow-up experiments all showed that loggerheads use light from the outside world to set their magnetic ‘compasses’ and then remember the ‘correct’ direction.”可知选B。

4What is the significance of Lohmann's research work?

  • A.It enables researchers to keep track of turtles.
  • B.It contributes to the studies of the magnetic field.
  • C.It offers a new solution to environmental pollution.
  • D.It helps protect the loggerheads' living environment.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4. D细节理解题。由尾段首句“Lohmann's work has led others to protect the habitat of this endangered species.”可知选D。

5What could be the best title of the passage?

  • A.Experiments on Loggerheads
  • B.The Survival of the Sea Turtle
  • C.The Loggerhead's Built-in “Compass”
  • D.Comparison of Loggerheads and Other Animals
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

5. C主旨大意题。通读全文可知，继首段引出主题后，文章都在讲述一种海龟是如何通过自身的磁场感应能力来辨别方向的，故选C。

外刊延伸

我用这个方法，治好了狗狗的分离焦虑症

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    When Serenity Strull rescued her dog Margot, she knew right away that the three-year-old pitbull mix struggled with anxiety.

    当塞伦妮蒂·斯特鲁尔（Serenity Strull）救助她的爱犬玛戈（Margot）时，她一眼就看出这只三岁的比特混血犬正深受焦虑症的困扰。

  2. 02

    Once she got Margot home, Strull quickly learned her dog's biggest trigger was being left alone. She consulted various trainers and doctors and tried several different calming techniques to no avail. Finally, one thing worked: playing classical music or other instrumental tracks that didn't have intense percussion.

    把玛戈领回家后，斯特鲁尔很快发现，独处最容易诱发它的焦虑。她咨询了多位训犬师和医生，尝试了各种安抚技巧，却都无济于事。最后，她终于发现了一个方法：播放古典乐，或者其他没有强烈打击乐节奏的器乐曲。

    no avail
    We use this expression when someone tries very hard to do something, but it does not work or it doesn't get the result they want.
    instrumental tracks
    We use the term "instrumental tracks" for pieces of music that are just made instruments.
  3. 03

    One of Margot's trainers recommended Through a Dog's Ear, a series of slow-tempo, predominantly piano compositions designed to help reduce stress in dogs. It was created by Joshua Leeds, a psychoacoustic expert, and Susan Wagner, a veterinary neurologist, who have studied the effects of this music on dogs.

    玛戈的一位训犬师向她推荐了《听懂犬心》（Through a Dog's Ear），这是一系列节奏缓慢的乐曲，其中多是钢琴乐，专门用于缓解犬类的压力。该系列由心理声学专家约书亚·利兹（Joshua Leeds）和兽医神经学家苏珊·瓦格纳（Susan Wagner）共同创作。

    slow-tempo
    "Slow-tempo" describes music that has a slow speed, a slow rhythm.
    predominantly
    something is mostly one thing, even if there are small parts of something else.
  4. 04

    They observed over 150 dogs in private homes and kennels and found that more than 70% of kennel dogs and 80% of dogs in homes exhibited fewer anxiety symptoms (including pacing, trembling, and panting) after listening to predominantly piano music.

    通过对私人家庭和犬舍中150多只狗狗的观察研究，他们发现，在听过这些以钢琴为主的音乐后，超过70%的犬舍狗狗和80%的家庭宠物犬表现出的焦虑症状（包括来回踱步、发抖、气喘）显著减少了。

  5. 05

    "The most sensible explanation is that there is something inherently relaxing about this genre of music, perhaps resulting in the release of chemicals that bring about a pleasurable state of mind," says Deborah Wells, an animal behaviour researcher at Queen's University Belfast.

    “最合理的解释是，这类音乐本身就带有放松特质，或许它能诱发大脑分泌某些化学物质，从而带来愉悦的心境，”贝尔法斯特女王大学（Queen's University Belfast）的动物行为研究员德博拉·威尔斯（Deborah Wells）表示。

    a pleasurable state of mind
    This means a feeling where someone feels happy, calm, or comfortable.
    inherently relaxing
    If something is "inherently relaxing", it naturally makes people feel calm, without needing effort or explanation.
    genre of music
    A genre of music is a type of music, the style of music, with similar features.
  6. 06

    Whether you try Through a Dog's Ear or a personally curated mix of classical music, finding what works for your pet will likely take some trial and error. But if Margot's experience is any indication, the right soothing sounds can make all the difference.

    无论你是尝试《听懂犬心》系列组曲，还是亲手为宠物精心拟定一份古典乐歌单，寻找合适音乐的过程往往需要一些摸索和试错。但如果说玛戈的经历能给我们一点启示，那便是一旦选对了安抚心灵的声音，一切都会大不相同。

    soothing sounds
    "Soothing sounds" are gentle, quiet sounds that help people relax or feel calm.

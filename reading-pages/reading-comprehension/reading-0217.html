阅读理解

10-2016课标全国Ⅲ，B

学习、阅读与教育方式

On one of her trips to New York several years ago, Eudora Welty decided to take a couple of New York friends out to dinner. They settled in at a comfortable East Side cafe and within minutes, another customer was approaching their table.

“Hey, aren't you from Mississippi?”the elegant, white-haired writer remembered being asked by the stranger.“I'm from Mississippi too.”

Without a second thought, the woman joined the Welty party. When her dinner partner showed up, she also pulled up a chair.

“They began telling me all the news of Mississippi,”Welty said.“I didn't know what my New York friends were thinking.”

Taxis on a rainy New York night are rarer than sunshine. By the time the group got up to leave, it was pouring outside. Welty's new friends immediately sent a waiter to find a cab. Heading back downtown toward her hotel, her big-city friends were amazed at the turn of events that had changed their Big Apple dinner into a Mississippi state reunion(团聚).

“My friend said:‘Now we believe your stories,’”Welty added.“And I said:‘Now you know. These are the people that make me write them.’”

Sitting on a sofa in her room, Welty, a slim figure in a simple gray dress, looked pleased with this explanation.

“I don't make them up,”she said of the characters in her fiction these last 50 or so years.“I don't have to.”

Beauticians, bartenders, piano players and people with purple hats, Welty's people come from afternoons spent visiting with old friends, from walks through the streets of her native Jackson, Miss., from conversations overheard on a bus. It annoys Welty that, at 78, her left ear has now given out. Sometimes, sitting on a bus or a train, she hears only a fragment(片断)of a particularly interesting story.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1What happened when Welty was with her friends at the cafe?

  • A.Two strangers joined her.
  • B.Her childhood friends came in.
  • C.A heavy rain ruined the dinner.
  • D.Some people held a party there.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

1.A　细节理解题。根据文章第三段...the woman joined the Welty party. When her dinner partner showed up...可以得知选A。干扰项为C项,虽然有大雨,但是没有破坏他们的晚餐,所以排除C选项。

2The underlined word“them”in Paragraph 6 refers to Welty's _____ .

  • A.readers
  • B.parties
  • C.friends
  • D.stories
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

2.D　猜测词义题。根据上文中的Now we believe your stories可以推断出选D。

3What can we learn about the characters in Welty's fiction?

  • A.They live in big cities.
  • B.They are mostly women.
  • C.They come from real life.
  • D.They are pleasure seekers.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

3.C　推理判断题。根据第八段中的I don't make them up和最后一段,可以推断出她的小说中的人物来自真实的生活。

解锁伦敦顶级荣誉，第一件事竟是赶羊过桥？

词汇梳理
  1. 01

    What does it mean to get the Freedom of the City of London?

    获得“伦敦金融城自由民”这一荣誉称号，意味着什么？

  2. 02

    BBC Radio London presenter Robert Elms has become the latest person to receive the Freedom of the City of London. It's an honour that has been presented to those from royalty and sports stars, to Hollywood actors and business leaders. But what does it really mean — and why is it seen as being connected to sheep?

    BBC伦敦电台主持人罗伯特·埃尔姆斯（Robert Elms）近日成为最新一位获授“伦敦金融城自由民（Freedom of the City of London）”荣誉的人。这一称号曾被授予过王室成员、体育明星、好莱坞演员以及商界领袖。但它究竟意味着什么——又为什么会和“羊”扯上关系呢？

  3. 03

    "It is really, really old," says Laura Miller, the City's Clerk to the Court of the Chamberlain. The common belief is the honour was first given in the 1230s.

    “这是一个非常非常古老的传统，”伦敦市财政法院书记官劳拉·米勒（Laura Miller）说道。一般认为，这项荣誉最早可以追溯到十三世纪三十年代。

  4. 04

    While being a "freeman" originally meant a person who was not the property of a feudal lord, the definition changed over time. In London, it came to mean someone who had been granted the Freedom of the City and was therefore allowed to trade within the Square Mile.

    “自由民（freeman）”一词最初的含义是指那些不隶属于封建领主、拥有个人自由的人。随着时间推移，这一定义发生了变化。在伦敦，它特指被授予“城市自由权”的人——也就是说，他们被允许在一平方英里大小的金融城内进行贸易。

    feudal
    封建制度下的；feudal lord 指掌控土地、对依附者享有权力的封建领主。
    granted
    被正式授予的；had been granted the Freedom 指获得了伦敦市的身份或荣誉。
  5. 05

    Ms Miller says these days the Freedom of London is more like a "voluntary citizenship" that gives recipients "a sense of belonging".

    米勒表示，如今的“伦敦城自由民”更像是一种“自愿的公民身份”，它赋予获得者一种“归属感”。

    voluntary
    自愿参与的；voluntary citizenship 是将现代荣誉比作个人自愿认同的市民身份。
    citizenship
    公民身份；在本文中是比喻，用来说明荣誉带来的归属感。
    recipients
    获得者；指伦敦市授予这项荣誉的人。
  6. 06

    Every year a celebrity and hundreds of the City's freeman drive a small flock of sheep across a London bridge for charity. This quirk goes back to a time when there was only one crossing over the Thames into the City with tariffs being charged to use it — although not for those who had the Freedom.

    每年都会有一位名人，以及数百位获得该荣誉的“自由民”，把一小群羊赶过伦敦的一座桥，为慈善事业募款。这一奇特的传统可以追溯到很久以前，那时泰晤士河上只有一座桥可以进入伦敦城，而过桥需要缴纳通行税，持有“自由民”身份的人则可免税。

    flock
    一群羊或鸟；drive a flock of sheep 在这里是把一小群羊赶过桥。
    quirk
    不同寻常的小细节或惯例；指获授荣誉的人会赶羊过桥这一趣事。
    tariffs
    规定收取的费用；这里说过桥的通行费，免缴通行费曾是获授身份者的特权。
  7. 07

    "As a freeman or free sister, you could bring your goods across and until the 12 or 1300s, really the most valuable thing was sheep... so being able to bring them across that bridge without paying extra taxes was valuable," says Ms Miller.

    米勒解释道：“作为‘自由民’或‘自由姊妹’，你可以携带货物过桥。而在十三、十四世纪时，最珍贵的货物其实是羊……所以能够免税将羊群带过桥，可是件极有价值的事。”

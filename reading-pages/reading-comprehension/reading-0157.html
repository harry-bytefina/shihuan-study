06-2021全国甲，B

生态、气候与城市自然

Port Lympne Reserve, which runs a breeding(繁育)programme, has welcomed the arrival of a rare black rhino calf(犀牛幼崽). When the tiny creature arrived on January 31, she became the 40th black rhino to be born at the reserve. And officials at Port Lympne were delighted with the new arrival, especially as black rhinos are known for being difficult to breed in captivity(圈养).

Paul Beer, head of rhino section at Port Lympne, said:“Obviously we're all absolutely delighted to welcome another calf to our black rhino family. She's healthy, strong and already eager to play and explore. Her mother, Solio, is a first⁃time mum and she is doing a fantastic job. It's still a little too cold for them to go out into the open, but as soon as the weather warms up, I have no doubt that the little one will be out and about exploring and playing every day.”

The adorable female calf is the second black rhino born this year at the reserve, but it is too early to tell if the calves will make good candidates to be returned to protected areas of the wild. The first rhino to be born at Port Lympne arrived on January 5 to first⁃time mother Kisima and weighed about 32kg. His mother, grandmother and great grandmother were all born at the reserve and still live there.

According to the World Wildlife Fund, the global black rhino population has dropped as low as 5500, giving the rhinos a “critically endangered” status.

全部题目与逐题讲解

1Which of the following best describes the breeding programme?

  • A.Costly.
  • B.Controversial.
  • C.Ambitious.
  • D.Successful.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

1.D　推理判断题。根据第一段第一句中的 “has welcomed the arrival of a rare black rhino calf”和最后一句中的“as black rhinos are known for being difficult to breed in captivity”可知, 圈养状态下繁育黑犀牛是困难的,而保护区迎来了一头新生黑犀牛幼崽,所以这个繁育项目是非常成功的, 故答案为D项。

2What does Paul Beer say about the new⁃born rhino?

  • A.She loves staying with her mother.
  • B.She dislikes outdoor activities.
  • C.She is in good condition.
  • D.She is sensitive to heat.
查看答案与讲解

答案：C

2.C　细节理解题。根据第二段中 “She's healthy, strong and already eager to play and explore.” 可知,新生犀牛身体状况良好,故答案为C项。

3What similar experience do Solio and Kisima have?

  • A.They had their first born in January.
  • B.They enjoyed exploring new places.
  • C.They lived with their grandmothers.
  • D.They were brought to the reserve young.
查看答案与讲解

答案：A

3.A　细节理解题。根据第一段第二句中的“When the tiny creature arrived on January 31”及第二段中的“Her mother, Solio, is a first⁃time mum and she is doing a fantastic job.”可知Solio是一月份第一次产崽。根据第三段第二句中的“The first rhino to be born at Port Lympne arrived on January 5 to first⁃time mother Kisima”可知 Kisima也是一月份第一次产崽,故答案为A项。

4What can be inferred about Port Lympne Reserve?

  • A.The rhino section will be open to the public.
  • B.It aims to control the number of the animals.
  • C.It will continue to work with the World Wildlife Fund.
  • D.Some of its rhinos may be sent to the protected wild areas.
查看答案与讲解

答案：D

4.D　推理判断题。根据第三段第一句中的“but it is too early to tell if the calves will make good candidates to be returned to protected areas of the wild”可知,有些犀牛可能被送回野生动物保护区,故答案为D项。

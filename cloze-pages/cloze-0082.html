Regardless of the weather or the distance, Paul Wilson will make sure low-income students in his neighbourhood arrive at their college classes on time.

A retired engineer, 76-year-old Wilson has been free rides to college students for the past eight years. The students who he's have gone on to become physicians, teachers and engineers, but what they've also got out of their time in school is finding a role model and a friend in Wilson. Some students call him “Grandpa”.

Tina Stern rides from Wilson for all her four years in college, and the trips meant much more to her than just free .“It's not just a ride;you're not just sitting there in silence or with your headphones on,” Stern said.“He asks you questions and actually the answers, so the next time you ride with him, he'll those things.”

Wilson first worked as a driver through a student-support programme of the non-profit organisation, On Point for College. For many students, Wilson's help is not only appreciated, it's also entirely for them to be able to complete their college education.

第 16 空

原卷第 31 题回到原文

Family love is like a seed — it may take time to 16________, but once it does, it grows into something strong and beautiful.

第 18 空

第 19 空

原卷第 34 题回到原文

19________, we should look deeper to find the hidden love.

逐题讲解本站补充讲解

答案 CInstead
